## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): 4/5 for guidance pack, 4/5 for columns
variety used: Standard Nepali in Devanagari, consistent; official ङ् spellings (अङ्ग्रेजी, सङ्ख्या) with slips such as अंग / युक्तिसंगतता. Nepal legal vocabulary is real (देवानी, फौजदारी, हदम्याद, अपकृत्य, वारेसनामा, अधिवक्ता), not Hindi (no सितम्बर, no सुनवाई 2). Blog columns 005, 006, 008 and 009 drop into short spoken lines.
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- क्षेत्र / क्षेत्रीय slides between industry, region and “expectations”: "स्थलको जाँच तथा क्षेत्रका अपेक्षा".
- Rechtssubjekt calqued as कानुनी विषय / अधिकारको विषय instead of कानुनी व्यक्ति: "त्यो छुट्टै कानुनी विषय होइन".
- बाटो stock-calques channel, route and procedure: "चार भाषा मध्ये कुनै प्रयोग गर्न नसक्दाको बाटो".
- Nepal number format missing: "TWD 1.57M" and Western "TWD 500,000" / "TWD 2,000,000,000"; ROC years 109 and 112 without the CE year.
- Register split: formal legal prose in the guidance pack and in 001, 004, 007; one-line blog dumps with blank lines in 003 Q16–Q20, 005, 006, 008, 009.
- English chips left in UI and bios: "यो फिल्ड आवश्यक छ।", "फलो गर्नुहोस्", "म्यान्डेट".

Guidance pack: the required negatives hold (no Nepali consultation, no interpreter, no reply-time promise, no free first meeting, no result guarantee). Wei Tseng is feminine where Nepali marks gender ("फर्मकी प्रबन्ध अधिवक्ता", "विनिमय छात्रा", "उहाँले … गर्नुभयो"). Columns 001, 002, 004 and 007 are dense professional Nepali; 003 Q1–Q15 is the cleanest procedural prose. 005 correctly labels Korean bank practice as an example and generalises the hiring question to foreign nationals. 007 replaces the Korea child-residence frame with "ताइवान र अर्को देश" / "ताइवानबाहिर". 003 labels 119 / 110 / 112 as Taiwan numbers. Internal links are /ne/. No masculine verb for Wei Tseng: Nepali चाहन्छु / दिन्न / टुङ्ग्याउँछु does not mark gender, and 006/008/009 use खुलाइहालेकी, हेरेकी, बताएकी, ताइवानकी.
