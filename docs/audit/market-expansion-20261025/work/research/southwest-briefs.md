Source-supported thesis: Separate the direct record route in Company Act210 from court inspection under245. A small investment does not create an unlimited right to every invoice, and an unanswered demand does not remove the statutory threshold for court inspection.

Verified primary law:

The board keeps articles, shareholder meeting minutes and financial statements; shareholder registers and corporate bond counterfoils are kept at the company or the designated agent as specified. A shareholder or creditor must show interest and specify the requested scope to request inspection/copying. Explain the actual record category rather than promising all underlying ledgers.
- Company Act245, current operative text: https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=J0080001&flno=245 . A shareholder who has continuously held at least1% of issued shares for at least6months may provide reasons, evidence and necessity and ask the court to appoint an inspector within the necessary scope concerning business accounts, property, specified transactions and records. The threshold applies to245, not automatically to210.
- Current full law and commencement context: https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawAll.aspx?pcode=J0080001 . Latest amendment shown2025-12-26; the later commencement language concerns amended provisions, not a new2026 creation of210/245.

## HI 929 — Indian technical-service invoice and Taiwan treaty classification

Proposed slug: indian-technical-service-fees-taiwan-tax-treaty
Working Hindi title: ताइवान से तकनीकी सेवा का भुगतान: भारत–ताइवान कर समझौते में10% कब लागू होता है?
Reader and trigger: An Indian resident service company invoices a Taiwan customer for engineering, technical consultancy, management or a mixed software/technology contract. The payer proposes withholding and asks for treaty documents before releasing payment. The decision is how to classify the actual fee and assemble evidence; not an instruction to choose a label to reduce tax.
Source-supported thesis: The current India–Taiwan agreement contains an express fees-for-technical-services rule in Article12. The10% gross ceiling has conditions and does not make every service payment a flat10% case.

Primary source opened through the official MOF download, saved and text-extracted:
- Official treaty PDF https://www.mof.gov.tw/eng/download/10458 .
- Local preserved PDF: work/research/southwest-india-treaty.pdf; SHA256 d4b811c8f1deebe2d8bcd6fb445e9f8d41bfa687fbbcf0f8d12e5ec5c482309d.
- Text: work/research/southwest-india-treaty.txt.
- Official treaty network: https://www.mof.gov.tw/eng/singlehtml/264?cntId=82780 . Updated2026-09-04, expressly lists India among comprehensive agreements signed and in force. Use this current network for treaty existence, not a search vendor's treaty summary.
Article12(1) permits recipient-territory tax;12(2) allows source tax under domestic law but limits it to10% of the gross amount when the beneficial owner is resident in the other territory. Article12(3)(b) defines technical-service fees as managerial, technical or consultancy services, including technical/other personnel, except payments within14/15. Article12(3)(a) separately defines royalties; classify software/IP/license/equipment elements on their actual facts. Article12(4) routes effectively connected PE/fixed-base income to7/14. Article12(5) contains source rules. Article12(6) excludes the excessive part caused by a special relationship from the negotiated cap. Protocol1 preserves more beneficial domestic treatment; Article28 addresses arrangements whose primary or one primary purpose is treaty benefits and entities without bona fide business activities.
Critical correction: This treaty provision is Article12, not12A. Its technical-service definition has no “make available” condition. A Hindi search returned a third-party tax page that was internally inconsistent on this point; do not copy it.
Status: Current official treaty text, signed2011-07-12; current network confirms an existing comprehensive agreement. Do not announce a new2026 tax rate or claim2026 cooperation MoU itself changes the treaty. Exact effective date and application procedure can be omitted unless separately re-opened and verified.

Current Taiwan procedure independently opened by researcher and HI author: 適用所得稅協定查核準則 Article25, https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawAll.aspx?PCode=G0340125 , amended2025-04-08. The recipient supplies the other territory's tax-authority residency certificate and evidence of beneficial ownership under the treaty; the withholding declaration identifies the treaty article and includes calculation evidence. Technical-service/royalty supporting documents include the contract with a Chinese translation and detailed fee calculations. Articles20/21 also support classification of mixed payments and priority of specifically addressed treaty income. No refund deadline is asserted here.

## HE 933 — Jointly owned Taiwan patent and a third-party licence

Proposed slug: israeli-taiwan-joint-patent-license-consent
Working Hebrew title: פטנט משותף בטאיוואן: אפשר לייצר לבד, אבל מי רשאי לתת רישיון?
Reader and trigger: An Israeli company and Taiwanese R&D/commercial partner jointly own a granted Taiwan invention patent. The Israeli company plans to let an unrelated contract manufacturer or licensee use the invention. The business must distinguish its own implementation from a third-party licence and obtain the necessary consent.
Primary law opened:
- Taiwan Patent Act64: https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=J0070007&flno=64 . In joint ownership, acts other than own exploitation—including assignment, trust, licensing, pledge or abandonment—require all coowners' consent. Anchor article to the exact wording and distinguish exploitation by the coowner from authorizing an independent third party; do not assume every contractor is automatically covered by “own exploitation.”
- Patent Act7(3): https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=J0070007&flno=7 . Paid commissioned R&D ownership is determined by contract; absent agreement the application/patent right belongs to the inventor, while the funder may exploit the invention. Funding alone is not a statutory shortcut to sole ownership. Explain only if the reader first needs to verify that a claimed joint patent is actually owned as assumed.
- Current full law: https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawAll.aspx?pcode=J0070007 . Current displayed amendment2022-05-04; no pending-effective notice for these provisions.
Patent65 verification completed after the browser fetch failed: the same official HTTPS endpoint was fetched with process-local public DNS resolution and normal TLS verification. Current original HTML and extracted text are preserved as work/research/southwest-patent65-official.html and .txt; HTML SHA2562211a9c80fb2b745f34edb8abfeb2f6eea09698efe521df88471ea4dc51b458d. Official URL: https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=J0070007&flno=65 . A coowner may not assign, entrust or pledge its share without the other coowners' consent; an abandoned share belongs to the other coowners. The provision itself does not state a proportional-allocation formula. Distinguish dealings in a share (65) from the jointly owned patent (64).
Thesis: Patent64's own-exploitation exception is a distinct legal decision from ownership of confidential information or software copyright. A patent coowner cannot assume that independently exploiting the invention includes granting an outside business a licence.
Next action: Verify patent register, named owners, commissioned-research and employment assignments; identify the actual third party, activities, territory, sublicensing and improvements; obtain the necessary written coowner agreement before commercial authorization.
