## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): N/A for guidance pack (out of scope; part a), 4/5 for columns
variety used: Standard written Estonian (kirjakeel), consistent. No dialect mixing, no foreign-script leftovers (no Hangul, kana or Cyrillic anywhere in 010–018); the Traditional Chinese glosses are intact and sit on the right terms. The decimal comma is right ("0,5 meetri"), "100 %" keeps the required space and the quotation marks are the Estonian „…“. Thousands still use a period instead of a space, and two files still carry invisible U+200B characters.
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- "uurida" is still the all-purpose verb for English "consider / check / verify", so the reader is told to investigate things he is meant to weigh: "võib uurida kriminaalkaebust (告訴)" (uurima appears 23× in 016, 19× in 018).
- "kinnitada" is the all-purpose verb for "establish / determine / identify", which is not what kinnitama means: "tuleb esmalt kinnitada loetelu ja pärandvara õiguslik iseloom" (39× in 016, 22× in 014).
- "haldusrahv" is not an Estonian word — the noun is trahv: "Haldusrahve vale või liialdatud reklaami (虛偽誇大廣告) eest on TWD 40.000–TWD 200.000".
- Thousands still use a period instead of a space: "TWD 1.579.589".
- "Kuigi" still does duty for "even if": "Kuigi üks seadusjärgsetest eeldustest on täidetud".
- Verbs that need a case form or an et-clause still take a bare da-infinitive: "Ei piisa esitada kogusummat".

012 is native professional; the r4 defects ("kavatses", "ega … tulemust") are fixed and only the 本所 calque "see büroo" is left. 010 and 013 are close behind: "Tegelikud õigused ja kasutatavad võimalused", "loetakse tõendatuks", "24. jaanuari 2022. aasta esimese astme otsusega", "kahte isikut tõendavat dokumenti" and "ei ole ülemäära range" all read natively now. 011, 014, 016, 017 and 018 are professional with the local defects below; 014 in particular lost almost all of its r4 problems ("kaks alternatiivset", "jõustub", "mõistlikku proportsionaalsussuhet", "Tööandja kontrollnimekiri"). The product facts hold: attorney Wei Tseng is "Advokaat Wei Tseng (曾雋崴)" in 011, 014, 016 and 017 and no masculine form, pronoun or title is used for her ("Meesüliõpilane" in 010 is the male plaintiff; "Esindasin" and "soovitan" are unmarked first person). The consultation lock holds — 013 and 018 name English, Chinese (中文), Japanese and Korean only and both refuse a reply-time promise; there is no Estonian consultation, no interpreter, no free first meeting, no result guarantee, no win rate and no "best/only" wording. "Võite tasuta uurida" in 015 is Taipei city's own free pre-check, not a free meeting with the firm, and the "garantiid" in 017 are contractual warranties in an M&A clause. "Eelnev on juhis lepingu sõnastamise kohta." in 018 is not listed below: the English source says the same ("The foregoing is contract-design advice."), so it is a source question, not a translation defect.
