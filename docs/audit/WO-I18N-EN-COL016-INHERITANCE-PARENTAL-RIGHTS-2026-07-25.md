### 1. Statutory Heirs and Intestate Shares

FAQ 1 must be the first paragraph. Explain Article 1138’s order—descendants,
parents, siblings, grandparents—and Article 1144 concurrent spouse
inheritance. Check parentage, adoption, order of death, succession by
representation, disqualification, and waiver. Explain that an intestate share
does not itself assign a particular account or property.

### 2. Wills and Identifying the Estate

Explain that a valid will may alter distribution, subject to reserved portions
and other mandatory rules. Before calculating shares, identify assets, debts,
legal and beneficial ownership, co-ownership, security interests, beneficiary
designations, trusts, retirement or death benefits, lifetime transfers, and
governing law.

### 3. The Surviving Spouse’s Residual-Property Claim

FAQ 2 must be the first paragraph. Keep the Article 1030-1 claim separate from
inheritance. Address the statutory marital-property regime, property acquired
during marriage, marital debts, inherited or gifted property, solatium and
other exclusions, and possible judicial adjustment when equal distribution
would be manifestly unfair. Never infer entitlement from marriage duration,
title, or acquisition date alone.

### 4. Inherited Debts and Waiver of Inheritance

State that Article 1148 generally limits inherited-debt liability to the value
of property acquired from the estate, subject to statutory procedures and
exceptions. Address inventory, creditor notice or payment, preservation, and
the possible loss of limited-liability protection for statutory misconduct.
Under Article 1174, waiver requires a written declaration to the court within
three months after the heir learns of the right to inherit; oral statements,
private agreements, or non-participation are insufficient.

Include exactly:

`The Ministry of Finance’s Taiwan Tax Portal page “Inheritance Procedures and Required Documents” was updated on June 25, 2026. It describes general three-month periods for court procedures concerning an estate inventory and waiver of inheritance, and the general six-month period for filing an estate tax return. The starting date, extensions, exceptions, and jurisdiction must still be checked for the matter at hand; this general guidance must not be used to calculate an individual deadline.`

### 5. The Surviving Parent’s Rights and Duties

FAQ 3 must be the first paragraph. Explain Article 1089 using parental rights
and duties, not an automatic custody award. Cover care and upbringing,
residence, health and education decisions, legal representation, property
management, existing orders, restriction or suspension, conflicts, and
cross-border issues. Distinguish parental rights from inheritance.

### 6. Guardian Designation and Court Involvement

Explain that Article 1091 guardianship applies when a minor has no parent or
both parents cannot exercise rights and assume duties. One parent’s death does
not alone commence guardianship while the other can act. Cover testamentary
appointment under Article 1093, the statutory order and court selection
authority under Article 1094, the best-interests criteria under Article
1094-1, and applications by relatives or other statutorily authorized
persons.

### 7. Protecting a Minor’s Inherited Property

FAQ 4 must be the first paragraph. Explain Articles 1087 and 1088, ownership
by the child, segregation and records, parental joint management, use and
income, the statutory limit on disposition, Article 1086 conflicts and special
representation, and guardian inventory, reporting, disposition limits, and
court supervision.

### 8. Cross-Border Family Issues

Address nationality, domicile, habitual residence, asset location, foreign
marriage or divorce, existing parental-rights orders, international
jurisdiction, recognition and enforcement, foreign wills and status
documents, authentication and translation, and tax and registration
obligations. Require analysis under the Act Governing the Choice of Law in
Civil Matters Involving Foreign Elements and any other applicable regime.
