A renter in Taipei wired a deposit before signing a lease, backed out the same day, and sued for it back. They lost.
The court said the lease already existed: rent and a one-year minimum were agreed at the viewing.

It was NT$21,800, a month's rent. Their stuff wouldn't fit, they said. The company said no.

Going through the ruling, I noticed the LINE was the renter's own evidence. It called the money a "rental deposit" (承租訂金). Backing out over space was on them, with no sign of company fault.
