## FACTS

Held, so a later edit should not reverse them:

- The 24 hours in art. 93 para. 2 run from 拘提 or 逮捕, not from arrival at the prosecutor. “검찰에 도착했다고 시간이 새로 시작되지는 않는다” and the matching JA/EN/ZH sentences are right.
- If the prosecutor does not apply for 羈押, art. 93 para. 3 requires release, and bail, 責付, or 限制住居 only where an art. 101 or art. 101-1 ground exists and detention is unnecessary. The drafts’ “법정 요건 / 法定の要件 / statutory conditions / 符合法定情形” hedge can stay.
- Art. 93-1 item 5 (counsel-intent wait, cap 4 hours) and item 6 (interpreter wait, cap 6 hours) are caps on time actually spent waiting without questioning, not an automatic add-on. “不得有不必要之遲延” and “經過時間內不得訊問” are stated in all four. The art. 31-1 exception is a different 4-hour rule (appointed counsel still absent after more than 4 hours, and the defendant proactively asks to be questioned). All four keep both conditions. Do not merge the two 4-hour rules.
- A judge orders 羈押, and only after questioning the defendant (art. 101 para. 1). The prosecutor does not order it.
- “Not every foreigner gets a free lawyer from the first police interview” matches art. 31 para. 5 (disability or indigenous status at the investigation stage) and art. 31-1 (the detention hearing). Keep the denial.
- A spouse or parent may independently retain counsel (art. 27 para. 2, 配偶 and 直系血親). The article does not require them to be in Taiwan.
- Art. 93-3 para. 1: an investigation-stage restriction imposed by the prosecutor 不得逾八月. “8 months,” not 8 days, is right. What is missing is item 2 below.
- EN “Foreign nationality is not itself a listed detention ground” matches the art. 101 list. It does not say weak local ties are irrelevant to flight risk. Keep it.
- EN on counsel (attend, notes, views, art. 245 para. 2 limits) and EN/ZH on reading or showing the record (art. 41 paras. 2–3, applied to police by art. 43-1) match the text. A requested change is 附記, appended, not an automatic rewrite of the original lines.
- Art. 91 as now in force is not a 24-hour release rule. If the designated place cannot be reached within 24 hours, the person is sent first to a nearer court or prosecutors’ office to check identity (https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=C0010001&flno=91). Do not add a release-at-24-hours rule from art. 91. Art. 93-1’s cross-reference is that travel clock, not a second release deadline.
- Immigration Act art. 21 para. 1 item 1 matches facts.md (移民署 prohibits departure when a judicial authority notifies a restriction). The drafts do not use it. Do not turn this column into an immigration-detention piece.
