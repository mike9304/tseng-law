# Taiwan Injury Settlement: Who Can Withdraw the Complaint, and Until When?

Signing a settlement and withdrawing a criminal complaint are two separate acts in Taiwan. No article was found that makes a private settlement erase a complaint. As of October 2026, the Code of Criminal Procedure attaches its effects to the second act: the complainant withdrawing the complaint (撤回告訴).

## Which injury offenses need a complaint

[Criminal Code Art. 287](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=C0000001&flno=287) lists three offenses prosecuted only on complaint (告訴乃論): [Art. 277](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=C0000001&flno=277), paragraph 1 (injury to another person's body or health); [Art. 281](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=C0000001&flno=281) (violence against a lineal blood ascendant that causes no injury); and [Art. 284](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=C0000001&flno=284) (injury by negligence). This column covers the first and the last. The proviso carves out one case only. A public official who commits the Art. 277, paragraph 1 offense while carrying out official duties falls outside the complaint requirement.

Injury that causes death or serious injury is Art. 277, paragraph 2. It is not on the list, so the complaint period and withdrawal rules below do not apply to it. Negligence that causes serious injury stays inside Art. 284 and remains complaint-only.

## Six months, counted for each person

The complaint period is six months. Under [Code of Criminal Procedure Art. 237](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=C0010001&flno=237), it runs from the time a person entitled to complain learns who the offender is. If several people are entitled and one of them misses the period, the others are not affected.

Who is entitled? A victim may complain ([Art. 232](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=C0010001&flno=232)), and so may the victim's statutory agent or spouse, independently ([Art. 233](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=C0010001&flno=233), para. 1). An authorized agent may file the complaint. The written authorization goes to the prosecutor or judicial police officer, and either may order the complainant to appear in person when they consider it necessary ([Art. 236-1](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=C0010001&flno=236-1)).

Sometimes an investigation is already under way and nobody has complained. Under [Art. 242](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=C0010001&flno=242), paragraph 2, a prosecutor or judicial police officer who finds during an investigation that all or part of the facts amount to a complaint-only offense with no complaint filed must ask the victim, or any other person entitled to complain, whether they wish to complain. The question is put when that person appears and gives a statement, and the answer goes into the record.

## Withdrawal is open until oral argument closes

In a complaint-only case, the complainant may withdraw the complaint before oral argument closes at first instance. The person who withdraws may not complain again ([Art. 238](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=C0010001&flno=238)). A complaint against one accomplice, or a withdrawal for one, takes effect for the other accomplices too ([Art. 239](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=C0010001&flno=239)).

After a withdrawal, the stage of the case decides what follows. During the investigation the prosecutor must issue a non-prosecution disposition under [Art. 252](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=C0010001&flno=252), item 5. Once the case has been indicted, the court must pronounce a judgment that the case is not entertained (不受理) under [Art. 303](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=C0010001&flno=303), item 3. Withdrawing before the settlement money arrives is the subject of [a separate column](/en/columns/taiwan-criminal-settlement-withdraw-complaint).

## When mediation counts as withdrawal

Four conditions appear in [Art. 28](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=I0020003&flno=28), paragraph 2 of the Township and County-Administered City Mediation Act (鄉鎮市調解條例). The matter is a complaint-only criminal case. Mediation succeeds during the investigation or before oral argument closes in the court of first instance. The mediation agreement (調解書) states that the parties agree to withdraw. And the court approves it (核定). When all four are met, the complaint or private prosecution (自訴) is deemed withdrawn at the time mediation succeeded.

The Act places these committees in township, town and county-administered city offices (鄉、鎮、市公所, [Art. 1](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=I0020003&flno=1)), and [Art. 35](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=I0020003&flno=35), paragraph 2 applies it to district (區) mediation committees in special municipalities and cities. Within ten days of the day mediation succeeds, the office must send the mediation agreement and the file to the referring or competent court for review ([Art. 26](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=I0020003&flno=26), para. 1). Without court approval there is no deemed withdrawal.

[Art. 27](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=I0020003&flno=27) adds two effects after approval. The parties may not sue, complain or bring a private prosecution over that matter again. Where an approved criminal mediation provides for payment of a fixed quantity of money, other fungible things or securities, the mediation agreement may also serve as an enforcement title (執行名義).

What if mediation fails? Under [Art. 31](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=I0020003&flno=31), when a person entitled to complain applied for mediation of a complaint-only criminal matter and the mediation does not succeed, the office refers the matter to the competent prosecutor for investigation, on the application that person made to the mediation committee. The complaint is then deemed to have been filed at the time mediation was applied for. The six months of Art. 237 are tested against that deemed filing.
