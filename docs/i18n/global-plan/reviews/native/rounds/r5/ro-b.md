## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): N/A for guidance pack (part a, out of scope), 4/5 for columns
variety used: standard Romanian of Romania, consistent. Not Moldovan. Diacritics ă â î ș ț with comma-below throughout; quotation marks are Romanian „ ”. Dates are day month year (24 ianuarie 2022, 13 septembrie 2025, 1 ianuarie 2023, 4 februarie 2026, 5 iunie 2026, 25 iunie 2026, 1 iulie 2026, 17 septembrie 2026) and the first of the month has no ordinal. Thousands take a dot (1.579.589; 25.000.000; 100.000.000), decimals a comma (0,5 metri), currency stands after the amount (1.579.589 TWD). One currency slip remains: „dolari din Taiwan” instead of „dolari taiwanezi” (011). All Chinese glosses are Traditional; no Simplified character, no Hangul, no stray English, German or French word anywhere in these nine files.
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- English “alone / mere” is still rendered „singur(ul/a)”, which in Romanian says “the only”: "Singura hotărâre oficială de primă instanță nu permite confirmarea" (8 places, 010, 011, 016, 017).
- The digit 1 still stands where Romanian needs the article or pronoun un/o/unul: "când 1 dintre părinți nu poate exercita" (016, 017, 018).
- Numerals of 20 and above are not followed by „de”, although 017 itself writes „20 de vehicule”: "preaviz de 30 zile" (014, 011, 018).
- The French/English negation order survives as „orice X nu Y” instead of „nu orice X Y”: "Totuși, orice investiție străină nu urmează același organ și aceeași procedură".
- Abstract Latin-looking nouns stand where Romanian has a concrete term: „subiect” for entity, „comerț” for business, „facultăți” for powers, „tablou” for table, „cărțile” for records: "Facultățile de administrare, de uz, de fructe și de dispoziție".
- 013 and 015 still carry U+200B-only lines and sentences broken across a blank line, unchanged since round 4; the identical artifact sits in columns-de, columns-fr, columns-it and columns-pl, so it is a pipeline defect, not a Romanian one: "indicarea regiunii este suficientă (exemplu: orașul Taipei). ​".

012 is native professional apart from three small slips. 015 and 013 read like counsel’s prose once the invisible characters and the calqued question headings are set aside. 010, 011, 014, 016, 017 and 018 are professional, but each still carries the recurring „singur”/digit/„de” defects, and 016 has one sentence that reverses the English meaning. Wei Tseng is feminine wherever she is named — 010 „Am fost avocata” and „cerere scrisă a avocatei”, 011 „Avocata Wei Tseng (曾雋崴)” and „avocatei responsabile”, 014 „Wei Tseng (曾雋崴), avocată în Taiwan”, 016 and 017 „Avocata Wei Tseng (曾雋崴)”. 013 and 015 say „un avocat din Taiwan”, not her; 012 and 018 do not name her. No masculine form for her remains. The consultation lock holds: „consultanța nu se face în limba română” (010, 013, 018), „nu promitem un termen de răspuns” (015), „nu garantează” (012), „Limbile de convorbire realmente disponibile … trebuie confirmate la momentul contactului” (018). No interpreter, no free first meeting, no 24-7, no win rate, no guarantee, no „cel mai bun” or „singurul cabinet”. „gratuit” in 015 is the Taipei premises inquiry, not a consultation. The jump from section 5 to section 7 in 018 and the missing attorney signature in 013 and 015 both match the English source and the other locales, so neither is a Romanian defect.
