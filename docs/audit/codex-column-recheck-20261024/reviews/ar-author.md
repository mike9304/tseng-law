Both alternatives require NIA-approved residence or permanent residence.

At body line 68, the former «أسرة المهني الأجنبي المؤهل» is replaced by «أسرة المهني الأجنبي الذي يزاول عملًا مهنيًا في تايوان، أو أسرة المهني الأجنبي المتخصص»، followed expressly by NIA residence/permanent-residence approval «في كلتا الحالتين». FAQ 3 at line 29 is aligned. This is a substantive eligibility clarification, not merely a meaning-preserving style edit. The ordinary-professional current-work condition is not imposed on the separate special-professional alternative.
