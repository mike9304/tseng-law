## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): N/A for guidance pack (out of scope; part a), 4/5 for columns
variety used: Standard written Estonian (kirjakeel), consistent. Quotation marks are already „ ”. Dates are day. month year (24. jaanuar 2022, 5. juuni 2026, 17. september 2026). The decimal comma is already right (0,5 meetri). Thousands still use a period, and the currency code often stands before the amount.
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- The currency code stands before the amount, and thousands use a period: "TWD 1.579.589". The same pattern runs through 011, 017 and 018.
- A date is still opened with a relative clause: "Alates kuupäevast, mis on 1. juuli 2026". Also "Alates kuupäevast, mis on 1. jaanuar 2023" in 015.
- Kinmen and Lienchiang are joined with *ja* in the 017 FAQ and in the repeated paragraph, while the next paragraph already uses *või*: "Kinmenis (金門) ja Lienchiangis ehk Matsus".
- Formal legal prose still uses the spoken verb *klappima*: "kokku lepitud töö klapivad". Also in 014 and 017.
- 013 and 015 still carry U+200B breaks, including lines that contain only that character: "piisab piirkonna märkimisest (näide: Taipei linn)."
- A legal object is still the wrong noun: "pidurdusvaru" (012, time to brake), "8 ühikut" (017, new trucks), "ühe kirje järeldus" (016, one issue).
Attorney Wei Tseng is unnamed by gender wherever she appears (010 *Esindasin … kohtuesindajana*; 011, 014, 016 and 017 *Advokaat Wei Tseng*). No masculine pronoun or title for her remains. The consultation lock holds: 013 and 018 name only English, Chinese, Japanese and Korean (both also have 中文), and both refuse an immediate reply. No Estonian consult, no interpreter, no reply-time promise, no free meeting, no result guarantee. "tasuta uurida" in 015 is the Taipei pre-inquiry, not a free consultation. "lapse parimad huvid" is the statutory best-interests test, not a superlative claim. 012 is close to native professional apart from *pidurdusvaru*. 014 and 016 are close; they already use *leppetrahv*, *sundosa*, *üldõigusjärglane* and *asenduspärimine*. 013 and 015 still read as a chopped post.
