# Hungarian native review — round 5, part a (hu)
reviewer: Claude Opus 5 (1M) · date: 2026-09-23 · scope: docs/i18n/global-plan/reviews/native/input/hu-guidance.txt (365 strings, all 10 guidance pages + team bios + inquiry form + FAQ answers + llms.txt notices); src/content/columns-hu/001, 002, 003, 004, 005, 006, 007, 008, 009 (all read in full; English/German versions consulted for 003, 005, 006, 007, 008)

## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): 4/5 for guidance pack, 3/5 for columns
variety used: standard Hungarian (köznyelvi + jogi szaknyelv), formal "Ön" address, consistent throughout; no regional or orthographic split. Rule compliance is clean: consultation is stated as English/Chinese/Japanese/Korean only, no interpreter, no reply-time, no free first meeting, no result promise; no win-rate, guarantee or "best/only" claim anywhere (E: nothing found). Wei Tseng is "ügyvédnő" / "vezető ügyvédnő" in every occurrence, guidance and columns alike (F: nothing found). No stray Korean, Japanese or Latin-script source text in the body copy; the Hangul in 008/009 is confined to the untouchable source `url:` frontmatter (G: one gloss issue only, row 47).
The guidance pack is close to publishable: the problems left there are a handful of officialese passives, two real meaning slips and some typography. The columns are the weak half — the legal substance is carefully rendered, but the surface still carries English/Korean sentence mechanics, English number and currency layout, and in the narrative tails of 003 and 008 the register collapses.
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- "azonnal" (= immediately) is used throughout where the meaning is "automatically/eo ipso", so time statements replace logical ones: "nem teszi azonnal az anyavállalat tajvani állandó telephelyévé" (004, 007 ×7 total).
- Verb-government errors repeat unchanged: "következtetni" takes -ra/-re, but the text writes "nem kell azt következtetni" (002, 004, 007 — 5 occurrences).
- English number/currency layout survived the translation: "legalább TWD 500.000", "50 %-ra", "1-nél több", "3. állambeli" — Hungarian puts the code after the number, spaces the thousands, closes up the percent sign and writes small numerals out.
- The em dash (—) is used as gondolatjel instead of the Hungarian en dash (–): "A menetet a határidők — köztük az elévülés — és a bizonyítékok teljessége szabják meg." (guidance 6×, columns 70+).
- Legal terms drift from Hungarian usage where an everyday word looked close enough: "befejezheti a munkaszerződést" (should be megszünteti), "büntetőjogi előélet", "a teljes verseny ára", "nemvagyoni kár".
- Register is not held in the Q&A/case-story tails: "A sértett ezért mélyen megbántódik, és haragra gerjed" (003 Q20), "a társaság állománya haragjában távozásra utasította" (008) — archaic or wrong-word storytelling inside a law-firm article.
