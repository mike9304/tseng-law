Observed in the continuing author/reviewer session on 2026-10-26 KST. These are focused reads, not a complete fresh legal review of every linked page. Manuscript source dates remain October 24–25. The three exact manuscript SHAs are in `lv-independent-manifest.json`.

- [Employment Service Act, official full English text](https://law.moj.gov.tw/ENG/LawClass/LawAll.aspx?pcode=N0090001): directly read Article 50 and the Article 53 employer-change context. The article's covered students have a 20-hour weekly limit outside summer/winter vacation. LV705 qualifies the students and preserves those exceptions. LV706 does not turn transfer-specific termination evidence into a condition for every additional employer. The page displayed amendment date 2025-01-20.
- [Foreign Professionals Act, official full English text](https://law.moj.gov.tw/ENG/LawClass/LawAll.aspx?pcode=A0030295): directly read Articles 12, 15, 16 and 17. Checked new-graduate/NIA-extension scope, spouse permit application, the two sponsor classes and common NIA condition, and the qualified extension route. The page displayed amendment date 2025-09-24.
- [The same Act's official history](https://law.moj.gov.tw/ENG/LawClass/LawHistory.aspx?pcode=A0030295): a direct open initially returned an error; following the official full-law page's History link returned the readable page. Read the amendment/commencement entry: the specified deferred provisions start June 30, 2026 and the remaining provisions, including Article 16, January 1, 2026. LV705's Article 16 commencement sentence is supported. The dates are not inferred from amendment date alone.
- [Residence regulations, official full English text](https://law.moj.gov.tw/ENG/LawClass/LawAll.aspx?pcode=D0080129): directly read Articles 7, 9, 11 and 20. Article 11 requires the extension application before existing residence expires; approval grants one year beginning after the prior authorized period ends, with one further extension up to two total. LV706 does not require approval completion before expiry. The law database displayed a data cutoff of 2026-10-16; no assertion is made about later unpublished changes.
