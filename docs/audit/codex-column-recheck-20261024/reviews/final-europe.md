### EU-L1 — Article 16(2) sponsor eligibility

Historical Italian 705:68 (initial reviewed hash `49f4fef1401a2d97005daf3254e5bef83a910a71eaf2284c8493ce161d6008b9`) described the sponsor with the exact span “familiari del professionista che abbia già ottenuto la residenza o la residenza permanente”. This omitted the ordinary foreign professional's professional work in Taiwan and did not distinguish that branch from the foreign special-professional category.

The [current official Article 16(2)](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=A0030295&flno=16) separates those two sponsor branches and requires prior NIA residence or permanent-residence permission for either. The repair direction was to state both branches and their shared prior approval, while retaining the eligible relatives, entry route and approval of the family's application.

The adopted Italian sentence at `src/content/columns-it/705-taiwan-visa-residence-work-study-family.md:73` is: “La persona da raggiungere deve essere un professionista straniero che svolge lavoro professionale a Taiwan oppure un professionista straniero specializzato; in entrambi i casi deve avere già ottenuto dalla NIA l’autorizzazione alla residenza o alla residenza permanente.”

The corresponding explicit scope now appears in FR 705:59–61, DE 705:66, ES 705:65–67 and PT 705:64; the PT FAQ at :29 is also consistent. I checked the final wording in all five, not just the authors' completion messages. Spouses, minor children and adult children unable to care for themselves because of physical or mental disability remain the stated relatives; parents are not silently included. Visa-free/visitor entry permits an application under the conditions, not automatic residence or guaranteed issuance.

Meaning assessment: a substantive eligibility correction/clarification, expressly authorized and supported; it must not be described as a purely stylistic rewrite.

### EU-L2 — first ARC through a Taiwanese spouse

The original PT 704:44 began: “No primeiro pedido como cônjuge de cidadão taiwanês com registo de agregado familiar em Taiwan, a NIA exige prova do casamento registado em Taiwan e a presença do cônjuge taiwanês.” It did not expressly state the sponsor's actual residence in Taiwan. DE and ES also needed this scope to be explicit where the first-card rule was described.

[Residence Regulations Article 12(1)(4)](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawAll.aspx?pcode=D0080129) uses the cumulative conditions of current residence in Taiwan and household registration. The [NIA 0902 guidance](https://www.immigration.gov.tw/5475/5478/141465/141808/141970) supplies the first-application attendance and one-year card instructions.

The adopted PT 704:47 begins: “Há condições específicas para o primeiro pedido como cônjuge de cidadão taiwanês que reside em Taiwan e aí tem registo de agregado familiar.” DE 704:25, :55 and :67 now carry both conditions; ES 704:52 supplies them and :54 ties the first-card rule to that category. The marriage-registration, attendance and one-year conditions remain. FR and IT already supplied the residence/household scope in the relevant category descriptions.

Meaning assessment: substantive scope clarification, not merely style. DE adds the direct regulation URL inline and in the source list; no prior URL was removed. This is not a general one-year rule for every family ARC.

### EU-L3 — Italian migrant-worker source attribution

An intermediate IT 705:60 at hash `fc2f92e…` attached the span “che regolano autorizzazioni, documentazione e possibilità di ricongiungimento per la loro categoria” to the NIA 0926 link. The [official page and its three-page attachment](https://www.immigration.gov.tw/5382/5385/7244/7250/7317/%E5%B1%85%E7%95%99/181964/) concern migrant ARC applications, extensions/changes and supporting documents; they do not establish family-reunification eligibility.

The repair direction was to confine that citation to what it supports and leave employment/family questions as category-dependent matters. The current IT 705:65 now says: “Ai lavoratori migranti delle categorie soggette al regime specifico è riservata una procedura NIA per le domande e i rinnovi dell’ARC, con i relativi documenti.” The next sentence separately explains that employment authorization and family routes depend on the particular category. The profession-portal limitation remains.

Meaning assessment: corrects an overbroad intermediate source attribution without changing the final family-eligibility conclusion. The full final 705, FAQ and ending were reread. The Italian independent reviewer expressly recorded this correction, including the limit on the earlier paragraph-combination suggestion.
