## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): 4/5 for guidance pack, 3/5 for columns
variety used: Khalkha Mongolian in Cyrillic, as written for readers in Mongolia; consistent. Not mixed with Traditional Mongolian script, Buryat, or Kalmyk. Formal Та is kept. Quotation marks are « ». Chinese glosses stay in Traditional characters.
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- German "begleiten" became "дагалдана", as if the firm escorts the client: "гадаадын үйлчлүүлэгчдийг Тайваньд шаардлагатай алхмуудаар дагалдана"
- English "alone" became the unidiomatic "нэгээр": "энэ лавлагааны үр дүн нэгээр бусад зөвшөөрөл"
- Money uses an English code-first amount, a comma for thousands, and "M": "TWD 1.57M", "TWD 500,000", "0.5 сарын"
- A statute (條例) is called a regulation (журам) while 法 is correctly "хууль": "Ажилтны тэтгэврийн тухай журам (勞工退休金條例)"
- From 003 Q16, and in 006, 008, 009, a personal blog is cut into one-line paragraphs, with "Эмэгтэй өмгөөлөгчийн хувьд" where the noun needs no gender marker
- Korea is labeled as one example (001, 004, 005, 008, 009); 007 already says "Тайвань болон өөр улс". The real misses are legal terms, not the Korea frame

No F findings. Wei Tseng is "Эмэгтэй өмгөөлөгч", "тэрбээр", "төлөөлж", "гаргуулсан". Mongolian verbs do not mark gender. The first person in 003, 006, 008 and 009 is not masculine; "би Эмэгтэй өмгөөлөгч" is an unnatural label, not a masculine title. No consultation-language, interpreter, reply-time, free-first-meeting, or result promise: the denials stay negative. "хүүхдийн хамгийн сайн ашиг сонирхол (子女最佳利益)" is the statutory test. "ердийн эсвэл баталгаатай үр дүн гэж байхгүй" and "хорих ялаас зайлсхийх амлалт биш" are denials. "цорын ганц гарал үүслийн улс биш" denies that Korea is the only home country. The one advertising-like line is the 008 table cell "Хялбар". Columns 001, 002, 004 and 007 read as professional legal Mongolian. 003 is the same until Q16. 005 generalized the Korean remitter and the hiring question; the opener still tells the reader to open the article they are already reading. The Hangul `url` slugs in 008 and 009 match the source posts and should not be rewritten.
