## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): N/A for guidance pack (out of scope; part a), 4/5 for columns
variety used: Standard Finnish (yleiskieli), consistent. Dates are day–month–year with the month in the essive (13. syyskuuta 2025, 4. helmikuuta 2026, 5. kesäkuuta 2026, 1. heinäkuuta 2026, 25. kesäkuuta 2026, 1. tammikuuta 2023, 17. syyskuuta 2026, 24. tammikuuta 2022). The decimal comma is right (0,5 metrin). Thousands still use a period.
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- Thousands still use a period: "1.579.589 TWD".
- "Pelkkä seikka, että" still wraps an ordinary fact: "Pelkkä seikka, että allekirjoitettu teksti on olemassa".
- "kaupunginhallitus" is the Finnish municipal board, not 市政府: "Lisäksi kaupunginhallitus (市政府) vaatii".
- 013 and 015 still hide U+200B, and several lines are only that character: "seudun ilmoittaminen riittää (esimerkki: Taipein kaupunki)."
- Decide/consider/disclose is still "määrätä", "tutkia" or "ei salli": "on määrättävä, kenelle maahantuonti luovutetaan".
- Every read-time label is a bare partitive: "10 min lukuaikaa".

012 is native professional apart from the verb "poiketa … mukaan" and "oikeus" for the regulations. 015 is native apart from the invisible characters, "kaupunginhallitus" and one wrong subject ("tarkastus olisi vapautettu"). 018 is native professional apart from "käy tarkastuksen" and the missing 中文 gloss. 010, 011, 013, 016 and 017 are professional with local defects. 014 is strong legal Finnish apart from "määräaikainen" for periodic training and a cluster of agreement and word-order errors. Attorney Wei Tseng is unnamed or "asianajaja" wherever she is named (011, 014, 016, 017). 010 "Olin … oikeudenkäyntiasiamies" is the statutory office in the Code of Judicial Procedure, used for women; the first person is unmarked. 013 "minulle" / "olen" / "suosittelen" is unmarked. No masculine pronoun or title for her remains. The consultation lock holds: 013 names English, Chinese (中文), Japanese and Korean; 018 names the same four and omits the 中文 gloss. No Finnish consult, no interpreter, no reply-time promise, no free meeting, no result guarantee, no win rate, no "best/only". "Lapsen etu" is the best-interests test, not a superlative claim. The section jump from 5 to 7 in 018 matches the English source. Kinmen is "tai" Lienchiang (Matsu). The Q3 review in 013 correctly says the review is not particularly strict.
