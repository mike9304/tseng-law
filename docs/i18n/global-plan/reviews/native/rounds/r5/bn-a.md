variety used: Modern standard Bengali (চলিত), consistent for Bangladesh and West Bengal. No Hindi or Sadhu mixing, no stray Korean, English, German or Chinese outside the intended glosses (the one 한국어 string is the language-option label and is correct). দেওয়ানি / ফৌজদারি keep the short spelling throughout; ন্যাসভঙ্গ (背信) is the statutory Bengali term; মোক্তারনামা and সন্তানের সর্বোত্তম স্বার্থ are now right. The decimal in `0.5 মাস` is a point. Amounts at or above one lakh still use Western grouping, and one bio amount still uses an English million mark.
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- “It does not follow that …” is still rendered with বেরোনো (to come out), which carries no inferential sense: "এটা বেরোয় না যে"
- “Neither … nor” is coordinated with a clause-initial না, which Bengali does not do: "না আপনাআপনি প্রক্রিয়া, না বাধ্যতামূলক ধাপ"
- English/German abstract nouns are taken literally — সুবিধা for a physical facility, দক্ষ for “efficient”, পছন্দ for “option”: "স্থির সুবিধা"

- German apposition and relative-clause order survive in the guidance pack: "পাতা “যোগাযোগ” বলে আমরা যোগাযোগের উপায় কীভাবে যাচাই করি।"
