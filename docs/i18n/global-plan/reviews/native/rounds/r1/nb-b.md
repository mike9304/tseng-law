## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): N/A for guidance pack (out of scope), 3/5 for columns
variety used: Bokmål, with Danish still mixed into 014 and in patches of 016 and 017 (*Grænsen*, *rets kompetence*, *vælg*, *at* + infinitive, comma before *at*). Dates are day + lowercase month + year. The decimal comma is right (0,5 meter; 1,57 millioner), but thousands still use a period (1.579.589, 25.000.000, 30.000.000). No Nynorsk, and no capital De. Wei Tseng is *jeg* / *Advokat*, with no masculine form. *Han* in 012 is motorcyclist A; *ham* in 014 is the employee. *Den ansvarlige* in 018 is common gender.
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- Chinese glosses still sit on the wrong Norwegian word: "det er ikke rettslig rådgivning (損害賠償)" (010; 011 *tjenesteyteren (化粧品)*).
- *Subjekt* calques the regulated person or the license holder: "subjekt-innehaver av tillatelsen" (017; also 011 and 018).
- Danish spelling, *at* + infinitive and the comma before *at* still cover 014 and break 016 and 017: "og vælg den passende investeringsvei".
- Taiwan ministries are called *ministerium/Ministeriet*: "Ministeriet for økonomiske anliggender" (013; also 011, 014, 016, 017 and 018).
- 013 and 015 still break almost every clause into its own paragraph: "Utover markedssituasjonen" / "er et annet viktig punkt".
- 012 is close once *etterlatte* and *bremsereserve* are fixed. 016 now has *foreldreansvar*, *pliktarv* and *stedfortredende arv*. 018 is close apart from *inndra* and one sentence that reverses the English. 014 is not client-ready Bokmål.
