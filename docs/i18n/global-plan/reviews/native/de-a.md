## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): 4/5 for guidance pack, 3/5 for columns 001–006 (007–009 still to score)
variety used: Standard German (DACH), Sie-Anrede in the pack and 001–005; 006 shifts into first-person blog register. Mixed *taiwanisch* (pack, 001–002, 004–005) vs *taiwanesisch* (003, 006).
systemic patterns so far:
- English chrome: `OUR TEAM`, `OFFICES`, `(English)`, `Q&A`, English job titles.
- Korea left as the default reader where English already generalised: Korean parent, remittance from Korea, hiring Koreans, Taiwan–Korea treaty without the country-specific caveat.
- Hangul leftover in the 005 body link.
- Advertising-rule sentences inherited from the Korean source (maximum compensation, typical sentences).
- Legal calques: *Haft* for 拘役, *Kräfte* for staff, *Methode* for 辦法, *PE* left in running text.
- Register split: 001/002/004 are dense usable legal German; 003 Q16–Q20 and 006 keep a Korean-blog voice.

Gender of attorney Wei Tseng: no finding (`Rechtsanwältin`, `geschäftsführende Anwältin`, `sie`, `taiwanesische Anwältin`, `Austauschstudierende`). 006 “einem Anwalt in Taiwan” is a generic lawyer, not her.
