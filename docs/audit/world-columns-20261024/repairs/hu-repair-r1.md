## 879 — working holiday

### MUST M1 — ACCEPTED

The reviewer is correct. [Labor Occupational Accident Insurance and Protection Act, article 6(1)(1)](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=N0050031&flno=6) gives four alternative employer qualifications:

> 受僱於領有執業證照、依法已辦理登記、設有稅籍或經中央主管機關依法核發聘僱許可之雇主。

Its opening requires workers aged at least fifteen to participate through the employer as the insurance unit. The original omitted the employment-permit alternative and rendered the professional-practice licence as an operating licence.

Old → new, exact changed span:

> működési engedéllyel, bejegyzéssel vagy adóregisztrációval

→

> szakmai gyakorlási engedéllyel, törvény szerinti bejegyzéssel, adójogi nyilvántartással vagy a központi illetékes hatóság által jogszerűen kiadott foglalkoztatási engedéllyel

The original mandatory duty, worker age, absence of a five-worker threshold in this route, and article link are retained. This is a substantive correction of the listed employer conditions. FAQ 3 already says that the employer's occupational-accident insurance obligation must be examined separately; neither that FAQ nor the summary reproduces the defective list, so neither needs alteration.

The [official history](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawHistory.aspx?pcode=N0050031) confirms commencement on 1 May 2022: “定自一百十一年五月一日施行”.
