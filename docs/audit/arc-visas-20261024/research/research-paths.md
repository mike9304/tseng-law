## Drafting decision

705 answers the decision before the first ARC application: which lawful entry/residence route fits the proposed work, course or family relationship? Article 704 owns the actual first-card process; 706 owns renewal, changes and re-entry. Do not duplicate their fee tables or deadlines here.

Suggested Korean title: 대만 취업·유학·가족 체류, 입국 전에 정해야 할 비자 경로. Suggested English title: Taiwan visas for work, study and family residence. Each language author should use an independent opening and local reader terminology, while keeping the legal conditions below identical.

The intended reader has a job offer, admission letter, Mandarin course enrollment or plan to join family in Taiwan. The concrete useful next action is to identify the entry document and status, the residence basis and evidence, and the correct agency before purchasing a travel plan that assumes an in-country change. This route-comparison angle is useful across languages; language does not establish citizenship or eligibility.

## Draft-safe path structure

1. Open with the reader's actual purpose. A residence card does not itself answer whether the proposed job is authorized. For an employee, identify the work-permit category or verified exemption before presenting the residence step.
2. Explain ordinary short stay versus resident visa and residence permission briefly. Do not write that every long stay always requires the exact sequence “resident visa → ARC”: P02/P03/P05 are verified direct NIA alternatives.
3. Professional employment: start from the employer/individual's applicable authorization and cite P02. Distinguish the legal category foreign professional from all foreign workers. Migrant workers must not be told to follow the professional online portal or the same dependent rules.
4. Study: a full-time academic program and Mandarin-center enrollment have different routes. Keep P06 and P07/P08 separate; school admission is evidence for the application, not residence approval.
5. Family: distinguish spouse of a Taiwan national with household registration from spouse/children of an eligible foreign professional. Ask whose status supplies the residence basis, what relationship exists, and how the applicant entered.
6. Link niche routes only after their current official basis is confirmed. Gold Card, entrepreneurship, working holiday and digital nomad are not interchangeable labels for an ordinary employee ARC.
7. End with the missing document or decision: current passport/entry status, work approval or admission, relationship evidence, and intended travel date. No general claim of visa-agent services, guaranteed approval or consultation in all 14 languages.

## Source conflict / translation traps for all authors

- Current professional direct residence is Article 16. NIA 0915's legal-basis and notes paragraphs still contain references to old numbers (including Article 12 in the visa-exempt timing note), even though its eligibility paragraph cites Article 16 in places. Link the current Act for the legal proposition. Do not reproduce the old numbering.
- NIA's English 0915 study eligibility says “more than three months” in one place; the Chinese statute says 三個月以上 and the same guide's document section says at least three. Use at least three months.
- Do not say visa-free entry is always convertible or never convertible. P02 and P03 are express professional exceptions; P06/P07 are study-route restrictions. Nationality, entry scheme and visa annotations also matter.
- A date in a guide's footer is not a new law's effective date. P01 resolves the 2026 Act commencement.
- Avoid 15-day or 30-day universal filing language in 705. First-card and change deadlines belong to the route-specific 704/706 evidence.
- Foreign professionals, foreign specialist professionals, foreign senior professionals, migrant workers and newly named foreign technical personnel are distinct categories. Do not translate them all into “foreign talent.”
- NIA 0915 expressly distinguishes trial visa exemptions, landing visas, conditional Southeast Asian online travel authorization, ABTC and working-holiday entry. Do not recommend conversion to a Thai/Indonesian/other reader based only on the language of the article.
- [BOCA's foreign-resident-spouse visa page](https://www.boca.gov.tw/fp-166-281-b948e-2.html), published 2024-03-15, includes a restriction on obtaining that resident visa after visa-free entry. It must not erase the separate direct NIA residence route available to qualifying professional dependents under current Article 16(2). Name the route and agency; do not splice the pages into an absolute statement.
- [BOCA's white-collar resident-visa page](https://www.boca.gov.tw/cp-166-276-48430-2.html), updated 2024-05-01, contains a separate contract-performance rule in Notice 7 for work over six months. Do not use its visa-first sequence as a universal description of Article 16 employees. Korean 497 and Japanese 135 already handle equipment-engineer/contract-work fact patterns.
- The BOCA resident-visa page asks for more than six months remaining on certain work approvals; NIA direct-residence guidance uses at least six months for its route. These are not interchangeable checklists. 705 should avoid this numerical detail and send the reader to the checklist for the selected route.
- A website-wide footer update is not the page's own update date. For example, professional online guide 0927 still identifies its content date as 2024-04-10, despite a 2026 website footer.

## Audience and local drafting application

The shared Taiwan-law thesis is fixed: choose the legal purpose and entry route before treating the ARC as the next form to file. A local opening should identify a concrete decision, not assign a job or citizenship to every speaker of that language.

- Korean/Japanese/English readers can be addressed at the point of a Taiwan offer, assignment or school admission. The existing specialist employment columns make an ordinary route-selection article distinctive. A contract installation visit requires its own qualification check.
- Vietnamese/Thai/Indonesian readers need an explicit distinction between professional employment and migrant-worker systems. This is a legal-category distinction, not a claim that readers of those languages are all migrant workers. Degree study and joining family remain equally visible routes.
- French readers already have a PVT article; 705 should help decide among a job, degree/Mandarin course and family residence, with PVT as a short alternative. German/Italian/Spanish/Portuguese authors should not assume that every reader's passport has the same working-holiday arrangement.
- Hindi and Arabic articles should explicitly separate language, passport nationality and current place of residence. A UAE or Saudi residence permit does not itself establish eligibility for Taiwan's visa-exempt/digital-nomad route. Portuguese readers should similarly not be assigned Portuguese citizenship solely from the language.
- Traditional Chinese is for foreign readers and families handling the same Taiwan process; distinguish the foreign-national scheme from Taiwan nationals without household registration, Mainland-area, Hong Kong and Macau systems. 705 does not provide the latter groups' full procedures.

Suggested FAQ choices, to be worded independently in each locale: “Can I enter first and change later?” (route-specific answer); “Does an ARC allow me to work?” (P12/P13); “Does admission to a Mandarin course immediately qualify me for residence?” (P07/P08). These should not duplicate 704's submission/payment FAQ or 706's renewal/re-entry FAQ.
