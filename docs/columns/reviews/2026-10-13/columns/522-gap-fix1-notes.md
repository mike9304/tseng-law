## MUST VERIFY (checked on law.moj.gov.tw single-article pages, 2026-10-12)

MUST VERIFY 1: CONFIRMED — https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=C0000001&flno=287 — 「第二百七十七條第一項、第二百八十一條及第二百八十四條之罪，須告訴乃論。但公務員於執行職務時，犯第二百七十七條第一項之罪者，不在此限。」 The text must list Art. 277 para. 1, Art. 281 and Art. 284 as complaint-only and limit the public-official exception to Art. 277 para. 1. All four versions now name the three articles, say the column covers 277(1) and 284, tie the exception to a public official's 277(1) offence, and add that Art. 277 para. 2 (death or serious injury) is not on the list while negligent serious injury stays inside Art. 284.

MUST VERIFY 2: CONFIRMED — https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=I0020003&flno=28 — 「告訴乃論之刑事事件於偵查中或第一審法院辯論終結前，調解成立，並於調解書上記載當事人同意撤回意旨，經法院核定者，視為於調解成立時撤回告訴或自訴。」 The text must state all four conditions (complaint-only criminal matter; mediation during the investigation or before oral argument closes in the court of first instance; the 調解書 records the parties' consent to withdraw; court approval) and the effect (complaint or private prosecution deemed withdrawn when mediation succeeded). All four versions list the four conditions as four, in body and FAQ, and include 自訴.

MUST VERIFY 3: CONFIRMED — https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=I0020003&flno=31 — 「告訴乃論之刑事事件由有告訴權之人聲請調解者，經調解不成立時，鄉、鎮、市公所依其向調解委員會提出之聲請，將調解事件移請該管檢察官偵查，並視為於聲請調解時已經告訴。」 The text must keep the whole sequence: a complaint-only case, the person entitled to complain applied, mediation failed, the office refers the case to the competent prosecutor on that person's application to the mediation committee; only then is the complaint deemed made at the time of the mediation application. All four versions state the sequence in one sentence. The wording that the six-month start "moves" to the application date is removed from all four.
