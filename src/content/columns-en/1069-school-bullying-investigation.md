# How long should a Taiwan school take to handle a bullying report?

Twenty working days. After a report of suspected student-on-student bullying arrives, [article 25](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=H0020081&flno=25) of the Campus Bullying Prevention Guidelines gives the investigating school that long to say in writing whether it will take the case.

Keep proof of the date it arrived. The notification requirement does not apply where the reporter cannot be notified.

The investigating school is the school the actor belonged to when the conduct happened. [Article 18](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=H0020081&flno=18) lets the targeted person, a statutory agent, or the actual caregiver file there. Someone else who knows of the event may report to that school or to the competent authority. If the actor is a current or former principal, the report goes to the authority over the school at the time of the conduct.

Under article 18, a written report must be signed or sealed and give the reporter's name, contact number, date and facts, with any evidence. A report by the targeted student, their legal representative or actual caregiver must also identify the student's school and class. If one of those people reports orally in person, the school must help put it in writing. Retaliation against a reporter or someone assisting them is prohibited.

Does one insult meet [article 4](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=H0020081&flno=4)?

The definition asks for continued conduct by a person or a group, done intentionally, by words, writing, pictures, symbols, physical acts, electronic messages, the internet, or another method, directly or indirectly. The conduct depreciates, excludes, bullies, harasses, or mocks, and it leaves the other person in a hostile or unfriendly environment, with mental, physical, or property harm, or with ordinary learning disrupted. One remark does not, by itself, satisfy the word "continued."

Sexual bullying that meets subparagraph 3, item 3, of article 3 of the Gender Equity Education Act is handled under that Act. Article 4, paragraph 2, of the guidelines says so.

[Article 5](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=H0020081&flno=5), paragraph 1, sends student-on-student events at every school level through review, conciliation, investigation, and a decision under these guidelines. Cases involving teachers or principals follow other tracks under paragraphs 2 to 6. For an established full-time teacher at a senior high school or below, the relevant teacher-dismissal regulations apply.

Rejection has its own clock. The written notice must give reasons. From the day after the reporter receives it, [article 26](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=H0020081&flno=26) allows 30 days to petition the school's competent authority.

Only one petition is allowed for the same event.

Acceptance starts a smaller clock. [Article 27](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=H0020081&flno=27) says the school forms a handling group within five working days after the review group decides to accept the case. The group conciliates or investigates. It is generally three or five people. More than half must be external members recruited from the designated student-on-student bullying personnel pool. At a remote school, at least one third must be recruited externally from that pool; the authority must help if recruitment is difficult.

Two months is the next span. [Article 43](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=H0020081&flno=43) counts it from the handling group's first conciliation or investigation meeting, and the report is finished inside that span. The school may extend twice. Each extension is no more than one month, and the parties are told.

Once the report is finished, the prevention committee reviews it. [Article 46](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=H0020081&flno=46) gives the school 15 working days from that resolution, or from receiving the investigating school's resolution, to make the final decision. Within 10 further working days the school writes to the actor and the targeted person, with the facts, the reasons, and the report. The actor is told the remedy, the period, and the office that receives it. The targeted person is told how to petition. A minor's statutory agent or actual caregiver is notified.

From the day after that writing arrives, [article 49](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=H0020081&flno=49) gives the targeted person, a statutory agent, or the actual caregiver 30 days to petition the competent authority. Again, once for the same event.

A criminal case does not freeze this. [Article 42](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=H0020081&flno=42) says the conciliation, investigation, and handling go on whether or not a judicial proceeding is under way, and whatever that proceeding decides. Losing the original status, as a student or otherwise, does not stop the procedure either.
