## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): N/A for guidance pack (out of scope; part a), 3.5/5 for columns — 010 and 012 read 4/5; 013 and 015 read 4/5 once the invisible characters are removed; 011, 014, 016, 017 and 018 read 3/5 because of the defects below.
variety used: Icelandic (íslenska), consistent, with one Norwegian/Danish loan (*inntrykk*) in 011. Dates are day–month–year with a period after the day and a lowercase month (13. september 2025, 4. febrúar 2026, 1. júlí 2026, 5. júní 2026, 25. júní 2026, 17. september 2026). The decimal comma is right (0,5 metra) and thousands take a period (1.579.589 TWD, 25.000.000 TWD, 30.000.000 TWD). What is not Icelandic style is that small cardinals are set as digits throughout.
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- Cardinals one to ten are set as digits where Icelandic writes words, and the same file writes both one line apart: "## 2. Vöruskráning og PIF eru 2 aðskilin fyrirkomulög" against "eru tvö aðskilin fyrirkomulög" on the next line. The English source writes them out ("two or more shareholders").
- *Greinast* is the all-purpose verb for "differ", which is not what it means: "Þau greinast í lögaðilastöðu, ábyrgð höfuðstöðva, bókhalds- og skattalega meðferð".
- Connectors are carried over word for word: "Öfugt," for *conversely*, "Einnig þegar" for *even where*, "má ekki þegar beita" for *must not immediately apply*.
- English abstract nouns become Icelandic words that mean something else: "háð við leyfishafann" (mockery), "matarniðurstaða" (food), "heildarinntrykk" (Norwegian).
- Nominal chains stand where Icelandic uses a verb: "Ákvæðin skulu hönnuð með endurspeglun raunverulegrar þjálfunarfjárfestingar".
- 013 and 015 still carry U+200B on eight lines, unchanged since round 4, and 013 still mixes ‘…’ with „…“ inside one file.
