## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): N/A for guidance pack (out of scope; part a), 4/5 for columns
variety used: Bokmål, consistent. No Nynorsk (no *ikkje*, *ein*, *kva*). One Danish time-relative in 016 (*den dag, hvor*). Dates are day–month–year with a lowercase month (24. januar 2022, 5. juni 2026, 1. juli 2026, 25. juni 2026, 1. januar 2023, 17. september 2026). The decimal comma is right (0,5 meter; 1,57 millioner). Thousands still use a period. Currency follows the amount (40.000 til 200.000 TWD; 新臺幣).
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- Thousands still use a period: "1.579.589 TWD".
- U+200B is still in 013 and 015, including lines whose only character is that space: "angivelse av området er tilstrekkelig (eksempel: byen Taipei)."
- One checklist tells the reader to keep the PIF *etter* the statutory period and *etter* the place: "oppbevare det etter den lovbestemte perioden og det lovbestemte stedet."
- *Ser sammen* and *uten utelatelse* are still calques: "Man ser sammen om arbeidstakeren faktisk oppnår en ytterligere fordel".
- The closing formula *bestemt til … å forklare* does not parse: "et undervisningsdokument bestemt til alminnelig å belyse".
- One false label still names contract drafting above a registration comparison: "Avsnittet over er veiledning om hvordan kontrakten utformes."

013 is native professional once the invisible characters, *Når senere*, the doubled *dette*, and the English single quotes are repaired. 015 is native professional once those characters are removed and the assisted-inquiry title, the exemption sentence, and *juridiske personen* are repaired. 012 is native professional aside from the outcome sentence, *en kjøring*, *rommet*, and *han*. 010, 011, 014 and 016 are professional with the local defects below. 017 is professional aside from the membership card, the remittance verb, and two prepositions. 018 is clear aside from the false label, *inndelingen av investoren*, and the branch-cell word order. The outline in 018 jumps from 5 to 7, then 8, as the English source does. Round-3 repairs hold: the gym is asked to preserve the footage, *den konkrete instruksjonen som ble gitt*, punitive damages stop at the actual loss, *reiseoversikten*, PIF kept apart from product registration, the manufacturer or importer address, *prøvingen er likevel ikke overdrevent streng*, *ytterligere omkring 1 måned*, *utlendinger nylig forsvinner*, the closed sentence on restaurant use at a commercial office, *Hvordan fratredelsesvarselet*, the Ministry file number with a Norwegian label, *bekreftelse av testamentet (檢認)*, *verifikasjon av det overførte investeringsbeløpet*, and *geografisk begrenset*. *Horn og frontlykter* is a grammatical subject. Attorney Wei Tseng is unnamed or titled *advokat* / *prosessfullmektig* / *den ansvarlige advokaten* (010 *Jeg var*; 011, 014, 016 and 017 *Advokat Wei Tseng*; 018 *den ansvarlige*). No masculine form for her. The consultation lock holds: 013 says advice is not given in Norwegian; 015 and 018 name only English, Chinese, Japanese and Korean. 010, 011, 012, 014, 016 and 017 stay general information. 012 says compliance with Article 101 does not itself ensure a particular later outcome, though the wording is still off. 018 says the scenarios do not establish that a method is lawful or better. *Barnets beste* is the best-interests test. No win rate and no free meeting.
