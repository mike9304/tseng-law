## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): n/a for guidance pack (part a), 3/5 for columns 010–012 so far
variety used: standard Lithuanian (bendrinė), consistent. Decimal comma is right in 012 (“0,5 metro”); money in 010–011 still uses a German thousands dot.
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- ROC case years read as ordinary year-counts: "109 metų vartotojų bylos Nr. 7"
- “matas” stands in for a legal standard: "pagal profesionalų ar techninį matą"
- “eilė / eiga” calques a sequence or a procedure: "signalų ir kelio užleidimo eilė"
- German thousands dots on TWD amounts: "TWD 1.579.589"
- Bare “atlyginimas” where a client hears wages, not damages: "1-ojoje instancijoje TWD 1.570.000 atlyginimas"
- News headlines are comma lists with no verb: "Studentas, tarpslankstelinio disko plyšimas (椎間盤破裂) po 90 kg mirties traukos"

Guidance pack is out of scope. Wei Tseng is feminine throughout this slice (“proceso atstovė”, “advokatės vardu”, “Advokatė Wei Tseng”, “atsakingos advokatės”). No consultation-in-Lithuanian, interpreter, reply-time, or result promise. 012 is the most readable of the three. 010–011 are real legal prose with calques and three meaning breaks.
