## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): 3/5 for guidance pack, 2/5 for columns
variety used: standard contemporary Russian (with ё), consistent; not mixed with Ukrainian or Belarusian; German-calqued syntax in the pack and 001/002/004/007, Korean-blog voice in 003 Q16+, 005, 006, 008, 009
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- Masculine title for Wei Tseng against feminine verbs: "уполномочена … является руководящим адвокатом" / "я — … адвокат Тайваня"
- Name split: pack uses «Вэй Цзэн», columns use Latin «Wei Tseng (曾雋崴)»
- Korea left as the default reader where English already generalized: "Даже когда корейская материнская компания выходит на Тайвань"
- Digit «3» for *third party* and German/English number forms: "3 лиц (第三人)", "TWD 500.000", "TWD 1.57M"
- English/Hangul leftovers: "OUR TEAM", "Q&A", percent-encoded Hangul slug in 005
- Typical-sentence and result language in 003/008: "обычно назначают 4 месяца" / "получить высокое выходное пособие"

Guidance pack is meaning-faithful and the consultation-language / no-promise lines are correctly negative (FAQ «Нет.», no interpreter, no reply-time, no free first meeting, no result). A Russian-speaking client can follow it, but the site still reads as a German legal translation with English labels. Columns 001–002 and 004/007 are dense, grammatically strained legal German; 003 Q1–Q15 is the strongest stretch; 005–006 and 008–009 feel like a Korean blog with a Russian overlay. 006/008/009 body gender for Wei Tseng is otherwise correct («Не выдала ли я», «хотела бы», «говорила», «вела»). No invented P3; files 001–002 are usable once the Korea-DTA frame and number format are fixed.
