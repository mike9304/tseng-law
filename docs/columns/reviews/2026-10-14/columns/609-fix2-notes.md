## L1 — who received the benefit, all four

The test “who was left with the benefit / 이익이 누구에게 남았는지 / 利益が誰に残ったか / 利益最後在誰身上” is gone. Each version now separates (a) who received the benefit from this transfer under Article 179 from (b) Article 182’s knowledge and the benefit at receipt or at later knowledge. A reply that a relative withdrew the cash does not, by itself, identify the recipient, and it does not rewrite the debtor as that relative. The articles do not name whoever now holds the cash, and they do not make the named account holder liable in every case. The column does not predict which version a court would accept. No third-party recovery rule was added.

## L2 — EN closing

The sentence that said the confirmation and the living-expenses reply show who received the benefit, what remains, and when that person learned there was no legal cause is gone. The close now says the confirmation records the destination, the reply should be kept, and those three questions still have to be examined. ko/ja/zh already pointed at questions rather than a concluded proof; they were aligned to the same limit so the two papers are not treated as establishing the Article 182 facts. No new facts were invented to make the old conclusion true.

## L3 — Article 181 is not a reduction rule, all four

“Articles 181 and 182 can reduce / 범위를 줄이면 / 範囲を縮める / 把範圍縮小” is gone from the body and from FAQ 2, so the FAQ no longer repeats the wrong attribution. Article 181 is the benefit, further acquisitions from it, and value when return is impossible. Article 182 is what changes the duty when the recipient did not know and the benefit is gone, or knew at receipt or learned later. Full recovery of every wrong transfer is still not promised. The four Article 180 exclusions, including knowledge at the time of payment and the recipient-only unlawful-cause exception, stay.
