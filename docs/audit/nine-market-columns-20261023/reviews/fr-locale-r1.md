The article supplies a usable invoice-by-invoice reconciliation and distinguishes a disputed deduction from an accepted credit. French invoice origin does not establish the governing law. The two-year rule, six-month follow-up after a demand and twenty-day objection period have separate triggers. FAQ answers track the body. The statement about the claim and performance under CPC 511 was independently checked against the current Chinese provision.
- 675: The text distinguishes filing from publication, opposition from invalidation, and cancellation from a negotiated assignment. Prior use, related knowledge, imitation intent and consent are not collapsed into the existence of a distribution contract. The five-year rule is not waived simply by alleging bad faith under ground 12. Trademark Act 60 was also opened: its current balancing exception exists, but the manuscript does not promise automatic cancellation and therefore does not contradict it. The title and FAQs do not imply that a French registration creates a Taiwan title. The single crowded sentence in the optional table can be simplified.
- 676: The three-month agency notice is expressly confined to the indefinite relationship defined by article 558; it is not imported into every buy-resell contract. `résolution` is used for the particular sale and is distinguished from the end of the distribution relationship. Existing inventory, trademark exhaustion and platform/customer-data handover remain separate questions. The reader is given lot, payment, account and order information to collect. The article does not promise an unconditional right to block an account or repurchase every item.
- 677: The actual France connection is document execution, not a nationality assumption about every reader. The deceased's nationality at death, the heir's residence, real-property formalities, bank-specific requirements, authentication and substantive succession rights remain distinct. The current article 2 does use closest nationality connection; no Taiwan-nationality priority proviso was found in that current provision. The estate-tax provision explicitly starts from the day following death, matching the French sentence. `légalisation`, `procuration`, `acte de notoriété`, `nom de naissance` and `nom d'usage` fit this readership. The latest PDF's page-nine row needs the evidence qualification below.
- 678: `salarié`, `créance salariale`, `quittance`, `transaction`, `indemnité de rupture`, `liquidation` and `déclaration de faillite` are used coherently. Actual employee status, employer contributions, a recognized closure and an unpaid demand are retained; no French AGS equivalence is invented. The wage look-back period is kept separate from the combined retirement/severance ceiling. The inability to confirm the debt does not remove the evidence requirement. The foreign-bank payment option is not mistaken for eligibility. All three FAQs preserve these limits.
