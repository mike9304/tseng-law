## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): n/a for guidance pack (part a), 3/5 for columns
variety used: Standard Malay for Malaysia (ms_MY / DBP) intended and mostly kept (peguam, syarikat, boleh, peletakan jawatan); number format and some verbs follow Indonesian/European convention (1.579.589, 0,5, memotret); French/German legal diction (status sivil, kerugian moral, orang fizikal, orientasi) is one European→Malay pass, not a second Malay variety
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- Digit+noun and 一審/營業項目 calques: “kes perbicaraan 1”, “hon 2 kali”, “sekitar 10 cabang”
- False friends a client would file under the wrong heading: gimnasium (school hall), automotif (car industry), cabang (branch office), rakan kongsi (partners), temu ramah (job interview)
- European/Indonesian numerals on a Malaysia site: “[1.579.589 TWD]”, “0,5 meter”, “TWD 25.000.000”
- French/German glue: subjek, pelupusan, orientasi, kepakaran, orang fizikal, «guillemets»: “pilihan subjek pengimport”
- Korean-blog leftovers on a law-firm page: 😊, “Contoh)”, “**Nasihat**：”, “tinggalkan komen”, empty `​`
- 投資審議司 as Suruhanjaya in 013/018 while 011 already has Jabatan; Invest Taiwan still `lang=jpn`

014 and 016 are the densest, most usable legal Malay in this set once “dilikuidasi”, “pelepaskan” and “jagaan/監護” are fixed. 011 is careful cosmetics copy except 劑型 and the Japanese Invest Taiwan link. 010 fails a native gym/instance check. 012 never says Taiwan is right-hand traffic (Malaysia is the reverse). 013 and 015 still read as a Korean/Chinese blog. 017 is structurally sound until the title word automotif. 018 restates part a’s 股東→rakan kongsi error and invites a “temu ramah”. Gender: Malay does not inflect; “Saya ialah peguam”, “Peguam Wei Tseng”, “Wei Tseng (曾雋崴), peguam di Taiwan” are epicene. No Encik, no masculine verb/title for her. Consultation lock: 010, 012, 014, 016, 017 keep the negative (bukan nasihat; bukan janji hasil). 011 “tidak menegaskan bahawa kebenaran… akan diperoleh” is still negative, only weaker than menjamin. 013/015 CTAs invite comments / “pada bila-bila masa” with no language limit. 018 correctly says bahasa temu ramah yang tersedia mesti disahkan. No win-rate or “best/only” firm claim; “kepentingan terbaik anak (子女最佳利益)” is the statutory best-interests test, not advertising.
