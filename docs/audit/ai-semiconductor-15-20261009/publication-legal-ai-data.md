### Article 03: copyright and reserved patent check

- [TIPO Article 12](https://www.tipo.gov.tw/tw/copyright/694-17729.html) and [MOEA current Copyright Act](https://law.moea.gov.tw/LawContent.aspx?id=FL011264) support the default commissioned-work allocation and contract exception at original line 16. Employee and commissioned work are not conflated.
- [TIPO email 951212a, 2006-12-12](https://www.tipo.gov.tw/tw/copyright/692-12637.html) concerns a commissioned design and states the funder's purpose-limited utilization. The original applies that principle cautiously rather than inventing unlimited resale/sublicensing.
- [TIPO email 1140829, 2025-08-29](https://www.tipo.gov.tw/tw/copyright/692-57511.html) supports possible reproduction during protected-image AI training even when the result comprises parameters/weights. The original retains “可能”, authorization/exception conditions and avoids claiming all training infringes. [Article 65](https://www.tipo.gov.tw/tw/copyright/694-17672.html) supports the four-factor analysis, not an automatic AI research exemption.
- [TIPO email 1140522c, 2025-05-22](https://www.tipo.gov.tw/tw/copyright/692-34252.html) supports the human-creative-contribution distinction at original line 36; the article preserves individual judicial assessment. Article 10-1 supports the expression/idea boundary, without proving model weights always are or never are protected.
- Source freshness caveat resolved by supplementation: the [TIPO whole-law page](https://www.tipo.gov.tw/tw/copyright/693-8432.html) has history ending in 2019. The [MOEA history](https://law.moea.gov.tw/LawContentSource.aspx?id=FL011264) shows 2022-06-15 amendment, with the separately pending 2022-05-04 criminal-enforcement changes. [TIPO legislative announcements](https://www.tipo.gov.tw/tw/tipo1/890.html) were also checked.
- The original makes no patent-law claim. For any incoming patent subsection, [Patent Act Article 7](https://law.moea.gov.tw/LawContent.aspx?id=FL011249) was independently opened: commissioned R&D application/patent ownership follows contract; absent agreement it belongs to the inventor/creator/designer, while the funder may implement. - [2026-07-20 AI copyright guideline hearing record](https://www.tipo.gov.tw/tw/copyright/721-90647.html), posted August 3 and updated August 4, remains labeled draft hearing material. No final guideline or statutory exemption is asserted by the manuscript.
