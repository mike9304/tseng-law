## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): 3/5 for guidance pack, 2/5 for columns
variety used: standard contemporary Ukrainian (not surzhyk); orthography consistent (’ apostrophe, і/ї/є); register mixed — stiff German-calqued legalese in guidance and 001/002/004/007 vs first-person blog voice in 003 Q16+, 005, 006, 008, 009
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- German syntax copied into Ukrainian: "Увесь тутешній зміст є загальним"; also "треба отже", "одним потоком", "може наслідувати покарання".
- Digit "3" used for English *third party*, so it reads as "three people": "спільно з 3 особою".
- Korea left as the default reader where English already generalized: "На відміну від Кореї" (EN: "unlike some jurisdictions, for example Korea").
- Masculine title **Адвокат** / "керівним адвокатом" for Wei Tseng, against feminine verbs ("уповноважена", "казала").
- Thousands/dates in German/English form, not Ukrainian: "TWD 500.000", "27/12/2023", "TWD 1.57M".
- English/Korean leftovers on a Ukrainian page: "OUR TEAM", "Q&A", Hangul in `url` of 008/009, related links "яка може спілкуватися корейською".

Guidance pack is usable legal Ukrainian with capital Ви, correct 資遣費 → вихідна допомога, 工作規則 → правила внутрішнього трудового розпорядку. It still reads as a German legal translation. Columns 001–002 and 004/007 are dense calque; 003 Q1–Q15 is the strongest stretch; 005–006 and 008–009 feel like a Korean blog with a Ukrainian overlay. 006/008/009 body gender for Wei Tseng is correct (адвокатка, казала, вела).
