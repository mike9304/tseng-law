## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): 3/5 for guidance pack, 3/5 for columns
variety used: Standard Myanmar Burmese (မြန်မာစာ), one variety throughout. Modern spaced Unicode, no Shan/Mon/Palaung mix. Columns and `date_display` use Arabic digits; 003 and the column dates use month names (မေ၊ ဇူလိုင်၊ စက်တင်ဘာ), while 001, 004 and 007 write the month as a bare number (`12 လ 27 ရက်`).

systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- 資遣費 is named နုတ်ထွက်ကြေး, which a Myanmar reader hears as money paid because the worker resigned: "တရားဝင် နုတ်ထွက်ကြေး (資遣費"
- 民法 / 民事 is ပြည်သူ့ (“the people’s”) in 007, while 003 correctly says တရားမဥပဒေ for the same code: "ထိုင်ဝမ် ပြည်သူ့ဥပဒေ (民法)"
- “None of the four languages” is written as “cannot use one or another of them”: "ဘာသာစကား လေးမျိုးအနက် တစ်မျိုးမျိုး မသုံးနိုင်ပါက"
- 發行 is ထုတ်ဝေ (“publish”), so an issuer and issued shares sound like a publishing house: "သီးခြား ထုတ်ဝေကုမ္ပဏီ (發行公司)"
- Dates copied from “2023年12月27日” as `12 လ 27 ရက်`, which also reads as a duration of 12 months and 27 days: "2023 ခုနှစ် 12 လ 27 ရက်တွင် အသက်ဝင်"
- Blog skeleton in 003 Q16–Q20, 005, 006, 008 and 009: one clause per line, blank spacers, "ဖတ်ရှုသူများ၊ ကျွန်မမှာ"

Wei Tseng is ရှေ့နေမ, သူမ, ကျွန်မ and ဖလှယ်ကျောင်းသူ. ဦး B in 008 is a male client and should stay. No masculine form for her. The consultation lock stays negative: four languages only, no Burmese consultation, no interpreter, no reply-time promise, no free first meeting, no result guarantee. 003 Q17–Q18 already refuses a “typical” prison term and a promise of 緩刑, and Q20 does not claim maximum compensation. 001 and 004 already limit the Korea tax treaty to Korea. 005 Q1 is already marked as a Korea-bank example. 007 already says “another country”, not Korea, for the cross-border family. 001, 002 and 004 are usable professional legal Burmese once the rows below are fixed. 003 Q1–Q15 matches that register. Q16–Q20 and 006, 008, 009 drop into blog lines.
