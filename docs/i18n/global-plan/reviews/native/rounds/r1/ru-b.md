## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): N/A for guidance pack (out of scope), 3/5 for columns
variety used: Standard literary Russian, consistent; formal вы (Вы is capitalized in 011, 013, 015 and 018). No Ukrainian or Belarusian mix. ё is often omitted, which is ordinary.
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- Registered “title” is rendered as a personal name: `"долю в совместном имени"` (016); also `"передать сменой имени в регистрации транспортного средства (過戶轉讓)"` (017).
- Thousands use a period: `"TWD 1.579.589"` (010; also 011, 017, 018). Russian uses a space; 012 already has the decimal comma in `"0,5 метра"`.
- 戶籍 is not household registration: `"регистрации по месту жительства (戶籍)"` (016); 017 also says `"регистрацию семейного состояния"`.
- Vesting is calqued as accounting allocation: `"момент отнесения"` (014; also `"условий отнесения"`).
- 013 and 015 still break almost every clause into its own paragraph: `"Помимо рыночной ситуации"` then `"ещё одним важным обстоятельством является,"`.
- A contact invitation on the Russian page does not name the four consultation languages: `"желаемый язык беседы"` (018). The no-reply-time clause in 013 and 015 should stay.

012 is native professional Russian apart from two phrases. 010 and 014 are close: labor terms such as `"авансовая выплата (預付性給付)"` and `"новых работников"` are already natural. Attorney Wei Tseng is feminine where she narrates (010 `"выступала"`, 013 `"я согласна"`). The byline `"Адвокат Wei Tseng"` is the ordinary professional title for a woman; no masculine pronoun for her remains. 012’s `"он"` is motorcyclist A. 018 has no gendered byline.
