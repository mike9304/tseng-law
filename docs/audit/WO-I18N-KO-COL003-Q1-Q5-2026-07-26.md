## Legal and copy contract

### Q1 — May a driver leave the scene?

Separate the two cases:

1. When anyone is injured or killed, the driver must immediately take rescue
   measures, notify police, preserve the vehicle and scene evidence, and must
   not treat the other party's informal consent or a recording as permission
   simply to leave. If the case involves injury and all parties agree, the
   vehicle should first be marked and then moved to a place that does not
   obstruct traffic.
2. When there is property damage only and the vehicles can move, record the
   vehicle positions and scene traces by marking, photos, or video, then move
   them promptly to safety. Leaving without the required handling can still
   trigger an administrative sanction.
3. Criminal Code Article 185-4 concerns a driver who leaves after a traffic
   accident causing injury or death. Do not reduce the analysis to “wait until
   fault is clear,” “state your real name,” or “the other person agreed.”

### Q2 — What evidence should be preserved?

Cover personal safety and warning signs first, then 110/119 or 112 as
appropriate, wide and close photos/video, vehicle positions and damage, road
markings/signals/weather, witness contacts, nearby CCTV and dashcam retention,
party/vehicle/insurance information, medical records, and the police-issued
accident documents. Explain that personal recording supplements rather than
replaces police handling in injury/death cases.

State the current police-document timetable precisely: at the scene, the party
can receive the `교통사고 당사자 등록연락서`; from seven days after the
accident, the party can request the `현장도` and `현장사진`; from thirty days
after the accident, the party can request the
`도로교통사고 초보분석판정표`.

### Q3 — What claims and time limits apply after injury?

- Criminal Code Article 284 provides negligent-injury and negligent-serious-
  injury offenses.
- Article 284 is complaint-based under Criminal Code Article 287.
- The complaint must generally be filed within six months from when the
  person entitled to complain learns the offender's identity, under Code of
  Criminal Procedure Article 237.
- A tort damages claim generally expires two years after the claimant knows
  both the damage and the person liable, and in any event ten years after the
  tort, under Civil Code Article 197.
- An incidental civil action is available to a person damaged by the crime
  after the criminal case is prosecuted and within Article 488's window.
  Explain that it ordinarily avoids an upfront judgment fee, but not every
  procedural outcome is cost-free; Article 503 can require litigation costs
  if a dismissed criminal case is transferred at the plaintiff's request.
- Do not recommend one procedure as universally best. State that interruption
  of prescription, defendants, evidence, insurance, and forum require
  case-specific review.

### Q4 — What if both sides were negligent?

- Criminal liability requires each person's own breach of a duty of care and
  a causal connection to the other person's injury; “any fault automatically
  establishes negligent injury” is prohibited.
- Civil Code Article 217 allows a court to reduce or eliminate damages when
  the injured party contributed to the occurrence or expansion of loss.
- If using the 50:50 illustration, identify all figures as New Taiwan dollars:
  a TWD 1,000,000 proven loss may be reduced to TWD 500,000 before other
  adjustments. State that an appraisal or preliminary analysis does not
  mechanically bind the court to a percentage.

### Q5 — What should a settlement cover?

Explain that a written settlement should identify the accident, parties,
payment amount and timing, insurance payments, covered claims, reserved
claims, future treatment or later-discovered injury, document delivery, and
the relationship between payment and any complaint withdrawal.

Under Civil Code Articles 736–737, a settlement ends or prevents a dispute
through mutual concessions, and rights are extinguished only to the extent
they were waived under the agreement. Do not claim every settlement always
bars every future claim.

For a complaint-based offense, Code of Criminal Procedure Article 238 allows
withdrawal before the conclusion of first-instance argument and prohibits a
new complaint after withdrawal. A private settlement does not automatically
terminate prosecution of a non-complaint-based offense. Do not say a party
“must unconditionally withdraw” a complaint.
