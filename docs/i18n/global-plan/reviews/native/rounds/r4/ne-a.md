## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): 4/5 for guidance pack, 4/5 for columns
variety used: Standard Nepali (नेपाली), Devanagari, consistent — not mixed with Hindi running grammar. Gregorian dates use Nepal day–month order (13 सेप्टेम्बर 2025, 27 डिसेम्बर 2023, 1 जनवरी 2024, 17 नोभेम्बर 2021, 29 मे 2026, 1 जुलाई 2026, 25 जुलाई 2026). The decimal in `0.5 महिना` is the period Nepal uses. Amounts at or above one lakh still use Western grouping.

systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- Amounts at or above one lakh use Western grouping, and the bio uses an English million suffix: "TWD 1.57M"
- Guidance and column 002 still calque severance as a hyphenated compound: "वैधानिक सेवा-अन्त भत्ता"
- “Whether a channel exists” is still written as “if a channel exists”: "यदि त्यस्तो मार्ग छ भने"

Honorific "हुनुहुन्छ / गर्नुभयो" does not mark gender. "जाँचिएको" agrees with the article. "श्री लिन" / "चलाउँथे" (006) and "श्री B" (008) are other men; the statement he was asked to sign stays "घटाउँछु". No masculine form for her.
