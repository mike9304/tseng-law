## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): 4/5 for guidance pack, 3/5 for columns
variety used: Bokmål, consistent. Du-address. Guillemets « ». Dates as 13. september 2025. Decimal comma in 0,5 måneds. Not Nynorsk. One Danish tax word in 004 (*nulsats*). Columns 001, 002, 004 and 007 stay in translated legal prose; 003, 005, 006, 008 and 009 are more spoken.
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- Modal *må* (must) where the source means permission: "heller ikke at aksjeeierne eller den driftsansvarlige straks må arbeide i Taiwan"
- Period as thousand separator, which Norway does not use: "på minst 500.000 TWD"
- German clause shape, with the verb parked at the end: "skiller de rettsforhold som skal forberedes, seg etter, med hvem og hvilke kontrakter det inngås"
- Danish missing double definite: "den årsakssammenheng som denne tilsidesettelse har"
- Calque of attorney-client relationship instead of *klientforhold*: "et forhold mellom advokat og klient"
- English firm name plus Norwegian -s looks like a plural: "Hovering International Law Firms arbeidsområde"

Gender of attorney Wei Tseng: no finding. The pack and these columns use *advokat*, *hun*, *ledende advokat* and *utvekslingsstudent*. Consultation lock stays negative (only four languages; no interpreter; no reply-time promise; no free first meeting; no result promise). *Sluttvederlag (資遣費)* is already fenced off from other countries’ schemes.
