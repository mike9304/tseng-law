## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): 4/5 for guidance pack, 3/5 for columns
variety used: standard Croatian (ijekavica), consistent across the pack and all nine columns; one Slovenian adjective „uporabljiv“ in the inquiry form. Not mixed with Serbian or Czech
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- Hangul left in the public URL of 008 and 009 („post/대만-노동법：대만에서-퇴직금-받기-어렵다고“).
- „Odgojna“ for an educational article; odgoj is upbringing („isprava opće informacije odgojne naravi“ in 001; also 002, 004 and 007 „odgojna građa“).
- 戶籍 called matica (the civil register) while the same article also says kućna evidencija („red matice (戶籍)“, „Zakona o matici (戶籍法)“).
- 條例 / 辦法 rendered as statut or uredba, and statut in 002 already means 章程 („Statuta industrijske inovacije (產業創新條例)“, „Uredbe o mirovini radnika“).
- „Prestati s ugovorom“ plus one-line blog breaks in 006, 008 and 009 („čak i kada radnik obično prestane s ugovorom o radu“).
- Guidance is careful legal Croatian; consultation locks stay negative; Wei Tseng is feminine throughout (odvjetnica, Pregledala, Zastupala, ovlaštena, Magistra, željela bih) — no F finding. Thousands with a period, decimal comma and „13. rujna 2025.“ are already right for Croatia; the bio „TWD 1.57M“ is the exception. 003 adds „nije obećanje“ and does not claim a typical sentence.
