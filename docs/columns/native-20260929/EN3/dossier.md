## 確認 못 해서 본문에서 뺀 것 / 표현을 낮춘 것
1. Grace period after permit revocation / how many days a dismissed white-collar worker may stay before NIA orders departure: no official figure found (forum "60 days" claims rejected). Draft says no fixed figure was located and to ask NIA at once.
2. Whether the 6-month extension (停留居留辦法 Art 10 / 人才專法 Art 17) can be filed after the employer has already reported termination and the ARC has been revoked (statute says "before expiry"; NIA form says filed within 3 months before expiry): practice not verified. Draft flags it.
3. Whether administrative 勞資爭議調解 (not court) counts as 爭訟程序 for Immigration Act Art 31(4)(5): not verified.
4. Whether Gold Card holders must report dismissal or whether their card can be affected by it: not verified; draft says the card is personal and not employer-linked, per official Gold Card site.
5. WDA guidance on whether foreign professionals' contracts may be fixed-term: none found; statute only forces fixed-term for 46(1) 8-10.
6. Non-renewal / "last resort" case law (Supreme Court 109 台上 1516) came only from a search snippet: excluded.
7. Limitation periods for severance/wage claims: not verified, excluded (draft only says no short deadline to contest was found in the statutes read).
8. Gold Card holders' access to unemployment benefits: only PR status confirmed (人才專法 Art 25).
