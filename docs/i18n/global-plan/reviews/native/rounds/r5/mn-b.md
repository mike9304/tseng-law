## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): N/A for guidance pack (out of scope; part a), 4/5 for columns
variety used: Khalkha Mongolian in Cyrillic, consistent throughout. No Buryat, Kalmyk or traditional-script Mongolian, no Inner-Mongolian vocabulary. Dates use the Mongolia order year.month.day (2022.01.24, 2023.01.01, 2026.02.04, 2026.06.05, 2026.06.25, 2026.07.01, 2026.09.17). Currency is written code-first with comma thousands ("TWD 1,579,589"); that is the internationally used form and a Mongolia reader reads it without trouble, so I am not re-raising the earlier space-separator note.
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- The unstable-vowel rule (the last vowel drops before a case ending) is broken on isolated words in seven of the nine files, while the same word is spelled correctly elsewhere: "аюулгүй байдлын үүрэгийн (安全義務) агуулга" (010) beside correct "үүргийн" in the same file.
- Negative-polarity words ("хэн ч", "ямар ч") are used with an affirmative verb, so an English "anyone may" reads in Mongolian as "no one may": "Хэн ч газрын бүртгэлийн газраас (地政事務所) ямар ч хаягийн ... хүсэж болно." (015)
- Zero-width spaces (U+200B) survive from an earlier round in 013 (lines 34, 58, 72, 86, 108) and 015 (lines 28, 36, 52), some as the only character on a line: "бүс л бичиж болно (жишээ нь: Тайбэй хот). ​" (013)
- A quantity question is left without its unit, so the sentence stops mid-thought: "ердийн нөхөх хугацаа хэдэн вэ" (014, also line 105).
- One English term is rendered two different ways inside the same file, and the colloquial variant is the non-legal one: "Хэрэглэгчийн гомдол, сөрөг үзэгдэл" (011) against "гомдол, гаж нөлөө" eight paragraphs earlier.
- English connectives and negations are calqued rather than rewritten: "Үүний эсрэг" for "By contrast" and "авах ёстой биш" for "Not every ... must obtain" (both 018).

012 and 010 are otherwise native professional prose; 016 is strong except for the divorce term and three spellings; 013 and 015 are natural blog register apart from the zero-width characters and the two items below. Attorney Wei Tseng is nowhere marked masculine — Mongolian verbs carry no gender, the byline is the neutral «Өмгөөлөгч Wei Tseng (曾雋崴)» in 011, 014, 016 and 017, and the male student in 010 («эрэгтэй оюутан») is the client, not the attorney. The consultation lock holds: 018 names only English, Chinese (中文), Japanese and Korean and says the language actually available is confirmed on contact; 013 and 015 refuse a reply-time promise. No Mongolian consultation is implied anywhere, no interpreter, no free first meeting, no 24-7, no win rate, no guarantee, no "best/only" claim. "Төлбөргүй шалгаж болно" in 015 is the Taipei municipal enquiry system, not a free consultation. Traditional-Chinese glosses are used consistently and correctly; no Simplified characters appear.
