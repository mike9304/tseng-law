# C6 cross-review and native-voice edit — 2026-10-16

Reviewed the four final draft files against the fetched text of 勞動基準法 Articles 15 and 16 in `research/C6-statutes.md` and the matching official single-article pages. Only Article 15(1)–(2) and Article 16(1)–(3) are discussed. Article 15(2) requires advance notice when the employee ends an indefinite-term contract and imports the Article 16(1) periods, not Article 16(2) or (3). The three Article 16(1) brackets are continuous service of at least three months but under one year → 10 days; at least one year but under three years → 20 days; three years or more → 30 days. Article 16(1) has no bracket below three months. No rule on a longer contractual clause or consequences of insufficient employee notice was inferred.
