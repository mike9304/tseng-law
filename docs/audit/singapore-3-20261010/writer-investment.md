## Scope and original contribution

The root approved a revised brief after comparison with published article 494. This article concerns a Singapore holding company funding a newly established Taiwan subsidiary. Its decision is whether the corporate records, upstream evidence and proposed bank instructions support the investment application and later remittance. It is not an AI-chip acquisition, subsidiary-versus-branch comparison, merger-control explainer or tax-planning article.

Singapore-specific material is the ACRA Business Profile/register/extract distinction, matching the applicant by name and UEN, assembling records across the secretary/group legal/treasury functions, and documenting a Singapore-dollar budget's actual conversion and capital receipt. Ownership classification is an essential but bounded Taiwan-law prerequisite. No statistics, client anecdotes, Singapore legal licence, Singapore office or guaranteed approval time is claimed.
