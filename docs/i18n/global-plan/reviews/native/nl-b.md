## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): n/a for guidance pack (part a), 2/5 for columns
variety used: Standaardnederlands intended for NL/BE; consistently Flemish/Belgian legal diction (Neen, dading, van bij het begin, eenvormig, arrondissement) plus German calques; one French leak in 018 — not a second Dutch variety mixed in
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- German reflexive *sich* kept as Dutch *zich*: “lost zich niet automatisch op”, “deelt zich niet”, “opent zich niet één enkele procedure”
- 股東 of a 有限公司/股份有限公司 still rendered as “vennoten” (VOF partners): “In beginsel zijn 2 vennoten of meer vereist (股份有限公司)”
- FAQ answer pasted as the first sentence of the section, often a bare “Neen.”: “Neen. Het recht op verdeling van het verschil van echtelijk restvermogen”
- Digit-memo style and *takken* for 營業項目 (read as company branches): “men wil vaak ongeveer 10 takken in één keer inschrijven”
- Belgian glue plus French/German quotes: “Neen.”, “dading”, «horecazaak», „inrichtingsperiode“
- Register split: 012 is the only stretch a client would finish; 013 is leftover Korean blog (😊, Q&A, “laat een reactie achter”); 014/016/017 are unreadable Kanzlei-Dutch
Gender: no masculine form, pronoun or title for Wei Tseng. Sign-offs are epicene *Advocaat Wei Tseng*; 016 “erft hij” refers to *de overlevende echtgenoot*, not to her. Consultation-language lock is not restated in these nine files; 012 and 018 keep the no-result / confirm-languages negatives intact. 012 is the strongest file; 013 is the weakest; 018 is the most useful until *vennoten* and *prestataire*.
