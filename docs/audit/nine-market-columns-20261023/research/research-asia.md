## Core claim/source ledger — opened official text on 2026-10-23

Every legal row is Taiwan jurisdiction unless specifically stated. These are drafting ceilings: a writer must reopen any extra provision added to an article. A page-wide future-amendment banner does not make unaffected provisions future law.

### L1–L4. Payroll and foreign-worker wage records

- L1 — 勞動基準法 (Labor Standards Act) §22: wages ordinarily paid in full directly to worker; statutory or agreed exceptions in the text. https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=N0030001&flno=22 . Effective current provision; not a new 2026 change. Avoid saying all deductions are automatically unlawful.
- L2 — Same §23: calculation details, wage register retained five years. https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=N0030001&flno=23 . Current effective provision. Do not turn the payment-frequency rule into “every employer must pay twice monthly” because the article contains agreement/monthly-advance exceptions.
- L3 — Same §26: no advance deduction from wages as contractual penalty or damages. https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=N0030001&flno=26 . Current effective provision. A disputed loss must not be converted into an automatic wage deduction.
- L4 — 雇主聘僱外國人許可及管理辦法 (Regulations on the Permission and Administration of the Employment of Foreign Workers) §66: for second-category foreign workers, Chinese plus home-country-language salary statement; actual/gross pay/calculation/payment method and enumerated lawful deductions; five-year employer retention; full direct payment subject to lawful amounts, with proof for another payment method; competent authority may order unpaid wages paid by a deadline. https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=N0090027&flno=66 . Current revision 2025-12-30; §73 provides revised provisions effective on promulgation except its stated older exceptions. Current full text: https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawAll.aspx?pcode=N0090027 . Do not cite old §43: §§42–49 were deleted in this revision. WDA promotional pages can still contain old §43 references.

### L5. Employer insolvency and wage fund

- 勞動基準法 §28 covers employer closure, liquidation or bankruptcy; distinct priority claims and fund advance categories; short wage period plus retirement/severance combined cap. https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=N0030001&flno=28 . Current effective provision.
- BLI detailed procedure opened: https://www.bli.gov.tw/0007239.html . The employer must be closed, liquidating or declared bankrupt, with an unpaid requested claim; a temporary production stoppage with possible resumption is insufficient. Fund contribution is required; subsequently remedied employer arrears can allow application. Qualifying foreign workers have the same application conditions/range. Covered wages are those owed under the labor contract within the six months preceding closure/liquidation/bankruptcy; covered retirement/severance together are capped at six months' average wage. Do not promise all unpaid salaries or termination-related items are covered.
- BLI requires closure evidence, claim evidence, payroll and payment records; claim proof can involve employer/liquidator/trustee acknowledgment with the specified mediation process or qualifying enforceable decisions. Workers ordinarily make one joint application through a worker representative. This is official administrative procedure, not a guaranteed award or a new statutory entitlement. Reuse `src/content/columns-en/606-taiwan-unpaid-wages-insolvent-employer-payment-fund.md` with these current conditions.

### L6. Household caregivers

- Keelung government's current labor administration explanation (published/updated 2026-10-14) distinguishes industrial/institutional caregivers covered by 勞基法 from household caregivers/domestic help, whose wages/rest and other conditions follow the contract: https://www.klcg.gov.tw/tw/financial/4883-323546.html . Official administrative guidance, not legislation. It cites 勞基法 §3 and 就業服務法 §46.
- 民法 (Civil Code) §482 employment contract entails service for remuneration: https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=B0000001&flno=482 . §486 sets contractual/habitual remuneration due dates, with statutory defaults where neither exists: https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=B0000001&flno=486 . Current effective provisions. Civil Code banner identifies unrelated §166-1 commencement unresolved and §1223 amended 2026-08-17 with six-month delayed commencement; do not describe the whole Civil Code as already fully amended/effective.
- Apply L4 where second-category foreign-worker status is established. Do not promise 勞基法 overtime, severance, minimum-wage coverage or wage-fund coverage for all household caregivers. Do not assert national salary amounts; contract and current verified rule control.

### L7. Passport and agency conduct

- 就業服務法 (Employment Service Act) §57(8) prohibits an employer's unlawful retention/misappropriation of a hired foreign worker's passport, residence documents or property. https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=N0090001&flno=57 . Current effective provision.
- §40(1)(3),(4),(5),(10): private employment agencies and personnel may not retain documents against the person's wishes; withhold property/take placement guarantee deposits; demand/receive unauthorized fees or improper interests; or retain authorization/identity/related documents against the employer's/worker's wishes. https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=N0090001&flno=40 . Current effective provision. Do not equate any voluntary safekeeping with unlawful detention. Indonesia-side recruitment debt/contract validity requires Indonesian advice.

### L8. Factory occupational injury

- 勞動基準法 §59: employer compensation for occupational injury/illness/death/disability; necessary medical expenses and wages during medical treatment when unable to work; qualifying statutory employer-funded compensation can be offset. https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=N0030001&flno=59 . Current effective provision. Avoid exact disability/death sums unless scope includes those separate statutory criteria. Workplace occurrence alone does not resolve occupational causation or every insurance issue.

### L9. Post-employment noncompete

- 勞動基準法 §9-1: protectable legitimate business interest; employee access/use of trade secrets; reasonable temporal/geographic/activity/employer scope; reasonable compensation for restriction (not ordinary employment-period payments); failure of any listed condition invalidates the covenant; maximum two years, overlong term reduced to two. https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=N0030001&flno=9-1 . Current effective provision. Do not call confidentiality or all restrictive clauses void. Overseas offer does not remove Taiwan-contract questions; India-side enforcement/Indian law not resolved.
- 勞動基準法施行細則 §7-3: monthly compensation at least 50% of one-month average wage at departure is one of the cumulative reasonableness considerations; living needs, proportionality to restriction/loss and other circumstances also matter. Agree payment after departure as one advance lump sum or monthly. https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=N0030002&flno=7-3 . Current effective provision. Do not substitute basic salary for statutory average wage or imply 50% alone validates the covenant. Author independently verified §§7-1/7-2; the mature Japanese source below covers their written-contract and scope standards.

### C1–C4. Account-provision and investigation

- C1 — 洗錢防制法 (Money Laundering Control Act) §22: prohibited provision of accounts subject to ordinary commercial/financial custom, trusted family/friend relationship or other proper-reason exception. Warning and criminal tracks differ. Criminal §22(3) includes consideration, three or more accounts, or repetition within five years after specified warning; maximum three years/NT$1 million is not an automatic sentence. https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=G0380131&flno=22 . Current effective provision. Account provision is not a conclusive fraud verdict; fraud/money laundering allegations need their own elements/evidence.
- C2 — 刑事訴訟法 (Code of Criminal Procedure) §95: suspect charges, silence, counsel and favorable-evidence rights; consult exact conditions before describing interruption of questioning. https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=C0010001&flno=95 . §100-2 applies chapter to police questioning of criminal suspects: https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=C0010001&flno=100-2 . Current effective provisions.
- C3 — §99 provides interpretation for language incompatibility and applies correspondingly to other persons examined/questioned, subject to different legal rules. https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=C0010001&flno=99 . This is procedural interpreting; not a promise that the firm speaks Thai/Indonesian or provides an interpreter free/on demand.
- C4 — 存款帳戶及其疑似不法或顯屬異常交易管理辦法 §§3,5,9,10: warning account is official notification for criminal investigation; all transaction functions suspended; account holder queries origin-reporting authority; lifting follows that authority's notice or lawful expiry. https://law.fsc.gov.tw/LawContent.aspx?id=FL039015 . Revised 2026-08-31; §20 makes this revision effective on publication (2014 §9 exception is historical). Bank compliance checks, AML §22 restrictions, warning accounts and seized money are different routes; do not promise immediate unfreezing after report/repayment/nonprosecution.

### B1–B4. Cross-border invoice recovery

- B1 — 涉外民事法律適用法 (Act Governing the Choice of Law in Civil Matters Involving Foreign Elements) §20: party choice of governing law; otherwise closest connection with stated presumptions. https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=B0000007&flno=20 . Current effective provision. Do not assume Taiwan substantive law simply because a party is Taiwanese; jurisdiction/arbitration separate.
- B2 — 民法 §127(8): two-year period for qualifying merchants/manufacturers/artisans' goods/product price claims where Taiwan law governs. https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=B0000001&flno=127 . §130: demand-based interruption is deemed ineffective absent suit within six months. https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=B0000001&flno=130 . Both effective, unrelated future Civil Code banner noted in L6. No “every commercial debt is two years” claim.
- B3 — 民事訴訟法 (Code of Civil Procedure) §509 excludes payment-order procedure where reciprocal performance remains unperformed or service must be abroad/by publication. https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=B0010001&flno=509 . §516 allows debtor objection without grounds within 20 days after service: https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=B0010001&flno=516 . Effective provisions. A foreign creditor is not categorically disqualified. No guarantee of uncontested order or recovery.
- B4 — Same §§522–523: monetary/convertible monetary claim may seek preservation by provisional attachment; requires risk future enforcement impossible/very difficult. https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=B0010001&flno=522 and https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=B0010001&flno=523 . Current effective provisions. Do not turn ordinary late payment into automatic attachment. Security/evidentiary rules need §526 if discussed.
