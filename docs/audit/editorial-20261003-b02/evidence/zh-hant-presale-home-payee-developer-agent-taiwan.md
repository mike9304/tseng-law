## Scope and audience

- Reader: a Taiwan consumer buying a presale home, faced with different developer/seller, sales agent and payee names, or a new payment instruction.
- The question is who is entitled to receive this installment and how that payment relates to the chosen performance security. It is not a generic foreign-buyer/tax guide, an assertion that different names establish fraud, or a promise that a trust bank guarantees refund.
- Taiwan-specific treatment is necessary because the five official presale performance-security choices, the document-delivery requirements and the consumer-contract rules determine the analysis. This is independently written for that need, not translated from an English BEC column.
- No actual buyer, project, company, account or lawsuit is alleged. The opening describes a possible transaction configuration; it does not invent a personal consultation or a reported incident.
