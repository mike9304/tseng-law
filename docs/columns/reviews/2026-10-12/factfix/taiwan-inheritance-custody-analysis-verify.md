### Current statute text (第 1030-1 條, 5 paragraphs)

第1項:
「法定財產制關係消滅時，夫或妻現存之婚後財產，扣除婚姻關係存續所負債務後，如有剩餘，其雙方剩餘財產之差額，應平均分配。但下列財產不在此限：一、因繼承或其他無償取得之財產。二、慰撫金。」

第2項:
「夫妻之一方對於婚姻生活無貢獻或協力，或有其他情事，致平均分配有失公平者，法院得調整或免除其分配額。」

第3項:
「法院為前項裁判時，應綜合衡酌夫妻婚姻存續期間之家事勞動、子女照顧養育、對家庭付出之整體協力狀況、共同生活及分居時間之久暫、婚後財產取得時間、雙方之經濟能力等因素。」

第4項:
「第一項請求權，不得讓與或繼承。但已依契約承諾，或已起訴者，不在此限。」

第5項:
「第一項剩餘財產差額之分配請求權，自請求權人知有剩餘財產之差額時起，二年間不行使而消滅。自法定財產制關係消滅時起，逾五年者，亦同。」

Amendment history (LawHistory page): 「中華民國一百十年一月二十日總統華總一義字第 11000004151 號令 修正公布第 1030-1 條條文；並自公布日施行」. This is the latest entry that touches Art. 1030-1; the newest entry on the page (115.8.17) amends only Art. 1223.

Earlier version (LawOldVer page, version of 101.12.26, 4 paragraphs), second paragraph: 「依前項規定，平均分配顯失公平者，法院得調整或免除其分配額。」 So 顯失公平 is the wording replaced on 110.1.20 (2021-01-20); the factor paragraph (now 第3項) does not exist in that version.

Official English translation, fragments quoted by the tool: "provided that equal distribution is unfair"; "may adjust or waive the share of distribution"; "the court shall consider synthetically the factors such as household labor"; "household labor, caring and nurturing of the child, the collaboration of contribution to the family,"; "the time of receiving the property acquired in marriage or each party’s economic ability."

### 1a — Adjustment threshold

Claims as published:
- ko (§3, 4th paragraph): 「균등 분배의 결과가 현저히 불공평하면」
- ja (§3, last paragraph): 「均等分配の結果が著しく不公平となるときです」
- en (§3, last paragraph): "if an equal distribution would be manifestly unfair"
- zh-hant (§3, 4th paragraph): 「平均分配顯失公平時」

Verdict: CONFIRMED-ERROR in all four languages. 현저히 불공평 / 著しく不公平 / manifestly unfair / 顯失公平 reproduce the pre-2021 threshold 顯失公平. The current 第2項 says 有失公平: the court may act when equal distribution is unfair, without the "manifestly" qualifier. Correct statement: equal distribution is unfair (有失公平).

Not treated as an error: the column gives the condition in summary form and leaves out the lead-in 「夫妻之一方對於婚姻生活無貢獻或協力，或有其他情事」. That lead-in ends in a catch-all (或有其他情事), so "equal distribution is unfair" still states the operative condition correctly. Left as written.

### 1b — What the court may do

Claims as published:
- ko: 「법원이 분배액을 조정할 수 있다」
- ja: 「裁判所が分配額を調整できる場合もあります」
- en: "a court may adjust the distribution amount"
- zh-hant: 「法院得…調整或免除分配額」

Verdict: CONFIRMED-ERROR for ko, ja, en — they name adjustment only, while 第2項 says 「法院得調整或免除其分配額」 (adjust or waive). Correct statement: the court may adjust or waive the distribution amount. zh-hant: ORIGINAL-RIGHT (already says 調整或免除); used as the reference and left unchanged on this point.

### 1c — Factors the court weighs

Claims as published:
- ko: 「혼인 기간, 가사노동과 자녀 돌봄, 경제적 기여, 직업상 사정, 재산 취득과 관리 상황 등 법이 요구하는 요소」
- ja: 「婚姻期間、家事労働と子の養育、経済的貢献、職業上の事情、財産の取得と管理の状況など、法律が求める要素」
- en: "The factors required by law … including the duration of the marriage, household work and childcare, economic contributions, occupational circumstances, and how property was acquired and managed"
- zh-hant: 「依婚姻共同生活及協力情形等因素」

Verdict for ko, ja, en: PARTLY. The three versions present the list as the factors the law requires, but only household work and childcare match 第3項. The rest does not:
- 직업상 사정 / 職業上の事情 / occupational circumstances — not in 第3項.
- 재산 … 관리 상황 / 財産の…管理の状況 / how property was … managed — not in 第3項; the statute names the time of acquisition (婚後財產取得時間), not management.
- 경제적 기여 / 経済的貢献 / economic contributions — not in 第3項; the statute names overall contribution and cooperation given to the family (對家庭付出之整體協力狀況) and, separately, both parties' economic capacity (雙方之經濟能力).
- 혼인 기간 / 婚姻期間 / duration of the marriage — 第3項 names the length of cohabitation and of separation (共同生活及分居時間之久暫); 婚姻存續期間 appears only as the period in which the household work, childcare and cooperation took place.
Correct statement: the six factors of 第3項 — household work during the marriage, care and upbringing of children, overall contribution and cooperation given to the family, length of cohabitation and of separation, time at which property was acquired after marriage, economic capacity of both parties (the statute's list ends in 等因素, and the column keeps its 등 / など / "including").

Verdict for zh-hant: ORIGINAL-RIGHT. 「婚姻共同生活及協力情形等因素」 is a shortened reference, not a list presented as complete: both named items are in 第3項 (共同生活及分居時間之久暫; 對家庭付出之整體協力狀況) and 等因素 is the statute's own closing. Left unchanged.
