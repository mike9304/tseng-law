## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): 3/5 for guidance pack, 2/5 for columns
variety used: Modern Standard Arabic aimed at Gulf/Levant readers, consistent in the guidance pack (guillemets «»، comma ،, question mark ؟). Columns 001–002, 004 and 007 are usable legal MSA; 003 Q16–Q20, 005, 006, 008 and 009 drop into a Korean-blog register. Guidance says الإنجليزية; 001 and 007 say الإنكليزية (Levant) — mix, not Egyptian dialect. Traditional Chinese glosses (居留, 工作許可, 臺北) stay Traditional and should stay.
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- German/English chrome left on the Arabic site: "OUR TEAM", "OFFICES", "الملف الكامل (English)".
- Korea still the default reader in columns: "الشركات الكورية", "تحويل رأس المال (資本額) من كوريا", "بخلاف كوريا", "يعيش الابن في كوريا".
- Calque of 不意味著 / 具體: "وليس معنى أن", "على نحو ملموس", "القضية المفردة" (native: ولا يعني ذلك أن / تفصيلًا / القضية المعيَّنة).
- Opaque Sino-Korean leftovers: "أحرف هان", "الهواكياو", Hangul slugs, "إرشاد司法院" with 司法院 unglossed in the running label.
- Blog leftovers in 003/005/006/008/009: "يمكن تركها في التعليقات", "الاتصال في أي وقت", "هل كشفت عمري", title "؟؟".
- Gender of Wei Tseng is feminine in bios, bylines and 008 ("المحامية المديرة", "مثَّلت", "أنا المحامية"); the one clear miss is masculine "طالب تبادل" on her education line.

Guidance pack is careful, inclusive, and correctly negative on Arabic consultation, interpreter, reply time, free meeting and result. A Gulf/Levant client can follow it, but "مجموعات العمل" and the English eyebrows still read as translation. Column 002 renders 拘役 as "الحبس القصير" and 007’s gloss of 會面交往 ("الزيارة والتواصل") is clearer than the guidance pack; 003 Q16–20, 005 and 008–009 must not go to a client as they stand.
