# The new Taiwan–Singapore tax agreement applies from 2027: what changes for dividends, interest, royalties and service projects?

Your Singapore company can qualify for new 10% ceilings on Taiwan-source dividends (股利), interest (利息) and royalties (權利金). The beneficial owner (受益所有人) must be a Singapore resident (居住者), and treaty exceptions still apply. For Taiwan tax paid or withheld at source (就源繳納或扣繳之稅款), the new agreement applies to income payable from January 1, 2027. As of November 23, 2026, it is in force but those provisions await application ([Ministry of Finance announcement](https://www.mof.gov.tw/singlehtml/384fb3077bb349ea973e7fc6f13b6974?cntId=fc1efb8360554ee0b248f688b42766ae)).

Service work through employees or other engaged personnel can create a permanent establishment (PE, 常設機構), exposing attributable business profits to Taiwan tax. The new test counts periods of service activities in Taiwan for the same or connected projects, subject to the exclusions in [Article 5(4)](https://www.president.gov.tw/File/Doc/0e675f7d-760c-4de8-bf1c-5529044716ab).

## Which date controls a Taiwan payment?

Signed on December 31, 2025, the agreement entered into force on February 13, 2026, as confirmed by the [March 20 presidential gazette](https://www.president.gov.tw/File/Doc/eb3c8402-9070-4cba-b52e-a6c7e2af07b3).

For Taiwan tax paid or withheld at source (就源繳納或扣繳之稅款), [Article 28(2)(b)](https://www.president.gov.tw/File/Doc/0e675f7d-760c-4de8-bf1c-5529044716ab) makes the new provisions applicable to income payable (應付) from January 1, 2027. For other covered Taiwan taxes, they apply to income for taxable periods beginning on or after that date. A dividend that became payable in December 2026 remains subject to the earlier rules even if the subsidiary transfers it in January 2027 (a fictional example).

For Taiwan tax paid or withheld at source, income payable before January 1, 2027 needs assessment under Taiwan's domestic rules and the original agreement signed on December 30, 1981. Article 28(3) ends the original agreement's application to covered cases when the new provisions apply.

## Comparing the three payment ceilings

For Taiwan tax paid or withheld at source on qualifying income payable from January 1, 2027, [Articles 10–12](https://www.president.gov.tw/File/Doc/0e675f7d-760c-4de8-bf1c-5529044716ab) set these ceilings:

| Income paid by a Taiwan resident to a Singapore resident | New ceiling |
|---|---|
| Dividends from a Taiwan-resident company | 10% of the gross dividend, Article 10(2) |
| Taiwan-source interest | 10% of gross interest, subject to exemptions, Article 11(2)–(3) |
| Taiwan-source royalties | 10% of gross royalties, Article 12(2) |

Each requires payment by a Taiwan resident to a Singapore resident and a Singapore-resident beneficial owner. Article 4 defines residence by reference to the territory's tax law. Under Article 4(3), a company resident in both territories is treated as resident where its place of effective management (實際管理處所) is located, and if that place cannot be determined, the two competent authorities must resolve the question by mutual agreement. Interest and royalties paid by a Taiwan resident are generally treated as Taiwan-source income under Articles 11(6) and 12(5).

Domestic rates provide the comparison, as of November 23, 2026. [Article 4 of the withholding-rate standards](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=G0340028&flno=4) (各類所得扣繳率標準) sets 21% for dividends distributed by a domestic enterprise to an enterprise headquartered abroad. For enterprises without a fixed place of business (固定營業場所) in Taiwan, [Article 3](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=G0340028&flno=3) sets interest withholding generally at 20%, specified instruments at 15%, and royalties at 20%. These domestic rates do not settle every pre-2027 payment's treaty treatment.

Under the original agreement, dividend tax plus corporate income tax on the underlying profits could not exceed 40% of the taxable income corresponding to the dividend. That corporate tax included amounts relieved under economic-development laws. The ministry's comparison also records the old royalty ceiling of 15% and absence of a separate interest article. By contrast, the new dividend ceiling uses the gross dividend, has no minimum shareholding and preserves tax on the subsidiary's underlying profits.

Interest under Article 11 includes debt-claim and specified bond income. Late-payment penalties are excluded. Interest arising in Taiwan and paid by a Taiwan bank to a Singapore-resident bank that is its beneficial owner is taxable only in Singapore under Article 11(3)(b); other exemptions have their own conditions.

Royalties under Article 12(3) are payments for the use of, or right to use, copyright in literary, artistic or scientific works (including films and films or tapes for radio or television broadcasting), patents, trademarks, designs or models, plans, secret formulas or processes, or for information concerning industrial, commercial or scientific experience. ## When the ceiling gives way to another rule

If your Singapore-resident company is the beneficial owner and conducts business through its Taiwan PE, an effectively connected shareholding, debt claim, or royalty right or property brings the income under Article 7. The ordinary ceiling provisions do not apply. [Articles 10(4), 11(5) and 12(4)](https://www.president.gov.tw/File/Doc/0e675f7d-760c-4de8-bf1c-5529044716ab) require this result; Article 14 concerns an individual's fixed base (固定處所). Article 11(5) also displaces the bank-to-bank exemption when its connection conditions are met, without displacing every Article 11(3) exemption.

If a special relationship between the payer and beneficial owner, or between them and a third party, causes interest or royalties to exceed what the payer and beneficial owner would have agreed without that relationship, Articles 11(7) and 12(6) apply those articles only to that amount, and each territory must tax the excess under its own laws, taking the agreement's other provisions into account.

Benefits for the income must be denied under [Article 27(1)](https://www.president.gov.tw/File/Doc/0e675f7d-760c-4de8-bf1c-5529044716ab) if, considering all facts and circumstances, it is reasonable to conclude that obtaining them directly or indirectly was one of the principal purposes of the arrangement or transaction. An exception applies where granting them is shown to accord with the relevant provisions' object and purpose.

Where Singapore taxes that income by the amount remitted there or received there rather than its full amount, Article 27(2) provides that Taiwan treaty relief applies only to the part of that income remitted to or received in Singapore. If the income qualifies for Singapore exemption under Article 23(1)(b), paragraph 3 instead requires Taiwan to apply treaty relief to its full amount. Singapore treatment needs separate confirmation.

## Service days and construction months

Under [Article 5(3)(b)](https://www.president.gov.tw/File/Doc/0e675f7d-760c-4de8-bf1c-5529044716ab), services, including consultancy, through employees or other engaged personnel meet the service PE threshold when those activities in Taiwan continue or accumulate for more than 183 days in any twelve-month period. Count the same or connected projects together. Exactly 183 days falls short. Article 5(4)'s exclusions apply; the original agreement had no service PE test.

Construction, installation, assembly, building sites and related supervision have a separate test: continuing for more than nine months under Article 5(3)(a), subject to Article 5(4). The ministry records the old construction test as more than six months cumulatively within one year, or six consecutive months within two years.

Subsidiary ownership alone does not create a PE under Article 5(7). Other PE grounds, including fixed places and dependent agents, remain subject to Article 5's conditions and exceptions. [Article 7](https://www.president.gov.tw/File/Doc/0e675f7d-760c-4de8-bf1c-5529044716ab) permits Taiwan to tax profits attributable to a Taiwan PE through which the enterprise conducts business; the payment ceilings supply no general service-fee rate.

## Other changes and the contracts behind your payments

Only the enterprise's territory of residence may tax its international shipping and air transport profits under [Article 8](https://www.president.gov.tw/File/Doc/0e675f7d-760c-4de8-bf1c-5529044716ab), and interest on funds connected with those operations counts as operating profit, outside Article 11.

On transfer pricing, [Article 9(2)](https://www.president.gov.tw/File/Doc/0e675f7d-760c-4de8-bf1c-5529044716ab) requires the other territory to adjust its tax on previously taxed profits reallocated and taxed by the first territory on independent-enterprise terms, to the extent it agrees with that profit adjustment; in determining the adjustment, it must take the agreement's other provisions into account, and the two competent authorities must consult if necessary.

The new [Article 13](https://www.president.gov.tw/File/Doc/0e675f7d-760c-4de8-bf1c-5529044716ab) allocates taxing rights over property gains, including allowing Taiwan to tax a Singapore resident's gain from transferring Taiwan real property. Under Article 23(2)(b), a Taiwan-resident company receiving dividends from a Singapore-resident company can claim a credit for Singapore tax paid on the underlying profits (間接稅額扣抵) if it owns at least 25% of the payer's share capital directly or indirectly; the credit cannot exceed the Taiwan tax attributable to that included income before credit. Article 23(3)'s tax-sparing credit (視同已納稅額扣抵) treats tax relieved under economic-development laws as paid; both transitional rules apply to Taiwan corporate returns for 2027–2029, as the ministry confirms.

The withholding agent (扣繳義務人) must withhold tax when the dividend is paid to an enterprise headquartered abroad under [Income Tax Act Article 88](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=G0340003&flno=88) (所得稅法). For administration, the ministry directs taxpayers to its rules on applying income tax agreements (適用所得稅協定查核準則).
