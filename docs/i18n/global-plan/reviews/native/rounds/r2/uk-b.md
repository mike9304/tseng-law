## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): N/A for guidance pack (out of scope; part a), 4/5 for columns
variety used: standard modern Ukrainian, consistent. Formal Ви is capitalized. Dates are day–month–year (24 січня 2022 р., 5 червня 2026 р., 17 вересня 2026). The decimal comma is already right (0,5 метра). Thousands still use a period, and the currency code sits before the amount.
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- Thousands are grouped with a period, and TWD precedes the figure: "TWD 1.579.589".
- 013 and 015 still publish one clause per paragraph, and several of those breaks are only U+200B: "Окрім ситуації на ринку" then "інший важливий пункт".
- "обробка" stands in for accounting treatment or for consequences: "бухгалтерська та податкова обробка".
- A rule or a file is told to be "kept" or "managed": "треба вести разом із моментом реєстрації".
- Registered title and beneficial ownership are still partly a name or an economic owner: "не стають його економічними власниками".
- A stock cleft copies the English "what must be confirmed is who": "те, що треба спершу підтвердити, — хто імпортує".
Attorney Wei Tseng is feminine wherever she narrates or is named (010 "Я була"; byline "Адвокатка Wei Tseng" in 011, 014, 016 and 017). 013 "я з цим погоджуюся" is unmarked present tense. 012 and 018 have no gendered byline. 012 "він" is motorcyclist A. No masculine title or pronoun for her remains. 013 "Я дізналася" is the reader’s question, not her. The consultation lock holds: no Ukrainian consult, no interpreter, no reply-time promise, no free meeting, no result guarantee. 013, 015 and 018 name only English, Chinese (中文), Japanese and Korean. Round-1 errors that are gone include the final advertisement called a product, Kinmen joined with і, 過戶 as a change of name, vesting as "віднесення", and "100 % часток". 012 and 017 are close. 014, 016 and 018 are close. 013 and 015 still read as a chopped post.
