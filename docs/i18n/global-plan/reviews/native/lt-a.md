## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): 4/5 for guidance pack, 3/5 for columns
variety used: standard Lithuanian (bendrinė), consistent; no dialect split. German-style thousands separators and a few calques from the German sister text. City names in the guidance pack (Taipėjus, Gaosiongas, Taidžongas, Pingdongas) are the right exonyms; column 001 breaks Taipei.
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- Company dissolution (解散) is “paleidimas”, which in Lithuanian means a launch: "paleidimo registraciją (解散登記)"
- Republic of China years are written as ordinary Lithuanian year-counts: "112 metų 4-ajame konstituciniame sprendime"
- Money uses German thousands “500.000” and English “1.57M” instead of “500 000” / “1,57 mln.”: "TWD 500.000 (新臺幣)"
- “be” is used as German “neben” and with the wrong case: "Be atlygio gali atsirasti teismo rinkliavos"; "be regos negalia"
- Team bios and a few UI roles stay in English: "Anksčiau Ministry of Education, Legal Affairs Division"
- Columns 006, 008 and 009 (and 003 from Q16) drop into a spoken blog, one sentence per line: "Daugelis Jūsų tai jau žinote."

Guidance pack: consultation-language lines, no interpreter, no reply-time promise, no free first meeting and no result promise are correctly negative, and Wei Tseng is consistently “advokatė” / “vadovaujančioji advokatė”. A Lithuania client can use the pack. Columns 001, 004 and 007 are dense but real legal Lithuanian, and 001/004/005 already mark the Taiwan–Korea tax and banking material as a Korea example. Column 002 is undermined by “paleidimas”. No masculine title or pronoun for Wei Tseng in this part (“Taivano advokatė”, “Tvarkydama”, “pati nagrinėjau”, “norėčiau”). “Geriausių vaiko interesų” is the statutory term (子女最佳利益), not a firm “best” claim. “Geriausią rezultatą” in 008 is a fact about client B, not about the firm.
