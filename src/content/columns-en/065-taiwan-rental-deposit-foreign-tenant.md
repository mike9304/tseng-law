# Getting a Taiwan residential deposit back as a foreign tenant

[Rental Housing Market Development and Regulation Act Article 7](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=D0060125&flno=7) caps the deposit at two months' rent and requires the landlord to return the deposit, or what remains after set-off, when the lease ends, the tenant returns the dwelling and pays debts arising from the lease. Article 4 excludes short stays under thirty days, tourism use and certain public or cooperative housing from the Act.

[Land Act Article 99](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=D0060001&flno=99) likewise caps housing security money at two months' rent and lets the tenant apply any excess to rent.

## When the landlord may take the dwelling back

For unfixed-term housing leases, [Land Act Article 100](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=D0060001&flno=100) bars reclaim unless a listed ground exists: self-use or rebuilding, subletting in breach of Civil Code Article 443 paragraph 1, arrears of two months after applying the deposit, illegal use, breach of contract, or unrepaired damage to the dwelling or fittings. Fixed-term leases turn first on expiry, renewal and early-termination clauses, which still sit under mandatory and prohibited terms in the MOI standard residential lease notice.

## Records that matter in a deposit fight

Move-in and move-out photos, inventory checklists, rent transfers, repair chats and proof that a termination notice arrived are the usual backbone. Each deduction should match a contractual or statutory ground and actual loss. Article 12 of the Rental Housing Act allows certain costs of abandoned belongings after demand to come out of the deposit.
