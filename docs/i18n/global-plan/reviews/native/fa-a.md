## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): 3/5 for guidance pack, 3/5 for columns
variety used: Iranian standard Persian (فارسی معیار), not Dari. Body copy of the guidance pages and of columns 001, 002, 004 and 007 uses ZWNJ, Persian comma ،, and «» quotes consistently. Meta descriptions, the footer, one FAQ answer, the llms notice, and the link labels «میپذیریم» / «بین المللی» drop the ZWNJ. Columns 003 Q16–Q20, 005, 006, 008 and 009 switch to a Korean-blog line break.
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- Nim-fasele dropped on می‌ and on compounds, so some strings become another word: "تنها نقشهای برای یافتن" (نقشه‌ای read as نقش‌های).
- Privacy and llms calques that shift the German: "چه میگرداند" (erhebt), "نمایانی" (Anzeige), "پروندهٔ llms.txt" (Datei), "انبار شیء", "رونوشت شد".
- حکم کردن used for “assess”, which in a law firm means a court ruling: "باید بر سراسر دورهٔ فعالیت حکم کرد".
- Korean-blog voice in 003 Q16–Q20, 005, 006, 008, 009: "به تجربهٔ من" / "سلام، من وکیل تایوانی".
- Minguo years left bare: "شماره 644 سال 109", "حکم قانون اساسی شماره 4 سال 112".
- Column 004 puts Korea back where the English already says home country: "آیین سرمایه‌گذاری در خارج در کره".

Guidance pages on home, services, about, contact and FAQ are careful Iranian legal prose, and the refusals (no Persian consultation, no interpreter, no reply-time, no free first meeting, no result) stay negative and readable. Gregorian dates with Persian month names (13 سپتامبر، 29 مه، 1 ژوئیه) are the form an Iran reader expects. Wei Tseng is not put in a masculine form: وکیل، دانشجوی تبادل and the past verb are unmarked, which is correct for a woman in Persian. Columns 001, 002 and 007 can go to a client after the rows below; 003 from Q16, and 005, 006, 008 and 009, still sound like a translated blog. Several English hazards are already softened in Persian (no “maximum compensation”, no comments box, 009 already says the result is not promised, 008’s anecdote no longer claims a high payout).
