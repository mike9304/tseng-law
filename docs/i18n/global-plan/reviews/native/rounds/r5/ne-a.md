## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): 4/5 for guidance pack, 4/5 for columns
variety used: Standard Nepali (नेपाली), Devanagari, consistent — Nepali running grammar, not Hindi, apart from the individual items in rows 9, 12, 13 and 20. Gregorian dates use Nepal day–month–year order with Nepali month names (13 सेप्टेम्बर 2025, 27 डिसेम्बर 2023, 29 मे 2026, 25 जुलाई 2026). Western digits throughout, which is normal for Nepali legal prose; only the thousands grouping (row 23) is foreign.

systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- Cataphoric "यो … कि" complement clauses copied from the source's dass/that-clause; Nepali puts the clause first and closes it with भन्ने: "यो आश्वासन होइन कि हरेक अवस्थामा दायित्व लगानी रकममै रोकिन्छ"

- मध्ये is written detached from its noun 9 times in the guidance and 6 times in the columns: "चार भाषा मध्ये कुनै प्रयोग गर्न सक्नुहुन्न भने"
