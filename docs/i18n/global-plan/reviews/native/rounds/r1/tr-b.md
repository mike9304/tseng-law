## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): N/A for guidance pack (out of scope), 3/5 for columns
variety used: Standard Turkish of Türkiye, consistent. Formal *siz*. Circumflex *dâhil* in 011, 014 and 016; *dahil* in 013 and 015. Dates are day-month-year; thousands use a dot and the decimal comma (0,5 metre).
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- *Sabitlemek* stands in for “determine / establish”, and the infinitive is sometimes in the wrong voice: `"lansman tarihi sabitlenmek yerine"`.
- The responsible person is a grammatical *özne* (主體): `"sorumluluğu üstlenecek özne sabitlenmelidir"` (also 014 `"düzenleyen özneyle"`).
- Statutory requirements “fill up” instead of being met: `"yasal şartın dolduğu da kabul edilmemelidir"` (also 016 `"yasal şartlar dolduğunda"`).
- *Hareket* is used for a response or for “in practice”: `"Gerçek haklar ve hareket biçimi"` / `"Gerçek harekette poliçe"`.
- 013 and 015 still break almost every clause into its own paragraph: `"Piyasa durumunun yanı sıra"` then a blank line.
- 013 and 015 invite a written consultation without naming English, Chinese (中文), Japanese or Korean: `"görüşmeye ihtiyacınız varsa İletişim sayfasından yazabilirsiniz"`. 018 asks for the preferred language and says the languages actually available are confirmed on contact.
Attorney Wei Tseng is not given a masculine pronoun, adjective or title. *Avukat* does not mark gender. First person in 010 (*oldum*) and 013 (*katılıyorum*, *öneririm*) is gender-neutral. 012’s *A* is the motorcyclist. 018 has no byline.
012 is native professional apart from two phrases. 014, 016 and 017 are close (*kıyasen*, *saklı pay*, *zıya*, *navlun*, *hemzemin geçit*, *fren payı*). 010 and 011 still move the legal object. 018 inherits the English jump from §5 to §7 and the stray “contract-design” sentence; those are not translation defects. No win-rate, “best/only”, interpreter, free-meeting or reply-time promise remains. Negated guarantees stay negated.
