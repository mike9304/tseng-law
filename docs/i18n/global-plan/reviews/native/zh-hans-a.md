## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): 3/5 for guidance pack, 2/5 for columns
variety used: Simplified characters throughout, but the register is Taiwanese Mandarin (台湾华语) written in简体 — not mixed with another Sinitic language. Proper-name Traditional glosses (昊鼎國際法律事務所, 國立臺灣大學, 112年憲判字第4號) are correct; most other parentheses wrongly repeat Simplified. Columns 003 Q16–20, 005, 006, 008, 009 drop into Korean-blog first person. No masculine form for 曾雋崴 (Chinese 律师 is unmarked). The FAQ “可以用中文咨询吗？可以。” is the intended positive lock for this locale and is not a rule breach.
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- Parentheses repeat the same Simplified word instead of the Taiwan Traditional term: "居留证（居留）或工作许可（工作许可）"
- Taiwan legal Chinese (检视 / 须 / 得 / 税捐 / 智慧财产 / 资遣 / 声请) is used as if it were mainland usage, with no first-use 知识产权（智慧財產權）-style gloss
- Korea leftovers were not generalized for mainland readers: "即使韩国母公司进入台湾，也不得只看法台湾法令"
- Late columns keep Hangul slugs, /ko/ “韩语律师” CTAs, and “请留在评论中”
- Corner quotes 「」 and TWD 500,000 mixing, against mainland “ ” and 新台币50万元
- First-person blog tone and missing periods in 003 Q16–20, 005–006, 008–009: "您好，我是台湾律师曾雋崴。"

001–002, 004 and 007 are dense but competent Taiwan legal Chinese; they fail as mainland-reader copy, not as meaning. 003 (from Q16), 005, 006, 008 and 009 would embarrass the firm in front of a mainland client.
