## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): N/A for guidance pack (out of scope), 3/5 for columns
variety used: Standard Danish (rigsdansk), consistent. Dates are day + lowercase month + year (13. september 2025; 5. juni 2026; 17. september 2026). Thousands use a period (1.579.589; 25.000.000) and the decimal comma is used in 012 (0,5 meter).
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- The regulated person is still called *subjekt*: "det centrale ansvarssubjekt i kosmetikreguleringen" (also 017 *subjekt-indehaver* and 018 *et subjekt, der kan sælge*).
- *Frist* is used for a duration, not only a deadline: "under forudsætning af 1 bestemt fast frist".
- Traditional Chinese glosses sit on the wrong Danish word: "retlig rådgivning (損害賠償)" (also 016 and 018).
- A legal verb is one step off the Danish term: "Når arbejdstageren ophæver en tidsubegrænset arbejdskontrakt" and "indtræder arvingen globalt".
- *Bilag* stands for evidence, and in 014 for the signature itself: "kan være et bilag, der bekræfter aftalens eksistens".
- 016 is close to a native inheritance note once *globalt* and three glosses are fixed. 013 and 015 are usable Q&A. Wei Tseng is not given a masculine form (*Advokat*; first person *jeg*). One *ham* in 014 refers to the worker, not to her.
