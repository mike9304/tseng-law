## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): N/A for guidance pack (out of scope), 3/5 for columns
variety used: Standard German (DACH), Sie where the source addresses the reader; 010 and 013 keep first-person case narration. Adjective *taiwanesisch* is consistent across these nine files (the Duden headword is *taiwanisch*).
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- False friends point at the wrong legal object: `"Regeln zu Kennzeichnung, Bewerbung und Werbung"` (011, a job application), `"Anteile bei Gesamtnamen"` (016), `"Kundenreaktion bei Vertragsende"` (017).
- 011 and 017 still calque the regulated person as *Subjekt* and pair-split every role: `"zentrales Verantwortungssubjekt der Kosmetikregelung"` / `"Logistikdienstleisterin oder Logistikdienstleister"`.
- 013 and 015 still read as a blog, with a blank line after almost every clause: `"Neben der Marktlage"` then `"ist ein weiterer wichtiger Gesichtspunkt,"`.
- 014 coins the labour terms a statute would not use: `"eine andere vorleistende Zahlung (預付性給付)"`, `"Neueintritte"`.
- Attorney Wei Tseng is feminine wherever she is named (010 *Prozessbevollmächtigte*; 011, 014, 016, 017 *Rechtsanwältin*). 012’s *er* is motorcyclist A. 018 has no gendered byline. No masculine form for her remains.
- 012 is native professional German apart from two phrases. 014, 016 and 018 are close. 018’s earlier English leftovers (company-form names, Investment Commission, NT$, *offen*, body *Erstberatung*) are fixed; the H3 still says *Aktiengesellschaft* and §8 still says *erste Beratung*.
