variety used: Serbian ekavian as used in Serbia, Latin script, consistent. Not Croatian and not ijekavian, except „žmigavac“ in 012. Dates are already right for Serbia (24. 1. 2022., 13. septembra 2025., 5. 6. 2026., 17. septembra 2026.). The decimal comma is right (0,5 metara). systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- "i" joins Kinmen and Lienchiang where the rule is alternative: "u Kinmenu (金門) i Lienchiangu, odnosno Macuu".
- "žmigavac" is the Croatian word; in Serbia the turn signal is "migavac" or, in this register, "pokazivač pravca": "desnim žmigavcem".
- "podupirati" is attached to the loss itself: "isprave koje podupiru štetu".
- 013 and 015 still contain U+200B, including lines that are only that character: "dovoljno je navesti područje (primer: grad Tajpej)."
