# Someone Died in the Taiwan Home You Bought or Rented: Disclosure Forms, Rescission and Deadlines

Many buyers assume a home where someone died by suicide or violence can simply be handed back. No Taiwanese statute says so. Neither the Civil Code nor the Consumer Protection Act defines a "haunted house" (凶宅).

What exists is a form. The Interior Ministry's mandatory terms for standard-form resale home contracts attach a Building Condition Confirmation (建物現況確認書), whose item 7 asks whether homicide, suicide, carbon monoxide poisoning or any other non-natural death has occurred in the unit. What was ticked there, and how fast the buyer moved, decide whether the sale can be unwound or only discounted.

## Two periods, two different questions

Item 7 was in the version announced on October 31, 2019, and a revision of the form in force since April 1, 2026 left its wording unchanged. It covers "this building (exclusive portion)", is signed and dated by both sides, and asks two things:

- During the seller's ownership (於產權持有期間): did any of these events occur? Yes / No.
- Before the seller's ownership (於產權持有前): the seller confirms none / knows one occurred / does not know whether one occurred.

The first question has no time limit. Any event during the seller's ownership counts.

The second question is softer. A seller who truly knew nothing about a previous owner's years fills it in correctly by ticking "do not know". Disputes then turn on what the seller really knew.

Stairwells, rooftops and other flats in the complex sit outside the "exclusive portion". Buyers who care should ask in writing before signing.

## Buying through an agent adds a second document

Brokered sales come with a real estate disclosure statement (不動產說明書). A licensed broker signs it, and the central authority sets its contents ([Real Estate Broking Management Act art. 22](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=D0060066&flno=22)). Agents explain the property with it after the client has signed it (article 23), and once the buyer signs it too, it counts as part of the contract ([article 24](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=D0060066&flno=24)(2)).

Rules for the statement, amended on January 13, 2026, still require it to state for resale homes "whether homicide, suicide, carbon monoxide poisoning or other non-natural death occurred in this building (exclusive portion) during the ownership period, and if so, describe it."

Nothing asks about earlier years, so a buyer holding only this statement has no written answer about them.

## Developer or private seller

Announced mandatory terms form part of a standard-form contract even when left out of it ([Consumer Protection Act art. 17](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=J0170001&flno=17)(5)), and a standard-form contract is one built on a business's pre-drafted terms ([article 2](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=J0170001&flno=2)(7), (9)). Buying from a developer or property company, the point 2 duty to confirm the building's condition on the form is part of the contract even if the written contract leaves it out.

Where private parties used the ministry's model contract, its signing notes (item 1) call it a reference; the signed forms are the starting point.

## Is a death in the unit a legal defect?

[Civil Code article 354](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=B0000001&flno=354) makes the seller warrant that, when risk passes, the home has no defect destroying or reducing its value or its ordinary or agreed utility; trivial reductions do not count. The article lists no examples, so a court decides on the facts whether a death in the unit is a defect.

Signing note 3(5) of the model contract ties the form to defects reducing value or utility, and point 7 of the mandatory terms calls untrue entries in items 5 to 7 "a material defect".

On its wording, the warranty does not require the seller to have known. The buyer's knowledge matters instead. A buyer who knew at contracting gets no warranty; one who missed it through gross negligence gets none unless the seller guaranteed its absence, and a seller who intentionally concealed it loses that second defence ([article 355](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=B0000001&flno=355)).

So the form cuts both ways. A "yes" above the buyer's signature is evidence the buyer knew; a "no" records what the seller put in writing.

## Rescission or a price cut

[Article 359](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=B0000001&flno=359) lets the buyer rescind or demand a price reduction. Where rescission would be manifestly unfair in the circumstances, only a reduction is available. The article gives no list of such cases. Courts weigh them one by one.

Damages are a third route where a guaranteed quality is missing or the seller intentionally concealed the defect ([article 360](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=B0000001&flno=360)).

What about a clause selling the home "as is, without warranty"? It is void where the seller intentionally concealed the defect ([article 366](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=B0000001&flno=366)). Business sellers' standard-form contracts may not exclude the Civil Code warranty after handover at all (prohibited term 2 of the resale mandatory terms).

## Six months, five years, one year

A buyer takes delivery of a Taichung flat in March 2026. In September a neighbour mentions a suicide in the unit while the seller owned it (an invented example). Item 7(1) on the form says "no".

[Article 356](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=B0000001&flno=356) requires a buyer who later finds a hidden defect to notify the seller at once, or be deemed to have accepted the property. Rescission and reduction rights then lapse six months after that notice, or five years after delivery ([article 365](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=B0000001&flno=365)).

Intentional concealment changes this. [Article 357](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=B0000001&flno=357) disapplies article 356, so there is no inspection or notice duty and no deemed acceptance, and the six-month limit goes too (article 365(2)). The five years remain.

For the September notice, six months runs to about March 2027. Proof that the buyer knew at signing would send the case back to article 355.

No form is prescribed, but dated written notice is the best evidence.

## When the seller lied

A seller who ticked "no" knowingly also faces fraud. Under [article 92](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=B0000001&flno=92), a declaration induced by fraud may be rescinded; fraud by a third party, such as an agent, counts only if the other party knew or could have known.

It must come within one year of discovering the fraud, and never more than ten years after the declaration ([article 93](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=B0000001&flno=93)). Declared to the other party ([article 116](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=B0000001&flno=116)), it makes the act void from the start ([article 114](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=B0000001&flno=114)), and the price becomes a benefit without legal ground that must be returned ([article 179](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=B0000001&flno=179)).

Warranty rescission follows [article 259](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=B0000001&flno=259) absent other rules in law or contract: money returns with interest from receipt (item 2), and the buyer returns the home (item 1).

## Mortgage money already moving

Once a buyer hands the bank the loan disbursement authorization (撥款委託書), point 7 of the resale mandatory terms bars cancelling, rescinding or changing the loan or asking the bank to delay or halt payment, except for a material defect where items 5 to 7 of the form are untrue. Item 7 is the death question. Other loan issues are in [our article on resale loan shortfalls](/en/columns/taiwan-resale-home-loan-shortfall-cash-difference-rescission).

## Renting instead

Tenants see the same question in item 8 of the Rental Property Condition Confirmation (租賃標的現況確認書), annexed to the mandatory terms for standard-form residential leases in force since September 1, 2020. Same deaths, same split between the ownership period and the time before, and both parties sign. Later Gazette amendments changed other points (6, 11, 18 and prohibited term 10).

[Article 5(1) of the Rental Housing Market Development and Regulation Act](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=D0060125&flno=5) deems landlord and tenant to have a consumer relationship and applies the Consumer Protection Act. Citing that 2023 amendment, the ministry repealed its separate terms for non-business landlords on May 19, 2023. Leisure or travel rentals, housing run by the government, a dedicated body it set up, or a cooperative, and leases under 30 days are excluded ([article 4](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=D0060125&flno=4)).

Fraud rescission under articles 92 and 93, in the Civil Code's general part, applies to leases too. Ordinary mid-term exits are covered in [our article on early lease termination](/en/columns/taiwan-tenant-early-lease-termination-notice-penalty).

## The agent's share

A broker acting for both sides with written consent must disclose defects it should have found professionally and help with inspections ([Real Estate Broking Management Act art. 24-2](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=D0060066&flno=24-2), items 4 and 5). Brokerage and agent are jointly liable for harm the agent causes intentionally or negligently ([article 26](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=D0060066&flno=26)(2)).
