## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): N/A for guidance pack (out of scope), 4/5 for columns
variety used: standard Latvian (latviešu literārā valoda), consistent; no Latgalian. The decimal comma is already right in 012 (“0,5 metru”). Body dates such as “2026. gada 5. jūnijā” are already Latvian. Frontmatter still drops “gadā” (“13. septembrī 2025”, “4. februārī 2026”, “17. septembrī 2026”). Thousands still use a dot.
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- “Mēraukla” stands in for a standard, a threshold or a criterion: `"pēc sniegšanas brīža profesionālās vai tehniskās mērauklas"` (010); also 011, 014, 016 and 017.
- “Gaita” is used for a legal procedure: `"Uzturēt gaitu reakcijai"` (011); also 010, 013, 015 and 016.
- “Nākt vērā” and “kopīgi redz / jāredz” calque “come into consideration” and “see”: `"Ja tomēr nāk vērā cits prasījuma pamats"` (010); `"Kopīgi redz, vai darbinieks"` (014); `"Jāredz klienta prasības"` (018).
- Thousands use a dot, which Latvian does not: `"TWD 1.579.589"` (010); the same pattern is in 011, 017 and 018.
- “Pieskaitīšana” is used for vesting, and “brīdinājuma termiņš” for the resignation notice period: `"pieskaitīšanas brīdis"` and `"aiziešanas brīdinājuma termiņu"` (014).
- 013 and 015 still put clauses on their own lines, including a sentence that ends on a comma: `"uzturēšanās apliecību (外僑居留證),"` (013).

Wei Tseng is feminine wherever she is named (010 *procesuālā pārstāve* / *advokātes*; 011 and 014 and 016 and 017 *Advokāte Wei Tseng* / *atbildīgo advokāti*; 013 *man kā advokātei*). 013 and 015 say *advokāti vai advokātu*. 015’s *advokātam* is any lawyer who can request a transcript, not her. 012’s *A* is the motorcyclist. 018 has no gendered byline. No win-rate or “best firm” claim. “Bērna labākajām interesēm” in 016 is the best-interests test, not an advertising superlative. No P1 remained.
