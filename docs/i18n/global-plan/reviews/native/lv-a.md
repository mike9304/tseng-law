## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): 3/5 for guidance pack, 3/5 for columns
variety used: standard Latvian, consistent; German-calqued syntax and thousand-separators throughout; English job titles on the team page; Korean blog voice in columns 006, 008 and 009
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- Agreement breaks on "katrs/pats" plus a feminine or masculine head: "Katra lietas apjoms" / "Pati fakts"
- German thousands and an English million: "TWD 500.000" and "TWD 1.57M"
- Calques a Latvia lawyer would not write: "nepubliskā objektu krātuvē", "parāda esība", "no kā tiek barota"
- Debitive "jā-" used with an accusative object: "Izsniegšanas brīdi … jāapstiprina"
- City names stay English or hybrid ("Taipei", "Taibei") where Latvian has Taipeja
- Columns 006, 008 and 009 keep a chopped Korean blog voice: "Vai atceraties tradicionālo frizētavu laikus"

Guidance pack is meaning-faithful. Consultation-language, no-interpreter, no reply-time, no free first meeting and no-outcome lines stay correctly negative. Columns 001, 003, 004 and 007 can be read as legal Latvian once the agreement and term errors are fixed. 002 has two sentences that do not parse. 005 still opens on a Korean bank checklist. 006, 008 and 009 read as a translated blog. Canonical URLs in 008 and 009 are Hangul because they are the original post URLs (same as English); leave them.
