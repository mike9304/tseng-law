### 3. Trial exemption, TAC and language-versus-nationality

NIA 0915 notes 1 exclude trial exemptions, landing visas, Southeast Asian advance online authorization, ABTC and working holiday entry from the described residence route, unless another regulation provides otherwise. Thus the broad statutory Article 16 professional route must be accompanied by the actual entry-scheme boundary; Thai trial entry or an Indian/Indonesian TAC must not silently be treated as ordinary qualifying visa exemption. The guide retains an old Article 12 cross-reference in its timing note; the current professional direct route is Article 16. [NIA 0915](https://www.immigration.gov.tw/5475/5478/141465/141808/141824/cp_news), [current Act](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawAll.aspx?pcode=A0030295).

BOCA's 2026-07-17 page lists eligible Thai nationals, excluding diplomatic/official passports, for up to 14 days through 2027-07-31. That limited policy does not describe all Thai-language readers or long-term residence. Arabic language, UAE residence, UAE nationality, Saudi nationality and Omani nationality are distinct facts; the visa-exemption page contains nationality-specific entries, not an Arabic/GCC umbrella. No new nationality-day table is needed in these articles. [BOCA visa exemption](https://www.boca.gov.tw/cp-149-4486-7785a-2.html).

BOCA and the NIA TAC landing screen currently name India, Indonesia, Laos, Myanmar and Vietnam. The screen contains the 2026-08-01 Cambodian change and 2023 Vietnamese documentary limitation. The landing screen does not establish every present detailed TAC eligibility condition. The old worker-history exclusion should not be promoted into a complete current checklist without the current detail screen. It is sufficient to state that TAC is conditional and does not itself authorize work or residence. [BOCA TAC](https://www.boca.gov.tw/cp-152-274-8c0e2-2.html), [NIA TAC screen](https://niaspeedy.immigration.gov.tw/nia_southeast/).

Digital-nomad eligibility is also nationality-based on the currently opened BOCA page: only nationals from visa-exempt countries. Third-country residence alone does not meet that condition. It is a visitor-visa route, not an automatic ARC or Taiwan-employer work authorization. [BOCA digital-nomad guide](https://www.boca.gov.tw/cp-158-7718-c0382-2.html).

### 4. Migrant workers, professionals, family and study

- NIA 0926's migrant-worker online system uses intermediary/direct-employer account procedures. Its evidence, payment and collection stages differ from a professional applicant's own-account route. The WDA Thai and Indonesian notices also confirm personal counter filing is possible; a broker is not universally mandatory. [NIA 0926](https://www.immigration.gov.tw/5475/5478/141465/141469/367185/cp_news), [Thai WDA notice](https://fw.wda.gov.tw/wda-employer/home/file/2c95efb38d75b733018d7821eb450592), [Indonesian WDA notice](https://fw.wda.gov.tw/wda-employer/home/file/2c95efb38d75b733018d78209430058c).
- The professional/dependent portal is `foreign-white-collar`; migrant workers use `foreign-labor`; students use `student/entry`. Merely having a job offer does not select the professional portal. The professional guide is dated 2024-04-10 and retains historical statutory numbers; current-law propositions must use current law. [NIA 0927](https://www.immigration.gov.tw/5475/5478/141465/141469/367142/cp_news), [NIA student guide](https://www.immigration.gov.tw/5475/5478/141465/141808/417829/cp_news).
- Article 16's foreign-professional family route is spouse, minor children and adult children unable to care for themselves due to physical/mental disability, following approved sponsor residence/permanent residence and subject to entry conditions. Article 23's broader family provisions expressly exclude some sponsors, including specified migrant-worker employment. Neither provision creates a generic right for every parent, adult child or ARC holder's family. [Act 16](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawAll.aspx?pcode=A0030295), [Immigration Act 23](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=D0080132&flno=23).
- ARC alone does not authorize every job. Employment Service Act 48(1)(2) requires marriage to a Taiwan national with household registration plus approved residence for that work-permit exemption. Foreign specialist/senior professionals' qualifying resident spouses may apply to MOL under the separate Article 15 route; the dependent card is not automatic work permission. [Employment Service Act 43](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=N0090001&flno=43), [48](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=N0090001&flno=48), [Foreign Professionals Act](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawAll.aspx?pcode=A0030295).
- If document-retention rights are mentioned, preserve the statute's actual actor and condition: employer unlawful withholding under Article 57(8), private employment-service withholding against the person's wishes under Article 40. Do not describe a lawful short handover for an authorized filing as inherently criminal. [57](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=N0090001&flno=57), [40](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=N0090001&flno=40).
- Degree study and Mandarin study need separate entry and evidence descriptions. Mandarin's four completed months plus at least three further registered months must not become “more than three.” BOCA also specifies 15 weekly classroom hours and absence including leave no more than one quarter if those details are used. The ordinary study conversion restrictions cannot erase the separate professional route. [BOCA full-time students](https://www.boca.gov.tw/cp-166-283-c4da3-2.html), [Mandarin residence](https://www.boca.gov.tw/fp-166-285-041cb-2.html).

### 5. Fees, processing and travel

- Ordinary ARC: NT$1,000 per year, with less than a year charged as one; direct residence after visitor/visa-exempt entry adds NT$2,200. Overseas-compatriot student reduction is not a reduction for all international students. Lost/damaged ARC replacement is NT$500. Do not convert these to foreign currencies or treat a longer paid term as entitlement to that term. [Fee regulations 3, 7](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawAll.aspx?pcode=D0080135).
- Do not charge NT$500 for every information update. Current entrepreneur instructions expressly distinguish passport-number, address and service-place registration as no-fee changes; the professional/migrant instructions enumerate different replacement circumstances. Keep article fees confined to the proven category. [Entrepreneur fee section](https://www.immigration.gov.tw/5382/5385/7244/7250/7317/%E5%B1%85%E7%95%99/348924/).
- General NIA 0902 says ten working days excluding return, supplementation and interview; the clock is recalculated after supplementation. Professional and migrant-worker online guides specify five with qualifications. WDA's five/ten-day comparison is migrant-worker guidance. None is guaranteed total delivery or unconditional card receipt. [NIA 0902](https://www.immigration.gov.tw/5475/5478/141465/141808/141970).
- Student online instructions differentiate an electronic ARC of six months or less and physical-chip collection. Mandarin-extension duration is not safely replaced by a universal one-year renewal statement. Do not confuse the card format's six-month ceiling with a universal six-month period for all student residence. [NIA 0929](https://www.immigration.gov.tw/5475/5478/141465/141808/417829/cp_news).
- Article 31 does not make printed expiry a guarantee after residence purpose disappears. Its under-30-day reapplication provision requires continuing reason and a penalty; it is not a grace period. [Act 31](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=D0080132&flno=31).
- Article 34 requires re-entry permission before departure, with the APRC exception. The professional online guide says departure while its application is pending discontinues that application. Do not extend that portal-specific effect to every application without evidence. [Act 34 in consolidated law](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawAll.aspx?pcode=D0080132), [NIA 0927 notes 9](https://www.immigration.gov.tw/5475/5478/141465/141469/367142/cp_news).
- Valid ARC/APRC holders need a passport valid on arrival under the NIA FAQ, not a universal six remaining months. That is distinct from visa-application passport requirements and remains subject to entry examination. [NIA passport FAQ, updated 2026-06-29](https://www.immigration.gov.tw/5385/12162/12197/414977/).
