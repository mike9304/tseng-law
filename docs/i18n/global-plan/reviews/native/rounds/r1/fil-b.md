naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): N/A for guidance pack (part a), 3/5 for columns
variety used: Filipino (Tagalog-based, Philippines), formal kayo/ninyo. Not a dialect mix. Orthography is not unified: aktuwal in 010–017, aktwal in 018; konkreto in 011, kongkreto in 016. Philippine date order (Enero 24, 2022) and 0.5 / 1,579,589 separators are already native and are not listed.
012 is the file a native editor would pass after two gloss cleanups. 016’s sentences are native professional; what remains there is untranslated family-law nouns plus two coinages. 015 is close. 014 is legally careful in structure and still English at the noun. 018 is still an English company-law memo with Filipino verbs. Reply-time and guarantee lines that appear are negative and were left that way.
