- summary: The figure a U.S. parent can later convert back out of Taiwan on an approved share transfer, withdrawal or capital reduction is the investment amount the Ministry of Economic Affairs verified, not the amount in its approval (Act for Investment by Foreign Nationals, Article 12(2)). That figure is built from the wire as it landed: currency sold for NT dollars counts net of fees and related costs, and currency kept as foreign exchange is converted at the receiving bank's buying rate when it issues the transaction voucher. The authority's published Q&A says the remittance documents must be in the investor's name and that only a co-investor may remit on the investor's behalf. Verification is applied for within two months of each contribution, or within two months after the last tranche for all of them, and a cash capital increase is registered only after the whole amount is verified and a CPA has certified the new capital; no provision says which comes first. Any approved contribution not made within the approved period is revoked when it ends, unless an extension applied for with good cause before expiry is approved.
