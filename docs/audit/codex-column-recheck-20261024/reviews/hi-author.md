## First-two-paragraph deletion test

704: Opening sentence gives the resident-visa holder's 30-day duty; the next gives the next-day starting point. The short ARC definition supplies the unfamiliar document's meaning. The last sentence identifies NIA and preserves the integrated-document exemption. Paragraph two states the different clock for a resident visa issued inside Taiwan and the records needed to determine it. The old standalone sentence saying that BOCA's instructions describe the rule was deleted; its link now sits on the rule itself.

705: The opening question names the concrete decision between obtaining a resident visa and applying directly for ARC. The answer immediately identifies the professional direct route and explains why study/family cases depend on the entry visa's purpose and conditions. Paragraph two preserves passport nationality, purpose and entry-scheme eligibility plus the BOCA source. The statement about Hindi not proving nationality was removed; passport-based eligibility already conveys the material condition. There is no introductory promise to explain the topic.

706: The first paragraph states the employment/study/family trigger, Article 31 cancellation/revocation consequence and conditional exceptions. The redundant instruction that the reader cannot postpone considering the event until card expiry was deleted. Paragraph two distinguishes renewal, a recorded address/workplace change and approval of a new basis, then identifies the event and expiry dates used by Articles 7/9. Removing these sentences would remove either the trigger or the relevant process.
