## Content-fidelity contract

### Q6 — Preliminary analysis, appraisal, and review

- Describe the `道路交通事故初步分析研判表` as a preliminary police
  analysis, not a court judgment; it does not bind the court or fix a fault
  percentage.
- Do not present the preliminary report, statutory appraisal, review, and an
  academic appraisal as an automatic or mandatory ladder.
- State that an eligible party may apply for a vehicle-accident appraisal, a
  handling authority may transfer a matter, or a judicial authority may
  commission it.
- A party application is ordinarily made within six months of the accident.
  If investigation or trial is already pending, appraisal generally proceeds
  through judicial commissioning instead of a new direct application.
- A dissatisfied party may apply for review, but review is limited to one
  time.
- Appraisal and review opinions are evidence or reference material; the court
  independently evaluates statements, video, scene records, and the whole
  record.

### Q7 — Conditional categories of damages

- Start with Civil Code Article 184: unlawful infringement, causation, and
  proof of damage are required. An accident does not automatically establish
  every listed item.
- Explain Article 216 actual loss and lost profit as the scope standard.
- Injury:
  - Article 193 necessary medical expense, care, travel, assistive-device and
    other increased needs, proven temporary lost income, and loss of earning
    capacity;
  - Article 195 non-pecuniary damage.
- Death:
  - Article 192 applicable pre-death medical/increased-need expense, funeral
    expense, and loss of support for a person legally entitled to support;
  - Article 194 non-pecuniary damage for qualifying relatives.
- Property:
  - Article 196 proven actual property damage, including repair expense or
    diminution in value where supported.

### Q8 — Additional evidence versus changing a claim

- Preserve receipts, diagnosis certificates, and medical records; organize
  medical necessity and causation to the accident.
- Continuing treatment material may supplement evidence in line with the
  procedural schedule and existing claim, but late material or an expanded
  claim is not guaranteed to be accepted.
- Distinguish supplementing medical evidence from changing or increasing the
  amount or scope of the claim.
- Adding medical receipts after filing a criminal-attached civil action does
  not by itself automatically create a court fee.
- Under Code of Criminal Procedure Article 504, after transfer to the civil
  division, a change/addition/expansion beyond the pre-transfer claim can
  create a court-fee issue for the excess. Verify transfer stage, filing time,
  and claim scope case by case.

### Q9 — Professional and family care

- A diagnosis certificate or medical opinion is useful evidence of care need,
  but not conclusive by itself.
- Require evidence of accident causation, need for care, actual provision,
  duration, and a reasonable amount.
- Unpaid care actually provided by a relative may be valued as damage in an
  appropriate case, but is not automatically allowed merely because a
  relative provided it. Consider the nature, duration, and customary cost.

### Q10 — Treatment travel expense

- Connect travel records to treatment and the accident-related injury.
- Cover route, number and date of visits, transport method, fare, necessity,
  and reasonableness.
- Receipts, fare records, route records, and treatment records are possible
  evidence.
- A taxi receipt is neither the only proof nor automatically sufficient.
