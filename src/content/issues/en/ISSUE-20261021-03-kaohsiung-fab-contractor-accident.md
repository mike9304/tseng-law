# Four dead at a Kaohsiung fab site: Taiwan safety duties for owners and contractors

Four contractor workers died after the October 20 steel-installation accident at TSMC's F22 construction project in Kaohsiung, according to [United Daily News's October 21 update](https://udn.com/news/story/7320/9827672). The paper reported that the worker initially hospitalized in intensive care died at 11 p.m. on October 20.

[CNA's October 20 evening report](https://www.cna.com.tw/news/asoc/202610200361.aspx) had put the toll at three dead and one injured. Citing the Southern Taiwan Science Park Bureau, CNA described a suspected bolt failure on a third-floor exterior steel connecting plate. The workers and steel components fell from height during lifting and installation. Steel-lifting work on F22 P4 had stopped, and the bureau was still investigating the cause.

For a US equipment vendor sending field engineers to a Taiwan project, site instructions can matter alongside the employment contract. [Article 51(2)](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=N0060001&flno=51) applies the Occupational Safety and Health Act to people working at a business's site under its site head's direction or supervision as if they were that business's workers, except for Article 20's physical and health examinations. Who directed the work is therefore a concrete question for the visiting team.

## Rescue and reporting have their own timetable

An employer must take necessary first-aid and rescue measures immediately after an occupational accident and investigate, analyze and record the event with worker representatives. For a fatality, [Article 37 of the Occupational Safety and Health Act](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=N0060001&flno=37) requires notification to the labor inspection authority within eight hours. The same reporting rule covers the other serious accidents specified in that article, including an accident affecting at least one person who needs inpatient treatment.

Rescue comes first. Beyond necessary rescue, the employer may not move or disturb the scene of a reportable accident without permission from the judicial or labor inspection authorities. Waiting for a full internal explanation does not replace the reporting obligation.

Records of calls, rescue measures and site changes can help a foreign project team establish what happened during those hours. An internal incident email and a statutory notification serve different recipients. Whether each required notification was made is a factual question; the public report does not answer it.

## The contract chain does not end with the immediate employer

Where a business contracts out its own business, [Article 25](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=N0060001&flno=25) makes the contractor responsible as employer under the Act for the contracted part. It also preserves joint occupational-accident compensation responsibility for the business that contracted out its own work, called the original business unit (原事業單位) in the Act. The rule extends to subcontracting.

Whether a chipmaker commissioning a new plant is contracting out its own business is a question for labor inspectors and, if disputed, the courts. The reports so far do not answer it. The construction contract, subcontract scope and actual division of work are relevant evidence.

For employment relationships covered by the Labor Standards Act, [Article 59](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=N0030001&flno=59) provides occupational-accident compensation for medical costs, wages during qualifying inability to work, disability and death, under the conditions for each category. Employer-funded statutory compensation for the same accident may be credited as the article allows. [Article 62](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=N0030001&flno=62) applies when a business contracts out its own business and the work is subcontracted again. For workers used on their respective portions, contractors and intermediate contractors share compensation responsibility with the final contractor. A business or contractor that pays may seek reimbursement from the final contractor.

A family therefore needs more than a list of companies. Employment documents, wage records, medical evidence and the chain of contracts help identify both the claimant's entitlement and the parties against whom it may arise. They identify wages, injuries and contractual roles that the public account leaves open.

## Risk assessment and coordination before the accident

The applicable safety duties reach the arrangements made before work began. Under [Article 26](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=N0060001&flno=26), a business outsourcing its own business must first assess the risks, tell the contractor the assessment results concerning the work environment, hazards and required safety equipment and measures, and ensure implementation during the contract. Contractors outsourcing their portion have corresponding obligations.

Joint work brings additional duties. For outsourced work within a business's own business, [Article 27](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=N0060001&flno=27) requires coordination when its workers and contractor or subcontractor workers work together. Measures include a coordination organization, a designated site head, work coordination, inspections, training assistance and entry controls. If a business contracts out its own work to two or more contractors working together without joining that work itself, it must designate one contractor to bear the original business unit's responsibilities under the first paragraph.

Taiwan's Occupational Safety and Health Administration, part of the Ministry of Labor, confirms that the amendment took effect on July 1, 2026 in its [implementation notice](https://www.osha.gov.tw/48110/48713/207079/207115/207278/post). Articles 26 and 27 use the amended wording.

Project owners have a separate route of responsibility for construction above the specified scale. [Article 15-1](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=N0060001&flno=15-1) addresses design-stage hazard analysis, safety plans and costs, and oversight of implementation. [Article 27-1](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=N0060001&flno=27-1) requires an owner commissioning two or more constructors to designate one for integrated safety management. These provisions do not use the own-business condition. The [implementing regulation](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawAll.aspx?pcode=N0060088) sets the project thresholds; its designation rule took effect in July 2026, while several design and related duties are phased in during 2027 and 2028. Applying them to this site requires its classification, contract timing and management records, which the reports do not supply.

Who was coordinating the lifting operation? What assessment and instructions covered it? These remain matters for evidence. The public report cannot establish compliance or noncompliance with each duty.

## Compensation, damages and a criminal investigation

Article 25 treats joint damages separately from occupational compensation. Its second paragraph requires the original business unit's violation of the Act or related safety rules to cause an occupational accident affecting a contractor's worker. A safety violation and the causal connection must be established; the occurrence of an accident alone does not prove them.

Other claims may require different evidence. [Civil Code Article 184](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=B0000001&flno=184) addresses tort liability, while [Criminal Code Article 276](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=C0000001&flno=276) concerns causing death through negligence. Neither makes liability follow automatically from a job title or a contractor's presence on site.

No criminal finding follows from the report. Under [Code of Criminal Procedure Article 154](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=C0010001&flno=154), an accused person is presumed innocent until a conviction is final, and criminal facts must be proved by evidence. The suspected bolt failure remains under investigation.
