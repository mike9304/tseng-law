## Issues

No major issue. Two minor points in ja, both fixed by me (see below). Nothing is required of the editor.

### J1 — ja 「呼気0.15mg/L以上、血中0.03％以上で禁止」 (minor, applied)

- Original: 「運転は呼気0.15mg/L以上、血中0.03％以上で禁止です」
- Problem and reason: the comma lets the two thresholds be read as cumulative. 安全規則 第114條 says 「…零點一五毫克或血液中…百分之零點零三以上」, either one.
- Fix (applied): 「運転は呼気0.15mg/L以上か血中0.03％以上で禁止です」
- Facts preserved: both numbers, both units, 以上, the citation. Length unchanged.

### J2 — ja three sentences in a row ending on a parenthetical citation (minor, applied)

- Original: 「…裁判所が没収できます（刑法38条）。刑事罰が優先しても、免許処分など別種の行政罰は科せます（行政罰法26条）。確定した罰金が…納めなければなりません（處罰條例35条12項）。」
- Problem and reason: SENTENCE-VARIETY-RULE 2-2 allows two such sentences in a row within a paragraph. Not one of the counted self-check items and not a checker FAIL, so not a monotony FAIL; fixed because it cost one character.
- Fix (applied): 「[行政罰法26条](…)により、刑事罰が優先しても免許処分など別種の行政罰は科せます。」
- Facts preserved: the link, the article, criminal priority, "may" (科せます). ja is now 1,399 characters.
