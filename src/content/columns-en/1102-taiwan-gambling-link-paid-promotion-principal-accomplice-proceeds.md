Gambling-site promotion can involve criminal participation without a personal bet. [Criminal Code Article 268](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=C0000001&flno=268) addresses supplying a gambling venue or assembling people to gamble with intent to profit.

## A customer-referral bonus formed part of a shared operation

In [New Taipei District Court's February 12, 2025 judgment](https://judgment.judicial.gov.tw/FJUD/printData.aspx?id=PCDM%2C113%2C%E7%B0%A1%2C5467%2C20250212%2C1), the defendant published gambling-site advertisements in Facebook groups to attract customers, with the court adding that she could receive a NT$1,000 bonus for each new customer recruited.

Joint intent and divided work were established. She was convicted as a joint perpetrator.

Its reasons described her as a downstream agent.

[Article 28](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=C0000001&flno=28) provides that people jointly carrying out a criminal act are all principals. Whether a particular promoter shared that role must be established from the evidence, rather than assumed from an advertiser's job title.

## Another court found assistance, with no proven payment received

Taoyuan District Court's [October 1, 2024 judgment](https://judgment.judicial.gov.tw/FJUD/printData.aspx?id=TYDM%2C113%2C%E5%AF%A9%E7%B0%A1%2C1080%2C20241001%2C1) involved an arrangement to pay NT$0.50 per promotional post. The defendant posted the gambling site's description and customer-service details in public Facebook groups and used LINE to introduce its game methods to prospective players.

The court treated this as assistance. It found helping intent and acts outside the direct execution of supplying the venue and gathering gamblers, and applied the accessory provision in [Article 30](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=C0000001&flno=30).

That judgment also corrected the prosecution's description of money received. The defendant said she had not been paid, and the court found no affirmative evidence of benefits, remuneration or a share of the operators' proceeds.

No proceeds were confiscated from her. Unproved payment did not prevent conviction.

Both judgments imposed three-month terms with provisions allowing conversion to a fine at the rate specified in the ruling. Later appeals and finality remain unverified.

## Intent and the work performed both require examination

The cases do not make every game advertisement criminal.

Under [Article 13](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=C0000001&flno=13), intent includes knowing and intending the facts constituting an offence, and foreseeing those facts while accepting their occurrence. Instructions about attracting bettors, explanations of wagering and customer referral payments can be relevant to that assessment.

Article 30 defines an accessory as someone who helps another carry out a criminal act, even if the person helped is unaware of the assistance. It permits reduction of the accessory's punishment from the principal's; classification as an accessory still requires examination of the actual helping conduct and intent.

Does a small fee settle the question? Neither its size nor its label alone establishes what the promoter agreed to do or their role in carrying out the gambling operation.

Conviction under Article 268 carries up to three years’ imprisonment and may also carry a fine of up to NT$90,000.

## Promotion work carried out in Taiwan

Territorial application follows [Article 3](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=C0000001&flno=3), covering offences committed within Taiwan's territory. [Article 4](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=C0000001&flno=4) treats an offence as territorial where either its act or its result occurs there.

For promotion work performed from Taiwan, the locations of the posting and other acts, the gambling arrangements being promoted and the participants' roles need assessment.

Neither cited case determines the legal status of a particular US-licensed platform or an exclusively overseas transaction. A claimed foreign licence should be examined with the actual proposed activity, rather than treated as a Taiwan criminal-law clearance.

## The original offer can explain what the poster agreed to do

Keep the proposed script or post, links, referral conditions, instructions, exchanges about payment and any actual receipts together. If content has already been published, preserve its original version and publication history for counsel's review.
