A Taiwan trial court voided a divorce signed and registered the same day, and held they were still spouses.
One witness never directly checked that the husband wanted out.

The wife's old friend met only the wife at a restaurant and signed, the couple's lines still blank, and had not met or contacted the husband around then. Neither spouse disputed this.
The court did not count that friend. Even if the other witness counted, the two required were not met, so the divorce itself was void.
