variety used: standard Slovene as used in Slovenia, consistent. The reader is addressed with vikanje. Dates are day–month–year (24. 1. 2022, 13. septembra 2025, 4. februarja 2026, 1. 7. 2026, 5. junija 2026, 1. 1. 2023, 25. 6. 2026, 17. septembra 2026). The decimal comma is already right (0,5 metra). systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- "podlaga" stands in for a record or a file: "upravljanje aktualnih podlag in predajo ob koncu pogodbe".
- 013 still sets the second half of a sentence as its own paragraph: "in ali tuji subjekti ne uvajajo sredstev pod drugo pretvezo."
- Zero-width spaces (U+200B) remain in 013 and 015: after "zadostuje navedba območja (primer: mesto Tajpej)."
- Dual "sta" is still used where the subjects are not a pair: "kot sta blagovna znamka (商標) in podobe".
- A company is named with a bare genitive of its products: "Tuja podjetja sestavnih delov za polprevodnike".
- "nositev stroškov" is used as if it were an ordinary noun: "in nositev stroškov preizkusov".
012 is professional apart from the braking-time sentence, the singular "mnenje", and "napredovala". 015 is professional apart from the repeated zoning point and U+200B. 010, 011, 014, 016, 017 and 018 are professional with local defects. 013 is readable, but it still breaks sentences and it turns two identity documents into a double identity check.
