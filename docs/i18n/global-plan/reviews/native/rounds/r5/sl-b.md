## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): N/A for guidance pack (out of scope, part a), 4/5 for columns
variety used: standard Slovene of Slovenia, consistent; vikanje throughout; no Croatian or Serbian mix; Slovene »…« quotation marks only, no straight or curly quotes anywhere in the nine files; decimal comma correct (`0,5 metra`); dot as thousands separator correct (`1.579.589`, `25.000.000`); dates in Slovene order (`13. septembra 2025`, `24. 1. 2022`, `1. 7. 2026`, `25. 6. 2026`, `17. septembra 2026`).

systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- A thin layer of calqued verbs survives every round — "videti" for *examine*, "trditi" + object for *assert*, "enotno" for *uniformly*, "tvoriti" for *constitute*: "Treba je videti zahteve tajvanske stranke".
- German/Korean "k" + dative instead of "o"/"glede" for what an opinion or notice is *about*: "ni pravno mnenje k konkretni zadevi" (also 011, 012, 014, 017).
- Nominalised neuter adjective used as a sentence subject, which no native writes: "Spodnje je splošna informacija" (018: "Zgornje je napotek za zasnovo pogodb.").
- 013 and 015 still break single sentences across three paragraphs, and still carry U+200B-only lines: "Poleg tržnih razmer" / "je pomembno preveriti še," / "ali naslov leži v coni, kjer sme obratovati restavracija."
- Digits are used where Slovene spells the numeral out, and the spelling is inconsistent within one sentence: "Če je 3 mesece ali več in manj kot eno leto: 10 dni".
- Currency code placement is split across the set: 010/017 write "25.000.000 TWD", 011/018 write "TWD 30.000.000".

010, 012 and 016 read as professional Slovene apart from the rows below; 018 is professional apart from "Oporišče" and "nastavitev kapitala"; 011, 014 and 017 are professional with a dense local defect layer; 013 and 015 are the weakest files in the part, entirely because of the unrepaired paragraph chopping and invisible characters, not because of their Slovene.
