## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): n/a for guidance pack (part a), 2/5 for columns 010–012 so far
variety used: Standard Modern Greek (monotonic Demotic) mixed with Katharevousa morphology (-εως, έκαστος, ενδείκνυται, αποσβέννυται), same mix as part a; not Cypriot
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- “Mere X” still rendered as «η μόνη X»: "Η μόνη επίσημη απόφαση του 1ου βαθμού δεν επιτρέπει"
- Katharevousa no 2026 client would write: "ενδείκνυται", "έκαστος", "αποσβέννυται"
- Korea put back where English had already generalised: "Οι μάρκες της Κορέας μπορούν επίσης να προσφύγουν"
- «Υπόδειξη» for official guidance continues from part a: "η υπόδειξη της αρμόδιας αρχής"
- Digit-ordinal «3ο πρόσωπο» for third party
- Related-reading stock calque «εύρος της εργασίας» still on column footers

Wei Tseng gender (010–012): no masculine pronoun or title. 010 first person «Ήμουν δικηγόρος» is epicene; 011 sign-off «Δικηγόρος Wei Tseng» is epicene.

012 is the most readable of this trio (short, no guarantee slip). 010 is a usable case note under katharevousa. 011 is the weakest: Korea as default reader, «επιρροές» for influencers, Japanese Invest Taiwan link.
