## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): 4/5 for guidance pack, 4/5 for columns
variety used: Standard written Burmese (မြန်မာစာ), Unicode, consistent. No Zawgyi, and no Shan, Mon, or Rakhine mix. Guidance stays in formal ကျွန်ုပ်တို့ / ယင်း / သင့်. Columns 003 from Q16, 006, 008 and 009 switch to ကျွန်မ for attorney Wei Tseng.
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- The coined title ရှေ့နေမ puts the negative particle မ directly before ဖြစ်, so the qualification line reads as a denial: "လုပ်ကိုင်ခွင့်ရှိသော ရှေ့နေမဖြစ်ပြီး"
- ပါတီ for a legal party reads as a political party: "ပါတီများ၏ ဆက်ဆံရေး"
- မူပိုင်ခွင့် is the pack’s own word for copyright (著作權), but the about page also uses it for patent filings and for intellectual property: "ကုန်အမှတ်တံဆိပ်နှင့် မူပိုင်ခွင့် လျှောက်ထားခြင်း"
- 003 Q16–Q20, 005, 006, 008 and 009 are still one sentence per line, with blank zero-width spacers: "Q16. မတော်တဆမှု ဖြစ်ပြီးနောက် အာမခံကုမ္ပဏီထံ အားလုံး အပ်နိုင်ပါသလား။"
- Cross-border is clipped to နယ်စပ်ဖြတ်: "နယ်စပ်ဖြတ် မိသားစုကိစ္စများ"
- ပြသဘာသာ is a calque of Anzeigesprache: "စာမျက်နှာ ပြသဘာသာ"

Gender of attorney Wei Tseng: pronouns are clean. Guidance uses သူမ and ဖလှယ်ကျောင်းသူ; 003, 006, 008 and 009 use ကျွန်မ. No ကျွန်တော်, no ဦး, and no masculine title for her. ဦး B in 008 is a male client and should stay. The remaining problem is the coined suffix ရှေ့နေမ / အမှုဆောင်ရှေ့နေမ, which is not a Burmese title and, before ဖြစ်, reverses the sentence. Consultation lock is intact (four languages only; no interpreter, no reply-time promise, no free first meeting, no result promise). Korea treaty and banking passages in 001, 004 and 005 are marked as country-specific examples. 008 now says the company must not be assumed to pay severance every time, and 009 splits the right according to which side is at fault. 002 is professional legal Burmese aside from ပါတီ and the byline title. 004’s body is the same aside from one mislabeled source. 007 is the same aside from the dangling section opening, one status calque, and the ROC year.
