## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): n/a for guidance pack (covered in part a), 2/5 for columns
variety used: Standard Modern Greek (monotonic Demotic) mixed throughout 010–018 with Katharevousa morphology (-εως, έκαστος, ενδείκνυται, αποσβέννυται, δι’ αυτοκινήτου); not a regional split and no Cypriot mix
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- “Mere X” rendered as «η μόνη X», which a Greek reader hears as “the only X”: "Η μόνη επίσημη απόφαση του 1ου βαθμού δεν επιτρέπει"
- FAQ answers dumped under a different heading, so the first word is a stranded «Όχι.»: "Πότε παράγει αποτελέσματα το σύμφωνο ελάχιστης περιόδου υπηρεσίας;"
- Katharevousa no 2026 client would write: "ενδείκνυται", "έκαστος", "αποσβέννυται", "δι’ αυτοκινήτου"
- Official guides/notices still become «Υπόδειξη» (a hint): "η υπόδειξη της αρμόδιας αρχής"
- Digit-ordinal «3ο πρόσωπο» for third party, and related-reading stock calque «εύρος της εργασίας»
- 013 and 015 keep a Korean/Wix blog voice (emoji, «Π.χ.)», Q&A, comment CTA) against the denser legal pages 011/014/016–017

Wei Tseng gender: no masculine pronoun, participle or title found for her. 010 «Ήμουν δικηγόρος» and the sign-offs «Δικηγόρος Wei Tseng» are epicene; 013/015 do not name her.

012 is the most native piece (short, cautious, no-result line intact). 014 and 016 are legally densest and often accurate on Taiwan terms, but the register is foreign. 011 is the weakest public page (Korea as default reader, «επιρροές» for influencers, Japanese Invest Taiwan link). 013 and 015 would embarrass an Athens client. No consultation-in-Greek lock is restated in this set; the 015 «ανά πάσα στιγμή» line is the E slip.
