# Greek native review — part a (el)
reviewer: Grok 4.6 · date: 2026-09-21 · scope: el-guidance.txt (365 strings); columns-el/001–009

## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): 3/5 for guidance pack, 2/5 for columns
variety used: Standard Modern Greek (monotonic Demotic) on the guidance pages; columns mix Demotic with Katharevousa morphology (-εως, έκαστος, ενδείκνυται, Χαίρετε) throughout 001–009, consistently, not a regional split (no Cypriot mix)
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- “Mere X” rendered as «η μόνη X», which a Greek reader hears as “the only X”: "Η μόνη σύσταση της εταιρείας δεν παρέχει"
- Columns stay in lawyer-Katharevousa no native 2026 client would write: "έκαστος", "ενδείκνυται", "συστάσεως", "υπ’ όψιν"
- Korea left as the default reader where English had already labelled it as one example: "Ακόμη και όταν μητρική της Κορέας εισέρχεται στην Ταϊβάν"
- Guidance calques from German/source syntax: "συνοδεύει εντολείς", "εύρος της εργασίας", "ομάδες εργασιών"
- Korean Hangul and “Korean-speaking lawyer” CTAs survive on Greek pages: "Δικηγόρος της Ταϊβάν που ομιλεί κορεατικά"
- Official “guides/notices” become «Υπόδειξη», which in Greek means a hint, not a government handbook

Wei Tseng gender: feminine forms are used correctly (η δικηγόρος, διευθύνουσα δικηγόρος, της δικηγόρου, είμαι η δικηγόρος). No masculine pronoun or title found for her in this scope.

Guidance pack is usable: negatives on Greek consultation, interpreter, reply time and free first meeting stay negative. Columns 001–004 are legally dense but foreign; 005–009 still address a Korean reader. 002 and 007 are the strongest legally; 005 and 008 are the weakest as Greek public pages.
