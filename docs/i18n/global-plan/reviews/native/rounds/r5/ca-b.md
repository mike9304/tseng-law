## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): —/5 for guidance pack (part a, not in scope), 4/5 for columns
variety used: Standard written Catalan of Catalonia, which is also the written standard an Andorran reader expects. No Valencian or Balearic morphology except two present-subjunctive forms, «consigne» in 010 and 014, where the rest of both files uses central forms (tingui, valori, reuneixi). Formal *vostè* in 013, 015, the close of 017 and 018 (redacti, cerqui, fixi, presti atenció, confirmi, Suposi, organitzi, indiqui, Enviï). Thousands with a point (1.579.589, 25.000.000, 10.000.000, 30.000.000, 100.000.000), decimal comma in «0,5 metres», percentage with a space in «100 %», currency as «1.579.589 TWD» and elided correctly in «d’1.570.000 TWD». The Chinese glosses in 017 keep the Chinese thousands comma (新臺幣2,500萬元), which is correct inside a Chinese term but is the one number a Catalan eye misreads; row 26 below. 012 is native professional legal Catalan; its two rows are register polish, not error. 011, 014, 016 and 018 are close to publishable: what remains in them is a handful of repeated words and two dropped verbs, not a rewrite.
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- After «sinó» or a coordinating «i», the second «s’ha de / s’han de» is dropped and the clause is left verbless: "sinó confirmar-se les resolucions de divorci existents"
- «veure» is still the verb for *examine / take into account*, where Catalan wants examinar or atendre a: "No s’ha de veure només el nom de la formació"
- Two Castilianisms survive in legal vocabulary, subsanar and recolzar: "la possibilitat de subsanar"
- An advertising claim is «reivindicació», the word for a demand or a patent claim, not the regulatory al·legació: "la reivindicació d’eficàcia mèdica"
- «de forma + adjectiu» is the default adverb (58 times in these 9 files) where a native alternates with the plain adverb: "de forma automàtica"
- 013 and 015 still carry the U+200B padding (5 + 3), and 013 additionally has the one broken sentence and the one misspelling of this part

Gender of attorney Wei Tseng: no masculine form, pronoun or title anywhere. 011, 014, 016 and 017 close with «Advocada Wei Tseng (曾雋崴)»; 011 and 018 say «l’advocada responsable»; 013 and 015 offer «una advocada o un advocat». 010 «vaig actuar» and 012 «aquest despatx» do not mark gender. In 016 «entre ell i el fill» still refers to «el progenitor supervivent», not to the attorney. Rule compliance holds: 013 and 015 say to write through the Contacte page and promise no reply time; 011 denies any guarantee of authorisation, registration, saleability or timeline; 012 denies any promise about the outcome; 017 says the end of the whole procedure cannot be assured; 018 says the scenarios guarantee nothing, asks for the preferred consultation language and says the languages actually available must be confirmed on contact. No win rate, no «millor/únic», no interpreter or free-consultation promise, no Catalan-language consultation implied. No Korean, English or unglossed Chinese left in the running text; the only bare Chinese string is the Ministry of Labor circular number in 014, which is a document reference. The 018 heading numbering jumps from 5 to 7; the English source does the same, so it is not a Catalan defect and is left out of the table.
