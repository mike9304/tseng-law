## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): n/a for guidance pack (part a), 3/5 for columns
variety used: Khalkha Mongolian in Cyrillic, as written for readers in Mongolia; consistent. Not mixed with Traditional Mongolian script, Buryat, or Kalmyk. Formal register is kept. Quotation marks are « ». Chinese glosses stay in Traditional characters.

systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- "даатгах / даатгагч / дахин даатгах" is insurance language used for entrusting, outsourcing, and subcontracting: "Даатгагч ачаа илгээгч эсвэл ачаа зуучлагч эсэх"
- English "alone" is still "нэгээр" in 010; from 014 onward the same idea is the natural "ганцаар": "эхний шатны шийдвэр нэгээр хоёрдугаар шатны үр дүн"
- Money stays code-first with an English comma: "TWD 1,579,589", "TWD 25,000,000", "TWD 30,000,000"
- Wei Tseng is labeled "Эмэгтэй өмгөөлөгч"; the noun needs no gender marker, and the form is not masculine
- "объект" calques "the object of" a handover, a calculation, or a claim: "шилжүүлэх объект, арга"
- 013 and 015 are cut into one-line blog paragraphs; 014, 016, and 018 are continuous legal prose: "Тайванийн банкны ажилтан надад өөрөө хэлсэн нь"

No F findings. Verbs do not mark gender. "Би эмэгтэй өмгөөлөгчийн хувьд" (010, 013) and the byline "Эмэгтэй өмгөөлөгч Wei Tseng" (011, 014, 016, 017) are an unnecessary sex label. 018 has no byline. No consultation in Mongolian is promised: 018 names the four languages (англи, хятад (中文), япон, солонгос) and says the language actually available is confirmed at contact. 013 and 015 already say a reply time is not promised. No interpreter, no free first meeting, no win rate. "хүүхдийн хамгийн сайн ашиг" is the statutory test, but "ашиг" alone sounds like profit. "харьцангуй хялбар" matches the English "relatively easier" and is not a guarantee. 012, 014, 016, and 018 are the stretches a native can read as professional legal Mongolian. 017 is the file a client should not see until the insurance words are replaced.
