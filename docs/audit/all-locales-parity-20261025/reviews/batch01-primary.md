## Meaning checks and numeric differences

The current sources, official-source refresh, F01–F24 comparison and independent manuscript reports were used together. Source versions differ in their level of detail. Their deadlines and legal logic were not harmonized by changing the law: entry-next-day differs from domestic visa issuance; address-next-day differs from residence-basis event-day; student review, payment and collection have separate clocks; visitor-family rules concern permitted stay, not visa expiry.

Article 16(2) was checked as `[(general professional AND Taiwan professional work) OR specialist professional] AND [NIA residence OR permanent residence]`, followed by the family's own qualifying entry and limited kinship categories. Work authorization is distinct from ARC possession, extended residence and card replacement. Article 31 exceptions are conditional, APRC remains a reentry exception, and the specific online departure rule is not generalized to all applicants.

The final audit's numeric-token differences were read as prompts for comparison, not automatic equivalence or rejection:

- RU/SV `1 000` and `2 200`, NL/TR `1.000` and `2.200` remain the same NT-dollar fees in their contexts; currency and annual/shorter-than-year treatment remain.
- Turkish and Chinese use spelled-out counts for some days/months/years. The body/FAQ comparison verifies the actual thirty-day, fifteen-day, six-month, one/two-year, four-plus-at-least-three-month and student working-day conditions; a missing Arabic token alone is not a deleted rule.
- Added 0902/0915/0926/0929/1150506 values identify agency guides/documents, while article/paragraph numbers identify retained or added sources. They are not new fees or waiting periods.
- Source-check dates differ between October 24, October 25 and the explicitly stated October 24–25 scope. Published/lastmod for all additions are October 25. No new complete gazette review is claimed from these dates.
- Added maximum-first-spouse-year, qualified graduate exemption, sponsor duration or administrative detail is tied to its official provision. Where the accepted English source summarizes a longer parallel source, the concise translation is assessed for accuracy and its actual stated scope, not numerical occurrence-count equality.

Titles, summaries and FAQ conditions were compared with their bodies through the author and independent reviews. Locale is not nationality; the general foreign-national system is distinguished from Mainland/Hong Kong/Macao residents and Taiwan nationals without household registration.
