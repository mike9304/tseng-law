## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): N/A for guidance pack (out of scope), 4/5 for columns
variety used: Simplified Chinese for mainland readers, with Taiwan legal terms glossed in Traditional. Script is consistent. 012 is native professional apart from three phrases. 014, 016 and 018 are close. 013 and 015 are still a clause-per-paragraph blog.
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- A few legal terms still name the wrong object: `"曾有台湾银行职员表示"` (013, Bank of Taiwan), `"教材或设备税费"` (014, taxes), `"扣抵或特留分"` (016, set-off).
- Taiwan webpage diction remains in the simplified body: `"下列标题仅在呈现各连结内容"` (010); the same 连结/纪录 pair runs through 011, 014 and 016, where mainland copy says 链接/记录.
- Amounts still use a Western comma: `"新台币1,579,589元"` (010); the same pattern in 011, 017 and 018.
- 013 and 015 still break almost every clause into its own paragraph: `"投资审议司须核对该资本额是否确实用于投资目的，"` then a blank line.
- Case-discussion lines on 010, 013 and 015 never name the four consultation languages: `"如需就具体案件讨论，请通过联系页提交案情摘要"`.
- Attorney Wei Tseng is unnamed in 010 and 012, and 律师 / 台湾律师 elsewhere (011, 014, 016, 017; 018 has no byline). No masculine form for her. 012’s A is the motorcyclist.
