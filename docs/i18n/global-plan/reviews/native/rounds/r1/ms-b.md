## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): N/A for guidance pack (out of scope; part a), 4/5 for columns
variety used: Bahasa Malaysia (ms-MY), consistent — not mixed with Indonesian. Thousands use a comma (1,579,589; TWD 25,000,000) and the decimal point (0.5 meter). Dates are day–month–year. anda is lowercase. Branch is cawangan; counsel is peguam.
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- A corrected heading left the old word in the body: “Terbitan pengaruh dan lain-lain”, “kerugian luar harta”, “jagaan anak bawah umur (未成年人監護)”, “rizab waris”, checklist “kalendar”
- French/civil-law glue that a Malaysia reader does not use for these ideas: “pada pelupusan pengguna”, “Statut pelaburan”, “penikmatan”, “organ siasatan”
- 013 and 015 are still one clause per paragraph, and several of those lines end in a zero-width space: “pernyataan wilayah mencukupi (contoh: bandar Taipei).”
- Agency names disagree with columns 001 and 003: “Jabatan Perdagangan” for 商業發展署, “Jabatan Am Jalan Raya” for 公路局
- dijamin is used where the English is preserve or obtain: “dokumen yang masih boleh dijamin”
- “kedua” stands for both, so it reads as “the second”: “mengatur kedua syarat di mana pemotongan dilarang”

014 and 017 are native professional aside from the rows below. 016 is the same once the doubled pampasan, jagaan/監護, and rizab waris are closed. 012 is clear Taiwan traffic Malay aside from the driving-side note and Kepakaran. 010 and 011 read well where the last pass landed, and still shift meaning in the rows below. 013 and 015 are legally cleaner than the last pass and still chopped like a blog. 018 is usable until the Temu ramah heading and the open language line. Gender of attorney Wei Tseng: Malay does not inflect. “Saya ialah peguam”, “Peguam Wei Tseng (曾雋崴)”, and “Wei Tseng (曾雋崴), peguam di Taiwan” are epicene. No Encik, no masculine pronoun or title. The male student in 010 is the client. Consultation lock: 010, 012, 014, 016, and 017 stay negative. 011 stays negative, but “tidak menegaskan … jualan akan mungkin” is not the guarantee sentence. 013 names the four languages and excludes Malay. 015 refuses a reply time and does not offer Malay. 018 asks the reader to name a preferred consultation language and never says Malay is unavailable. No win-rate or “best/only” firm claim. “kepentingan terbaik anak (子女最佳利益)” is the statutory best-interests test.
