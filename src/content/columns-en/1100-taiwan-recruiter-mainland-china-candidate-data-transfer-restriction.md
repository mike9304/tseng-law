The [Ministry of Labor’s February 20, 2023 notice](https://ppg.ly.gov.tw/ppg/SittingRelatedDocumentReportMatter/download/agenda1/02/pdf/10/07/07/LCEWA01_100707_00170.pdf) restricts covered Taiwan recruitment agencies from transferring personal data to mainland China.

It took effect immediately. Using a US platform still requires checking who processes the data and where the records are stored or sent.

## Which Taiwan entity is covered?

Under [Article 2 of the recruitment-sector data-security regulations](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawAll.aspx?PCode=N0090045), coverage includes licensed private employment-service institutions under Employment Service Act Article 34, and institutions licensed under Article 35(3) of the People with Disabilities Rights Protection Act to provide employment services for persons with disabilities.

An employer recruiting its own staff is not automatically within that definition. A licensed recruitment agency remains covered when it belongs to a US group.

Candidate files can contain names, contact details, education and employment histories. These fall within the identifiable personal data described by [Personal Data Protection Act Article 2](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=I0050021&flno=2). That provision defines international transfer through cross-border processing or use; checking only where the original document was collected misses later handling.

## The official explanation reaches beyond the server named in the contract

The [official explanation](https://ppg.ly.gov.tw/ppg/SittingRelatedDocumentReportMatter/download/agenda1/02/pdf/10/07/07/LCEWA01_100707_00170.pdf) identifies three prohibited arrangements: commissioning a mainland business to process personal data, locating the data center or server there, and transmitting the data there.

Where will the complete record go? Ask the provider to identify the production location, backup and recovery locations, the businesses doing the processing, and any onward transfer. If technical support receives a copy of an applicant’s file, record where that copy goes and who handles it.

A “US hosting” clause may cover only the production database. The provider’s answer should identify the selected service, its configuration and each processing location.

## A US stop on the route does not answer the destination question

[Article 14 of the sector regulations](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawAll.aspx?PCode=N0090045) requires the agency to check restrictions under Article 21 before an international transfer. The [currently applicable Article 21](https://law.pdpc.gov.tw/LawContentHistory.aspx?hid=1&id=FL010627) authorizes the central authority for the relevant industry to restrict transfers on specified grounds. Those include inadequate protection in the receiving jurisdiction with a risk to individuals, and routing through a third jurisdiction to evade the Act.

Routing records through the US does not itself remove a later transfer to mainland China.

As of November 6, 2026, the [official law page](https://law.pdpc.gov.tw/LawContent.aspx?id=FL010627) marks revised Article 21 as awaiting an effective date. That amendment was published in November 2025.

## Evidence for the platform decision

A review file can pair the Taiwan entity’s license with the provider contract, processor list and transfer diagram. Unanswered destinations should remain visible.

[Articles 12 and 23 of the sector regulations](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawAll.aspx?PCode=N0090045) require supervisory procedures for commissioned processing and records of data delivery and transfers. Keeping the provider’s answers with the relevant terms and settings connects the records to the arrangement being reviewed.

Collection and use still need a lawful basis, with notice obligations assessed under [PDPA Articles 8, 19 and 20](https://law.pdpc.gov.tw/LawContentHistory.aspx?hid=1&id=FL010627). Consent cannot override this transfer restriction. Medical records and other data listed in [Article 6](https://law.pdpc.gov.tw/LawContentHistory.aspx?hid=1&id=FL010627) need separate analysis before entering a shared recruitment file.
