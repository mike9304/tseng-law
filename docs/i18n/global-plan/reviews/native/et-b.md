## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): n/a for guidance pack (part a: 3/5), 3/5 for columns
variety used: standard Estonian (ühiskeel), consistent; no Võro and no Finnish mix. Formal **teie** stays lowercase, which is right. The decimal comma in “0,5 meetri” is right. Thousands still follow German dots.
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- Dates stay in the nominative after a verb or “alates”: “alates 1. juuli 2026” (also “kohustas 24. jaanuar 2022”, “uuendatud 25. juuni 2026”, “alates 1. jaanuar 2023”).
- German thousands, unit before the amount: “TWD 1.579.589” and “TWD 25.000.000”.
- “Kindlustama” where the source means preserve: “Kiireloomulist kindlustamist vajav vara” reads as property that must be insured.
- A shareholder of an aktsiaselts (股份有限公司) is written as “osanik”: “2 osanikku või rohkem (股份有限公司)”. The 有限公司 paragraph’s “osanik” is the right word and should stay.
- Calques a Tallinn firm would not publish: “peakeskkondja”, “täitmiskohtadena”, “õppeaine”, “teine tähtis koht”.
- A private-employment lock-in is named as civil service: “Miinimumteenistuse tähtaja kokkulepe”.

Gender: no masculine title, pronoun or agreement for Wei Tseng. Estonian **ta** / **advokaat** are epicene. 010 first person (“Olin”), 013 (“olen sellega nõus”, “soovitan”) and the byline “Advokaat Wei Tseng” do not mark sex. “Meesüliõpilane” is the male Korean student; A and B in 012 are the riders. 012 keeps the no-outcome negative. 013 and 018 keep the language lock (English, Chinese, Japanese, Korean) and 013 refuses a reply-time promise; that closing should stay negative. 016 “üldõigusjärglasena” is the right term for universal succession, and 018 keeps 監察人 (järelevalvaja) apart from 會計師 (vandeaudiitor). 012 is the strongest file. 011 and the company-form paragraph of 018 are the ones a client would stumble on. 013 and 015 are still one sentence per line.
