## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): 4/5 for guidance pack, 3/5 for columns
variety used: standard ekavian Serbian (srpski ekavski), consistent; not mixed with Croatian or ijekavian. Dates (13. septembra 2025., 27. 12. 2023.) already follow Serbian usage. Thousands with a period (TWD 500.000) and the decimal comma (0,5) are already right for Serbia; only TWD 1.57M is not.
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- Severance is otpremnina in the guidance pack and otpremnica (a dispatch note) in columns 008 and 009 (`"da li zaposleni dobija otpremnicu ako sam da otkaz"`).
- 戶籍 / 戶政 is the household register, written as evidencija prebivališta in the guidance pack and as matična evidencija in 007 (`"organa matične evidencije (戶政機關)"`).
- Head office is glavna poslovnica, which in Serbia is a branch outlet (`"strane glavne poslovnice (本公司)"` in 001 and 004).
- 條例 is called uredba or statut, and 營利事業所得稅 is porez na dohodak, which in Serbia is personal income tax (`"Uredbe o penziji radnika (勞工退休金條例)"`).
- Hangul left in the public URL of 008 and 009, the name blank OOO in 008, and English role lines in the bios (`"Paralegal"`, `"TWD 1.57M"`).
- Blog voice in 003 from Q16, in the whole of 006, and in the 008–009 anecdotes (`"odajem svoje godine"`). Consultation locks stay negative. Wei Tseng stays feminine (advokatkinja, Zastupala, ovlašćena, vodila, Studentkinja) — no F finding.

001, 004 and the body of 007 are the strongest prose. 002 is usable once the quorum sentence and the tax name are fixed. 005 is short and mostly clear. 006, 008 and 009 would embarrass a Belgrade reader.
