variety used: standard Bulgarian as used in Bulgaria, consistent; not mixed with Macedonian. Formal Ви is kept (Ви, Вас, Вашето). Quotation marks are the Bulgarian „ “. Two UI buttons drop into ти.

- German calques a client would not write: "съпровожда клиенти", "изходният текст" for Originaltext, "още нужните документи"

- Korea is usually labeled as one example (001, 004, 005); the real miss is the labor title "самоволно напускане" for voluntary resignation
