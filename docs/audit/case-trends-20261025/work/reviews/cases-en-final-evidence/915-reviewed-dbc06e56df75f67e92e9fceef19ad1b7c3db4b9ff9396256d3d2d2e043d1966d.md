An arbitration award for NT$44,417,150 did not survive a Taiwan hospital contractor's court proceedings. The contractor had obtained a mediation recommendation about extra time, then pursued money in arbitration without a recommendation on that changed claim. The [Supreme Court's explanation of judgment 106年度台上字第723號](https://jirs.judicial.gov.tw/GNNWS/NNWSS002.asp?id=274693) identifies that gap as decisive.

On July 12, 2017, the Supreme Court dismissed the contractor's appeal. The annulment stood. Its explanation expressly records that the case was final. The problem was the absence of an agreed or statutorily deemed arbitration agreement for the concrete money dispute.

## Money, extra time, then money again

The contractor's request changed during mediation:

| Stage | Relief and procedural record |
|---|---|
| Initial application | Monetary compensation was sought. |
| Amended application | The claim became a request for an extension of time. |
| Board recommendation | The board addressed the extension; the public entity did not agree. |
| Later claim | The contractor returned to monetary compensation, which received no recommendation or proposal before arbitration. |

A certificate of unsuccessful mediation did not fill the gap. As the [court explained](https://jirs.judicial.gov.tw/GNNWS/NNWSS002.asp?id=274693), the entity needed an opportunity to accept or reject a recommendation on the particular dispute. The Supreme Court corrected the lower court's reasoning about which version of the law applied, while preserving its decision to annul the award.

## The current statutory route is conditional

[Government Procurement Act Article 85-1](https://law.moj.gov.tw/ENG/LawClass/LawAll.aspx?pcode=A0030057) allows contract-performance disputes to be taken to the Complaint Review Board for Government Procurement for mediation. A public entity cannot refuse a supplier's mediation application. For construction works and technical services, the board must offer a mediation recommendation or proposal; if mediation fails because the entity disagrees with it, the entity cannot refuse the supplier's referral to arbitration.

Statutory consent has those limits. In the hospital case, the court found neither an agreed arbitration clause nor the facts needed for statutory consent. The court identified the absence of an arbitration agreement under [Arbitration Act Article 40(1)(2)](https://law.moj.gov.tw/ENG/LawClass/LawAll.aspx?pcode=I0020001) as the ground for annulment.

Contract classification comes first. Today's Article 85-1 covers construction works and technical services; that does not place every equipment purchase or other public-sector contract within this particular route. A separate arbitration clause may already govern.

## Why this matters for US project teams now

The US International Trade Administration's [September 30, 2026 energy report](https://www.trade.gov/market-intelligence/taiwan-energy-industry) describes anticipated Taiwanese power and grid procurements in late 2026 and 2027, including opportunities for US suppliers. These are planned opportunities. The report does not establish that a particular tender has opened, that a company qualifies or that its eventual contract will count as construction or technical services.

For a bidder, the dispute clause and procurement category belong in the initial contract review. For a contractor already facing delay, the distinction becomes practical when a schedule request develops into a claim for additional costs. A claims team may regard both as the same commercial problem while the procedural record identifies different relief.

Has the board addressed the payment claim now being drafted for arbitration? The answer should come from the application and its amendments, the recommendation or proposal, the entity's response and the intended arbitration demand. A general statement that “mediation failed” leaves out the comparison that mattered in this judgment.

Changing a figure does not necessarily create a different dispute. Neither does refining a legal argument. Conversely, reusing the same project number does not answer the question. The scope requires a reading of the actual facts and relief; the 2017 decision concerned the documented move between time relief and monetary compensation.

When those documents leave a gap, counsel can assess whether an agreed arbitration basis exists or whether further mediation is needed for the proposed claim. A [Taiwan contract-dispute inquiry](/en/contact) can start with the project category, the relief sought and the next procedural date. The file to assemble is the sequence of applications, amendments and responses that led to the unsuccessful mediation.
