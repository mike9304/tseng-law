# Taiwan Company Setup — Advanced Guide 3: Finding a Business Location

![Featured image](../images/015-taiwan-company-setup-pitch-location/featured-01.jpg)

(Requirements differ by local government in Taiwan. The following article uses opening a restaurant business in Taipei City as an example.)

![](../images/015-taiwan-company-setup-pitch-location/img-01.jpg)

When starting a food and beverage business, the first issue is finding a business address.

In addition to market conditions,

another important consideration is

whether that address is located in an area where “restaurant/food service business” operations are permitted.

​

If you are starting a business in Taipei City,

you can use the Taipei City Department of Commerce’s “Business Location Advance Inquiry” system.

<https://www.businesslocationinfo.gov.taipei/BLBQS/Home/Notice>

​

You can freely check whether “restaurant/food service business” can be operated at your desired address.

However, when making the inquiry you must upload the “Building Registration Type II Transcript” (建物登記第二類謄本), which records detailed information about the building.

Anyone can apply at a land office (地政事務所) for the “Building Registration Type II Transcript” for any address.

If it is difficult to visit the land office (地政事務所) in person,

you can ask a Taiwan acquaintance, the landlord, a real estate agent, or a lawyer for help.

If the inquiry result shows that restaurant/food service use is not permitted, the city government may refuse registration when you apply for company registration.

Later

Please therefore take care!

​

**Q. Must every industry check whether business is permitted through the “Business Location Advance Inquiry” system?**

It is recommended that every industry confirm whether business is permitted through the “Business Location Advance Inquiry” system.

However, when registering a company it is common to register multiple business items—often more than ten items at once.

Therefore, in practice you do not need to inquire for every business item at company registration.

Under the “[Taipei City Business Location Assisted Inquiry Service Operating Guidelines](https://www.laws.taipei.gov.tw/Law/LawSearch/LawArticleContent/FL080687)” (臺北市營業場所協助查詢服務作業須知),

only business items that fall under “business items subject to proactive inquiry” (主動查詢之營業項目) **must** be inquired into in advance,

and the inquiry results must be submitted together to the Taipei City Government at company registration.

If you open a restaurant at a location where restaurant operations are not permitted, you may later be fined by the competent authority.

!["Business items subject to proactive inquiry" (主動查詢之營業項目)](../images/015-taiwan-company-setup-pitch-location/img-02.jpg)

"Business items subject to proactive inquiry" (主動查詢之營業項目)

That concludes this guide. If you have further questions, please feel free to contact a Taiwan lawyer at any time.

Administrative agency rules may change frequently, so it is advisable to confirm the latest regulations before company registration.
