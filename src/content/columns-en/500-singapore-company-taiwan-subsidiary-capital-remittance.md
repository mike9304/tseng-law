Before sending capital for a new Taiwan subsidiary, a Singapore holding company should settle which investment regime applies and obtain the required approval. Taiwan's [published establishment procedure](https://www.moea.gov.tw/Mns/dir/Investment/DirApply.aspx?kind=A&menu_id=42814&sub_menu_id=42812) places the investment application before capital remittance, followed by verification of the investment amount and company registration.

The company secretary, group legal team and treasury may hold different parts of the evidence: Singapore registration information, upstream ownership documents and payment instructions. Reconcile them against the same investor and proposed investment before authorising the transfer.

## What the Singapore records establish

Singapore's Accounting and Corporate Regulatory Authority, ACRA, offers several [information products](https://www.acra.gov.sg/resources/buying-business-information/types-of-acra-information-products/). A Business Profile gives basic entity information, including its Unique Entity Number, or UEN, registration date and position-holder information. Registers identify members or officers; extracts provide copies of documents previously filed, such as annual returns. Choose the record that answers the question being asked.

Start with the exact legal name and UEN of the company that will own the Taiwan subsidiary. Reconcile any former name, recent share transfer or change of directors with the proposed application. A group trading name or a profile for a sister company leaves the applicant's identity unresolved.

An ACRA record is evidence about the Singapore entity. It is not a determination of that entity's Taiwan investment status. Nor should a group assume that buying one Business Profile supplies every upstream ownership agreement or control arrangement needed for the Taiwan analysis.

## Follow the ownership chain beyond the applicant

Taiwan's ordinary [Act For Investment by Foreign Nationals](https://law.moea.gov.tw/LawContent.aspx?id=FL011158), Article 3, determines a foreign legal person's nationality by its law of incorporation. A separate rule nevertheless applies to certain companies incorporated outside mainland China. Under [Article 3 of the Mainland Area investment permit rules](https://law.moea.gov.tw/LawContent.aspx?id=FL049681), a third-area company falls within that regime where Mainland Area persons or entities directly or indirectly hold more than 30% of its shares or capital, or control it. Either test can apply; a holding at or below 30% does not resolve control.

For a holding-company structure, prepare a dated ownership chart and supporting records for each relevant level. The Ministry of Economic Affairs' [ownership interpretation](https://law.moea.gov.tw/LawContent.aspx?id=GL001108) applies a layer-by-layer calculation. Where an upstream third-area company qualifies as a Mainland Area investor, its entire holding in the next company is counted for that assessment. Simply multiplying percentages down the chain can therefore produce the wrong classification. The chart needs enough detail to apply the rule at each level.

Governance documents require a separate reading. The Ministry's [control interpretation](https://law.moea.gov.tw/LawContent.aspx?id=GL001107) covers specified voting, policy and board powers, as well as control under the cited accounting standards. Collect the operative constitution, shareholders' agreements and relevant voting or appointment arrangements. Describe what those rights allow; do not infer either control or its absence from a document labelled “minority protection”.

This classification affects which permission regime and investment scope apply. If the Singapore vehicle meets the Mainland Area test, its Taiwan investment does not proceed under the ordinary Foreign Nationals Act. Resolve that point before commissioning a filing on the assumption that Singapore incorporation settles eligibility.

## Assemble a pack that can be checked across departments

The following is a preparation pack for the investment review, rather than a claim that every item is a mandatory attachment in every case:

| Record | Point to reconcile |
|---|---|
| Singapore entity documents and current member information | The applicant's identity and immediate ownership |
| Dated group chart with supporting records | The relevant upstream holders, jurisdictions and percentages |
| Operative governance agreements | Who can exercise voting, appointment or policy powers |
| Investment plan and internal authorisations | The proposed Taiwan activity, investor, amount and signatories |
| Proposed remittance instructions | The remitter, receiving account, currency and investment purpose |

Ask the person responsible for each record to identify changes expected before funding. A pending group reorganisation or shareholder admission can make yesterday's chart incomplete even though the Singapore company's name and UEN remain unchanged. Keep the proposed structure separate from the current structure, with dates and supporting documents for both.

Confirm the accepted form of overseas documents with the authority handling the application before ordering certifications or translations. A certificate's availability from ACRA does not itself establish that it satisfies a particular Taiwan filing requirement. Resolve missing documents and inconsistent names before they become questions during review.

## Authorise the transfer against the approved plan

For an investment governed by the Foreign Nationals Act, [Articles 8 and 9](https://law.moea.gov.tw/LawContent.aspx?id=FL011158) address approval, implementation within the approved period and verification of the investment amount. The approval letter should therefore reach the finance team with clear instructions about the approved investor, amount and any relevant conditions.

Before treasury sends funds, reconcile the proposed remitter and receiving account with the application and bank arrangements. If a group treasury company will pay instead of the named investor, resolve the treatment in advance with the receiving bank and Taiwan adviser. Do not assume that a payment from any group account will be treated identically. For a budget held in Singapore dollars, agree how conversion, bank charges and the amount credited will be documented; avoid relying on a budget exchange rate as evidence of the capital actually received.

Retain the inward-remittance advice, foreign-exchange record and receiving-account evidence. The Ministry's establishment procedure identifies these types of records for investment-amount verification. Keep that verification and the subsequent company-registration documents with the original approval, so the completed file shows what was authorised and what was funded.
