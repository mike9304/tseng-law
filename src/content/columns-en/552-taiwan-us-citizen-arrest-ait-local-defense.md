# A US Citizen Arrested in Taiwan: AIT Contact and Local Defense

A US citizen arrested in Taiwan can ask the police or prison authorities to contact AIT immediately. This follows the arrest-notification guidance on the [US State Department's Taiwan page](https://travel.state.gov/en/international-travel/travel-advisories/taiwan.html). The American Institute in Taiwan provides US citizen and consular services within the United States' unofficial relationship with Taiwan; the page also says AIT can provide a list of English-speaking lawyers.

Notification starts a consular contact. It does not tell the family which lawyer has accepted the case, whether that lawyer can attend questioning, or what Taiwan authorities have alleged. Those questions need their own answers. Record the lawyer's name and the confirmed scope of the engagement when one is made.

## What consular assistance can do

According to the State Department's [arrest or detention abroad guidance](https://travel.state.gov/en/international-travel/help-abroad/arrest-detention.html), consular assistance may include lawyer lists, visits, requests for appropriate medical care and an overview of the local criminal process. Contacting family, friends or an employer normally requires the detainee's written permission. Consider the recipient. Permission to contact a relative should not be treated as an instruction to brief every colleague.

Can consular personnel act as the defense lawyer? The same guidance says they cannot obtain release, provide legal advice, represent a detainee in court, serve as an official interpreter or pay legal fees. A family therefore needs to distinguish confirmed consular contact from confirmed defense representation. Keep both names in the contact record.

## Rights during Taiwan questioning

[Article 95 of Taiwan's Code of Criminal Procedure](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=C0010001&flno=95) requires notice of the suspected offense, the right to remain silent, the right to retain defense counsel, and the right to request investigation of favorable evidence. It also addresses legal-aid assistance for people who meet the applicable conditions. US citizenship alone does not establish eligibility.

The same article provides a particular stopping rule. Where no defense counsel is present and the accused states that counsel has already been retained, questioning must stop unless the accused agrees to continue. The condition concerns an existing retention. Do not describe it as an automatic pause whenever someone merely plans to find a lawyer later. [Article 100-2](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=C0010001&flno=100-2) applies the interrogation chapter to police questioning of suspects.

For family members making calls, the useful question is concrete: has a lawyer actually accepted the engagement, and how can that lawyer be reached? Avoid filling the gap with an assumption. A name found on a list and a confirmed engagement are different stages of arranging defense.

## Understanding the questions

[Article 99](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=C0010001&flno=99) requires an interpreter where there is a language barrier, among other specified circumstances, and permits written questions or statements when necessary. Article 100-2 also applies this provision to police questioning of suspects. Consular notification is not a substitute for that procedural requirement.

Which words remain unclear? Identify the question, name, date or amount that was not understood and ask for clarification through the interpreter. If interpretation still leaves uncertainty, say so. A polite acknowledgement may not explain what the person actually understood.

## A contact record the family can use

Practical coordination can begin with the authority holding the person, the case identifier if known, the lawyer's confirmed contact details and the scope of any permission to notify others. Note who supplied each item and when. Mark missing information as unconfirmed instead of treating an unanswered call as proof that a requested step has happened.

Before the next call, identify the unresolved task: confirming notification to AIT, contacting retained counsel, conveying a medical need or clarifying a language problem. Direct it to the person handling that task. The resulting record should show which contacts have been made and which arrangements still need confirmation.
