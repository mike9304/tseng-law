Rules to apply (all locales):
- R2 Korea-centric framing → generalize for a reader of your locale. Korea stays ONLY as a labelled example of a foreign country.
  a. "Korean parent company / Korean investor / Korean national / from Korea / your bank in Korea" as the default reader → "a foreign parent company / a foreign investor / a foreign national / from the investor's home country / your bank in your home country".

  d.   e. Hiring FAQ "Can the company employ Korean nationals?" (005) → "Can the company employ foreign nationals?" (work-permit rules apply to foreigners generally).
  f. Related-link labels are already relabelled; if a sentence still says "Korean-speaking lawyer", rewrite to "the attorney" / "the firm".
