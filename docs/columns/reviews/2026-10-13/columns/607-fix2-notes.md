## L1 — Article 50 school type and Article 46(1)

Changed in all four. Item 2 now states public or registered-private schools at senior-high level and above (zh already had this). Employers hire the students. The Article 50 link stays on the hour cap. A separate sentence says employers hiring either category are not limited to the job categories in Article 46(1), with that article linked. The sentence does not exempt anyone from the student work permit or from other professional laws. Both categories, school levels, the 20-hour maximum, and the winter/summer exception remain.

## L2 — English Article 51

Changed. Paragraph 1 now says a teaching or research unit at the student's own school. Paragraph 2, with its own Article 51 link, states the two qualifications: language expertise plus Ministry of Education case approval and one of the two teaching situations after enrollment; or a student enrolled in a graduate institute who, with the school's consent, does research related to the courses being taken. The weekly cap in Article 50 and regulation Article 54(2) stays, and paragraph 2 sets no different cap.

## L3 — Fresh-graduate job types

Changed in all four. The WDA fresh-graduate page (updated 2026-01-30) is cited for no limit on the kind of work, and the next sentence says another law's professional qualification or practice registration must still be obtained. The page's example, a physician, is kept. No version says hours are unlimited. Associate degree or higher, fresh-graduate status (應屆畢業生, explained as that graduating cohort without a calendar-year definition), a lawful NIA extension, no work-permit application during that extension, and the two-year maximum remain. The 2025-09-24 date stays the amendment date, not Article 12's effective date.

## L4 — Article 74 gloss

Changed in en and zh. Removed "The work bar stays." and "不得再工作的那一句還在。" Article 74(1) keeps "unless the Act provides otherwise," immediate departure, and the bar on further work. Article 74(3)(1) withholds only the immediate-departure rule for foreign students, overseas Chinese students, and ethnic Chinese students hired under the Act, on expiry or Article 73 items 1–5. Article 73 is linked at first mention. Article 68(3) stays tied to an Article 43 violation. Ko and ja already lacked the extra gloss; their Article 74 wording was kept and Article 73 was linked. No permanent ban and no automatic new permission were inferred.
