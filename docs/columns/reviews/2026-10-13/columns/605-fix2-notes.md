## L1 — EN early repayment on the six-month note

Changed. The sentence that treated Article 204 early repayment as opening after one year on this note is gone. The note is still due in six months, at 2 percent a month, read as 24 percent a year. Article 204 stays as the general rule: rate over 12 percent a year, repayment of principal after one year, one month's notice, and the right cannot be removed or narrowed by contract. A following sentence says repayment on this note is due at six months, and the Article 204 right is the general rule for a loan that has not yet fallen due. It does not say Article 204 blocks payment when the six months end. The term of the example was not changed.

## L2 — ALL year-end interest and the missing payment date

Changed in all four, after the official English Article 477: agreed deadline first; if no deadline, payment at the end of the loan; the “unless” year-end clause is attached to that no-deadline case. Each body now says the agreed interest-payment date governs; if none was agreed, interest is paid when the loan ends; if there is still no payment date and the loan lasts more than one year, it is paid at the end of each year, with the Article 477 link on that paragraph. The fact sheet had left the relationship undecided. The official English “unless” sits on the no-deadline sentence, and the review required that connection, so the condition was added. Contractual priority, termination, the more-than-one-year condition, and year-end payment all remain.

## L3 — ALL two-fact proof and Article 474(2)

Changed in all four. The two facts (ownership passed, and an agreement to return the same sum) are limited to an ordinary loan of money just advanced, tied to Article 474 paragraph 1. A short qualification keeps Article 474 paragraph 2: an existing money debt can become a loan by agreement without a new advance. Article 277, including the other-statute and gross-unfairness exceptions, is linked in that proof paragraph as well as in the burden paragraph.

## L4 — KO/JA/ZH fee and compound-interest headings

Changed. ko “선공제 수수료와 복리 약정”. ja “先に差し引く手数料と複利の約定”. zh-hant “預扣手續費與複利約定”. EN heading left as “An upfront fee and interest added to principal”. Bodies still state Article 206 as a ban on a discount or other device beyond the interest Article 205 allows, and Article 207 paragraph 1 with the written-agreement conditions and the commercial-custom exception in paragraph 2. The upfront fee stays an illustration, not a court result.

## C1 — ALL unsourced foreign ceilings

Changed. The sentence that stated foreign ceilings as law and then said the figures were not checked is gone. Each version now says an interest ceiling in Korea, Japan, or a US state is a question for a professional there. No rate and no foreign-court practice added.

## C2 — ALL inline links on the service/default and proof paragraphs

## V3 — EN

The formation paragraph says the Code’s two paragraphs do not require a writing, an IOU can record the promise, and a promissory note is a separate instrument left to the end of the column. “The statute itself does not give that multiplier” is removed; 2 percent a month across twelve months = 24 percent a year stays as arithmetic.
