  *   *   *   *   *   *   *   *   *   *   * # TCCF MARKET to bring together the global content industry Spotlight Screenings target international buyers to propel Taiwan content onto the world stage

September 22, 2026

The highly anticipated 2026 Taiwan Creative Content Fest (TCCF) is set to take place this November. At the heart of industry transactions is TCCF MARKET, which this year will bring together 107 exhibitors from seven countries—Taiwan, South Korea, Thailand, Japan, Singapore, France, and the Philippines. Focusing on rights transactions for international film and television content and promising IPs, the event continues to serve as a key platform connecting Taiwan with the global content industry. In particular, the number of exhibitors in IP categories such as publishing, comics, and characters has increased by 13% from last year. This reflects the continued development of global IPs with established fan bases into screen adaptations and diverse licensing opportunities, an increasingly prominent focus in MARKET exhibitors’ strategies.
Other returning exhibitors include Singapore’s Mediacorp and Japan's TV Tokyo.

On the domestic front, 69 Taiwanese exhibitors will participate this year, spanning a diverse range of sectors including television, animation, publishing, gaming, and content marketing. These include Sanlih E-Television, Videoland, the Chinese Animation and Comic Publishers Association (CCPA), and channels under the Taiwan Broadcasting System such as Public Television Service and Hakka TV. First-time exhibitors include STR Network, Joint Journey Creative, So-net Entertainment Taiwan, Gamania, and Hope Content Marketing, reflecting the breadth of Taiwan’s content industry ecosystem.

 

TCCF MARKET will continue to focus on five major aspects—Copyright Transaction Zone, Production Resource Zone, Animation & Visual Technologies Zone, National Pavilions, and Industry Press Conferences—bringing together production, distribution, and investment professionals across sectors such as television, animation, gaming, and publishing to promote IP adaptation and international collaboration.

The 2026 TCCF will take place from November 6 to 9 at Taipei Nangang Exhibition Center, Hall 2, 7F. For the full list of exhibitors, detailed information on TCCF MARKET events, and how to register for matchmaking activities, please stay tuned to the TCCF official website and social media channels.--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  *   *   *   *   *   *   *   *   *   *   * # TCCF PITCHING unveils official selections! International co-productions top 60% as Taiwan becomes key Asian co-production hub

September 02, 2026

2026 TCCF PITCHING today (September 2) announced its lineup of selected projects. This year’s selections demonstrate that international co-productions have become a defining trend across the global film and television industry, while also reflecting the results of TAICCA’s efforts to promote international collaboration over the past two years. In addition to a significant increase in submissions from international partners such as South Korea, Taiwanese teams also delivered a strong showing, with international co-productions led by or involving Taiwan accounting for more than 60% of selected projects. This increasingly global landscape of collaboration further underscores Taiwan’s emergence as a key Asian hub for film and television co-productions.

Notably, this year’s selections also showcase the impressive potential of original IPs for cross-media adaptation. The feature film project A Sketch in Scarlet and series project Leaf and Tree originated as acclaimed novels previously selected for TCCF’s Story segment. Now moving beyond the written word into screen development, both have been selected to pitch in this year’s Project segment, demonstrating their strong potential to be transformed into high-quality productions. Their journey from award-winning works of fiction to screen projects not only highlights TCCF’s strength as a core platform for cross-sector collaboration, but also generates considerable market anticipation for their eventual adaptations.

New to TCCF PITCHING this year, each of the four main pitching sessions will offer its own major prize of at least USD 30,000, expanding on the previous structure centered around a single top prize contested across all four sessions. Together, the sponsored awards bring the total prize pool to more than TWD 10 million. Selected teams will also be eligible to apply for up to TWD 3 million in early-stage development support through TAICCA’s Creative Content Development Program, providing a powerful boost to Taiwanese film and television projects seeking access to international funding.

TCCF PITCHING’s Story segment brings together standout works across two categories: Fiction & Non-Fiction and Comics. In the Fiction & Non-Fiction session, author LIU Ka-shiang’s Alpha Scorpii in summer : Tadao Kano’s experience growing up in Taiwan recounts the remarkable life of a naturalist during the Japanese colonial era, while author Kevin CHEN’s latest novel, The Three Witches of Shetou, unfolds against the backdrop of Taiwan’s Changhua County. The Comics session likewise showcases a diverse range of subject matter and international visibility. Op.9 The Swan, a rare example of Taiwanese science fiction exploring the value of life, saw its audio drama adaptation win Bronze at the 2026 New York Festivals Radio Awards. Underdog Reborn, the first webtoon adapted from PILI Puppetry, received an Honorable Mention in the Comics Category of the 2026 Best IP Awards.

 

Born for the Spotlight director YEN Yi-wen, a jury member for this year’s Story segment, said that each work offers a glimpse into the questions about life that its creator is most eager to explore at this moment. KANO producer HSU Kuo-lun, meanwhile, said he was particularly drawn to adaptation projects, noting that compelling characterization is the key to dramatic tension.

 

The 2026 Taiwan Creative Content Fest (TCCF) will take place from November 6 to 9 at Taipei Nangang Exhibition Center, Hall 2, 7F. The event aims to help these outstanding projects continue to shine by connecting them with international collaboration opportunities across production, investment, sales, and distribution.
