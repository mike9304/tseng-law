# How to Operate a Logistics Business in Taiwan

![Featured image](../images/017-taiwan-logistics-business-setup/featured-01.jpg)

People in Taiwan have recently also begun to wonder how Coupang can sell goods so cheaply

and why its delivery speed is so fast.

I think it is only a matter of time before Coupang swallows the Taiwan market.

![](../images/017-taiwan-logistics-business-setup/img-01.jpg)

Recently Coupang has been actively recruiting Korean logistics companies,

so many Korean logistics companies are studying how to operate logistics business in Taiwan.

Here I will summarize the following methods:

1. **Establish a logistics company**
2. **Acquire a Taiwan logistics company**
3. **Outsource orders to a Taiwan logistics company**

**1. Establishing a Taiwan logistics company**

To establish a logistics company, you must obtain an “automobile freight transport business” (汽車貨運業) license.

The barrier to entry for this license is very high.

Not only must you inject capital of NT$25 million,

you must also purchase 20 new freight trucks, hold them for one year, and may not dispose of them.

Therefore it is a very large investment for a Korean enterprise.

In addition, the freight transport business license must be applied for separately with a Taiwan agency.

For detailed methods of obtaining an automobile freight transport business license, you may refer to the website of the Highway Bureau, Ministry of Transportation and Communications, R.O.C. (中華民國交通部公路局):

<https://www.thb.gov.tw/cp.aspx?n=392>

​

Here is a brief explanation of the procedure for establishing an automobile freight transport business:

1. Apply for investment with the Investment Commission of Taiwan’s Ministry of Economic Affairs (a procedure all foreign investment must go through)

2. Preparatory establishment stage (籌設階段): Prepare and submit an automobile freight transport business preparatory application (籌備申請書) to the agency, and obtain a preparatory establishment permit official document (籌設許可公文)

3. Pre-filing preparation stage (立案前準備階段): Complete company registration at the city government commerce department (市政府商業處), purchase 20 new freight trucks, and lease a legally registered parking lot dedicated to automobile freight transport

4. Filing and opening for business (立案與開業): Submit the company registration certificate, capital certificate, vehicle purchase contracts, lawful parking-lot lease certificate, and other materials to the agency to apply for filing (立案) (must be applied for within six months after receiving the preparatory establishment permit official document). Once filing is approved, you receive the license, join the trade association, and may apply to open for business

5. Obtain a work permit (工作證) and residence permit (as needed)

**2. Acquiring a Taiwan logistics company**

Because directly establishing a freight transport company costs a great deal and takes a long time,

many Korean logistics enterprises choose to acquire an existing Taiwan logistics company.

This way there is no concern about obtaining an automobile freight transport business license.

Here is a brief explanation of the general procedure for acquiring a Taiwan logistics company:

1. **Find a Taiwan logistics company and sign a company acquisition agreement**
2. **Apply for investment with the Investment Commission of Taiwan’s Ministry of Economic Affairs (a procedure all foreign investment must go through)**
3. **Remit capital after receiving Investment Commission approval**
4. **Handle company transfer, change of representative, and related matters**
5. **Obtain a work permit (工作證) and residence permit (as needed)**

Once the above procedures are completed, you can operate a logistics business in Taiwan.

**※ Notes when acquiring a Taiwan logistics company:**

1. **Confirm the target company’s condition, and verify the company’s assets and liabilities, among other matters, to avoid unnecessary risk**
2. **Draft the share acquisition agreement carefully, and clearly set out the obligations of the target company to reduce future disputes (for example: when is the share transfer deadline? Who pays government fees? Are there penalties for failing to perform contract terms?)**
3. **After the company acquisition is complete, for a foreign national to work in Taiwan it is safe to obtain a work permit (工作證) and residence permit. Otherwise, if reported, entry to Taiwan may be banned for three years**

**3. Outsourcing volume to a Taiwan logistics company**

Finally, the method with the least investment and the smallest risk is

outsourcing Coupang volume to a Taiwan logistics company.

This requires finding a Taiwan logistics company willing to cooperate.

The most important point in this method is drafting a thorough cooperation agreement.

The contract term, profit-sharing method, risk-allocation method, and so on must all be clearly fixed.
