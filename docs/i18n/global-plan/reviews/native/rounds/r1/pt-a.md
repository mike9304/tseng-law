## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): 4/5 for guidance pack, 3/5 for columns
variety used: European Portuguese, consistent (contacto, factos, anónima, viação, telemóvel in 003, ginásio, receção, coima, património, sítio, contributos). Not a Brazil/Portugal split of the legal standard. 005 and 008 say telefone móvel where 003 already says telemóvel. Guidance still spells carácter; the columns use caráter. Running dates are Portuguese (13 de setembro de 2025, 27 de dezembro de 2023, 1 de janeiro de 2024). Thousands use a period (TWD 500.000, 3.000.000) and the decimal comma is 0,5 and 1,57, which is the right format for Portugal and Brazil.
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- A separate Taiwan company is called filial, which in Portugal and Brazil is a branch without its own legal personality: "A filial taiwanesa (有限公司 ou 股份有限公司) é uma pessoa coletiva independente"
- Waiving a requirement is written with the verb that imposes one: "esta exigência é levantada"
- Royalties are glossed as staff perks: "os juros e os royalties (regalias)"
- An English office name sits inside a Portuguese sentence: "Trabalhou antes no Ministry of Education, Legal Affairs Division"
- French legal calques remain (ceder reciprocamente, instruir um assunto, posto de capital, comissão preparatória).
- 003 Q16–Q20, 005, 006, 008 and 009 are still a personal blog, one sentence a line: "Bom dia, sou Wei Tseng (曾雋崴), advogada em Taiwan."
Gender of attorney Wei Tseng: no finding.
