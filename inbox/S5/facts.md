# S5 facts — 3-year naturalization route after bereavement / DV divorce / for parents of Taiwanese children

Fetched 2026-10-20 from law.moj.gov.tw (cache research/cache/).

## 國籍法 (D0030001; amended 2024-05-24)
- Art 3 https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=D0030001&flno=3 — general: domicile; 183 days/year × 5 continuous years; (2) capacity under ROC law and own law; (3) no bad conduct and no police criminal record; (4) 相當之財產或專業技能，足以自立，或生活保障無虞; (5) basic language ability and civic knowledge.
- Art 4(1) https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=D0030001&flno=4 — domicile + Art 3(1)(2)-(5) + 183 days/year × 3 years and one of:
  - (1) 「為中華民國國民之配偶，不須符合前條第一項第四款。」
  - (2) 「為中華民國國民配偶，因受家庭暴力離婚且未再婚；或其配偶死亡後未再婚且有事實足認與其亡故配偶之親屬仍有往來，但與其亡故配偶婚姻關係已存續二年以上者，不受與親屬仍有往來之限制。」
  - (3) 「對無行為能力、或限制行為能力之中華民國國籍子女，有扶養事實、行使負擔權利義務或會面交往。」
  - (4) 父或母現為或曾為中華民國國民 (cited in FAQ as an example of "other items").
  - Self-support exemption is only in item (1) → items (2)(3) must satisfy Art 3(1)(4).

## 國籍法施行細則 (D0030022; amended 2024-11-19)
- Art 4 — 住所 = 「以久住之意思，住於我國領域內，且持有有效之外僑居留證或外僑永久居留證者」.
- Art 5(2) — excluded residence reasons: (1) migrant work under 就服法46(1)(8)-(10); (2) study; (3) exit ban at request of authorities; (4) awaiting restoration of original nationality; (5) labor dispute litigation; (6) occupational injury treatment; (7) crime victim/witness; (8) dependents of the above. Drafts cite (3),(5),(6),(7) and note death/DV divorce/child are not on the list (list checked: true).
- Art 6 — count backward from application; continuous; ≥183 days each year; overstay < 30 days does not break continuity but those days are not counted in the 183.
- Art 7(1)(1) — for 4(1)(2)(3) applicants: any one of (一) 國內之收入、納稅、動產或不動產資料 (二) 雇主開立之聘僱證明或申請人自行以書面敘明其工作內容及所得 (三) 專門職業及技術人員或技能檢定證明文件 (四) 其他 — 由內政部認定. Art 7(1)(2) others: e.g. 最近一年平均每月收入逾勞動部公告基本工資二倍 / 動產及不動產估價總值逾新臺幣五百萬元 etc.
- Art 8(1) — common documents: valid ARC/APRC; home-country police record; self-support proof (spouse exempt); language/knowledge proof; minors' consent; other identity docs.
- Art 8(2) — 4(1)(2): 受家庭暴力且未再婚之證明文件 or 與亡故配偶之親屬仍有往來事實且未再婚之證明文件 (contact proof exempt where marriage ≥ 2 years).
- Art 8(3) — 4(1)(3): 扶養事實 or 會面交往 proof; exempt if 行使負擔子女之權利義務，並依戶籍法為戶籍登記.
- Art 8(4) — 直轄市、縣（市）政府先行審查; 戶政事務所查明居留資料、入出國日期紀錄、刑事案件紀錄、戶籍資料 → 轉送內政部.

## 入出國及移民法 31(4) (D0080132; 2023-06-28)
- Items 1-3: 因依親對象死亡; 配偶因遭受家庭暴力離婚且未再婚; 離婚後對在臺設籍未成年子女有撫育事實、行使負擔權利義務或會面交往 → drafts say the Nationality Act keeps the 3-year route for "nearly the same" people (comparison, not legal equivalence).
