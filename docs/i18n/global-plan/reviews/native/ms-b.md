reviewer: Grok 4.6 · date: 2026-09-21 · scope: columns-ms/010–012 (in progress; 013–018 pending)

## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): n/a for guidance pack (part a), 2/5 for columns so far (010–012)
variety used: Standard Malay for Malaysia (ms_MY) intended; Indonesian/European numerals (1.579.589, 0,5) and some Indonesian verbs (memotret) persist; not a second Malay variety mixed in, but a French/German→Malay pass like part a
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- 一審/二審 still calqued as “perbicaraan 1” / “peringkat 2”: “kes perbicaraan 1”
- 健身房 rendered as school-hall “gimnasium” not commercial gim: “kecederaan di gimnasium”
- Digit+noun and European decimals: “hon 2 kali”, “0,5 meter”, “TWD 1.570.000”
- French/German legal glue: subjek, pelupusan, orientasi, kepakaran as appraisal: “subjek pengimport”
- Taiwan RHT overtaking left/right copied without saying Taiwan is opposite of Malaysia
- Invest Taiwan link still Japanese: “lang=jpn”

Gender: no masculine form for Wei Tseng in 010–012 (“Saya ialah peguam”, “Peguam Wei Tseng”). Korean student in 010 is the actual plaintiff, not a reader-default. Consultation lock: 011 disclaimer keeps the negative (bukan nasihat, tidak menegaskan kebenaran akan diperoleh); 012 “tidak … janji mengenai hasil tertentu” is correctly negative. Draft findings below for 010–012 only.
