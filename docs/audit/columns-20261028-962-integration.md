## EN metadata adjustment

- seoTitle: Nominee Director in Taiwan: Account Handover Warnings, Money Laundering Accomplice Risk, Resignation and Tax Exit Bans → Nominee Director in Taiwan: Laundering Risk
- summary: Taiwan's Money Laundering Control Act treats giving another person the use of a bank account without a legitimate reason as a breach that draws a police warning, and as a crime where payment was promised or received, three or more accounts were involved, or it happens again within five years of a police ruling (Article 22). Laundering itself is the heavier offense: when a company account moves fraud or gambling money, the director on the register can be questioned as an accomplice under that Act and Criminal Code Articles 13 and 30 without having made a transfer, and is presumed innocent until a conviction is final. Unpaid company tax of NT$2 million or more can lead to an exit restriction on the responsible person, who under Company Act Article 8 is the director, nominee or not. A director may resign at any time, but the company's representative files the change within 15 days, and until it is registered it cannot be asserted against third parties (Article 12). → When a Taiwan company account moves fraud or gambling money, the director on the register can be questioned as an accomplice without having made a transfer.
