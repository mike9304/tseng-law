# Ten days after the policy lapsed: why a missed renewal notice did not backdate Taiwan scooter insurance

September 28, 2014: the compulsory insurance on a scooter expired. October 8, in the evening: a crash. October 16: the policy was renewed. Those three dates were the whole dispute.

In the rider's view, the renewal, made within 30 days of expiry, should reach back and cover the day of the crash, because the insurer had not properly sent a renewal notice. On April 19, 2017, the Nantou District Court rejected that argument and upheld a first-instance order for the rider to repay NT$300,000 to the Special Compensation Fund ([Nantou District Court civil judgment 105 Jian-Shang No. 29](https://www.mvacf.org.tw/ckfinder/connector?command=Proxy&type=Attach&currentFolder=/tblResearches/&fileName=%E8%87%BA%E7%81%A3%E5%8D%97%E6%8A%95%E5%9C%B0%E6%96%B9%E6%B3%95%E9%99%A2%E6%B0%91%E4%BA%8B%E5%88%A4%E6%B1%BA105%E5%B9%B4%E5%BA%A6%E7%B0%A1%E4%B8%8A%E5%AD%97%E7%AC%AC29%E8%99%9F.pdf&c=7&p=4b83ef40cffa42ab832a1c003c56eedb&l=3)). The judgment states that no further appeal was allowed.

## A pedestrian crossing the road

It happened in Caotun Township, Nantou County. Riding a scooter registered to the rider's mother, the rider hit a pedestrian who was crossing the road. The pedestrian suffered head injuries with subdural and subarachnoid bleeding and was left with paralysis on the left side.

Both sides were at fault. The pedestrian crossed where a centre dividing line prohibited crossing and did not watch for traffic. The rider failed to watch the road ahead on a lit street at night. The court put the pedestrian at 70 percent and the rider at 30 percent. In the criminal case, the rider was convicted of negligent injury and given two months' imprisonment, convertible to a fine, and that judgment became final.

At the time, the scooter had no compulsory insurance. Under [Article 40(1)(ii) of the Compulsory Automobile Liability Insurance Act](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=G0390060&flno=40), the pedestrian claimed from the Special Compensation Fund, which paid NT$300,000 in medical and disability compensation.

## Backdating needs three things at once

[Article 15](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=G0390060&flno=15) requires the insurer to notify the policyholder to renew at least 30 days before the policy expires. If the insurer fails to do so, the accident happens within 30 days after expiry, and the policyholder renews with the start date backdated to the original expiry, the insurer must still pay.

All three conditions must be met. The crash was within 30 days of expiry, and the rider sought a backdated renewal. The remaining issue was whether the insurer had failed to give notice. Had it?

## An ordinary postcard counted

According to the rider, the insurer used ordinary mail, so the notice never arrived. In its reply to the court, the insurer said it had sent a postcard to the policyholder's address on August 21, 2014, and supplied a post office certificate of ordinary mailing. The policyholder was the rider's mother, and the rider said they lived together at that address.

On this point the court was brief. Article 15 does not require registered mail. The insurer had sent notice to the policyholder's address more than 30 days before expiry, so it had not breached its duty, and the later renewal could not be backdated. The court added that the person riding the scooter should have kept an eye on when the compulsory cover expired.

## Thirty percent was still enough

Assessed in full, the pedestrian's losses came to NT$2,107,392 for medical costs, nursing care, lost earning capacity and non-financial harm. The rider's 30 percent share came to NT$632,218. That was more than the Fund's NT$300,000 payment, so the Fund could recover all of it ([Article 42(2)](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=G0390060&flno=42)).

Interest at 5 percent a year was payable until repayment, on NT$200,000 from June 6, 2015 and on NT$100,000 from December 5, 2015.

## For anyone riding in Taiwan

Expiry dates for compulsory cover can be checked on the insurance card or with the insurer. The duty to insure falls in principle on the owner ([Article 6](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=G0390060&flno=6)), and after a move, a renewal notice may go to the old address. Borrowing a scooter from family or friends? Asking about the expiry date when the keys change hands takes a few seconds.

This rider renewed eight days after the crash. Those eight days were not backdated.
