## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): 4/5 for guidance pack, 3/5 for columns
variety used: Vietnamese (Vietnam, chữ Quốc ngữ), consistent; no Northern/Southern mix that would confuse a client. Number punctuation is mixed: guidance/001 FAQ use Vietnamese thousands (`500.000`) while 003–004 keep English commas (`TWD 1,000,000`) and the bio uses `TWD 1.57M`.
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- Korean-audience leftovers on a Vietnam page: `"khác với Hàn Quốc"` as the reader’s baseline (008/009); Korea–Taiwan 10% treaty presented as the working rate in 001/004.
- English chrome left on otherwise Vietnamese pages: `"OUR TEAM"`, `"OFFICES"`, `"Hồ sơ đầy đủ (English)"`, university and job titles untranslated in bios.
- Hangul slugs on Vietnamese articles: `"대만-노동법：대만에서-퇴직금-받기-어렵다고"` (also 009; percent-encoded Korean Wix path in 005).
- Blog register in 003 Q16–Q20, 005, 006, 008: first person, empty lines, `"Xin chào quý vị, tôi là Wei Tseng"`, `"xin để lại bình luận"`.
- Attorney-advertising wording: `"mức bồi thường cao nhất có thể"`, typical 3/4/6-month sentences, `"ông đã nhận được khoản trợ cấp thôi việc lớn"`.
- Sino-Vietnamese calque of Taiwan statutes: `"gánh vác quyền, nghĩa vụ"` (you bear duties, not rights); `"Điều lệ Quỹ hưu trí lao động"` for 條例 (an act, not bylaws).
Gender of attorney Wei Tseng: no finding. Vietnamese *luật sư* is epicene; bios and columns use *là luật sư*, *Sinh viên trao đổi*, *rà soát*, *tôi là Wei Tseng* with no *ông*, *anh*, or masculine title. 007’s `"thăm nom, tiếp xúc với con (會面交往)"` is clearer than the guidance pack’s `"thăm nom con"`. Columns 001, 002 and 004 are dense, usable legal Vietnamese once the Korea-tax hole is closed; 001 even opens with `"Doanh nghiệp Việt Nam"` and 007 correctly recasts the family example as `"Việt Nam và Đài Loan"`. 005 Q5 was generalised to foreigners. 006’s `"gội đầu kiểu Việt (越式洗髮)"` is the one stretch a native would actually write.
