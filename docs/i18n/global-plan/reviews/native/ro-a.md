# Romanian native review — part a (ro)
reviewer: Grok 4.6 · date: 2026-09-21 · scope: ro-guidance.txt; columns-ro/001–009 (establishment basics, capital withdrawal, traffic accidents, subsidiary vs branch, establishment advanced-2, massage law, divorce Q&A, labor severance, voluntary resignation)

## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): 3/5 for guidance pack, 2/5 for columns
variety used: Romanian of Romania (Daco-Romanian), consistent; no Moldovan mix. Columns are a French-pivot legal register; the guidance pack is a German-pivot formal register. Typography uses French «guillemets» instead of Romanian „ghilimele”.
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- Columns calque French legal syntax no Romanian lawyer would write: "Prezentul articol", "convine să se", "relative la", "piesele" (Fr. pièces = documents).
- Subsidiary rendered throughout as "societate fiică" (Fr. société fille), not the Romanian company-law term "filială".
- Guidance chrome calques German (Tätigkeitsgruppen / Umfang der Arbeit): "grupele de lucrări", "întinderea lucrării", mechanical "o avocată sau un avocat".
- Korea-centric address was not generalized for a Romania reader (tax treaty, capital remittance, labor comparison, "coreeană" CTAs).
- Internal "see also" links still go to `/ko/…`; some column URLs still contain Hangul.
- French date form 27/12/2023 and "un terț 3" (Ko. 제3자) instead of Romanian 27.12.2023 and "un terț".
No masculine forms found for attorney Wei Tseng (曾雋崴); titles and agreement stay feminine ("avocata", "autorizată", "Avocată coordonatoare"). Column 006 is the most natural of the nine; 001–004 and 007 are the most foreign.
