## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): N/A for guidance pack (out of scope), 3/5 for columns
variety used: standard Finnish (yleiskieli), teitittely, except the 011 cover alt “päästäksesi”. Dates are day + month in the essive + year. 012’s “0,5 metrin” uses a decimal comma. Thousands are written with a period (1.579.589, TWD 25.000.000), which is not Finnish. 018 skips from §5 to §7, as the English does.
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- Thousands use a period: "velvoitti … maksamaan [1.579.589 TWD]" (also 011, 017, 018).
- "Yksin" and "yhdessä" calque only/also, and "tutkia" calques consider: "yhdessä on tutkittava käyttäjän harjoituskokemus".
- A Chinese gloss is parked on the wrong word: "oikeudellista neuvontaa (損害賠償)".
- The responsible entity is still "subjekti": "lakisääteinen vastuusubjekti" (also 018 "tarvitaan subjekti").
- 013 and 015 break almost every clause into its own paragraph: "Markkinatilanteen lisäksi".
- Taiwan household registration (戶籍) is called the Finnish population register: "ilmoittaminen väestörekisteriin (戶籍)" (also 017).

012 is native professional apart from one phrase. 016 and 018 are close. 010, 011 and 014 still shift legal objects. 013 and 015 read as a chopped blog.

Gender of attorney Wei Tseng: no finding. "Asianajaja" does not mark gender. "Oikeudenkäyntiasiamies" in 010 is the statutory title. "Vastuuhenkilö" in 018 is gender-neutral; it should still name the attorney. "Yhtiömiehet" in 018 is about company members, not her. Consultation lock stays negative (013 closing note; 018 §8, only English, Chinese, Japanese or Korean). No win rate, guarantee, or best/only claim. "Parempi" in 018 sits inside a denial.
