## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): —/5 for guidance pack (not in this part), 3/5 for columns 010–012
variety used: Standard Myanmar Burmese (မြန်မာစာ), one variety. `date_display` uses month names (စက်တင်ဘာ၊ ဖေဖော်ဝါရီ). In-body dates in 010 and 011 still use a bare month number (`1 လ 24 ရက်`, `7 လ 1 ရက်`).

systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- A month written as `N လ` also reads as a duration of N months: "2022 ခုနှစ် 1 လ 24 ရက်တွင်"
- 對象 / target is ပစ်မှတ်, the word for a shooting target: "ပစ်မှတ် ထုတ်ကုန်ကို ထောက်ပံ့"
- “Operate / run a process” is လည်ပတ်, the word for running a business or a machine: "ခွဲ၍ လည်ပတ်ရသည်"
- 補正 (“please supplement the filing”) collapsed into ပြင်ဆင်မှု ရှိမရှိ (“whether preparation exists”): "အမှုနှင့် ပြင်ဆင်မှု ရှိမရှိအပေါ်"

Wei Tseng is ကျွန်မ in 010 and ရှေ့နေမ in 011. No masculine form for her. 012 does not name her. The Korean student in 010 stays Korean because the case is about him; ယောက်ျားလေး is the wrong register for that adult, not a gender error about the attorney. 011 already says နိုင်ငံခြား အမှတ်တံဆိပ်. Disclaimers stay negative: no result guarantee, no promise that every procedure succeeds, no fixed processing time. 012 is natural professional Burmese aside from one wrong traffic term. 010 and 011 are usable once the rows below are fixed; 011 is the more mechanical of the two.
