## Accuracy (8 / 8)

I compared every claim and number sentence by sentence. The two versions are substantively identical. Both carry:
- the 1–3 year period and the exclusion of death, life and minimum-3-years-or-more offences (253-1);
- the stop of the limitation period (253-1 para. 2);
- all eight conditions, including 40–240 hours of community service;
- consent for items 3–6 ("배상부터 치료까지 네 가지는 피고인이 동의해야 합니다/하고") and the civil-enforcement role of items 3 and 4;
- the requirements that conditions be noted in the decision and not outlast the period;
- service on the related victim (255), the complainant's 10-day reconsideration and the consent bar (256);
- the three revocation grounds, "직권으로 또는 고소인의 신청으로", no refund of what was already performed, and "조문에는 취소 신청의 기한이 적혀 있지 않습니다" (253-3);
- the defendant's 10-day reconsideration of a revocation (256-1);
- the bar on re-indictment and its exception (260);
- the "확인하지 못했습니다" caveat, the contact, the reference list with all seven article URLs plus 연혁, the 확인일 and the disclaimer.

MUST VERIFY item 1 (253-2 para. 2: items 3–6 need consent, items 3 and 4 can be enforcement titles) is stated correctly in both. Both cite "제2항" in the lead. I did not test the "근거가 될 수 있습니다" vs "근거가 됩니다" wording difference, because both versions use both forms.

MUST VERIFY item 2 (253-3: exactly three grounds, item 2 paraphrased, "或依告訴人之聲請" present) is also correct and identical in both. For example: "緩起訴 전의 고의범죄로 기간 중 징역형 이상을 선고받았거나".

No fact was lost by either version relative to the other. Each has one small mark against it:
- X attaches no inline 253-2 link to the eight-item, consent, "처분서에 적어야 하며" and period-limit sentences. The paragraph "그 기간 동안 검사는 피고인에게 여덟 가지를 명할 수 있습니다…" carries only 253-1 links. 253-2 is linked in the lead (제2항) and in the reference list, so no URL or article number is dropped. It is a sourcing-placement gap against the rule that legal claims carry their source.
- Y's lead asks "배상 조건이 적힌 緩起訴, 그 부분은 판결 없이 집행할 수 있을까요?". "판결 없이" is not in the sources. It follows from the meaning of 民事強制執行名義, so I treated it as a defensible inference. A stricter reader could flag it as an added claim.

## Voice (X 8, Y 6)

X reads as one compact, native-sounding column. It opens on the result: "緩起訴 처분서에 피해자에게 배상하라는 조건이 붙었다면, 그 부분은 민사 강제집행의 근거가 될 수 있습니다". It also keeps the logic of the ending intact: "조건이 실제로 지켜지는지 고소인에게 알려 주는 절차가 법에 있는지는 확인하지 못했습니다. 처분서에 적힌 조건과 기간을 먼저 기록해 두시고…". Its weak spots are dense paragraphs and the vague second sentence "이름은 불기소와 닮았지만 성격이 다른 처분입니다". Y has the same claim.

Y moves nearly every citation into its own filler sentence of the form "제n조가 그 …을 정합니다". There are six of them:
- "제253조의2 제2항이 그렇게 정합니다."
- "제253조의1이 그 범위와 기간을 정합니다."
- "제253조의1 제2항이 그 정지를 정합니다."
- "제255조가 그 송달을 정합니다."
- "제256조가 그 열흘과 동의의 제한을 정합니다."
- "제260조가 그 예외를 둡니다."

These sentences add no new content, so they fail the "문장마다 새 내용이 있는가" test. In the second paragraph, two consecutive sentences each carry a link to 253-1. The staccato "이름은 불기소를 닮았습니다. 처분의 성격은 불기소와 다릅니다." is stilted. Y's good points are the natural question "취소 신청도 10일일까요?" and an ending that stays on the column's facts.

## Variety (X 8, Y 7)

X's checker result is OK with no violation: cv 0.65, short sentences 16.7%, cite_end 33%, no run of 3. It has 0 questions and a few long dense sentences, such as the three-ground sentence.

Y's metrics are stronger on paper: cv 0.73, short 26.5%, q=2, cite_end 0. It carries one WARN: 94% of sentences end in 니다. The six "…가 그 …을 정합니다" sentences are a new repeated template, about 18% of its sentences, which defeats the purpose of the rule. Both versions have "수 있습니다" 8 times, over the soft cap of 4.

## Hook (X 7, Y 8)

X: a concrete, result-first first sentence, followed by a weak contrast sentence.

Y: "배상 조건이 적힌 緩起訴, 그 부분은 판결 없이 집행할 수 있을까요? 민사 강제집행의 근거가 될 수 있습니다." This is a real reader question with an immediate answer and no clickbait. The opening is slightly clumsy, and the "판결 없이" nuance is the inference noted under accuracy.

## Facts lost

Neither version lost a fact relative to the other. Neither mentions the 253-1 start-of-period rule ("확정일부터"). The fact sheet did not require it, and both omit it equally.

WINNER: X
