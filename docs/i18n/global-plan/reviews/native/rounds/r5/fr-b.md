## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): N/A for guidance pack (out of scope; part a), 4/5 for columns
variety used: French of France, consistent. The reader is addressed as *vous*. *Taïwan* keeps the diaeresis. Dates are day–month–year. The decimal comma is right where it appears (*0,5 mètre*, *1,57 million*). *Taipei* is the modern France spelling.
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- Thousands are still marked with a point, and TWD often stands before the amount: "un capital minimum de TWD 25.000.000".
- The first of the month is still written *1*, not *1er*: "À compter du 1 juillet 2026".
- U+200B is still in 013 and 015, including lines whose only character is that space: "l’indication de la région suffit".
- The contact closings in 013 and 015 invite a legal exchange and do not name the four consultation languages: "d’un échange sur le droit de Taïwan, utilisez la page Contact".
- Ending an employment contract is still *éteindre*: "Lorsque le travailleur éteint un contrat de travail à durée indéterminée".
- *Mode d’opérer* and *facultés* still stand in for how business is done and for authority: "le mode d’opérer, les facultés et le partage de responsabilité".

012 is native professional. 016 is professional legal French aside from the checklist voice below; *réserve héréditaire*, *indignité*, *succession par représentation* and *représentant spécial* read as the right civil-law words. 013 and 015 read as a native FAQ once the zero-width spaces are removed and the contact line names the languages. 010, 011, 014, 017 and 018 are professional with the local defects in the table.
