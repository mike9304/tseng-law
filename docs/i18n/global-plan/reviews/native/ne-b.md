## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): not in scope (part a) for guidance pack, 3/5 for columns
variety used: Standard Nepali in Devanagari, consistent; official ङ् spellings (सङ्ख्या, अङ्ग्रेजी, सङ्केत) with युक्तिसंगतता in 014. Hindi slips in 012 (काफी) and 016 (जिन). No masculine form for Wei Tseng: 010 and 013 use gender-neutral first person (थिएँ, चाहन्छु, सहमत छु); sign-offs are अधिवक्ता Wei Tseng.
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- व्यवसाय शाखा calques 營業項目 (business item) as a company branch: "यो कम्पनीका व्यवसाय शाखा (營業項目) मा भर पर्छ।"
- क्षेत्र slides among industry, sector approval, business type and place: "धारा 35 अनुसार क्षेत्र स्वीकृति पूरा गर्नुपर्छ।"
- विषय stands in for a legal person, responsible entity or operating body: "विदेशी मूल कम्पनीभन्दा फरक कानुनी विषय।"
- अपेक्षा softens requirements into expectations: "ताइवानका स्थानीय सरकारका अपेक्षा फरक छन्।"
- Nepal amount grouping missing, and ROC year 109 has no सन् year: "TWD 25,000,000" and "वर्ष 109 को उपभोक्ता मुद्दा खण्ड 7।"
- Register split: dense legal prose in 010, 011, 014, 016, 017 and 018; one-sentence blog lines with blank lines in 013 and 015.

014 and 016 are the most professional (हदम्याद-level civil vocabulary: फलभोग, व्ययन, आरक्षित अंश, विशेष सम्पत्ति). 012 correctly tells Nepal readers that Taiwan drives on the right, so overtaking is on the left. The required negatives hold where this batch states them: 013 and 015 limit talks to अङ्ग्रेजी, चिनियाँ (中文), जापानी र कोरियाली and deny a reply deadline; 018 asks for those four consultation languages; 010 and 012 deny a certain outcome. No win-rate, “best/only”, free-meeting, or Nepali-consultation promise. Dates such as 13 सेप्टेम्बर 2025 and 5 जुन 2026 already follow Nepal order and month names.
