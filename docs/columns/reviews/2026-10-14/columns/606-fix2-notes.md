## L1 — Article 2 does not allocate the wage debt; branch and head office are not an either/or

Changed in all four. Article 2 is now described as the Act's definitions (worker, employer, wage, average wage, labor contract). The three employer limbs stay. The text no longer says the definition decides who owes a particular wage debt, and it no longer treats a Taiwan branch and a foreign head office as alternative employers. Shareholding alone still does not make another company the employer. Each hypothetical names the company on the labor contract. FAQ 3 matches that. Managers and representatives in the definition are not described as personally liable for every company wage debt. BLI's separate point is added with a link: the person in charge, and people in a mandate relationship (statutory agent, directors and supervisors of a company limited by shares), are not workers under Article 2, subparagraph 1, and are not listed as fund applicants.

Not added: the Taipei City letter of 97.02.12 on a branch's procedural capacity. Removing the false either/or was the required fix. A branch with independent personnel and finance can be a closure fact under regulation article 8 and the BLI note, which is a different proposition from "which entity hired the worker." The examples are local companies, and FAQ 3 asks about a shareholding parent. Putting the branch path in would open a corporate-law distinction this column does not need.

## L2 — Fund eligibility was incomplete

Changed in all four, including summaries, FAQ 1–2, and the closing hypothetical.

- Advance is limited to workers whose employer has contributed. A later makeup of missed contributions also allows an application. Regulation article 7 and the BLI page.
- Statutory words stay "the portion under six months." Separately, BLI reads the wage advance as contract wages owed in the six months before closure, liquidation, or adjudicated bankruptcy. Older arrears are outside that wage advance.
- An unanswered demand meets article 28, paragraph 5, and does not by itself complete the file. Closure needs the local-authority record in regulation article 8. Debt proof is required. Liquidation or adjudicated bankruptcy also needs proof the claim was lodged with the liquidator or bankruptcy administrator (article 9). The KO and ZH door notices, and the JA/EN liquidation notices, are not those records. JA/EN do not call the liquidation notice a missing closure certificate, because those examples are liquidation cases.
- If the employer cannot be found or will not sign, BLI FAQ 7 asks for an original payment order, or a civil judgment plus a certificate of finality.
- If investigation still cannot fix the amounts and years of service, article 9, paragraph 2, allows the Bureau to use labor-insurance or employment-insurance insured salary and insured years. All four say this does not replace the average-wage formula.
- A temporary stoppage that may resume is not yet a ground to apply (same BLI page). 重整 was not added. The BLI sentence used here is about 停工 only.
- Applications go to the Bureau of Labor Insurance (regulation articles 2 and 10). Workers of the same employer generally file once, together. The Act's "may entrust" (paragraph 6) is kept, and the regulation's actual assignment to the Bureau is added.
- Hypotheticals assume a labor contract covered by the Act. An empty account does not itself establish a fund claim. Foreign workers who meet the conditions use the same method, conditions, and scope as Taiwanese workers (BLI).

Not added, because the review said they are unnecessary: the collected rate (regulation article 3 states 萬分之二．五; article 28, paragraph 4, still states only the 萬分之十五 ceiling, as of 2026-10-14), the 30-day / plus-20-day decision period in regulation article 12, and the form-by-form list. Article 28 is still described as not stating a number of days.

## L3 — "Not full payment" overstated the statute

KO, JA, and ZH no longer say full payment is impossible. They now say recovery in full is not guaranteed (KO 전액 회수를 보장하는 규정은 아닙니다, JA 全額を回収できるとは限りません, ZH 不保證能全額受償), next to the article 28 rank: same initial rank, pro rata, most preferential right for the unpaid balance, and not a rank that starts ahead of the secured claims. EN "Full payment is not promised." was left as written.
