## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): —/5 for guidance pack (not in this part), 3/5 for columns
variety used: Standard Khmer (ភាសាខ្មែរស្តង់ដារ), one variety. Orthography still mixes ឱ with ឲ and coeng-ដ (្ដ) with coeng-ត (្ត). Columns keep Arabic digits, comma thousands, and day–month–year dates.

systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- 018 sections 3–5 throw away the rules and leave counts, money figures, and agency names: "ភាគទុនិក (股東) 2 1 ឬ 2 100% TWD 30,000,000"
- 院 / Yuan is written យួន, the everyday word for Vietnamese: "យួនតុលាការ (司法院)"
- 013 and 015 are still the blog skeleton: one clause a line, no final ។ : "ក្នុងរយៈពេលថ្មីៗនេះ មានមនុស្សជាច្រើនបានបង្កើតក្រុមហ៊ុននៅតៃវ៉ាន់"
- គោលដៅ stands in for 對象 / target, including an acquisition target: "ក្រុមហ៊ុនគោលដៅដែលជាម្ចាស់លិខិតអនុញ្ញាត"
- ឧបត្ថម្ភ is still “support”, so a third party reads as a patron: "មានភាគីទីបី (第三人) ឧបត្ថម្ភ"
- Orthography is not one standard: "ម្តងហើយម្តងទៀត" and "ការឲ្យខ្ចី" beside ឱ្យ and ្ដ

Wei Tseng is នាងខ្ញុំ in 010 and 013, and មេធាវីស្ត្រី in 011 and 016. 012, 014, 015, 017, and 018 do not gender her. No masculine form. លោកអ្នក is the reader. The consultation lock stays negative where these columns state it: four languages only, no reply-time promise, no result guarantee. ប្រយោជន៍ខ្ពស់បំផុតរបស់កូន is the statutory “best interests of the child”, not a firm advertising claim. 014 is professional legal Khmer aside from the rows below. 010, 011, 012, and 017 are usable once those rows are fixed. 016 is usable once the institution name and the marital-property claim are renamed. 013 and 015 need to be put back into paragraphs. 018 must not go to a client until sections 2–5 are restored.
