## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): 4/5 for guidance pack, 3/5 for columns
variety used: standard Slovak (spisovná slovenčina), consistent; no Czech orthography, but a handful of Czech-shaped lexical forms survive ("Súvisiace sprievodce" in 004, "Obdržali sme" and "schôdny" in the pack, "u praxe" in 008, "pojednáva" in 004, un-vocalised "z starostlivosti" in 002). Dates are already Slovak (`13. septembra 2025`, `27. 12. 2023`, `25. 7. 2026`), the decimal comma is right (`0,5 mesiaca`) and percentages take a space (`5 %`, `21 %`). Gender of attorney Wei Tseng: no finding — *advokátka*, *riadiaca advokátka*, *oprávnená*, *Zastupovala*, *Posúdila*, *pripravila advokátka*, and in 003 *Nie som preto presvedčená* / *ktoré som viedla*; 008 *ktorú som skutočne viedla*. The consultation lock stays negative everywhere it appears (four languages only, no interpreter, no reply-time promise, no free first meeting, no guarantee of result), and 003 Q17–Q18 keep denying a typical or guaranteed sentence — those negations must stay as they are. No win-rate, "best/only" or guarantee wording was found; 008's case story is explicitly closed with "Tento príklad nie je prísľubom, že rovnaký výsledok nastane aj u ostatných zamestnancov." The pack is close to client-ready: 001, 003 Q1–Q15, 007 and 009 read as professional legal Slovak inside the lines. What drags the columns down is concentrated: 002 and 004 still contain sentences assembled in source order, 004 repeats a French/English "Nie preto, že …" frame four times, and the round-4 layout damage (zero-width-space lines, lost `##` headings) and the dot thousands separator are completely unfixed.
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- Administrative/bank "review" is rendered *prieskum* (a market survey) instead of *preskúmanie / posúdenie*, 13× in 001 and 1× in 002, although the same file names the authority "Odbor preskúmania investícií": "bankový prieskum a predpoklady zamestnávateľa v rámci pracovného povolenia treba však overovať osobitne"
- Source-order frames survive untranslated, above all the four-times-repeated "Nie preto, že X, Y": "Nie preto, že ústredie zaplatilo, sa môžu všetky náklady počítať ako odpočítateľné náklady taiwanskej pobočky"
- Numbers are still formatted for a non-Slovak reader: dot thousands separator 41× across 001–004, 006, 007, plus digits where Slovak spells the numeral out: "najmenej 500.000 TWD (新臺幣), obrat najmenej 3.000.000 TWD"

- Key terms drift inside the same text — porada/konzultácia (16 vs 23 in the pack), riaditeľ/člen predstavenstva for 董事, three names for 贍養費, zásadne (37×) vs v zásade (6×): "Porada prebieha iba v štyroch jazykoch: anglicky, čínsky (中文), japonsky a kórejsky."
