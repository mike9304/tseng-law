# When a Taiwan bank account is listed as a warning account

The [Regulations Governing Deposit Accounts and Suspected Illegal or Apparently Unusual Transactions](https://law.fsc.gov.tw/LawContent.aspx?id=FL039015) define a warning account as one a court, prosecutors' office or judicial police have notified the bank to mark for a criminal investigation. The bank suspends trading functions and, in principle, returns inbound transfers to the remitting bank.

Foreigners who opened accounts with an ARC or passport fall under the same rules. Salary, rent and daily transfers can stop at once, so the first task is to identify the notifying authority.

## Challenging the listing as the account holder

Article 9, paragraph 2 directs the holder who disputes the listing to the original notifying authority; the bank assists when needed. Article 10 ties release to that authority's notice or to expiry of the warning term. Derived control accounts and accounts the bank itself treated as unusual are released when the bank confirms the reason has disappeared.

Article 9, paragraph 1 sets a two-year term, with one extension of up to one year if the authority re-notifies before expiry.

## Victim residual funds are a different track

Whether money in the account is a victim remittance or the holder's own pay changes the path. Accounts flagged on or before 31 March 2025 still use Article 11 of the deposit-account regulations for residual victim funds. Accounts flagged on or after 1 April 2025 follow [Articles 52–53 of the anti-fraud compliance regulations for financial institutions and VASPs](https://law.banking.gov.tw/Chi/FLAW/FLAWDAT0202.aspx?lsid=FL104315): judicial police may notify the bank in writing to return remaining funds, with notice to the holder, and the victim submits ID, acceptance proof and an affidavit. The wording is permissive; return is not framed as automatic.

Accessory civil claims in a criminal case are a separate route described in [Accessory civil suits for fraud victims](/en/columns/taiwan-criminal-accessory-civil-suit-fraud). Unlocking the account does not fix the damages amount.
