A Taiwan couple registered their divorce, but a district court ruled it void and said they were still married.
One witness never checked that the husband wanted out.

Per the ruling's testimony, that witness, the wife's old friend, met her alone at a restaurant and signed as witness, the couple's lines still blank. The friend never met or contacted the husband then.

Under case law, each witness must know firsthand both spouses want out. Even counting the other one, that's fewer than two.
