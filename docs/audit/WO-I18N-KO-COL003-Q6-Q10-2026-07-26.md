## Legal and copy contract

### Q6 — Preliminary analysis, appraisal, and review

- Use the same public-facing term already introduced in Q2/Q4:
  `도로교통사고 초보분석판정표`.
- Explain that it is a preliminary police analysis and not a court judgment or
  a binding fault percentage.
- Do not present the preliminary table, vehicle-accident appraisal, review,
  and academic appraisal as an automatic or mandatory four-step pipeline.
- Explain that a statutory vehicle-accident appraisal may be initiated by an
  eligible party, transferred by a handling authority, or commissioned by a
  judicial authority under the governing rules.
- State that a party application is generally made within six months after the
  accident. Once a case is under investigation or trial, explain that appraisal
  proceeds through a judicial-authority commission rather than a fresh direct
  party application.
- Explain that review of the appraisal opinion is limited to one review.
- State that appraisal and review opinions are evidentiary/reference material
  and do not bind the court, which evaluates the whole record independently.
- Do not name or promote Feng Chia University or another private/academic
  institution. Do not say an institution calculates an “exact” percentage, is
  the “final” stage, or is “almost impossible” to overturn.

### Q7 — Potential damage categories

- Begin with the Civil Code Article 184 prerequisites: an actionable unlawful
  infringement, causation, and proof. A bare accident does not automatically
  create every listed entitlement.
- Separate the categories:
  1. injury: necessary medical expenses, increased living needs such as care,
     treatment travel and aids, lost income actually caused by incapacity,
     reduced earning capacity, and non-pecuniary damage;
  2. death: pre-death medical/increased-needs loss where applicable, funeral
     expenses, legally owed maintenance loss, and qualifying relatives'
     non-pecuniary damage;
  3. property: repair cost or diminished value and other proven actual loss.
- Tie death losses to Articles 192 and 194, injury losses to Articles 193 and
  195, damaged-property value to Article 196, and actual/lost-benefit scope to
  Article 216.
- Use natural Korean terms. Avoid untranslated Chinese-law calques and do not
  describe any category as automatically or always recoverable.

### Q8 — Later medical expenses and evidence

- Tell readers to preserve receipts, diagnoses, treatment records, and the
  medical necessity and accident-causation link.
- Ongoing treatment material may be submitted subject to the court's procedural
  timetable and the pleaded claim, but do not promise that every late document
  or expanded claim will be accepted.
- Distinguish adding evidence from changing or increasing the relief/claim
  amount.
- Explicitly reject the stale claim that merely submitting another medical
  receipt after filing an incidental civil action automatically creates an
  additional court fee.
- Explain narrowly that after an Article 504 transfer to the civil division,
  a change/addition/expansion beyond the pre-transfer claim scope can create a
  fee question for the excess portion. Advise case-specific checking of the
  transfer stage, timing, and scope.

### Q9 — Professional and family care

- Explain that a diagnosis or medical opinion is useful but not conclusive by
  itself.
- Require proof of accident causation, need for care, actual provision, period,
  and a reasonable rate/amount.
- State that unpaid care actually provided by a relative may be valued as loss
  in an appropriate case, but is not automatically awarded.
- Do not say a judge “recognizes” care costs whenever a diagnosis states a
  period or whenever a relative provided care.

### Q10 — Treatment travel costs

- Link each claimed trip to treatment records and the accident-related injury.
- Explain that the route, number and dates of visits, mode of transport, fare,
  and why that mode was necessary and reasonable may matter.
- Receipts, fare records, route records, and medical material are possible
  proof. A taxi receipt is neither the only permitted proof nor automatically
  sufficient.
- Do not frame taxi use, home/hospital address disclosure, or one proof method
  as a universal mandatory formula.
