## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): N/A for guidance pack (out of scope), 3/5 for columns
variety used: Standard Bulgarian, consistent. 010 and 013 keep first-person case narration; the reader is addressed with Вие. No dialect mixture.
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- Amounts use a dot as the thousands separator, against the Bulgarian space, and against the decimal comma already used correctly in 012 (`0,5 метра`): `"TWD 1.579.589"`.
- A gloss or a list item is attached to the wrong legal object: `"крайният продукт"` (011, the advertisement), `"под друго наименование"` (013, a pretext), `"делът при съвместно име"` (016).
- 013 and 015 still read as a blog, with a blank line after almost every clause: `"Наред с преценката на пазарната обстановка"` then `"друг съществен критерий остава"`.
- Contact lines in 013 and 015 invite a Bulgarian reader to write without naming the four consultation languages. 018 names them: `"английски, китайски (中文), японски или корейски"`.
- Attorney Wei Tseng is feminine wherever she speaks or is named (013 *съгласна съм*; 011, 014, 016, 017 *Адвокатка*). 010 still uses a masculine title for her own role. 012’s *той* is the motorcycle. 015 and 018 have no gendered byline.
- 012, 014 and 018 are close to professional Bulgarian. 014’s labour terms (*новопостъпили*, *предварително платена облага*, *неустойка*, *волеизявление*) now read as Bulgarian law, not as calques.
