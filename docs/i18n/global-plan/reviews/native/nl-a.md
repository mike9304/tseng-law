# Dutch native review — part a (nl)
reviewer: Grok 4.6 · date: 2026-09-21 · scope: nl-guidance.txt (365 strings); columns-nl/001–009 (establishment basics, capital withdrawal, traffic accidents, subsidiary vs branch, establishment advanced-2, massage law, divorce Q&A, labor severance, voluntary resignation)

## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): 2/5 for guidance pack, 2/5 for columns
variety used: Standaardnederlands intended for NL/BE; consistently Flemish/Belgian legal diction (vennoten, dading, van bij het begin, eenvormig) plus German calques; leftover English UI labels and Korean URLs/paths — not a second Dutch variety mixed in, but one German→Dutch pass
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- German reflexive calque “beoordeelt/beslist/laat zich” instead of a Dutch passive: “De werkelijke toepassing beoordeelt zich volgens ongevallensituatie”
- 股東 of a 有限公司/股份有限公司 rendered as “vennoten” (partnership members in NL law) instead of “aandeelhouders”
- Korean reader not generalized where English already did, plus leftover `/ko/` and Hangul slugs: “Zelfs wanneer een Koreaanse moedervennootschap Taiwan betreedt”
- Stock German-legal glue in every paragraph (evenwel, alsmede, teneinde, wezenlijk, terstond): readable, never how a Dutch firm writes
- French/German guillemets «…» on a Dutch site: “legt de pagina «Contact» uit”
- Register split: guidance is stiff Kanzlei-Dutch; columns 003 Q16–Q20 and 005–009 keep a Korean blog voice (“Ik was WEI”, “Goedendag, ik ben Wei Tseng”)
Gender: no masculine form, pronoun or title for Wei Tseng. Dutch *advocaat* is epicene; bios correctly use *zij*, *Uitwisselingsstudente*, *zij werkt*. Consultation-language lock and the negative no-Dutch / no-interpreter / no-free-first-meeting lines are intact (wording only). Columns 001–002 and 004 are dense but legally careful; 006 is the most natural stretch of prose.
