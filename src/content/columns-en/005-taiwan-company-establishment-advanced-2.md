# Taiwan Company Formation — Advanced Part 2

I hope the Q&A below will also help those considering company formation in Taiwan.



**1. What should I watch for when remitting capital from Korea to a Taiwan company preparatory account?**

In general, Korean banks require the investor **in person** to visit a Korean bank and remit funds from their own account.

Internet banking or remittance by a relative or acquaintance in Korea as a proxy is not allowed.

In addition, under Korea’s foreign exchange control laws, a person holding Korean nationality who establishes a foreign legal entity or acquires shares must file an “overseas direct investment report.” The report must be filed at the time capital is remitted for the Taiwan company; failure to report may result in sanctions for violating foreign exchange control law.

Please consult your main bank in Korea before remitting capital.



**2. When depositing company capital, can I transfer New Taiwan dollars from my own Taiwan account to the Taiwan company preparatory account?**

Yes, but you must submit documents proving the source of the New Taiwan dollar funds obtained in Taiwan.

For example, if the funds are salary income earned in Taiwan, you must submit a copy of the withholding certificate for salary income (薪資所得的扣繳憑單影本).

If the funds are dividends and profits from a Taiwan business investment, you must submit a copy of the withholding certificate for dividends and profits (股息和紅利的扣繳憑單影本).

If you remit from a Korean bank account, you do not need to attach source-of-funds documents.



**3. After capital is deposited into the company preparatory account, when can it be converted to a formal account?**

In general, after receiving the company registration documents,

the responsible person can go to the bank and convert the company preparatory account into a formal account.

However, each bank’s internal rules differ, so if you urgently need to use the capital, it is advisable to inquire with the bank first.



**4. After the company’s preparatory account is converted to a formal account, can I use internet banking right away?**

This varies by bank, but generally at least a mobile phone number is required.

Some banks may have additional requirements, such as the account having been in use for at least six months after establishment.



**5. Can the company hire Koreans as employees?**

A. No restriction for the first employee: managerial work for a general overseas Chinese / foreign investment enterprise (一般僑外投資事業主管工作)

B. Restrictions from the second employee onward: when there is more than one person, the employee’s relevant education and experience, average wage requirements for employment, and the company’s capital and revenue requirements are handled under the rules for “specialized or technical work” (專門性或技術性工作).

See the Taiwan Ministry of Labor site: <https://ezworktaiwan.wda.gov.tw/cp.aspx?n=A88DC323EF7C85FF>
