## Claim-to-source readback

### Article 02 — PASS

- Lines 16 and 37 preserve all three Trade Secrets Act article 2 elements. Labels, NDAs, data possession or payment are not treated as automatically sufficient. Lines 41 and 43 correctly distinguish article 4 commissioned-R&D ownership from the funder's business-use entitlement, and do not automatically extend that rule to subscriptions or declare model parameters protected. [MOEA Trade Secrets Act](https://law.moea.gov.tw/LawContent.aspx?id=FL011321).
- Line 49 retains the unlawful-benefit/harm intent and specified-act elements of 13-1, the overseas-use intent and predicate offense requirement of 13-2, and the separate possibility of civil liability. Accidental transmission and overseas service location are not equated with a completed crime. The [current history](https://law.moea.gov.tw/LawContentSource.aspx?id=FL011321) remains 2020-01-15; a fresh [TIPO 2026-10-02 case explanation](https://www.tipo.gov.tw/tw/tipo1/424-96505.html) also uses the same 13-1 structure.
- Line 51 now expressly separates National Security Act 3(1)'s action for the enumerated foreign/regional/controlled actors from 3(2)'s intent to use abroad. Both concern an announced core technology **and** a trade secret under Trade Secrets Act article 2, with specified acts. This is more precise than the original's general “境外關聯.” [National Security Act article 3](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawAll.aspx?pcode=A0030028).
- Lines 20–45 and 53 distinguish recommended contract/access/retention/acceptance arrangements from uniform statutory clauses. No fixed deletion deadline or guaranteed legal safe harbor is invented.

### Article 04 — PASS

This fits Trade Act 13 and SHTC regulation 15, including the latter's conditional power to waive certificate issuance for specified US/Japan exports. Lines 28, 49 and 57 fit articles 16, 19 and 21: required application/use documents, compliance with approved contents and timely change procedure, and five-year document retention. [Current MOEA regulation](https://law.moea.gov.tw/LawContent.aspx?id=FL011375).
- Lines 20, 24 and 32 accurately match the [ITA 2026-08-14 workflow](https://www.trade.gov.tw/Pages/Detail.aspx?nodeid=5146&pid=819076): specification and component/software/technical-document review, separate Russia/Belarus list where relevant, and entity/end-use checks even for off-list goods. - Lines 30–51 expressly label proposed handoffs, delay/refund/cost provisions and operational controls as arrangements to negotiate; payment, warranties and indemnity do not authorize an otherwise unlawful shipment.
- Line 55 accurately and conditionally summarizes EAR jurisdiction over US-located, US-origin and specified foreign-produced items, with exclusions. “Made in Taiwan” alone does not decide jurisdiction, and any US technology does not automatically control every output. [EAR 734.3](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/subtitle-B/chapter-VII/subchapter-C/part-734/section-734.3).

### Article 07 — PASS

- Lines 14, 22–26: 10-1 covers qualifying new own-use investment and an approved investment plan; 2025–2029 window; NT$1 million–2 billion eligible-spending band; 5% in delivery year **or total 3% over three years**; each-year 30% liability cap; combined 50% general cap with the stated last-year exception. The choice deadline matches regulation 9. [Act 10-1](https://law.moea.gov.tw/LawContent.aspx?id=FL054333).
- Lines 32–41 correctly avoid universal software/SaaS eligibility. Regulation 8 treats paid software licenses recognized as intangible assets as purchased software; regulation 9 governs amount, excludes government subsidy and restricts acquisition-cost components. Regulation 17 requires Taiwan deployment at the specified premises, with its domestic location exception. The article does not invent an across-the-board SaaS ban. Lines 49 and 61 correctly distinguish proof, online plan application and income-tax filing. [10-1 regulation articles 5, 7–9 and 12–17](https://law.moea.gov.tw/LawContent.aspx?id=GL000865).
- Lines 45–47: old orders retain their NT$1 billion sub-cap inside the combined NT$2 billion cap; two years from the day after ordering for delivery/service completion; one timely extension of up to two years; software-license starting date and service-completion date are separately used. These fit the [MOF 2026-03-30 explanation](https://www.mof.gov.tw/singlehtml/384fb3077bb349ea973e7fc6f13b6974?cntId=2a26f7a6395645e4807e5a921a4f930f) and regulation 8. The elapsed transitional six-month extension window is not incorrectly promoted as currently available.
- Lines 53–57: 10-2 rates are 25% for qualifying forward-looking R&D and 5% for qualifying advanced-process equipment, each with 30% liability cap and combined 50% general cap. Eligibility includes Taiwan innovation and key international supply-chain position, annual R&D of at least NT$6 billion **and** 6% of net revenue, applicable 2026 effective-tax-rate threshold of 15%, serious-violation restriction and an additional NT$10 billion of qualifying new own-use equipment spending for the equipment route. [10-2 regulation article 3](https://law.moea.gov.tw/LawContent.aspx?id=GL001467). The two approval-year exclusions now expressly cover all equipment or all R&D spending respectively, not merely the same invoice. [Act 10-2(3)–(4)](https://law.moea.gov.tw/LawContent.aspx?id=FL054333).
