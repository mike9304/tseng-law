## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): n/a for guidance pack (part a), 3/5 for columns reviewed so far
variety used: Khalkha Mongolian in Cyrillic, as written for readers in Mongolia; consistent so far. Not mixed with Traditional Mongolian script, Buryat, or Kalmyk. Formal register is kept. Chinese glosses stay in Traditional characters. Provisional until 013–018 are in.

systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- English "alone" is still "нэгээр": "Албан ёсны эхний шатны шийдвэр нэгээр хоёрдугаар шатны үр дүн"
- Money stays code-first with an English comma: "TWD 1,579,589", "TWD 40,000–200,000"
- "даатгах" is used for entrusting work, which a reader hears as insurance: "импорт, борлуулалтыг даатгах арга"
- Wei Tseng is labeled "Эмэгтэй өмгөөлөгч"; the noun needs no gender marker, and the form is not masculine
- "дагалдах" still calques "follow": "автоматаар дагалдах арга хэмжээ биш"

No F findings in 010–012. "Би эмэгтэй өмгөөлөгчийн хувьд" and the byline "Эмэгтэй өмгөөлөгч Wei Tseng" mark sex where Mongolian does not. Verbs ("оролцсон") do not mark gender. No consultation in Mongolian, no interpreter, no reply-time, no free meeting, and no win-rate. The no-guarantee lines in 011 and 012 stay negative. "хүүхдийн хамгийн сайн ашиг" is not in this slice.
