reviewer: Grok 4.6 · date: 2026-09-21 · scope: ms-guidance.txt; columns-ms/001–009 (001-taiwan-company-establishment-basics.md, 002-withdraw-capital-taiwan-company.md, 003-taiwan-traffic-accident-procedure.md, 004-taiwan-company-subsidiary-vs-branch.md, 005-taiwan-company-establishment-advanced-2.md, 006-taiwan-massage-history-law.md, 007-taiwan-divorce-lawsuit-qna.md, 008-taiwan-labor-severance-law.md, 009-taiwan-voluntary-resignation-severance.md)

## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): 3/5 for guidance pack, 2/5 for columns
variety used: Standard Malay for Malaysia (ms_MY / DBP) intended and mostly kept (peguam, syarikat, boleh); number format and some particles follow Indonesian/European convention (500.000, 0,5, ayat 1); French legal diction in 007 (status sivil, proses verbal); not a second Malay variety mixed in, but one German/French→Malay pass
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- 股東 of a 有限公司/股份有限公司 rendered as “rakan kongsi” (partners in a perkongsian) instead of “pemegang saham”: “dibahagikan antara rakan kongsi (股東)”
- Korean reader left as the default where English already generalised: “Walaupun syarikat induk Korea masuk ke Taiwan”
- German glue copied throughout: menemani (begleiten), kumpulan kerja (Tätigkeitsgruppen), hal (Sache): “Kami menemani klien dari luar negara”
- Indonesian/European numerals on a Malaysia site: “TWD 500.000”, “0,5 bulan”, “1 kali”, “peringkat 2”
- French/German guillemets and état-civil diction: “«pengisytiharan pelaburan langsung ke luar negara»”, “pejabat status sivil”
- English UI leftovers and a non-word “pliding”: “OUR TEAM”, “peguam pliding umum”

Gender: Malay does not inflect; “saya”, “beliau” and “peguam” are epicene. No masculine pronoun, title or verb for Wei Tseng. Bios use “Mewakili” / “beliau bekerja”; “Encik” appears only for Encik Lin (006) and Encik B (008), both male third parties. Consultation lock is intact in the FAQ and llms notice (Tidak. … hanya … Inggeris, Cina, Jepun dan Korea; no interpreter, no reply-time, no free first meeting, no result promise). Wording of those negatives can still be tightened. Guidance pack is stiff but usable; 001–002 and 004 are dense but legally careful once 股東 is fixed; 007 is the most French-legal stretch; 003 and 008 fail an advertising read; 005 is still a Korea remittance article; 006 is the most natural prose except “Hakim Agung”.
