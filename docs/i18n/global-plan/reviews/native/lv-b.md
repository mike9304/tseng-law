## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): n/a for guidance pack (part a), 3/5 for columns
variety used: standard Latvian, consistent; German thousand-separators; *mēraukla* declined as a masculine noun throughout
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- FAQ answer pasted under a topic heading, so the opening has no antecedent: "Tās nav tās pašas tiesības."
- Real word *mēraukla* (feminine) declined as masculine *mērauks*: "profesionālā vai tehniskā mērauka"
- German thousands and a locative date stamp: "TWD 1.579.589" / "13. septembrī 2025"
- Stock calques a Latvia lawyer would not stack: "atlīdzības esība", "atlīdzības pozīcijas", "nodokļu uzbūve"
- 條例 called *statūti* (bylaws) and a filing route called *logs*: "Ārzemnieku ieguldījumu statūta" / "tam pašam logam"
- Taipei stays "Taibei"; 013 and 015 are chopped into one-line paragraphs: "Taibei pilsēta" / "man kā advokātei teica,"

Guidance pack is out of scope. Wei Tseng is consistently "procesuālā pārstāve", "advokāte", "advokātei" — no masculine title or pronoun for her. Consultation-language lines in 013, 015 and 018 stay correctly negative (English, Chinese, Japanese, Korean only). "negarantē" and "nesola noteiktu iznākumu" deny a result; they are not guarantees. "Bez maksas" in 015 is the municipal address lookup, not a free meeting. "tā labāk der" compares two company forms. No win-rate or "best firm" claim. 012 is the most readable file (native traffic terms, decimal comma in "0,5 metru"). 014, 016 and 017 are legally serious but several openings and terms will mislead a client. 013 and 015 still read as a translated blog.
