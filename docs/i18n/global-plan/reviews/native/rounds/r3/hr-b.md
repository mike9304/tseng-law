## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): N/A for guidance pack (out of scope; part a), 4/5 for columns
variety used: standard Croatian (ijekavian) as used in Croatia, consistent. Not Serbian or Bosnian (uvijek, uvjet, mjesec, točnije, prijevoz; no tačno/uslov/uvek). Dates are day. month. year or numeric (13. rujna 2025., 24. siječnja 2022., 4. veljače 2026., 1. srpnja 2026., 5. 6. 2026., 1. 1. 2023., 25. 6. 2026., 17. rujna 2026.). The decimal comma is already right (0,5 metara). A dot as the thousands separator is accepted Croatian orthography (1.579.589 TWD). Quotation marks are Croatian „…“.
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- Universal "svaka … nije" reads as "not every": "Svaka ovlast nije u osobnom interesu roditelja".
- The contract names a recipient or a bare handover where English names which records or closing items: "urediti primatelja predaje".
- 013 and 015 still contain U+200B, and 013/015 still break one point across paragraphs: "Daljnja je važna točka ova:".
- A conclusion or a right is written as the grammatical agent: "Stvarni zaključak mora zajedno ocijeniti svrhu isplate".
- "odnosno" or a bare genitive collapses two activities or a sector: "špedicija odnosno otprema (運送承攬)".
- "nakon" treats a step as already done inside a period that is only long enough for it: "nakon pronalaženja adrese društva i sklapanja ugovora o najmu".
010 is professional apart from one agreement error and one calque. 012 is professional apart from two phrases. 015 is professional apart from U+200B and the zoning split; its 1. 1. 2023 paragraph is a full sentence. 018 is professional apart from the sector genitive and the distributor/agent swap. 011, 013, 014, 016 and 017 are readable legal Croatian with the local defects below.
