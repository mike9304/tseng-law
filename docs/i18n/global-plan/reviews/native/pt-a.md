## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): 3/5 for guidance pack, 2/5 for columns
variety used: European Portuguese (pt_PT) as the consistent target — Contacto, correio eletrónico, sítio, « », a contar de, perceber um salário; guidance pack is readable EP after the G2-1 Spanish-calque sweep; columns 001–004 and 007 are French legal prose in Portuguese clothes; 003 Q16–Q20, 005, 006, 008–009 keep a Korean-blog register. A Brazilian client can follow the guidance pack; they will stumble on EP-only words (sítio, correio) and, in the columns, on “regalias” and “conservatório”.
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- French legal calque in the columns: `"prisão a tempo"`, `"cocontratante"`, `"em contrapartida"` for “by contrast”, `"apelo público à poupança"`, `"O só facto"`, `"de forma de conjunto"`.
- German calque in the guidance pack: `"Grupos de atividade"`, `"para a procura local"`, `"os grupos que realmente tratamos"`, `"num mesmo fluxo"`.
- Korea-specific tax, banking, family and labour facts left as if they applied to a reader in Portugal or Brazil; English caveat `"It is not a worldwide investor rule"` dropped in 001 (also 004, 005, 007, 008, 009).
- Language leftovers: English eyebrows `"OUR TEAM"` / `"OFFICES"` / `"(English)"`; Hangul slugs in 005, 008, 009.
- Blog leftovers: `"deixe-as nos comentários"`, `"pode contactar-nos a qualquer momento"`, empty lines, first-person chat in 003 Q16–Q20 / 005 / 008.
- Taiwan 戶政 rendered as `"conservatório do estado civil"` throughout 007 — in Portuguese a conservatório is a music school; the office is a conservatória (PT) or cartório (BR).
Gender of attorney Wei Tseng: feminine throughout this part (`advogada`, `advogada diretora`, `Aluna de intercâmbio`, `habilitada`, `sou Wei Tseng (曾雋崴), advogada`, `Revisto pela advogada Wei Tseng`). No masculine form, pronoun or title found. The guidance pack’s `"regime de visitas (會面交往)"` is clearer than column 007’s `"relações pessoais (會面交往)"`.
