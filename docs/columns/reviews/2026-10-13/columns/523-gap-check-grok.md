## FACTS

MUST VERIFY 1: CONFIRMED — https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=C0010001&flno=376 — Para. 1 bars a third-instance appeal after a second-instance judgment for the nine listed categories, with a proviso: if the second instance sets aside a first-instance judgment of acquittal (無罪), exemption from prosecution (免訴), non-acceptance (不受理) or lack of jurisdiction (管轄錯誤) and enters a conviction, the defendant or a person who may appeal for the defendant’s benefit may appeal. Para. 2: where that proviso appeal is taken and the third instance sets the judgment aside and remands to the original court (發回) or transfers the case to another second-instance court (發交), the later judgment cannot be appealed to the third instance. The drafts do not track this text. See items 1–3.

MUST VERIFY 2: CONFIRMED — https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=C0010001&flno=361 and https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=C0010001&flno=382 — Art. 361 para. 3: if the notice does not state the reasons, the appellant must file a reasons brief with the original court within twenty days after the appeal period ends; if that deadline is missed, the original court must first set a period and order correction. Art. 382 para. 1: a third-instance notice must state the reasons; if it does not, a reasons brief may be filed with the original court within twenty days after the appeal is filed; if it is not filed, the court need not order supplementation (未補提者，毋庸命其補提). The drafts state the two clocks correctly and drop the “no order to supplement” half of Art. 382. See item 6.
