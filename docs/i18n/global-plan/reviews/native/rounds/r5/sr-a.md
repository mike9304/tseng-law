naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): 4/5 for guidance pack, 3/5 for columns
variety used: standard ekavian Serbian in Latin script, consistent; formal *Vi* throughout. No ijekavian, no Croatian lexicon (no *tisuća*, *povijest*, *tjedan*, *odvjetnik*, *suci*). Most of r4's rows were applied; the ones below are what is still wrong. The statutory/analytical sections of 001, 002, 004 and 007 read as real Serbian legal prose; the three narrative texts (003 Q16–Q20, 006, 008) are clearly still translated and carry most of the remaining damage.
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- Comparatives still take *nego* + nominative instead of *od* + genitive, 5 places incl. one newly introduced at 005: "postoji kraće nego jedna godina"
- *kao* is used for *kao što su* in 14 list appositions across 5 files: "Dalje etape, kao uvoz i izvoz"
- German source typography survives: the closing quote is “ (U+201C), not Serbian ” — guidance ×10 plus 004, 005, 008, 009: "„široko nebo“"
- German/English verb collocations are calqued rather than translated: "Pratimo strane investitore i preduzeća pri osnivanju"
- Bureaucratic *lice/lica* is used for ordinary people even in the story columns, where Serbian says *ljudi*: "Danas mnoga lica biraju masažu"
- Small numbers stay as digits in running prose where Serbian spells them out: "ako se bez opravdanog razloga ne pojavi 3 dana uzastopno"
