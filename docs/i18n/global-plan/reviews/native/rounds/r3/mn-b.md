naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): N/A for guidance pack (out of scope; part a), 4/5 for columns
variety used: Khalkha Mongolian in Cyrillic, consistent. No Buryat, Kalmyk, or traditional-script Mongolian. Dates already follow the Mongolia order year.month.day (2022.01.24, 2023.01.01, 2026.02.04, 2026.06.05, 2026.06.25, 2026.07.01, 2026.09.17).
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- Inclusive statutory thresholds (以上, "at least") are written as exclusive "X-аас дээш", so the exact boundary drops out: "3 сараас дээш 1 жил хүрээгүй бол 10 хоног" (014).
- A count is swapped for a nearby role: "менежерээс өөр гадаадын иргэнд" (018), where English is the second and every further foreign national.
- U+200B remains after a sentence and as blank lines: "Тайбэй хот). ​" (013); lines that contain only U+200B also sit in 013 and 015.
- An active participle reverses who is affected: "нөлөөлөх PIF-ийн материалыг" (011).
- «Ерөнхий» still attaches to the wrong noun: "ерөнхий компани байгуулалтаас" (017), for ordinary company formation.
- Thousands use a comma, and the decimal uses a period: "TWD 1,579,589" (010); "0.5 метр" (012).
