## OVERBROAD (low severity, no action by reader depends on it)

### O1. L37 and L39 present ITA "to date" totals as post-update totals
- Column (L37): "The 601 additions … bringing the total to 10,844 entities and individuals."
- Column (L39): "…the ITA added another 213 entities from Russia, Iraq, Iran, Mexico, mainland China and Yemen, bringing the total above 11,200."
- Primary text:
  - ITA 2025-06-15: 「相關更新作業主要參考聯合國安全理事會及友盟國家的制裁與管制名單，迄今已有10,844個實體與個人列入該名單。國際貿易署近日召開跨部會實體清單審查會議…新增…601個實體」 https://www.trade.gov.tw/Pages/Detail.aspx?nodeID=40&pid=802558
  - udn 2025-12-19: 「截至目前，列入我國出口實體管理名單的實體與個人已超過11,200個。國貿署近日召開最新一輪跨部會出口實體清單審查會議…決議新增213個受管制實體」 https://udn.com/news/story/7238/9214600
  - ITA 2026-04-01: 「迄今已有逾11,414個實體與個人列入該名單」 (then +67, -8) https://www.trade.gov.tw/Pages/Detail.aspx?nodeID=40&pid=813420
  - MOEA/ITA 2026-06-09: 「迄今已有逾11,480個實體與個人列入該名單」 (then +265, -13) https://www.moea.gov.tw/MNS/populace/news/News.aspx?kind=1&menu_id=40&news_id=122960 (page opened; dated 2026-06-09 17:00)
- What is off: the releases say only "to date" and do not say whether the total is before or after the update announced in the same release. Arithmetic across releases suggests the 2025-12 and 2026 figures are pre-update counts: 11,200 + 213 = about 11,413, which equals the April figure 11,414; 11,414 + 67 - 8 = 11,473, which is about the June figure 11,480. So "bringing the total above 11,200" probably states a post-update number the source does not give. For 10,844 (June 2025) the chain does not close (unknown updates between June and December 2025), so that one is only "not stated", not shown wrong. For reference, the live ITA dataset downloaded 2026-10-06 (https://www.trade.gov.tw/OpenData/getOpenData.aspx?oid=0F2CD336A579151B) has 11,664 data rows, which cannot settle it because rows are not the same unit as "entities and individuals". The June 2026 sentence in the column ("with more than 11,480 entities and individuals on the list") is neutral and fine.
- Suggested minimal edits:
  - L37: "…and included Chinese-funded companies such as Huawei and SMIC. The ITA's release said 10,844 entities and individuals had been listed to date."
  - L39: "…added another 213 entities from Russia, Iraq, Iran, Mexico, mainland China and Yemen; the report put the number of listed entities and individuals at more than 11,200."
