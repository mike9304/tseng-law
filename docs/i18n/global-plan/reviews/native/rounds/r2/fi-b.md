variety used: Standard Finnish (yleiskieli), consistent. Quotation marks are already ” ”. Dates are day–month–year (24. tammikuuta 2022, 5. kesäkuuta 2026, 17. syyskuuta 2026). The decimal comma is already right (0,5 metrin). Thousands still use a period.
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- The currency code stands before the amount in 011, 017 and 018, and thousands use a period: "TWD 25.000.000". 010 already puts the amount first, still with periods.
- Kinmen and Lienchiang (Matsu) are joined with *ja*/*tai*: "Kinmenissä (金門) ja Lienchiangissa tai Matsussa (連江、馬祖)".
- Three headings still name the wrong object: "Lääkekulut" (010, medical expenses), "lomakkeen hallussapito" (018, a legal or contractual form), "jarrutusvaraa" (012, time to brake).
- 013 and 015 still publish one clause per paragraph, and several breaks are only U+200B: "toinen tärkeä kohta on" then "sijaitseeko kyseinen osoite".
- A rule is still hedged into a purpose, and a calculation step does not parse: "tarkoitus on, ettei sen kustannusta voida muuttaa"; "voi tulla kyseeseen ensin heijastaa sen tulos".
- Coined legal nouns remain: "erillinen oikeussubjektius" (018; 011 has "oikeussubjektiivisuudessa"); "Ei-varallisuusoikeudellinen vahinko".
Attorney Wei Tseng is unnamed by gender wherever she appears (010 *Olin … oikeudenkäyntiasiamies*, the statutory name of the role; 011, 014, 016 and 017 *Asianajaja Wei Tseng*).
