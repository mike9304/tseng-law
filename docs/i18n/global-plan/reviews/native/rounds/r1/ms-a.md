## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): 4/5 for guidance pack, 3/5 for columns
variety used: Bahasa Melayu Malaysia (baku), consistent. “Sarjana” is the Malaysian master’s degree. “Pelikuidasi”, “perlembagaan syarikat”, “bukan pemastautin”, “plaintif”, and “minit bacaan” are Malaysian, not Indonesian. One Indonesian criminal-procedure word remains in the guidance pack (“terdakwa”). Thousand separators (TWD 500,000) and running dates (27 Disember 2023) match Malaysian usage, except ISO dates in 003.
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- English office names still sit inside Malay bio sentences: "Sebelum ini di Ministry of Education, Legal Affairs Division, dengan tumpuan pada hal pentadbiran dan sivil."
- Taiwan institutions and civil-law terms are calqued into the wrong Malaysian word: "pejabat kekal menurut perjanjian (常設機構, PE)"; "syarikat pengeluar (發行公司)"; "Hakim-hakim Besar Mahkamah Perlembagaan (大法官)"
- A space is left before the percent sign: "kadar maksimum 10 %"
- Q16–Q20 of 003, and the short posts 005, 006, 008, and 009, still pad paragraphs with zero-width blank lines: "Saya harap ia berguna kepada anda."
- “Parallel / flow” and range constructions are translated word for word: "mencari jalan lain di tempat anda secara selari"
- A bachelor’s degree is labelled “Ijazah”, not “Ijazah Sarjana Muda”: "Ijazah (B.A.) dengan pengajian berganda undang-undang dan kewangan"
Gender of attorney Wei Tseng: Malay does not inflect. “Peguam”, “beliau”, “Saya”, “Saya Wei Tseng (曾雋崴), peguam di Taiwan”, and “Wei Tseng (曾雋崴), peguam di Taiwan” are epicene. No masculine pronoun for her. “Encik Lin” (006) and “Encik B” (008) are male third parties. Consultation lock in the guidance pack stays negative. 001, 002, 004, and 007 are native professional legal Malay apart from the term errors below, including the Korea-treaty caveat and the written dates 27 Disember 2023 and 25 Julai 2026. Decimals such as 0.5 bulan match Malaysian usage. 003 Q1–Q15 is the same register except the tort, limitation, and Judicial Yuan wording. 003 Q16–Q20, 005, 006, 008, and 009 are still personal blogs; 008 and 009 keep Korean URL slugs, which must stay. The “generally three months” line in 003 and the “most cases” line in 009 have already been narrowed and disclaimed.
