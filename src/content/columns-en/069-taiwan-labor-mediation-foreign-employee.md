# Labor-management mediation in Taiwan for foreign employees

The [Act for Settlement of Labor-Management Disputes](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawAll.aspx?pcode=N0020007) lets a municipal or county authority mediate rights or adjustment disputes. [Article 11](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=N0020007&flno=11) allows appointment of a mediator or a mediation committee, including entrusted private groups. A successful mediation produces a mediation record with defined effects; failure leaves arbitration or litigation as next options.

Foreign professionals and Gold Card holders covered by the Labor Standards Act may use the same channel. Arrange interpretation if needed.

Unpaid wages, overtime, notice pay, severance and dismissal challenges are common topics. See [Labor severance](/en/columns/taiwan-labor-severance-law). Track residence timing apart from the mediation calendar.
