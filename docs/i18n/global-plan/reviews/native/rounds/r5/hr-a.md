## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): 4/5 for guidance pack, 3/5 for columns
variety used: standard Croatian (hrvatski standard, ijekavica), formal Vi, consistent register. Croatian lexis dominates (odvjetnik, uvjet, tisuća, utjecaj, sudjelovanje, tvrtka/društvo, prometna nesreća, rabiti). Serbian-leaning forms survive only in 002 and 004 (prijem, zloupotrebljavati, putevi). Dates are Croatian (`13. rujna 2025.`, `27. 12. 2023.`, `1. 1. 2024.`, `25. 7. 2026.`, `29. 5. 2026.`), thousands take the dot (`500.000 TWD`, `2.000.000.000 TWD`), the decimal comma is right (`0,5 mjeseca`), percentages take a space (`10 %`, `21 %`). Two number exceptions remain: `TWD 1.57M` in the lead-attorney bio, and small counts kept as bare digits all through the columns.
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- English "transaction" is rendered "operacija" (in Croatian: surgery or a military operation) in 001, 002, 004 and 007: "Kod operacija između povezanih osoba (關係人)"
- Impersonal "se" is built on an accusative object, which is non-standard in Croatian: "Mjesne partnere može se primiti kao dioničare"
- 本公司, the foreign head office, is "glavna poslovnica" (= main branch); 004 uses it 23 times: "nego dio glavne poslovnice, pa ta snosi dugove"
- Small numbers stay as digits where Croatian spells them out, even opening a heading: "## 1. 3 načina razvoda na Tajvanu"
- The closing quote is the German „…“ instead of the Croatian „…”; all 10 guidance strings and 005, 008, 009, 004: "Mjerilo „rada stručne ili tehničke naravi“"
- U+200B blank lines still chop 003 (from Q16), 005, 006, 008 and 009 into one-line fragments, and Q16–Q20 lost their heading marks: "Q16. Možete li nakon nesreće sve prepustiti osiguravatelju?"

Gender of attorney Wei Tseng: no finding. Every mention is feminine — *Odvjetnica Wei Tseng*, *Vodeća odvjetnica*, *ovlaštena je*, *vodeća je odvjetnica*, *Zastupala je*, *Pregledala odvjetnica*, *željela bih*. Generic *odvjetnik* appears only in the inclusive pair *odvjetnica ili odvjetnik*, in 003 Q20 for an unnamed lawyer, and for the male colleague Chang Rongxuan. The consultation lock is intact everywhere: only the four languages, no interpreter, no reply-time promise, no free first consultation, no promised outcome; the negatives read naturally. No advertising-rule breach: *najbolji interes djeteta* is the statutory test, *radnik s najboljim rezultatom* is the client's sales record, and *jednoliko najbolji* / *nadređeni* appear only inside negations. Chinese glosses are Traditional throughout and no Korean, English or German string is left in the running text.

What is genuinely good: the guidance pack is client-ready apart from the rows below — every page reads as Croatian written by a lawyer, the three-languages distinction, the privacy page and the disclaimer are natural and precise. Round 4's fixes hold: *pripremni račun*, *javnobilježnička ovjera ili legalizacija*, *društvo u koje se ulaže*, *porez na dobit poslovnih subjekata*, *računovodstveni tretman*, *vršenje prava i obveza prema maloljetnoj djeci*, *uzročna veza*, *Vanjske poveznice*, *za svaku navršenu godinu staža* and the 007 constitutional-test sentence are all correct now. 001 and 003 Q1–Q15 are native professional legal Croatian apart from the rows below. What remains is concentrated in four places: the transaction/head-office vocabulary of 001–004, the non-standard "se" + accusative pattern, the still-unrepaired layout of 003 Q16→, 005, 006, 008 and 009, and the sentence-level garbles in 008 and 009.
