## Source and wording review

All four complete `050-taiwan-accident-police-records.md` articles were read. Article prose, links, deadlines and publication settings are unchanged. The captions preserve these distinctions:

- Injury/death triggers the described driver duties; taking additional photographs remains conditional on safety.
- Seven and thirty days are measured after the accident and are request times, not promised delivery times. [NPA FAQ](https://www.npa.gov.tw/ch/app/faq/view?id=2144&module=faq&serno=A1084129)
- National online eligibility requires both police attendance at the scene and injury/death. Property-only cases and authentication problems need a different inquiry route. Third-party collection requires authorization. [NPA application notice](https://tm2.npa.gov.tw/NM105-505ClientRWD2/TM01A01Q_01.jsp)
- A litigation agent's settlement authority requires special authorization; a criminal complainant may still be required to appear despite appointing an agent. [Civil Procedure Article 70](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=B0010001&flno=70), [Criminal Procedure Article 236-1](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=C0010001&flno=236-1)
- Waiting for police records does not replace tracking complaint/claim deadlines.
