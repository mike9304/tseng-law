# Taiwan company responsible persons: duties to the company and joint liability to others

[Company Act Article 23, paragraph 1](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=J0080001&flno=23) requires responsible persons to act with loyalty and the care of a good administrator, and to compensate the company for harm caused by breach. Paragraph 2 adds joint liability with the company when the responsible person, in conducting business, violates law and harms another.

Directors appointed by a foreign parent to a Taiwan subsidiary remain inside these duties. Dissolution and liquidation issues are covered in [Company dissolution and liquidation](/en/columns/taiwan-company-dissolution-liquidation).
