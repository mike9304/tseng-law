## Verdict

variety used: standard Croatian (ijekavica), consistent across all nine columns; not mixed with Serbian or Slovenian. Quotes in 013–018 are Croatian „…“. Thousand separators use a period, the decimal comma appears in „0,5 metara“, and dates such as „13. rujna 2025.“ and „5. 6. 2026.“ are already right for Croatia
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):

- „Jedinstveno“ for across the board or categorically („ne smije jedinstveno isključiti sva unutarnja obuka“ in 014; also 017).
- „Statut“ for 條例, while 018 already uses statut for the articles („Statuta o ulaganjima stranih osoba (外國人投資條例)“ in 013; also 017).
- „Prestati s ugovorom“ for terminating a contract („Kad radnik prestane s ugovorom o radu“ in 014).
- An FAQ answer opens the section with no question („To nije isto pravo.“ in 016; also „Ne može je slobodno rabiti.“).
- 013 and 015 keep one-sentence blog lines („Osim tržišne situacije“ then „daljnja je važna točka“). The four-language lock at the end of both is correctly negative.

012 is the piece a reader in Croatia could publish after light polish. 011, 014, 016, 017 and 018 are dense and often legally careful, and they still read as translation. 010 is a usable case note with a few real word errors. 013 and 015 would look like a pasted blog, though the consultation sentence is right.
