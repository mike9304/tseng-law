# A 2021 crash, a 2025 suit by Taiwan's compensation fund: "we settled" and "it's too late" both failed

The motorcycle that hit a bicycle in Pingtung in 2021 had no Taiwanese compulsory insurance. About four years later, on October 23, 2025, the Taoyuan District Court's summary division ordered its rider to pay the Special Compensation Fund NT$1,419,327 plus interest ([Taoyuan District Court civil judgment 114 Tao-Yuan-Jian No. 60](https://www.mvacf.org.tw/ckfinder/connector?command=Proxy&type=Attach&currentFolder=/tblResearches/&fileName=%E8%87%BA%E7%81%A3%E6%A1%83%E5%9C%92%E5%9C%B0%E6%96%B9%E6%B3%95%E9%99%A2114%E5%B9%B4%E6%A1%83%E5%8E%9F%E7%B0%A1%E5%AD%97%E7%AC%AC60%E8%99%9F%E6%B0%91%E4%BA%8B%E5%88%A4%E6%B1%BA.pdf&c=7&p=169230b5914145568476202fbc11ee07&l=3)). It is a first-instance judgment under the summary procedure, declared provisionally enforceable by the court. An appeal could be filed within 20 days of service; whether one was cannot be confirmed from the public copy.

## A bicycle ahead

Shortly before 8 a.m. on September 1, 2021, at an intersection in Pingtung County, the defendant's motorcycle struck a bicycle travelling straight ahead of it. The court found the defendant had failed to watch the road ahead ([Road Traffic Safety Rules, Article 94(3)](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=K0040013&flno=94)). The cyclist suffered a serious head injury with lasting disability.

With no compulsory automobile liability insurance on the motorcycle, the cyclist claimed from the Special Compensation Fund ([Compulsory Automobile Liability Insurance Act, Article 40(1)(ii)](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=G0390060&flno=40)), which paid NT$1,419,327 in two instalments, on August 30, 2023 and March 21, 2024. The breakdown the court accepted was medical costs plus a grade-three disability benefit.

## Two defences

Two arguments came from the defendant. First, a private settlement had been reached with the cyclist on September 11, 2021, ten days after the crash. Second, it was unreasonable for the Fund to make its claim only three years after the crash.

On the first, the court noted that no proof of a settlement had been produced. In this case the settlement remained an assertion.

As for the delay, the defendant used the wrong starting point. [Article 42(3)](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=G0390060&flno=42) says the Fund's subrogated claim lapses if not exercised within two years from the day it paid compensation. What counts is the payment date. Here the payments were in 2023 and 2024, and the court found the 2025 lawsuit was within two years of each payment.

## A claim that moves on payment

Next, the court explained what kind of right the Fund holds. The Fund exists to give basic protection to victims who cannot obtain compulsory insurance benefits ([Article 38](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=G0390060&flno=38)), and in the court's view works like liability insurance, so the insurance rule against double recovery applies. Once the Fund pays, the victim's damages claim against the wrongdoer passes to the Fund by law, up to the amount paid, without any assignment by the victim ([Article 42(2)](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=G0390060&flno=42)). The defendant's liability rested on [Article 184(1), first sentence, and (2) of the Civil Code](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=B0000001&flno=184) and [Article 191-2](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=B0000001&flno=191-2).

Would a proven settlement have changed the result? The judgment did not reach that question. The statute does address it. Under [Article 43(1)](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=G0390060&flno=43), a settlement, waiver or other agreement by the claimant that obstructs the Fund's subrogation does not bind the Fund unless the Fund consented. Article 43(2) adds that compensation the claimant has already received from the liable party must be deducted when the Fund pays, and the Fund may recover any amount it should have deducted but did not.

## What a rider of an uninsured vehicle should keep

Anyone who settles with an injured person after a crash is better off with a written record of the amount, the date and what the payment covers. It also helps to know whether the injured person later applied to the Fund. The Fund's claim can arrive years after the crash itself.

The judgment runs interest at 5 percent a year from July 31, 2025, the day after the complaint was served.
