## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): n/a for guidance pack (part a), 3/5 for columns
variety used: standard Lithuanian (bendrinė), consistent. 012 uses a decimal comma (“0,5 metro”); money still uses a German thousands dot. 015 spells Taipei “Taipėjus”; 013 spells it “Taijaus”.
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- ROC case years read as ordinary year-counts: "109 metų vartotojų bylos Nr. 7"
- “matas” stands in for a legal standard: "pagal profesionalų ar techninį matą"
- German thousands dots on TWD amounts: "TWD 1.579.589"
- Private-employment “service” is calqued as “tarnyba” (civil service): "minimalaus tarnybos laikotarpio sąlyga"
- 013 and 015 keep a Korean blog, one clause per line, plus a leftover “Q.”: "**Q. Ar visos veiklos sritys per sistemą"
- A claim or a result “susiklosto”, which is how a situation unfolds: "atsakomybė nesusiklosto vien dėl to"

Guidance pack is out of scope. Wei Tseng is feminine wherever she is named (“proceso atstovė”, “advokatės vardu”, “Advokatė Wei Tseng”, “atsakingos advokatės”); 013 says “su advokate arba advokatu”. No masculine title or pronoun for her in this part. 013, 015 and 018 keep consultation inside English, Chinese, Japanese and Korean, and they do not promise a reply time or a result. “Geriausi vaiko interesai” in 016 is the statutory term (子女最佳利益), not a firm “best” claim. 經濟部投資審議司 is consistently “Investicijų peržiūros departamentas”. 012, 014, 016 and 017 are real legal Lithuanian; 013 and 015 are still a line-broken blog. U+200B zero-width spaces sit on otherwise blank lines in 013 and 015.
