## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): N/A for guidance pack (out of scope; part a), 4/5 for columns
variety used: Standard written Estonian (kirjakeel), consistent. Dates are day.month year with the month in the nominative (13. september 2025, 4. veebruar 2026, 5. juuni 2026, 25. juuni 2026, 17. september 2026, 24. jaanuar 2022). "Alates 1. jaanuarist 2023" in 015 is the right elative; "Alates 1. juuli 2026" in 011 is not. The decimal comma is right (0,5 meetri) and "100 %" has the required space. Thousands still use a period.
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- Thousands still use a period: "TWD 1.579.589".
- Closing disclaimers calque "may vary with" as "võib erineda … järgi": "Tegelik vastutus võib erineda õnnetuskoha, sõiduki liikumise".
- 013 and 015 still hide U+200B, and several lines are only that character: "piisab piirkonna märkimisest (näide: Taipei linn)."
- 012 parks Chinese glosses on the wrong noun: "muid säilitatud tõendeid (鑑定)".
- "omama" and "omadus" still stand in for obtaining a permit or for a legal role: "on omadus kaubaomanikuna või veo vahendajana".
- "Pelk asjaolu, et" is stiff but still legal Estonian; it is not listed below.

012 is native professional apart from "erineda … järgi", "uusim õigus" for the regulations, the heading "Kohad", and glosses sitting on the wrong word. 015 is native apart from the invisible characters. 018 is native professional apart from the thousand separator and "meelsasti". 010, 011, 013, 014, 016 and 017 are professional with local defects. Attorney Wei Tseng is "Advokaat Wei Tseng (曾雋崴)" in 011, 014, 016 and 017; the title is unmarked for gender. 010 "Esindasin" and 013 "ütles mulle" / "soovitan" are first person and unmarked. No masculine pronoun or title for her remains. "Linnavalitsus (市政府)" is the ordinary Estonian name for a city government; do not replace it. "Tööhõiveteenuste seadus (就業服務法)" is the employment-service statute, not a job-placement act. The consultation lock holds: 013 and 018 name English, Chinese (中文), Japanese and Korean, and both say no immediate reply is promised. 015 "Võite tasuta uurida" is the Taipei pre-inquiry, not a free meeting with the firm. No Estonian consult, no interpreter, no reply-time promise, no free first meeting, no result guarantee, no win rate, no "best/only". "Lapse parimad huvid" is the best-interests test. "Ainus aktsionär" is a sole shareholder. "Ainult tütarettevõte läbib … kontrolli" is the subsidiary-versus-branch distinction, and the verb is idiomatic. The section jump from 5 to 7 in 018 matches the English source. Kinmen is "või" Lienchiang (Matsu). The Q3 review in 013 correctly says the review is not overly strict ("ei ole ülemäära range").
