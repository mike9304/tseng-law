## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): n/a for guidance pack (part a), 3/5 for columns
variety used: Standard Hindi in Devanagari, consistent; French «guillemets» and CJK punctuation mixed in; English chips (Q&A, सिविल कोड, ओवरटेकिंग, kg) remain
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- Chinese/Korean numeral+noun calque: "1 सुनवाई", "2 शर्तें", "3 पक्ष", "हॉर्न 2 बार".
- French guillemets copied from the European source: «रसद», «भोजनालय», «दंड राशि», «सजावट अवधि».
- Western money grouping (1,579,589 / 25,000,000) instead of Indian लाख/crore grouping.
- First-person masculine for attorney Wei Tseng (woman): "मुकदमा प्रतिनिधि (訴訟代理人) था" (010).
- Officialese calques a client would not say: रसद for logistics, पंचांग for a training schedule, साझेदार for company shareholders.
- Blog leftovers on a law-firm page: 😊, "उदा.)", fullwidth "सलाह：", "टिप्पणी करें".

011, 014, 016 and 018 are the densest, most usable legal Hindi in this set. 010 fails a native gender check and still says "hearing 1/2" for 一審/二審. 013 and 015 still read as a Korean/Chinese blog (line breaks, emoji, CJK colon). 017 is structurally sound until the title word रसद, which an India reader will take as rations, not logistics. Disclaimers that a column is not legal advice / not a promised result are correctly negative where present. No win-rate or "best/only" advertising found. Hindi consultation is implied only by the 013/015 contact lines, not by an explicit Hindi-consult promise.
