## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): 4/5 for guidance pack, 3/5 for columns
variety used: Standard Nepali (Nepal), Devanagari, consistent — ङ्/अङ्ग्रेजी orthography, not Hindi अंग्रेजी. Running dates are Nepali (13 सेप्टेम्बर 2025, 25 जुलाई 2026, 27 डिसेम्बर 2023). One Simplified gloss in 005 (台灣法人) and one Hindi spelling in 004 (डेटा).
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- मिसिनु calques English “miss” where the source means “confused”: "यी प्रायः मिसिन्छन्"
- सङ्केत stands in for an instruction, a procedure, or transaction details: "कारोबार सङ्केत"
- Amounts above one lakh still use Western grouping, and one bio uses an English million: "TWD 1.57M"
- संस्था is an organization, but it is also used for a legal rule or a statutory calculation: "घोषणा विषयबाट बाहिर छन्"
- 營業稅 is called बिक्री कर, and trademark is the Indian व्यापार चिह्न: "ताइवानी बिक्री कर (營業稅)"
- 003 Q16–Q20, 005, 006, 008, and 009 are still a personal blog, with U+200B blank lines: "मैले आफैं हेरेकी एउटा मुद्दा बताउँछु।"
