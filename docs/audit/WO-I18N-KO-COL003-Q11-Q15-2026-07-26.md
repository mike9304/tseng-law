## Legal and copy contract

### Q11 — Temporary lost income

- Explain that a temporary lost-income claim requires proof that the
  accident-related injury made work wholly or partly impossible and actually
  reduced income during a supported recovery period.
- A diagnosis or recommended rest period is relevant but not alone conclusive.
- Mention useful evidence: diagnosis/treatment records, attendance or leave
  records, payroll and tax material, employer confirmation, and records
  appropriate for a self-employed claimant.
- Continuing to work or receiving unchanged pay is relevant to whether a
  temporary loss occurred, but it does not by itself decide Q12's separate
  permanent earning-capacity issue.
- Do not promise recovery merely because a diagnosis states rest, and do not
  state that a judge automatically denies the claim whenever a person worked.

### Q12 — Permanent reduction of earning capacity

- Use the consistent term `노동능력 감소 손해`; explain that it is separate
  from Q11's actually lost pay during a recovery period.
- Under Civil Code Articles 193 and 216, address accident causation, lasting
  functional impairment, the claimant's work and ability, ordinarily expected
  income, supported working-life assumptions, and proof.
- State that unchanged current pay does not automatically eliminate a
  permanent earning-capacity claim, but neither an impairment percentage nor
  current salary mechanically fixes the award.
- A medical appraisal may be useful evidence when lasting impairment is
  genuinely disputed; it is not mandatory in every case and no universal
  appraisal-fee amount may be stated.
- Do not use a fixed retirement age, salary, impairment percentage, monthly
  payment, or lump-sum example.
- Civil Code Article 217 comparative negligence and other adjustments remain
  relevant.
- If damages are paid as a lump sum, intermediate-interest discounting may be
  considered. The Judicial Yuan's Hoffman calculator is a calculation aid,
  not a legally mandatory method or a guarantee. Article 193 also permits a
  court, on a party's application, to order periodic payments with security.

### Q13 — Non-pecuniary damage

- Use `비재산적 손해에 대한 위자료` consistently.
- Explain that Civil Code Article 195 allows an appropriate amount for
  qualifying unlawful infringement of body or health.
- State the kinds of individualized circumstances a court may consider:
  injury and treatment, lasting effects, pain and life impact, age/status,
  social and economic circumstances, and the parties' evidence.
- Do not publish a “usual” range, predict that a claimant will receive less
  than expected, or discourage a particular amount without a case review.

### Q14 — Employer civil liability

- Civil Code Article 188 applies when an employee unlawfully injures another
  while performing duties; work hours alone do not automatically establish
  the required connection.
- Explain potential joint civil liability of employer and employee, the
  employer's statutory reasonable-selection/supervision or unavoidable-loss
  defense, the court's possible relief to an otherwise uncompensated victim
  under paragraph 2, and the employer's recourse against the employee after
  payment.
- Use civil terms such as `피고로 함께 청구` or `공동으로 손해배상을 청구`,
  not criminal `회사를 함께 고소`.
- Separate civil party selection from criminal liability. Criminal Code
  Article 284 liability is assessed for each natural person's own breach of
  duty and causation; do not state that the driver is categorically the only
  possible criminal actor in every factual pattern.
- Do not recommend adding an employer merely because it may have more assets.

### Q15 — Compulsory and voluntary motor insurance

- State that, under the Compulsory Automobile Liability Insurance Act Article
  6, the duty ordinarily belongs to the owner of a covered vehicle and in
  specified cases to its user or manager. Do not say “everyone” must buy it.
- Explain the basic no-fault statutory benefit function for a person injured or
  killed in a motor-vehicle accident, while distinguishing the Act's passenger
  or outside-third-person definition.
- Explain carefully:
  - a driver in a single-vehicle accident is generally outside that vehicle's
    compulsory cover;
  - in a multi-vehicle accident, a driver may be a claimant against another
    involved vehicle's compulsory insurer.
- For accidents on or after `2026-07-01`, state these current statutory limits:
  - necessary and reasonable injury medical expenses:
    `신대만달러 TWD 200,000`;
  - disability benefit by the 15 statutory grades:
    `TWD 80,000–3,000,000`;
  - death benefit:
    `TWD 3,000,000`;
  - combined death/disability/medical maximum per victim per accident:
    `TWD 3,200,000`.
- Do not describe disappearance as a separate current benefit category.
- State that the benefit standard amended on 2026-05-29 applies from
  2026-07-01; earlier accidents may use the earlier standard.
- Describe third-party liability, driver injury, and own-damage insurance only
  as contractual/voluntary products whose availability, insured person, limit,
  deductible, exclusions, fault and other conditions depend on the actual
  policy.
- Do not say third-party liability insurance automatically pays every amount
  remaining above compulsory insurance.
