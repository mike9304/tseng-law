## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): n/a for guidance pack (part a: 3/5), 2/5 for columns
variety used: Simplified characters throughout; the register is Taiwanese Mandarin (台湾华语) written in简体 — not mixed with another Sinitic language. Proper-name Traditional glosses that actually use Traditional (017 營業執照 is the wrong term, not a script mix) are rare; most parentheses wrongly repeat Simplified. 013 still has English “Q&A” plus 😊; 017 uses a Japanese middle dot in 连江・马祖. No Hangul and no /ko/ “韩语律师” CTAs in this batch (R1 already applied). No masculine form for 曾雋崴 (Chinese 律师 / 我 / 诉讼代理人 are unmarked; the male gym plaintiff in 010 is correctly male).
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- Parentheses repeat the same Simplified word instead of the Taiwan Traditional term: "劳动能力减损损害（劳动能力减损）"
- Default verb is 检视 (Korean 검토 / German prüfen); mainland legal prose uses 审查、核对、了解: "须一并检视使用者的运动经验"
- Digit ordinals copied from JP/KR: "第1审案件", also 第2审 / 第3人 / 第2类 / 第1顺位 / 第2名
- Amounts left as TWD 25,000,000（新台币） instead of mainland 新台币2500万元
- Corner quotes 「」 (Taiwan/Japanese) instead of mainland “ ”: "「场所预先查询」"
- 013 and 015 drop into Korean-blog first person, missing periods, 😊, and “leave a comment / contact at any time”

010 is a usable case note once 新课 and 姿势 are fixed; 011, 014, 016, 017 and 018 are dense but competent Taiwan legal Chinese — they fail as mainland-reader copy, not as meaning. 012 is the most native short piece except the 转弯/平交道 line. 013 and 015 would embarrass the firm in front of a mainland client.
