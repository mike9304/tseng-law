# Your spouse traded before the earnings came out. Can both of you be charged?

The spouse who traded can be charged when the elements of the ban are met. So can you, if the trade was yours, in your name or your spouse's. Marriage alone does not put your spouse on the list. Passing the news, with no trade of your own, is not the banned purchase or sale. If you were a covered source, you can share liability for damages to same-day investors on the other side of the trade. Criminal Code rules on joint principals and accessories can apply too, depending on knowledge and intent. [Article 157-1 of the Securities and Exchange Act](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=G0400001&flno=157-1) (證券交易法) requires actual knowledge (實際知悉) of precise (明確) material news (重大影響其股票價格之消息), then a purchase or a sale.

Once the news is precise, the ban runs until publication (公開) and through the eighteen hours that follow. Home conversation and office rumor are not publication.

## Who is covered when the account is the spouse's

As of November 19, 2026, the ban covers the company's shares and other equity securities that are listed or traded over the counter through securities firms. The five groups must not buy or sell, in their own name or someone else's (自行或以他人名義), once they actually know the news and the window is open.

Directors (董事), supervisors (監察人), managerial officers (經理人), and natural persons designated under Company Act (公司法) Article 27, paragraph 1, are the first. Shareholders over 10 percent are the second. An occupation (職業) or a control relationship (控制關係) is the third path. Loss of one of those three statuses less than six months earlier is the fourth. News heard from someone in the first four is the fifth.

Employees who are not directors, supervisors, or managerial officers, including at a subsidiary, are in group three when the job is how they learned the issuer's news. No managerial-officer titles are listed. Someone who counts as a managerial officer is in group one, so the spouse-share rule below applies. It does not reach group three. A friend who heard the news only from your spouse is outside group five unless that spouse is in the first four groups.

Does the account name decide the trade? It does not. Placing the order in your spouse's name makes it yours. After hearing the news, your spouse's own order makes your spouse the group-five trader and you the provider, if you were in the first four groups.

For groups one and two, the holding includes a spouse's shares, a minor child's shares, and shares held through another person's name. [Article 22-2, paragraph 3](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=G0400001&flno=22-2), is that rule. The last paragraph of Article 157-1 applies it to those groups and to people who lost group-one or group-two status less than six months earlier. Group three is outside it. So is someone who only lost group-three status. Officers and large holders have a separate six-month rule in [the six-month column](/en/columns/taiwan-short-swing-profit-six-months-disgorgement-officer).

Paragraph 2 bans only a sale of listed or counter-traded non-equity bonds, on precise news materially affecting the ability to pay principal and interest.

## When the news comes into being, and when it is public

Material news, under paragraph 5 of Article 157-1, may concern the company's finances or business, market supply and demand for that security, or a tender offer. The specific content (具體內容) has to materially affect the stock price, or importantly affect a legitimate investor's decision (正當投資人). Scope and the manner of publication are left to rules the competent authority adopts (主管機關).

Quarterly earnings are not an item under that name in [Article 2](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=G0400129&flno=2) of those rules. Item 11 is a material change in operating or pre-tax profit or loss versus the year-ago period, or versus the prior period when the cause is not seasonal.

A material gap between a published financial forecast and the actual figures is item 10. An updated or corrected forecast that materially differs from the original forecast is the same item.

Article 2 does not name a large customer order in its own words. Item 1 takes in whatever Article 7 of the Act's Enforcement Rules lists. Other finance or business news with that effect on price or on a legitimate investor's decision is item 18.

Item 9 covers missed Article 36 filings, errors or omissions Article 6 of the Enforcement Rules requires corrected and restated, audit or review opinions other than unqualified or modified unqualified, and going-concern doubt. A qualified opinion is excepted for a loss the law lets the company amortize over years. First-quarter, third-quarter, or half-year reports are the other exception when they use unaudited or unreviewed investee figures for a long-term equity investment and its gain or loss.

Coming into being (成立時點) for news under Articles 2 to 4 is set by [Article 5](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=G0400129&flno=5) of the rules: the earliest of the day the fact occurred, an agreement, a signing, a payment, an entrustment, a trade, a share transfer, an audit-committee or board resolution, or another day concrete evidence can pin down. Whether that news was precise is Article 157-1's own test. Neither text dates quarterly profit.

Finance and business news, and bond-payment news under [Article 4](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=G0400129&flno=4), are public under [Article 6](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=G0400129&flno=6) once the company enters them on the Market Observation Post System (公開資訊觀測站).

Supply-and-demand news under [Article 3](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=G0400129&flno=3) may use that same entry, a basic-market announcement (臺灣證券交易所股份有限公司基本市況報導網站 or 財團法人中華民國證券櫃檯買賣中心基本市況報導網站), or two or more national dailies' non-local pages, national television, or their e-papers. Only on that press route do the eighteen hours run from the latest of delivery, first broadcast, or e-paper posting. Morning papers count from 6 a.m. Evening papers count from 3 p.m. Investor meetings are covered in [a related note](/en/columns/issues/taiwan-investor-meeting-material-information-trading-ban).

## Damages, prison, and paying the proceeds back

Violating paragraph 1 or paragraph 2 brings damages liability to a same-day good-faith trader on the other side. The sum is the gap between that price and the average closing price over the ten business days after publication. Courts may raise the award to three times if the facts are serious and that trader asks, and may reduce it if the facts are minor. A request is required for the increase, not for a reduction.

Paragraph 4 makes the group-five person jointly and severally liable (連帶賠償責任) with the person in the first four groups who provided the news. That paragraph does not state the provider's own trade as a condition. A justified belief that the news was already public ends the provider's liability. The group-five trader is not given that excuse.

As of November 19, 2026, [Article 171](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=G0400001&flno=171) sets the prison term. Paragraph 1, item 1, covers a violation of either trading ban. The term runs from three years to ten. A fine from NT$10 million to NT$200 million may be added.

When property, or a property benefit, obtained through the crime reaches NT$100 million or more, paragraph 2 applies. Imprisonment then starts at seven years. Any added fine runs from NT$25 million to NT$500 million.

Paragraphs 4 and 5 apply to this offense. Surrender (自首) plus voluntary payment of all criminal proceeds reduces or remits the punishment under paragraph 4, and remits it if other principals or accomplices are found because of that surrender. A confession during the investigation (偵查中自白) plus the same full payment reduces the punishment under paragraph 5. If the others are found because of that confession, the reduction can reach one half.

Proceeds of a paragraph 1 or paragraph 2 offense that exceed the maximum fine let the court, under paragraph 6, raise the fine up to those proceeds. Harm to securities-market stability increases the punishment by as much as one half.

Proceeds of an offense under the first three paragraphs are confiscated under paragraph 7 when they belong to the offender. Confiscation also reaches proceeds another natural person, juridical person, or unincorporated group obtained in a situation listed in Criminal Code Article 38-1, paragraph 2. Sums that must go back to a victim, a third party, or a person entitled to damages are left out.

Carrying out a criminal act together makes every person a principal under [Article 28 of the Criminal Code](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=C0000001&flno=28). Helping makes an accessory under [Article 30](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=C0000001&flno=30) even if the person helped did not know about the help, and the punishment may be reduced. Neither article decides a home tip in advance.

## After the company or a prosecutor calls

A prosecutor who learns of the suspicion, by complaint, report, surrender, or otherwise, must investigate at once under [Article 228 of the Code of Criminal Procedure](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=C0010001&flno=228) (刑事訴訟法). The prosecutor may set a deadline and direct a prosecutor's investigator (檢察事務官), a judicial police officer (司法警察官), or judicial police (司法警察) to investigate and report. Summoning the defendant first is forbidden unless it is necessary.

A summons under [Article 71](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=C0010001&flno=71) states the case, time, and place and warns of compulsory appearance (拘提). [Article 75](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=C0010001&flno=75) allows that step after a lawful summons and an unjustified absence.
