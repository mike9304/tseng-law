## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): 3/5 for guidance pack, 2/5 for columns
variety used: standard Czech (spisovná čeština), internally mixed: guidance writes taiwanský while columns write tchajwanský; French guillemets « » instead of Czech „ “; columns 005–009 keep a Korean blog voice
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- Korean original not recast for a Czechia reader: Korea as default investor/spouse/employee, plus `/ko/` and Hangul URLs (`"Na rozdíl od Koreje na Tchaj-wanu povinnost vyplatit odstupné"`).
- Heavy German/English calques in legal Czech (`"doprovázíme klienty ze zahraničí"`, `"skupiny věcí"`, `"jazyky porady"`).
- English leftover labels and titles (`"OUR TEAM"`, `"Q&A k postupu"`).
- Number/date format not Czech: period thousands, `1.57M`, ISO dates (`"TWD 500.000"`, `"změněný 2026-05-29"`).
- Formal guidance vs first-person blog in 003 Q16–20, 006, 008, 009 (`"(Neprozrazuji tu mimoděk svůj věk?)"`).
- Wei Tseng is consistently feminine (`advokátka`, `Zastupovala`, `Posoudila`) — no F finding.

Guidance pack is usable legal Czech (Vy-capitalisation, negative consultation lock, no free-consult slip) but still reads translated. Columns 001–004 are dense and accurate on many Taiwan terms with Chinese glosses; 005–009 are visibly leftover Korean posts. 001–004 are the stronger set; 006 and 008 would embarrass a Prague reader.
