# Taiwan accident police records: what to collect before leaving

The cover is a hypothetical illustration, not a forensic reconstruction.

After a traffic accident in Taiwan, prioritize care and safety, then organize records for follow-up. Drivers involved in injury or death accidents must take rescue measures, notify police and preserve scene evidence. [Road Traffic Management and Penalty Act, Article 62](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=K0040012&flno=62)

If safe, photograph vehicle positions, damage, lane markings and signals. Preserve original dashcam files. Record the handling officer's contact and case details; arrange medical records and receipts by date. These are practical preparation steps, not an exhaustive list of legal requirements.

## When can you request police records?

The participant registration sheet can be requested at the scene; scene diagrams and photographs after seven days; and the preliminary analysis sheet after thirty days. These are application times, not guaranteed delivery dates. [National Police Agency FAQ](https://www.npa.gov.tw/ch/app/faq/view?id=2144&module=faq&serno=A1084129)

The NPA's national portal requires police attendance and injury or death. For property-only cases or authentication difficulties, contact the handling police unit. A non-party collecting records needs authorization. [Application portal](https://tm2.npa.gov.tw/NM105-505ClientRWD2/TM01A01Q_01.jsp)

## Before leaving Taiwan

Confirm how documents and notices will reach you. Authority to collect records differs from litigation authority. Certain civil acts, including settlement, require special authority; a criminal complainant with an agent may still be required to attend personally. [Civil guidance](https://www.judicial.gov.tw/tw/cp-1690-81113-995d9-1.html), [complainant representation](https://www.judicial.gov.tw/tw/cp-1654-2459-bd892-1.html)

Check any scheduled attendance before departure. For complaint and civil-claim deadlines, see [accident Q&A Q3](/en/columns/taiwan-traffic-accident-procedure#sec-3); waiting for police documents is a separate issue.

For a consultation, bring your account, police records, original footage, medical evidence, insurer correspondence and any proposed settlement. Requirements and responsibility depend on the case.
