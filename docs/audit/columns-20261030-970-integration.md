- seoTitle: Taiwan Insurance Act Art. 121 and Civil Code Art. 1145: When a Beneficiary or Heir Is Suspected in the Insured's Death → Suspected Beneficiary: Taiwan Life Insurance
- summary: In Taiwan, a beneficiary who intentionally causes the insured's death loses the right to the life insurance money, and if no beneficiary is left the money becomes part of the insured's estate (Insurance Act art. 121). Whether that person also loses the right to inherit turns on Civil Code art. 1145, and readings differ on whether the death limb requires a criminal sentence. While the criminal case runs, the two-year limitation period for insurance claims keeps running (Insurance Act art. 65); a written demand interrupts it only if a lawsuit follows within six months (Civil Code art. 130). → In Taiwan, a beneficiary who intentionally causes the insured's death loses the insurance money, and the two-year claim period runs during the criminal case.
