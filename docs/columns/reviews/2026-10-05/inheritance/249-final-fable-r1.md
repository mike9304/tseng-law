## 1. Law and facts (all four languages)

Every statement below was checked against the source text; the four versions agree on the law and differ only in the hypothetical family and emphasis.

- §58 main rule and proviso: quoted accurately in all four. Each version says only that the proviso makes a Taiwan Civil Code calculation necessary for Taiwan assets, that the statute does not say how the Korea-side heir's share is adjusted, and that the result is fact-specific. No version says which share prevails. Matches the topic brief's "do not overstate".
- 국제사법 §77 ① and ②: text verified on law.go.kr; the column's description of ② (express choice in the form of a will; habitual-residence law at the time of choice, effective only if habitual residence kept until death; or situs law for immovables) is exact.
- 민법 §1003 ①, §1009 ②, §1000 ①: verified. Arithmetic: spouse + 2 children = 1.5 : 1 : 1 → 3/7, 2/7, 2/7 (ko, ja, en); spouse + both parents = 3/7, 2/7, 2/7 (ja contrast case, zh-hant). Correct.
- 民法 §1144 (1) equal shares with first-order heirs → 1/3 each; §1144 (2) one half with parents (ja, zh-hant). Correct. Comparisons (child 1/3 > 2/7; spouse 3/7 > 1/3; spouse with parents 1/2 > 3/7) are right.
- §2 dual nationality (closest connection), §6 renvoi (quoted as the statute reads, conclusion hedged as "may/될 수 있습니다/可能性/可能"), §5 for US citizens (en only, general caution, "question for a US lawyer"): correct.
- 遺產及贈與稅法 §1 II (non-nationals taxed only on Taiwan-situs estate, no residence condition), §9 I (6), (8), (1) situs rules, §10 I/III valuation (ko, ja, zh-hant; en omits): correct.
- §18 II exemption equal; §17 II: items 1–7 excluded, items 8–11 only if incurred in Taiwan: stated precisely in all four, in the table, body and FAQ. en's extra sentence that the minor-child add-on is also lost is right (§17 I (2) is within items 1–7).
- 2026 figures: exemption 1,333萬; spouse 553萬; descendants 56萬 each; parents 138萬 each; funeral 138萬; bracket 5,621萬 / 10%, then 15%, 20%. All match the MOF 2026 announcement in statutes.md and carry "2026년 사망 기준 / 2026年に亡くなった場合 / deaths in 2026 / 115年（2026年）".
- §6 taxpayers, §23 I (six months from the day after death), §23 II (non-national → seat of central government), Q&A 3108 item 4 (臺北國稅局總局或所屬分局、稽徵所), §26 (written application before the deadline, 3 months in principle), §44 (fine up to 2× assessed tax): correct.
