# Taiwan Company Formation: Choosing a Business Location and Taipei's Prior Inquiry

![Choosing a business location in Taipei](../images/015-taiwan-company-setup-pitch-location/featured-01.jpg)

The location of a company or shop cannot be decided by rent, transportation, and customer base alone. Before signing a contract, you need to confirm whether the planned business items can be carried out at that address, on that floor, and with that building use. Using a Taipei company or business registration and a restaurant as one example, this article explains the current "business-location prior inquiry" (營業場所預先查詢).

![](../images/015-taiwan-company-setup-pitch-location/img-01.jpg)

A restaurant is a clear example because land-use, building, fire, health, and food-related checks overlap, but Taipei's prior inquiry is not limited to the food-service industry. In other counties and cities, the system's name, application method, required attachments, and review process may differ. Industry-specific permits are governed separately, so confirm the latest guidance from the local government with jurisdiction over the location and from the competent authority for each industry.

## 1. In Taipei, do only certain industries need a business-location prior inquiry?

No. Under Taipei City's current guidance, from January 1, 2023, an applicant seeking to establish a company or business (including a branch or branch establishment), change its registered location, or add business items must first inquire whether the business location and business items comply with land-use zoning and building-management regulations, and must attach a compliant result to the registration application. Do not determine whether an inquiry is required solely from the former list of industries subject to proactive inquiry.

Taipei City now comprehensively reviews the business location and business items before accepting a company or business registration. Under the current system, applicants generally use the [Taipei City business-location prior inquiry portal](https://www.businesslocationinfo.gov.taipei/BLBQS/Home/Notice), obtain a compliant result for the planned site and business items, and attach that result to the relevant registration application.

The current procedure is generally prepared in the following order.

1. Identify the exact address, floor, and scope to be used and the planned business items.
2. Obtain the current building registration materials and other required documents.
3. Apply for the business-location prior inquiry and undergo review of the combination of location and business items.
4. Attach a compliant inquiry result to the registration application to establish a company or business, change its registered location, or add business items.
5. Separately complete industry-specific permits, fire and health preparations, interior work, and any other requirements for starting operations.

According to Taipei City's current guidance, if no inquiry result is attached to the registration application, the authority does not automatically decide the application based on that omission. Instead, it sends a guidance letter asking the applicant to supplement the result, and the registration process continues after supplementation. If the result finds some business items noncompliant, consider the appropriate response based on that result, such as changing the business location or removing a noncompliant item from the registration application.

A single inquiry application may include up to five business items, and Taipei City's current FAQ directs applicants to select their principal business items for review. Distinguish this guidance from the case-linked proactive inquiry (隨案主動查詢) under point 5 of the operating directions described below.

The [Taipei City Business Location Assisted Inquiry Service Operating Directions](https://laws.gov.taipei/Law/LawSearch/LawArticleContent/FL080687) also retain a case-linked proactive inquiry (隨案主動查詢) process for certain business items, including restaurants and other food-service items. Under this supplementary mechanism, if an item listed in point 5 appears in the registration application but is not covered by the compliant result already submitted, the Taipei City Department of Commerce initiates an inquiry in connection with the registration case. The point-5 list does not revive the former view that only listed industries require a prior inquiry.

For the background of the system change and the covered registration events, see also Taipei City's [FAQ on the comprehensive prior review of business locations](https://www.gov.taipei/News_Content.aspx?n=EEC70A4186D4C828&s=E70ACC80BEEC5910&sms=87415A8B9CE81B16).

## 2. What building documents are required for a business-location prior inquiry?

Under Taipei City's current system, the applicant generally submits a building registration transcript (including a Type II transcript) issued within the last three months, or the building ownership certificate, so that the address, floor, area, and similar details can be verified. Where the building use is classified as 'primarily residential with ancillary office use,' for example, additional materials such as a declaration and land-use zoning certification may be required, so confirm the current required documents for the specific property and use.

First, check not only the address written in the lease or advertisements but also the formal address, building, floor, area, and use recorded in the building registration materials. Even within the same building, a different floor or scope of use can affect the determination, so an impression that "the building as a whole seems usable" is not enough. Prepare the planned business items using the official names that will appear in the registration.

A building registration transcript can be obtained at Taipei City land-office counters and convenience workstations, through the government's electronic transcript system, and through other current official channels. The available method may differ depending on the applicant's eligibility, the type of transcript needed, and the method of online identity verification. There is no across-the-board requirement to use a particular acquaintance or lawyer; first confirm the conditions at an official counter or through an official online service.

For a building classified as "primarily residential with ancillary office use," additional requirements may include a declaration concerning the actual-use proportions — residential use exceeding three-fifths of the whole and office use below two-fifths — and materials such as land-use zoning certification. The actual use, not just the wording on paper, must be consistent with the declaration. Because the required materials vary with the property's classification, use, area, and application details, follow the portal's latest notices.

Document supplementation will be required, affecting the planned schedule, if the materials are outdated, the address or floor does not match, the drawings or area cannot be verified, or required materials are missing. Obtaining the documents and confirming the combination of the candidate property and business items before finalizing the lease can reduce the cost and disruption if a change of location becomes necessary.

## 3. Can a leased address, borrowed registration address, or virtual office be used as a company address?

Whether an address may be used cannot be decided categorically from the name of the arrangement alone. The applicant should show authority to use the address through a lease agreement, the owner's consent together with ownership documentation, and similar materials; ensure that official notices can actually be received; and then confirm Taipei's business-location prior inquiry and the regulations governing actual use. If the registered address differs from the actual place of business, the actual site must separately satisfy land-use, building, fire, health, and other requirements.

The Ministry of Economic Affairs' guidance for limited-company establishment registration lists a copy of the lease agreement, or the owner's consent to use the property together with proof of ownership, as documents showing authority to use the location. Confirm the required combination in the [MOEA document checklist for limited-company establishment registration](https://gcis.nat.gov.tw/F/t70044_p) and the current guidance from the receiving authority.

These materials principally show authority to use the address as the company's location. The owner's consent or the existence of a lease alone does not confirm that the site complies with land-use zoning, building, fire, or health rules for every business item. Conversely, it is also inappropriate to decide that an arrangement can never be used solely because it is described as a "borrowed registration address" or "virtual office."

Before signing, check at least the following points.

- Whether the contracting party or consenting person has authority to allow use of the property.
- Whether the address, floor, unit, and scope of use to be used in the registration match the building materials.
- Whether administrative documents and other official notices addressed to the company can be reliably received and handled as needed.
- Whether a compliant prior-inquiry result can be obtained for the planned business items.
- If business is actually conducted at a place separate from the registered address, whether the checks required for that actual site have been performed.

For example, if the registered address serves as an administrative contact point while food is prepared and sold at a separate kitchen or shop, the shop's land use, building, fire, health, food-business registration, and industry-specific permits become independent issues. Assembling the materials for the registered address alone does not satisfy the requirements of a separate actual place of business.

## 4. If the prior-inquiry result is compliant, can the business operate there immediately?

No. The prior inquiry confirms whether the business location and business items comply with land-use zoning and building-management regulations. It does not approve the lessor's authority or lease terms, or satisfy fire-safety, health, environmental, signage, food-business registration, industry-specific permitting, or other requirements. Confirm which additional procedures must be completed between company or business registration and the start of operations.

A compliant inquiry result is important, but its scope is limited. It confirms that the identified location and the business items submitted for review comply with land-use zoning and building-management regulations. It does not validate the lease or approve the lessor's authority, equipment work, fire-safety equipment, smoke exhaust and drainage, waste, noise, signage installation, or qualifications under individual industry laws.

For a restaurant, company or business registration is only one step. The operator must also confirm the fire and building preparations appropriate to the premises' actual use, food sanitation management, food-business registration, requirements for personnel and equipment, and permits applicable to the operating model. For the scope and procedure of food-business registration, refer to the latest guidance in effect when operations begin, including the Taiwan Food and Drug Administration's [announcement on food-business registration](https://www.fda.gov.tw/tc/newsContent.aspx?id=11672).

Also, if the address, floor used, scope of use, business items, or the state of the building changes after the inquiry, the result already obtained may not be usable as is. Keep the application contents consistent with actual use, and if a change occurs, confirm with Taipei City and the relevant competent authorities whether a new inquiry or other procedures are required.

The prior inquiry, company or business registration, tax registration, industry-specific permits, and interior, fire, and health preparations each have a different purpose and review scope. Refer also to our [Taiwan investment and company formation services](/en/services#investment) and [Taiwan company formation, advanced guide 1](/en/columns/taiwan-company-establishment-advanced-1), and organize the steps from signing the property lease to opening as separate stages.

## 5. How long does the prior inquiry take, and how long is the result valid?

Taipei City's administrative processing standards set targets of five calendar days for ordinary cases and eleven calendar days for cases requiring consultation with external agencies. These targets do not guarantee a completion date because requests for additional documents, application volume, and other circumstances may affect the timing. Under the current portal, an inquiry result is valid for six months from the review completion date, and a new application is required once that period has passed.

The five days and eleven days stated in [Taipei City's current administrative processing standards](https://laws.gov.taipei/Law/SOPSearch/DownloadFile?sop_no=P04020118.pdf) are administrative processing targets in calendar days, not business days. The actual number of days may vary because of requests for additional documents, responses from other agencies, case volume, or the complexity of the property or business items. Do not set the lease start date, interior work, company establishment, or opening date solely by relying on these targets.

The six-month validity period runs from the review completion date. Plan the sequence of property selection, contracting, inquiry, registration, and business preparation so that a valid result showing compliance can be attached when the company or business registration is filed. If the validity period has passed or the inquiry contents have changed, do not rely on the old result; confirm whether a new application is required.

The business location greatly affects operations and costs after the company is established. At the stage of deciding on a candidate property, it is important to confirm, one by one, the exact address, floor, and area; the building materials; the business items; the contractual authority to use the site; the actual manner of use; and the industry-specific requirements.
