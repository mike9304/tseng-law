## Article 804 — mainland heirs of a Taiwan-area decedent

Reader and trigger: a mainland resident (hukou in the mainland area) whose parent, spouse or relative with Taiwan household registration died and left Taiwan assets (deposits, land); the first question is the deadline and how much can actually be received. Hypothesis (labelled): no demand data was used; the topic follows from the fact that the Cross-Strait Act treats this group differently from any foreign heir (3-year declaration, NT$2 million cap, state administrator) and that no existing zh-hans column covers it.

New angle versus existing columns: 016 is general Taiwan succession and custody; nothing covers arts. 66–68. The article links to 016 for ordinary distribution among Taiwan heirs.
