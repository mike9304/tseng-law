## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): N/A for guidance pack (out of scope), 3/5 for columns
variety used: standard Italian of Italy. Dates are “13 settembre 2025”; thousands use the point (1.579.589, TWD 25.000.000) and the decimal comma appears in “0,5 metri”. Guillemets « » from 013 on. Straight apostrophes in 010–012, typographic apostrophes in 013–018.
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- “precetto” names a payment writ, not a statutory provision: `"Il precetto che fissa il periodo"` (011; also 014 and 016).
- Traffic “lane” and company-law “business item” are the wrong Italian nouns: `"sorpasso nella stessa via"` (012), `"circa 10 rami in una sola volta"` (015; “rami” also in 017).
- The set still leans on “conviene”, “fissare” and “di modo che”, and “concludere” often has no object: `"Non è possibile concludere solo perché il cliente è a Taiwan"` (018).
- False friends point at the wrong legal object: `"Nell'azione reale"` (010), `"formulario concreto"` (018), `"pubblicità in fondo"` (011), `"statuto di soggiorno"` (017).
- Contact lines still omit the four consultation languages: `"la lingua di colloquio desiderata"` (018; also 013 and 015). The no-reply-time clause should stay.
- Attorney Wei Tseng is feminine wherever she is named (010 “Sono stata l'avvocata”; 011, 014, 016, 017 “avvocata”). 012, 013, 015 and 018 have no gendered byline for her. 015 “un avvocato” is a generic helper. No masculine form for her remains.
