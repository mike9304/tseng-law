## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): N/A for guidance pack (out of scope), 3/5 for columns
variety used: standard Estonian (üldkeel), consistent. Reader address is teie; 010 and 013 use the attorney’s first person, which does not mark gender. 012’s “0,5 meetri” uses a decimal comma. Thousands use a period (1.579.589, TWD 25.000.000), which is not Estonian. Dates after “kuupäeval” and “alates” stay in the nominative. 018 skips from §5 to §7, as the English does.
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- A Chinese gloss is parked on the wrong word: "õigusnõuanne (損害賠償)".
- Thousands use a period: "TWD 1.579.589" (also 011, 017, 018).
- Running dates stay in the nominative: "kuupäeval 24. jaanuar 2022" (also 011, 015, 016).
- The responsible entity is still "subjekt": "seadusjärgset vastutuse subjekti" (also 017, 018).
- Taiwan business registration (商業) is called commerce: "äriühingu või kaubanduse" (also 017).
- 013 and 015 break the text into one-sentence paragraphs, including a dangling clause: "Kui tegevus algab Taipei linnas,".

012 is native professional apart from two phrases. 016 is close: the civil-law terms (üldõigusjärglane, sundosa, asenduspärimine, leibkonna registreerimine for 戶籍) are right. 017 and 018 are close apart from the rows below. 010 and 011 still move legal objects and mis-attach glosses. 014 is readable but calqued. 013 and 015 read as chopped notes, and 013 changes what the investment review checks.

Gender of attorney Wei Tseng: no finding. "Advokaat" and first-person "esindasin" / "soovitan" do not mark gender. Consultation lock stays negative (013 closing note; 018 §8, only English, Chinese, Japanese or Korean, no immediate reply). No win rate, guarantee, or best/only claim.
