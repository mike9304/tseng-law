## Audience and independent editorial brief

English-speaking purchasing, finance and management personnel buying goods from a Taiwanese supplier. The decision is whether to accept an emailed change of bank details, and what to preserve if payment has already gone to the disputed account. Taiwan is the supplier's location; Taiwan governing law is expressly conditional. English language is not treated as US nationality or US jurisdiction. The US complaint route is described separately from Taiwan's criminal process.

This is an independently structured English column. Its entry point is an ongoing business relationship and invoice handling, rather than a translation of a Taiwan consumer-investment or real-estate article. The article moves from verification to the payment obligation, urgent bank contact, criminal reporting, and preservation of the original business records. It has no fabricated experiences, numbers, client quotations, success promises or generic consultation close.
