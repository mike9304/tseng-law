## FACTS

MUST VERIFY 1: CONFIRMED — https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=C0000001&flno=41 — Art. 41 para. 1 is both conditions together: the offense’s heaviest penalty is imprisonment of five years or less (or a lighter penalty), AND the declared sentence is imprisonment of six months or less or detention. Conversion is at NT$1,000, NT$2,000, or NT$3,000 per day. The proviso bars conversion when it would not correct the person or would be hard to maintain the legal order. Para. 2: if fine conversion was available and was not applied for, community service (社會勞動) may be credited at six hours per day. The drafts’ statements of those two propositions match the text.

MUST VERIFY 2: CONFIRMED — https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=C0000001&flno=74 and https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=C0000001&flno=75 and https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=C0000001&flno=75-1 — Art. 74 para. 1: a declared sentence of imprisonment of two years or less, detention, or a fine, plus one of the two record conditions, plus a finding that not executing yet is appropriate (認以暫不執行為適當), and the court may (得) declare probation of two years to five years, counted from the day the judgment becomes final. Condition 1: never sentenced to imprisonment or more for an intentional crime. Condition 2: previously so sentenced, and within five years after that sentence was fully served or pardoned, not again sentenced to imprisonment or more for an intentional crime. It does not say “five years must have passed.” Art. 75 para. 1 is mandatory revocation (no 得) in either of two cases: an intentional other crime, committed during probation or before it, for which a sentence of imprisonment of more than six months becomes final during the probation period. Art. 75-1 is discretionary (得) and only where the original probation is unlikely to achieve its expected effect and execution is necessary, on the four lighter grounds (a final sentence of six months or less, detention, or a fine for an intentional other crime before or during probation; a final imprisonment sentence for a negligent crime during probation; a serious breach of an Art. 74 para. 2 duty).

MUST VERIFY 3: CONFIRMED — https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=C0000001&flno=95 and https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=D0080132&flno=36 — Art. 95 is 得, not 應: a foreigner sentenced to imprisonment or more may be deported after the sentence is fully served or pardoned. The committee itself is required only before removing a foreigner who already holds a residence or permanent-residence permit (para. 4, main clause). The drafts’ “may, not must” and “may skip the committee” sentences match this text. The residence-permit limit is a separate column gap (item 8).
