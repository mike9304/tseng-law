# A Taiwan union agreement expires. Do the employment terms expire with it?

The end date on a Taiwan collective agreement may leave its employment conditions in place. [Article 21 of the Collective Agreement Act (團體協約法)](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=N0020006&flno=21) says that, when the agreement expires without a new one being signed, those conditions continue as terms of the employment contracts until different provision is made in those contracts.

For US headquarters approving a Taiwan subsidiary's payroll budget, an agreement's expiry date is therefore not enough to schedule the removal of a negotiated allowance. 

## Duration follows classification

Taiwan permits collective agreements for a fixed term, an indefinite term or completion of specified work ([Article 26](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=N0020006&flno=26)). The three-year ceiling in [Article 28](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=N0020006&flno=28) concerns fixed-term agreements. A longer fixed term is shortened to three years; that rule does not make the entire agreement void.

An ordinary civil agreement between a union and an employer does not automatically fall under that ceiling. In its October 28, 2026 report on Micron's Taichung union agreement, [CNA reported the Ministry of Labor's distinction between an ordinary agreement and a collective agreement, and its reference to civil litigation over disputed validity](https://www.cna.com.tw/news/ahel/202610280194.aspx). That report does not supply the undisclosed agreement for independent assessment.

## What has actually been signed

Article 2 defines a collective agreement as a written contract concerning labor relations and related matters, concluded by an employer or an employer association with legal personality and a union established under the Labor Union Act ([statutory definition](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=N0020006&flno=2)). A document's title does not settle its classification. Its parties, terms, form and conclusion procedure all require examination.

Civil agreements can impose obligations on their parties under [Civil Code Article 153](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=B0000001&flno=153). Calling a document an ordinary agreement does not make it worthless. Equally, that description supplies no basis for importing Article 19's automatic effect on individual employment contracts. Any effect on those contracts needs its own basis in the arrangements, authority and consent involved.

## Who acquired the employment terms

For a valid collective agreement, [Article 17](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=N0020006&flno=17) ordinarily covers the employer that is a party, employers and workers belonging to a party association, and those who join later, unless the agreement provides otherwise. Later entrants ordinarily receive the employment conditions from the date they become covered persons. A subsidiary-wide email is not the measure of statutory coverage.

Employment conditions enter the relevant employment contracts by operation of [Article 19](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=N0020006&flno=19). Inconsistent individual terms are invalid to that extent and replaced by the collective terms. Differences remain valid if the collective agreement permits them, or if they favor the worker and the agreement has not prohibited them. A better individual package is not an unconditional exception.

There is a subject-matter limit too. [Article 20(1)](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=N0020006&flno=20) excludes matters outside Article 12(1), subparagraphs 1 and 2, from the effects of Articles 17–19. Not every undertaking about union operations becomes an individual employment condition. And agreed working conditions may not fall below the statutory minimum under [Labor Standards Act Article 1(2)](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=N0030001&flno=1).

## The signature needs its approval record

Did the negotiating representative have authority to conclude the final text? Selection of representatives is addressed in [Article 8](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=N0020006&flno=8). Approval for signing in the association's name is addressed separately in [Article 9](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=N0020006&flno=9).

Article 9 recognizes the route provided by the association's constitution. Otherwise, approval requires more than half of the members or delegates to attend the relevant assembly and at least two-thirds of those attending to vote in favor; the alternative is notice to all members and written consent from at least three-quarters of the membership. Without the required procedure, the agreement has no effect until that procedure is completed by ratification.

Internal authority also matters for an ordinary agreement. [Labor Union Act Article 26(1), subparagraphs 10 and 11](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=N0020001&flno=26), reserves the maintenance or alteration of collective working conditions and other major membership-rights matters to the members' or delegates' assembly. The consequences of an internal defect for the external contract require further examination of representation and authority.

## Filing is not the same approval process

In a private-sector collective agreement, the worker-side party must send the signed agreement to its competent authority for record; changes and termination must also be reported ([Article 10(1)](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=N0020006&flno=10)). That provision does not declare an unfiled agreement automatically void.

Both parties must display the agreement and keep a copy available for covered persons to consult at any time ([Article 11](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=N0020006&flno=11)). An English management summary may be useful for headquarters. It may omit restrictions or exceptions that appear in the signed Chinese text.

## When implementation produces a claim

Employers bound by the agreement cannot validly terminate an employment contract because the worker asserts rights under that agreement or rights in the employment contract derived from it. [Article 22(2)](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=N0020006&flno=22) makes such a termination invalid. A dispute about the payroll change therefore also calls for care in how management responds to the employee raising it.

A worker seeking unpaid contractual wages and a union disputing the agreement's validity may need different claims and parties. Civil disputes arising from collective agreements and employment contracts fall within [Labor Incident Act Article 2](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=B0010064&flno=2); [Article 16](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=B0010064&flno=16) generally requires court labor mediation before litigation, subject to statutory exceptions. The procedure is covered in [Taiwan labor mediation for foreign employees](/en/columns/taiwan-labor-mediation-foreign-employee).
