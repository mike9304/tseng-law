## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): 4/5 for guidance pack, 3/5 for columns
variety used: Standard Bahasa Indonesia (EYD), consistent — not mixed with Malay. Numbers follow Indonesian convention (500.000, 0,5) except TWD 1.57M and ISO dates in 003. “Tionghoa” is the official Indonesian term; clients still look for “Mandarin” as the spoken consultation language.
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- English chrome left on the Indonesian site: “OUR TEAM”, “OFFICES”, “Profil lengkap (English)”, titles “Q&A …”
- German template bones in the guidance pack: “kelompok pekerjaan”, “Bahasa tampilan”, “Daftar halaman”, “satu alur penanganan”
- Hangul leftovers in columns: percent-encoded Korean Wix path in 005; Hangul `url` on 008 and 009
- Blog register in 003 Q16–Q20, 005, 006, 008, 009: “tinggalkan di kolom komentar”, “kapan saja”, “??”, empty `​` lines, first-person asides
- Advertising-risk sentences inherited from the Korean source: “ganti rugi sebesar mungkin”, typical “3 bulan / 4 bulan / 6 bulan”, “pesangon dalam jumlah besar”
- Title split: guidance pack says “Advokat”; columns 001–004, 007–008 sign off as “Pengacara Wei Tseng”
Gender: Indonesian does not inflect. “saya”, “ia”, “advokat”, “pengacara”, and “mahasiswa pertukaran” are epicene. No masculine pronoun, title, or verb for Wei Tseng. “Tuan B” (008) is a male third party. Consultation lock is intact in the FAQ and llms notice (`Tidak. … hanya … bahasa Inggris, bahasa Tionghoa (中文), bahasa Jepang, dan bahasa Korea`; no interpreter, no reply-time, no free first meeting, no result promise). Contact bullets, the inquiry notice, and several `guidanceAnswers` omit `hanya`. 001, 002, 004, and 007 are the strongest files: `pemegang saham (股東)`, `公開發行公司`, `背信`, `戶政機關` as administrasi kependudukan, and Korea→Indonesia reader framing (`perusahaan Indonesia`, `anak akan tinggal di Indonesia`) are already done; 005 Q5 was correctly broadened to `orang asing`. 003 Q16–Q20 and 008 fail an advertising read; 005 is still a Korea remittance FAQ; 006 is the most spoken prose except `Hakim Yuan Kehakiman`.
