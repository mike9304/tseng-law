## First-two-paragraph deletion test

934 now begins directly with the applicable short-stay rule and the Dutch machinery-supplier scope. Removing that sentence would lose the deemed-permit rule and its 30-day limit. In paragraph two, the first sentence adds Dutch eligibility for conditional 90-day visa-free entry; the second states the separate limitation on work requiring permission. Both facts are needed to prevent the 90-day entry period being read as 90 days of work permission.

Before freezing, the sentence “Voor een Nederlandse machineleverancier kan een langer verblijf van de installatiemonteur een werkvergunningaanvraag nodig maken” was removed as an announcement of the following rule. The Dutch-supplier scope was incorporated into the actual rule. “De ruimte op de reiskalender zegt dus nog niet hoeveel installatiewerk de monteur mag uitvoeren” was removed because the previous sentence already supplied the legal distinction. The summary's “eerdere werkdagen” became “eerdere werkperioden” to avoid implying that only weekdays or days actually worked are counted. This preserves and clarifies the period rule.

935 begins with non-opposability to third parties in good faith and then the separate written-contract requirement. Each sentence adds a distinct condition. Paragraph two explains that the registration rule alone does not invalidate the agreement between the parties, then connects this to the supplier's payment-after-installation planning. “Het contract blijft daarmee een eigen vraag” was removed because the next sentence says what that question is. No legal proposition was removed.
