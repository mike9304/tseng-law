## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): N/A for guidance pack (out of scope), 3/5 for columns
variety used: standard Romanian (Romania), consistent; diacritics and the decimal comma are in place. Address is mostly dumneavoastră; 011 also says „voi”. No Moldovan mix.
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- „A naște” is used without „se”, so the verb means „to give birth”: `"Controverse pot naște"` (010); also 016 `"poate naște"`.
- 014 still uses two false friends for the statutory tests: `"să observe perioada minimă de muncă"` and `"să o dispenseze"`.
- „Cauțiune” stands in for a rental deposit, a surety, and a parent-company guarantee: `"depozitul de cauțiune (押金)"` (013); also 016 and 018.
- 戶籍 is named as the civil-status register or as domicile: `"registrul de stare civilă (戶籍)"` (016); 017 has `"înregistrare de domiciliu (戶籍)"`.
- 013 and 015 still put almost every clause on its own line: `"Pe lângă situația pieței"` then `"un alt punct important este"`.
- A registered title or a shareholding is rendered with the neighbouring word: `"comparație a numelor"` (016), `"2 asociați sau mai mulți"` for a societate pe acțiuni (018). Attorney Wei Tseng is feminine wherever she is named (010 *avocata*; 011, 016, 017 *Avocata*; 014 *avocată*). 012’s *A* is the motorcyclist. 013’s first person and 018’s unsigned close do not use a masculine title. No masculine form for her remains. The negative lines on Romanian consultation, interpreters, reply times, and results stay negative. 012 is native professional Romanian apart from three phrases. 017 is close.
