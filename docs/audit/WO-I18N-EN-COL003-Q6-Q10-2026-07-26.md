## Fidelity contract

### Q6 — Preliminary analysis, appraisal, and review

- Describe the Road Traffic Accident Preliminary Analysis Determination Form
  as a preliminary police analysis, not a court judgment. It neither binds the
  court nor fixes a fault percentage.
- Do not present the preliminary report, statutory appraisal, review, and an
  academic appraisal as an automatic or mandatory ladder.
- State that an eligible party may apply for vehicle-accident appraisal, a
  handling authority may refer a matter, or a judicial authority may
  commission an appraisal.
- A party application is ordinarily made within six months after the
  accident. If investigation or trial is already pending, appraisal generally
  proceeds through judicial commissioning rather than a new direct
  application.
- A dissatisfied party may seek review, limited to one review.
- Appraisal and review opinions are evidence or reference material. The court
  independently evaluates statements, video, scene records, and the record as
  a whole.

### Q7 — Conditional damages categories

- Begin with Civil Code Article 184: unlawful infringement, causation, and
  proof of damage are required. An accident alone does not automatically
  establish every listed loss.
- Explain Article 216 actual loss and lost profit as the scope standard.
- Injury:
  - Article 193 necessary medical, care, treatment-travel, assistive-device,
    and other increased-need expenses;
  - proven temporary income loss and loss of earning capacity;
  - Article 195 non-pecuniary damage.
- Death:
  - Article 192 applicable pre-death medical/increased-need expenses, funeral
    expenses, and loss of support for a person legally entitled to support;
  - Article 194 non-pecuniary damage for qualifying relatives.
- Property:
  - Article 196 proven actual property damage, including supported repair
    expense or diminution in value.

### Q8 — Evidence supplementation versus changing a claim

- Preserve receipts, diagnosis certificates, and medical records; organize
  medical necessity and causation to the accident.
- Continuing-treatment material may supplement evidence in line with the
  procedural schedule and existing claim, but late material or an expanded
  claim is not guaranteed to be accepted.
- Distinguish supplementing medical evidence from changing or increasing the
  amount or scope of the claim.
- Adding medical receipts after filing an attached civil action does not by
  itself automatically create a court fee.
- Under Criminal Procedure Code Article 504, after transfer to the civil
  division, a change/addition/expansion beyond the pre-transfer claim can
  create a court-fee issue for the excess. Confirm transfer stage, filing
  time, and claim scope case by case.

### Q9 — Professional and family care

- A diagnosis certificate or medical opinion is useful evidence of care need,
  but is not conclusive by itself.
- Require evidence of accident causation, need for care, actual provision,
  duration, and a reasonable amount.
- Unpaid care actually provided by a relative may be valued as damage in an
  appropriate case, but is not automatically allowed merely because a
  relative provided it. Consider nature, duration, and customary cost.

### Q10 — Treatment travel expenses

- Connect travel records to treatment and the accident-related injury.
- Cover route, visit dates and number, transport method, fare, necessity, and
  reasonableness.
- Receipts, fare records, route records, and treatment records are possible
  evidence.
- A taxi receipt is neither the only proof nor automatically sufficient.
