# Norwegian Bokmål native review — round 2, part a (nb)
reviewer: Grok 4.7 · date: 2026-09-22 · scope: nb-guidance.txt (365 strings); columns-nb/001–009

## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): 4/5 for guidance pack, 3/5 for columns
variety used: Bokmål, du-tiltal. The guidance pack is consistent Bokmål. Columns 001–004 and 007 mix Danish lexicon and the old grammatical comma; 005, 006 and 008–009 are closer to spoken Bokmål, with leftover Danish in 009.
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- Danish comma before «at»/«som», and sometimes between subject and verb, in columns 001–004: "Veiene utenlandske virksomheter og enkeltpersonforetak bruker for å komme inn på det taiwanske markedet, varierer"
- Danish words left in the Bokmål: "kan falde ind under", "angivelsen sker", "Kernen", "pågjeldende", "hensyntaken", "Sommetider"
- Thousand separator is a period (Danish/German) in the columns, while the guidance pack correctly uses a space: "TWD 500.000" vs "1 570 000 TWD"
- 停業 is called "driftsstilstand", which in Norwegian means the operating state, not a business suspension.
- One disclaimer in the guidance answers still says "juridisk uttalelse" where the rest of the pack says "juridisk rådgivning".
- Gender of attorney Wei Tseng: no finding. The bio uses "Hun"; column signatures use "Advokat Wei Tseng". "han"/"ham" in column 008 refer to the male client hr. B.
