## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): 4/5 for guidance pack, 3/5 for columns
variety used: standard Slovene (knjižna slovenščina), consistent; » « quotes and dates (13. septembra 2025, 27. 12. 2023, 25. 7. 2026) already follow Slovene rules. 008 writes 0,5 with a comma, then still writes TWD 30.000 with a period.
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- False friend uporaben for “applicable” (`"od uporabnih predpisov"`; also 001–004 and 007).
- 戶籍 / 戶政 rendered as matične evidence, which in Slovenia are birth, marriage and death registers (`"organu matičnih evidenc (戶政機關)"`).
- Currency in English order with a period as thousands separator (`"TWD 500.000"`).
- Blog voice in 003 from Q16, in 006, and in the 008–009 anecdotes; Wei Tseng stays feminine (`"Sledi zadeva, ki sem jo vodila"`).
- Korean frontmatter URLs left in Hangul (`"대만-노동법：대만에서-퇴직금-받기-어렵다고"`).
- Head office as glavna poslovna enota, which already means a branch or PE (`"tuja glavna poslovna enota (本公司)"`).

Guidance is usable professional Slovene: the four-language lock, the refused interpreter and the “form is free / consultation is not” distinction all stay negative. 001, 004 and 007 are the strongest columns. 002 names the wrong tax. 006, 008 and 009 would embarrass a Ljubljana reader. No masculine form for Wei Tseng.
