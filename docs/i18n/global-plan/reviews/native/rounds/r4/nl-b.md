variety used: Standard Dutch, readable in the Netherlands and Belgium. Address is *u*. Dates are day–month–year with a lowercase month (24 januari 2022, 5 juni 2026, 1 juli 2026, 17 september 2026). Thousands use a dot (1.579.589; 25.000.000) and the decimal comma (0,5 meter; 1,57 miljoen). The currency code follows the amount in 010 (1.579.589 TWD) and precedes it elsewhere (TWD 40.000). systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- *Naar* still calques "depending on", and with *afwijken* it points the wrong way: "kan naar de plaats van het ongeval ... afwijken".
- *Handel* still names a business or an operation: "elke met logistiek samenhangende handel".
- Coined compounds stand in for an ordinary noun: "de werkelijke aanvraagontvangst".
