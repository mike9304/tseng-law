# Switching Employers Inside Taiwan on a Work Permit: The New Employer Files, and Your Start Date Follows the Permit

Nothing in Taiwan's Employment Service Act asks your current employer to approve a move to another company. What the statute watches is something else: whose application your work permit rests on, and the date a new one issues.

## A permit that rests on an employer's application

Unless the Act provides otherwise, a foreigner may work in Taiwan only with a permit obtained through an employer's application ([Employment Service Act Article 43](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=N0090001&flno=43)). A change of employer while that permit is valid needs a fresh application. Under [Article 53](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=N0090001&flno=53)(1), the new employer files for a new permit and attaches the worker's separation document.

For the new employer, this is no formality. Hiring a foreigner who has no permit breaches Article 57(1); the fine runs from NT$150,000 to NT$750,000, and a second violation within five years can be prosecuted ([Article 63](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=N0090001&flno=63)). The company has its own reason to wait.

## The permit date is the start date

So do you. Work done before the permit issues breaches Article 43 on your side as well, with a fine of NT$30,000 to NT$150,000 ([Article 68](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=N0090001&flno=68)(1)). Article 68(3) goes further: a foreigner who breaches Article 43 is to be ordered to leave and may not work in Taiwan again. The provision makes no allowance for a start that was only a few days early.

## Proof of service record

Paperwork is where an unhappy employer can slow things down, and the Labor Standards Act speaks to it. Under [Article 19](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=N0030001&flno=19), upon termination of a labor contract, "neither an employer nor the employer's agent shall reject a request from the worker for proof of service record." A refusal can be fined NT$20,000 to NT$300,000 ([Article 79](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=N0030001&flno=79)(3)). Whether that certificate also counts as the separation document the new employer must attach is left to the ministry's filing requirements; the statutes do not say.

How much notice you owe before leaving depends on your length of service, covered in [Resigning From a Taiwan Job: Notice for an Indefinite-Term Contract](/en/columns/taiwan-employee-resignation-notice-indefinite-contract).

## Your ARC between jobs

Once the employment ends, the old employer has three days to notify the local labor authority, the immigration authority and the police in writing ([Article 56](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=N0090001&flno=56)(1)). The end of employment is itself a ground for revoking the work permit ([Article 73](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=N0090001&flno=73), item 3). [Article 74](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=N0090001&flno=74)(1) then provides that, unless the Act says otherwise, the person is to be ordered to leave and may not work in Taiwan again. None of these articles sets a grace period.

Immigration status is handled by the National Immigration Agency. When the reason for residence disappears, the agency revokes the residence permit and cancels the Alien Resident Certificate ([Immigration Act Article 31](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=D0080132&flno=31)(4)). It may let the person stay on in limited cases, for example while a labor-dispute lawsuit against a Taiwan employer is pending or an occupational injury is still being treated. After the move, a change of workplace has to be registered within 30 days (Article 31(6)).

## Gold Card holders

The National Immigration Agency's [Gold Card FAQ](https://news.immigration.gov.tw/NewsSection/Detail/646ccc29-c816-4f3e-bf58-c755eec59980?lang=EN) answers the main question directly: the card acts as an individual work permit, so a new employer does not apply for a hiring permit. That follows from Article 9 of the [Act for the Recruitment and Employment of Foreign Professionals](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=A0030295&flno=9), which combines the work permit, resident visa, ARC and re-entry permit in one card. More on the card is in [The Taiwan Employment Gold Card, Filed by the Applicant](/en/columns/taiwan-employment-gold-card).

## Factory, fishing and care workers

Items 8 to 11 of [Article 46](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=N0090001&flno=46)(1) cover marine fishing, domestic and care work, and ministry-designated work such as manufacturing and construction. Workers in items 1 to 7, which include professionals, cannot move into them when they change employers or jobs (Article 53(3)). Those already in them generally may not change employers during the permit term (Article 53(4)) unless the Ministry of Labor approves one of four situations in [Article 59](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=N0090001&flno=59)(1): the employer or the person cared for dies or emigrates; the vessel is seized, sinks or is under repair and can no longer operate; the employer closes or shuts down, or the contract is terminated because wages were not paid as agreed; or another reason arises for which the worker is not responsible. The unpaid-wage route is set out in the FAQ above.

After approval, the public employment service has 60 days for the transfer, extendable once by 60 days in special circumstances. A worker whose permit was revoked after physical abuse by the employer, its staff or agent, a relative, or the person cared for is not held to that single extension ([Transfer Procedure Regulations Article 11](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=N0090023&flno=11)(1)-(2)). A separate end-of-term transfer applies when, two to four months before the permit expires, the worker and the original employer agree not to renew ([Article 23](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=N0090023&flno=23)).
