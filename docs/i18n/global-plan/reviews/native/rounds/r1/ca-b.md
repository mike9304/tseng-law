## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): n/a for guidance pack (part a; not reviewed here), 4/5 for columns
variety used: Standard Catalan (Catalonia and Andorra; IEC orthography), consistent. Central forms, «» quotes, decimal comma (0,5) and dot thousands (1.579.589 / 25.000.000). Not Valencian or Balearic. Dates follow "13 de setembre de 2025". Apostrophe before a figure read as "un" is kept in most places (d’1 any, menys d’1 segon) and missed in 010 and in "l’Llei".
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- Verb + "junts" calques English "together": "cal examinar junts l’experiència esportiva".
- Castellanisme "tal qual" where Catalan wants "tal com" or "sense més": "no es pot aplicar tal qual a un altre accident".
- Masculine "per si mateix" / "ell mateix" with a feminine or missing subject: "si es constitueix per si mateix una filial".
- Stock closer "material educatiu" / "material de propòsit educatiu": "Aquest article és un material de propòsit educatiu".
- A factor or a result "sees" or "is concluded as": "El tercer factor veu no només l’import".
- A Catalan civil-law word points at the wrong Taiwanese institution: "el procediment d’adveració (檢認)".

No masculine title or pronoun for Wei Tseng. The negative lines (no reply-time promise, no result guarantee, no promise that a permit will be granted) stay negative.
