# Many Victims, One Scam: How Taiwan's Fraud Crime Prevention Act Ties Sentences to Repayment and Lets Victims Sue Together

Two hundred people put money into the same fake trading app, run from Taiwan (an invented example). Some lost a few thousand dollars, some their savings. Do they each have to bring their own case? Taiwan's Fraud Crime Hazard Prevention Act, enacted on July 31, 2024, gives them another option, and it also gives defendants a reason to pay.

## Which frauds the Act covers

Scope comes first. The "fraud crimes" in [Article 2](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=D0080226&flno=2) are aggravated fraud under [Criminal Code Article 339-4](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=C0000001&flno=339-4), offences under Articles 43 and 44 of the Act, and offences tried together with them as a single offence. Aggravated fraud means impersonating officials, three or more people acting together, spreading the scheme to the public through broadcast, telecom, internet or other media, or using synthesized images or voices; it carries one to seven years. For an organized ring, the first question is whether three or more people acted together. A romance scam run by one person is different, and whether the Act's victim provisions apply there has to be checked separately.

## Lighter sentences only after payment

The Act links repayment to sentencing. [Article 47](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=D0080226&flno=47), as amended on January 21, 2026, allows a reduced sentence for a defendant who confesses during the investigation and at every trial level and, within six months of first confessing during the prosecutor's investigation, pays the full amount agreed with the victim in mediation or settlement. [Article 46](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=D0080226&flno=46) does the same for a defendant who turned themselves in, allowing reduction or even exemption on full payment within six months of surrender. If the payment also leads to the arrest of the ring's organizers, or to seizure of everything the ring took from victims, exemption becomes possible under paragraph 2 of each article.

Prosecutors and judges are pushed the same way. Under [Article 53](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=D0080226&flno=53), they may use mediation and settlement to get defendants to pay victims; where a defendant has not paid, the prosecutor may ask the court to weigh that in sentencing and must appeal if necessary. When deferring prosecution, prosecutors should give priority to ordering compensation. Courts must also pay particular attention, before the victim is paid, to whether the defendant has been living beyond ordinary standards: luxury goods or services, expensive transport, investments, high-end venues, gifts or loans to others, or monthly living costs ([Article 50(2)](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=D0080226&flno=50)).

The benchmark is the agreed amount. It need not match the full loss. What a victim controls is the paper: the amount, the payment date, and what happens if the money does not arrive.

## Suing together, at lower cost

Where some money is still sitting in a frozen account, there may be a quicker route. If a bank or virtual-asset provider suspended an account on police notice and a victim's transfer has not been withdrawn, it may return the remaining funds on police notice ([Article 11](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=D0080226&flno=11)). How a victim overseas reports the loss and gets the receiving account flagged is covered in [our column for overseas victims](/en/columns/us-victim-scam-funds-taiwan-bank-complaint).

Otherwise, civil court. [Article 55](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=D0080226&flno=55) lets victims whose main claims and defences are the same choose one or more of themselves to sue for all, applying the civil-procedure rules on appointed parties. The Article sets no minimum number of victims; what it requires is that their main claims and defences be the same. The appointed party sues for everyone who appointed them. Once that happens after the case is pending, the others drop out, and changing the appointed party only takes effect after the other side is notified ([Code of Civil Procedure Article 41](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=B0010001&flno=41)).

Costs come down too. A fraud-crime victim suing or appealing against those liable is temporarily exempt from court fees, and from enforcement fees when applying for enforcement. Security ordered for a provisional attachment, a provisional injunction or provisional execution cannot exceed one tenth of the claim ([Article 54](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=D0080226&flno=54)). Police, prosecutors and judges must tell victims who meet the legal-aid criteria that they may apply ([Article 52](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=D0080226&flno=52)).

Damages claims inside the criminal case are covered in [our column on the incidental civil action](/en/columns/taiwan-criminal-accessory-civil-suit-fraud). For a group of victims, three things come first: a list of who was hit, the transfer records, and the messages that show the same script.
