# Can Your Dog Leave Taoyuan Airport With You? Taiwan's Import Quarantine for Dogs and Cats

Will your dog leave Taoyuan airport in the same taxi as you? From Tokyo or London, it can, if the paperwork is right. From Seoul, Los Angeles or Toronto, the default is seven days in a designated facility, even with perfect papers.

## The rabies-free list decides the track

Taiwan's Animal and Plant Health Inspection Agency (APHIA) lists these places as rabies-free: Japan, Singapore, Australia, New Zealand, Hawaii, Guam, the UK, Sweden, Iceland, Norway (excluding Svalbard), Estonia and the Czech Republic. Everywhere else, including Korea, the US mainland, Canada and mainland China, counts as rabies-affected.

The detailed rules sit in the Quarantine Requirements for the Importation of Dogs and Cats (犬貓輸入檢疫條件), as amended by Ministry of Agriculture announcement Nong-Fang No. 1131867278 of February 27, 2024 and in force since February 29, 2024. Their legal base is [Article 33](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=M0130003&flno=33), paragraph 1, of the Act on the Prevention and Control of Infectious Animal Diseases (動物傳染病防治條例).

Residence counts, not nationality. Status is fixed when the animal is loaded for departure (point 2), and point 3 limits the rabies-free track to three cases:

- under 180 days old, and kept in a rabies-free place since birth;
- 180 days or older, and kept in a rabies-free place for the 180 days before import;
- exported from Taiwan, and kept in a rabies-free place from departure until return.

A dog that moved from Seoul to Tokyo three months ago still goes on the rabies-affected track (an invented example).

## Arriving from a rabies-free country

Point 4 asks for a microchip and a rabies shot given between one year and 30 days before import, at 90 days of age or older, using an inactivated vaccine or one approved in Taiwan. A three-year vaccine does not stretch that window, APHIA's Q&A says.

At least 20 days before arrival, you apply for an import quarantine permit (輸入檢疫同意文件) from the quarantine office at the port of entry, attaching your ID and the vet's vaccination certificate (point 5). That certificate must show, in Chinese or English, species, sex, age, chip number, vaccination date and vaccine brand or type.

The exporting authority's health certificate must record no clinical signs of rabies, the vaccination, and 180 days, or life since birth, in a rabies-free place (point 6). Present it in original with the permit copy and air waybill or customs declaration; if all matches, the pet goes home (points 7 and 8).

Connections can break this track. APHIA's Q&A treats Tokyo via Korea as transit through a rabies-affected country, which requires a crate sealed by the exporting authority with the seal number on the certificate, or proof from the transit port's quarantine office, customs or airline that the animal stayed airside without contact with susceptible animals. Otherwise: seven days in quarantine (point 9).

## Arriving from anywhere else

Point 10 governs ordinary dogs and cats from rabies-affected places. Chip and vaccine rules match. A blood test is added.

A WOAH rabies reference laboratory or an APHIA-designated one must find a neutralizing antibody titer of at least 0.5 IU/ml (point 1), normally on a sample drawn between one year and 90 days before import. Variants exist for an earlier passing test or a test done in Taiwan before leaving.

Ninety days, at minimum. Book the blood draw before you book the movers.

The permit is again due 20 days ahead and comes with a reserved quarantine place (point 11). Officers escort the animal there, and release follows seven days without signs of notifiable disease (points 14 and 24).

Can those seven days be avoided? Since 2024, sometimes. Point 11, paragraph 2, waives quarantine for a passing sample drawn 180 days to one year before import, plus one of these:

- the permit application was filed at least 120 days before arrival; or
- the exporting authority or the laboratory sent the result directly to APHIA.

A draw inside 180 days also qualifies if it came 180 days to one year after an earlier passing draw, again with the 120-day application or direct reporting. Pets tested in Taiwan before departure that return within one year of that draw skip quarantine too.

## Where quarantine happens, and who pays

Taoyuan, Songshan, Taipei Port and Taichung arrivals go to APHIA's Guanyin Inspection Station, Kaohsiung arrivals to National Pingtung University of Science and Technology's veterinary hospital. A third site, at National Chung Hsing University, is suspended.

You pay for detention, transport, care, testing, vaccination, return or destruction (point 23). A vaccination that passes the one-year mark during quarantine triggers a booster (point 25). APHIA does not escort or admit animals on weekends or public holidays.

Dates slip. A permit can be amended once, before it expires and at least 10 days before the new arrival date (point 5, paragraph 3; point 11, paragraph 6). A second change means a new application.

## When the papers are wrong

[Article 34-2](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=M0130003&flno=34-2) of the statute allows longer isolation, revaccination, a deadline to correct documents, return or destruction. Point 15 sets the outcomes for pets from rabies-affected places:

| Problem | Outcome |
|---|---|
| No microchip; no titer entry and no original report | Return, destruction or quarantine: vaccinate after 90 days of age, chip implanted on arrival if missing, draw blood 30 days later, stay until 97 days after the draw; on failure, repeat or stay 180 days |
| No original certificate; microchip number missing or wrong | Same, but pets back within a year of leaving Taiwan, with the Taiwan export certificate and an original passing report drawn before departure and within a year of import, stay 30 days with no shot or test |
| Species, sex or age missing or wrong, or no "no clinical signs" statement | Return, destruction or 30 days |
| Vaccination details do not meet point 10 | Return, destruction, or revaccinate and stay 30 days from that shot |
| Blood drawn outside the window | Return, destruction or stay until 97 days after the draw |

Rabies-free origin does not cure these defects. Under point 9, return, destruction or quarantine again apply: the 97-day route for a missing chip, a missing original certificate, a chip number missing or wrong, or no 180-day residence statement, with the 30-day exception only for the certificate and chip-number cases; revaccination plus 30 days for vaccine defects; seven days for species, sex, age or clinical-signs gaps and for affected-country transit.

Compensation is limited too. Under [Article 40](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=M0130003&flno=40), paragraph 3, nothing is paid for animals destroyed at import quarantine over disease concerns or dying in quarantine.

## Bringing a pet in without declaring it

Importers must apply for inspection at the port, travelers on entry; pets may not be mailed in ([Article 34](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=M0130003&flno=34), paragraphs 1 to 3).

Fines vary. A traveler who fails to declare faces NT$10,000 to NT$1,000,000 ([Article 45-1](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=M0130003&flno=45-1)). An importer who does not apply at the port faces NT$50,000 to NT$1,000,000 ([Article 43](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=M0130003&flno=43), item 11), plus an order to take necessary measures (Article 35, paragraph 4). Importing without an advance permit, or without a reserved quarantine place, costs NT$30,000 to NT$150,000 each ([Article 45](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=M0130003&flno=45), items 12 and 15).

Removing a pet from quarantine is worse. Late delivery to the facility, or moving the animal out without permission, means NT$50,000 to NT$1,000,000. If disease spreads as a result, the penalty is up to three years' imprisonment or detention, and the court may add a fine of up to NT$1,000,000 ([Article 42](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=M0130003&flno=42)).

## Breeds Taiwan does not admit

Under [Animal Protection Act Article 8](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=M0060027&flno=8), the then Council of Agriculture banned keeping or importing pit bull terriers, meaning the American Pit Bull Terrier and the American Staffordshire Terrier, from March 1, 2022 (announcement Nong-Mu No. 1100042728 of October 26, 2021). APHIA says pit bulls already kept on or before February 28, 2022 fall outside the import ban, but the owner must first give supporting evidence to the animal protection authority and have it confirmed before import quarantine proceeds. Otherwise, importing one brings a fine of NT$50,000 to NT$250,000 ([Article 26](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=M0060027&flno=26), paragraph 1, item 2) and possible confiscation ([Article 32](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=M0060027&flno=32), paragraph 1, item 5).
