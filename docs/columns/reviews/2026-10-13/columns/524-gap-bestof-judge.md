## Accuracy (8 / 8)

The front matter (title, summary, both FAQ answers), the reference list, the 확인일 line and the closing notice are identical in the two files. The legal body is the same in substance. Every item below is present and correct in both:

- Art. 41 para. 1: offence with a maximum of 5 years or less, sentence of 6 months or less, NT$1,000, 2,000 or 3,000 a day, and the correction/legal-order exception.
- Art. 41 para. 2: community service at 6 hours a day when conversion was not applied for.
- The 1-year limit and the fallback to the original sentence or to a fine.
- Art. 42: payment within 2 months, enforcement, labour at NT$1,000–3,000 a day with a 1-year cap, and instalments within 1 year.
- Art. 74: a sentence of 2 years or less, one of the two no-prior-sentence conditions, 2–5 years of suspension, the listed duties, and civil-enforcement force for compensation and the public-treasury payment.
- Art. 75: more than 6 months, intentional offence, must revoke.
- Art. 75-1: lighter grounds, may revoke.
- Art. 76: the sentence loses effect when the period ends without revocation.
- Art. 95: "may" deport, after execution or pardon.
- Immigration Act Art. 36 para. 4: no review committee when the court included deportation in a final judgment.
- All eight statute links, and the "확인하지 못했습니다" limitation sentence.

Neither version has a hypothetical scene, so there is no label to lose. Neither has bold.

## Variety (X 6, Y 8)

The checker shows no FAIL for either. Reading the text against SENTENCE-VARIETY-RULE 2-1 and 2-2 still separates them.

X, rule 2-2: three consecutive sentences in the 집행유예 paragraph end in a parenthetical citation:

> …근거가 됩니다([제74조]). … 더 가벼운 사유에는 취소할 수 있습니다([제75조의1]). … 효력을 잃습니다([제76조]).

The rule allows at most two in a row. This is the "주장 + (조문)" rhythm the rule diagnoses.

X, rule 2-1: that paragraph holds several sentences of 70+ characters, where the rule allows one per paragraph. The Art. 41 and Art. 74 paragraphs are dense blocks. X has no question (q=0), and the rule asks for a reason when there is none.

Y has two natural reader questions and a citation position that varies (as subject, as an opener, only one at the end of a sentence). Its short-sentence share is higher and its paragraphs are shorter.

- Questions: "환산을 신청하지 않으면 어떻게 될까요?"
- Citation position: "[제42조]에 따르면 …", "[입출국 및 이민법 제36조] 제4항은, …"
- Metrics: cite_end 0.036, short 0.179, contrast 0.

Y's weaknesses are the repeated "~지요" ending (four times) and one "다만".
