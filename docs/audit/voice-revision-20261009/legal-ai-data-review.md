## Shared fresh PDPA Article 19/20 evidence

At the parent's request, this paragraph is reusable source evidence for its separate Article 498 review; this helper has not reviewed or certified Article 498. [PDPC current text, Articles 19 and 20](https://law.pdpc.gov.tw/LawContent.aspx?id=FL010627), reopened directly in this run, retains the operative collection/processing vs utilization distinction. Article 19, excluding Article 6 special categories, requires a specific purpose and one statutory ground: express law; contract or similar relationship plus appropriate security; self-publicized or otherwise lawfully public data; qualifying public-interest academic/statistical research with unidentifiability; consent; public-interest necessity; generally available source subject to the superior-protected-interest exception and consequent stopping/deletion requirement; or no harm to the person's rights. Article 20 similarly excludes Article 6 data and normally restricts utilization to the necessary scope of the collection purpose. Purpose-external use requires its enumerated exception: express law, public-interest necessity, avoidance of danger to life/body/freedom/property, prevention of major harm to others, qualifying public-body/academic public-interest research with unidentifiability, consent, or benefit to the person's rights. Marketing refusal requires immediate cessation, and a free refusal method must be provided at the first marketing use. Article 7 requires a separately expressed, informed purpose-external consent, with the collector bearing proof of consent. These Articles 19/20 have no new commencement-undetermined annotation; the adjacent added Article 20-1 does. The [current history](https://law.pdpc.gov.tw/LawContentSource.aspx?id=FL010627) identifies neither Article 19 nor Article 20 as amended in November 2025. A commercial agreement about a customer list does not itself establish every affected natural person's data-use basis.
