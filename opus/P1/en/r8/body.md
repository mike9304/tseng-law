Nov 28, 2025, Taipei. A renter viewed a unit; rent, a one-year minimum and house rules came up.

At 2:51 p.m. they wired NT$21,800, then sent a LINE message calling it a "rental deposit."

That day they backed out, saying their stuff wouldn't fit and nothing was signed. The company said no.

In court, the renter filed that LINE.

The trial court found the terms agreed before signing, backed by the word "deposit." Backing out over space was on the renter. The claim, with interest, failed.
