# S8 facts — severance/retirement income tax for foreign employees leaving Taiwan

Fetched 2026-10-20. Gazette PDF: research/off/gaz161826.pdf.

## 所得稅法 (G0340003; law-level 修正日期 2026-09-11 — that amendment = Arts 17, 126 only, eff. 2026-01-01; Art 14 last amended 2019 per LawHistory)
- Art 2 — Taiwan-source income taxed; non-residents 「分別就源扣繳」.
- Art 7(2) — resident = (1) domicile in ROC and habitually resides, or (2) no domicile but 「於一課稅年度內在中華民國境內居留合計滿一百八十三天者」.
- Art 14(1) 第九類 退職所得: 「凡個人領取之退休金、資遣費、退職金、離職金、終身俸、非屬保險給付之養老金及依勞工退休金條例規定辦理年金保險之保險給付等所得。但個人歷年自薪資收入中自行繳付之儲金…於提繳年度已計入薪資收入課稅部分及其孳息，不在此限」; lump sum: ≤150,000×years → 0; between 150,000× and 300,000× → half; above 300,000× → full (base statutory amounts); 「退職服務年資之尾數未滿六個月者，以半年計；滿六個月者，以一年計」; instalments: annual total − 650,000 (base). Art 14(4): amounts adjusted when CPI rises ≥3% cumulatively.
- Art 71-1(2) — resident leaving midyear must file before departure (unless spouse remains resident).
- Art 73 — non-residents with Art 88 income: no annual return; tax withheld at source.
- Art 88(1)(2) — withholding on 退休金、資遣費、退職金、離職金、終身俸… paid by 機關、團體、事業 etc.
- Art 92(1) — withholding certificates by Feb 10 of next year (residents); (2) non-residents: pay within 10 days of withholding, 扣繳憑單 申報核驗 then given to taxpayer.

## 各類所得扣繳率標準 (G0340028; 2021-06-30; official English "Standards of Withholding Rates for Various Incomes")
- Art 2(1)(9) residents: 「退職所得按給付額減除定額免稅後之餘額扣取百分之六。」
- Art 3(1)(11) non-residents: 「退職所得按給付額減除定額免稅後之餘額扣取百分之十八。」 Art 3(1)(2): salary 18%, but 「全月薪資給付總額在行政院核定每月基本工資一點五倍以下者，按給付額扣取百分之六」 (en only).

## Worked example (labelled hypothetical in all locales)
- 6 years; NT$2,000,000 lump sum → 206,000×6 = 1,236,000 (zero); 414,000×6 = 2,484,000; 2,000,000−1,236,000 = 764,000; half = 382,000 retirement income. Withholding amounts deliberately NOT computed (the meaning of 「定額免稅後之餘額」 for the half-taxed band was not confirmed from an official example).
