## Current primary-law checks and limits

The author opened the official provisions cited below on 2026-10-23 using the web tool, including the actual Chinese statutory text and relevant official history/full-text pages. Article source sections carry the same actual checking date. The generated URL register later in this report lists every public external URL in each delivered file.

- 664: MOL domestic-care exclusion guidance; Civil Code §486; current N0090027 §66; Employment Service Act §59; Labor Dispute Resolution Act §§9/23. Current foreign-worker regulations are revised 2025-12-30; former §§42–49 are deleted. Section 66 is used for second-category workers' bilingual payslips, five-year retention, full direct cash payment less amounts lawfully borne by the worker, and documented other payment methods. No statutory factory overtime, minimum salary, severance or wage-fund entitlement is promised to every household caregiver. Contract nonpayment must lead to the relevant termination ground and central approval for transfer; a complaint alone is insufficient.
- 665: Employment Service Act §40(1)(10) agency document-retention prohibition and related fee rule, §57(8) employer prohibition, §59 transfer conditions; current WDA multilingual passport notice and migrant-worker assistance page. The notice's Indonesian translation has a potentially confusing name for a work permit; the article uses “izin kerja,” consistent with the Chinese/English text. Consent to temporary processing custody is distinguished from continued retention against the worker's wishes. A debt dispute does not itself establish a right to keep a passport.
- 666: AML §§2/19/22, Criminal Code §§13/30/339, Criminal Procedure §§41/43-1/71-1/95/99/100-2. AML §22's legitimate commercial/financial practice, trusted family/friend relationship or other proper-reason exceptions are preserved; consideration, three-or-more accounts and repeat conduct within five years after the specified warning are distinct criminal branches. The maximum three-year / NT$1 million language is not a predicted sentence. Fraud/AML acts and knowledge remain separate questions. “Kurungan” describes the short detention punishment; it does not suggest pretrial detention. A broad unsourced statement about false suspect testimony was removed from the final author draft.
- 667: Labor Standards Act §§3/59/60/61; occupational-accident insurance statute §§6/36/37/90/91. The 40-month original-compensation endpoint retains all conditions: still unhealed after two years, designated-hospital assessment of lost original-work capacity, and failure to meet the stated disability-compensation standard. Original wage and average wage are distinguished. No employer-fault proof is added to statutory compensation; §91 damages retain the employer's no-fault defense burden. Unregistered mandatory coverage does not automatically erase insurance benefits. Statutory offsets and two-year compensation / five-year insurance periods retain their separate entitlement starting points.
- 668: Taiwan choice-of-law §20; Civil Code §§227/258/354/356/357/358/359/360/361/364/365. Preserves ordinary/latent immediate-notice duties, intentional-concealment exceptions, risk-transfer defect inquiry, special storage/evidence duties and §358 rebuttable presumption, manifest-unfairness limitation on rescission, generic-goods-only statutory replacement, and distinct damages bases. The earlier of six months after the relevant notice and five years after delivery governs the specified rescission/price-reduction rights; intentional concealment removes the six-month limit only. Initial notice is not given six months, and repair conversations are not declared to suspend every deadline.
