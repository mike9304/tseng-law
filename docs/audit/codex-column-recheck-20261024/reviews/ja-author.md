## What changed

The original articles repeatedly told the reader to check something without explaining what the result would change. Short statements such as 「まず旅券を開きます。」「カードの名称まで確認します。」「日付だけでは判断できません。」 interrupted otherwise connected explanations. The repair rewrites titles, summaries, FAQ and paragraphs, retaining procedural lists only for documents, eligible portals and genuinely comparable deadlines.

704 now follows the application itself: entry status, documents, submission route, fees and collection. 705 separates degree study from Mandarin study and puts professional-entry conditions in the employment section. 706 opens with the effect of a change before the card expires, then distinguishes renewal, registration, a new residence ground and travel. The three introductions are respectively a filing duty, a reader's degree-study question and the consequence of a change in circumstances.

The word 確認 occurs 20→1, 15→1 and 24→2 times respectively, counting frontmatter and the retained source note. This count is descriptive, not an editorial quality score. Most removed occurrences were replaced by an explanation of the relevant condition or an identifiable action, not by a synonym.
