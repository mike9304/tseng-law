## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): 3/5 for guidance pack, 2/5 for columns
variety used: metropolitan French (France), consistent; guillemets « » and fine spaces before : ; ? are in place. The pack reads as German legal French after the G2-1 grammar sweep, not as a Paris firm’s site. Columns 001–002 and 004 are usable once the Korea-as-default holes are closed; 003 Q16–Q20, 005, 006, 008–009 keep a Korean-blog voice. Belgian/Swiss legalisms (`emprisonnement à temps`, `relations personnelles`, `à due concurrence`) sit next to Korea-centric examples a reader in France did not ask for.
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- German pivot in the guidance pack: `groupes d’activité` (Tätigkeitsgruppen), `pour le besoin local` (örtlicher Bedarf), `dans un même flux` (in einem Ablauf).
- Korea left as the default reader where English already labelled it as an example: `"Même lorsqu’une société mère coréenne entre à Taïwan"`.
- Digit-and-calque legalese: `"une détention"` for 拘役, `"emprisonnement à temps"` for 有期徒刑, `"de façon d’ensemble"`, `"comme droit de conflit"`.
- English chrome and Hangul leftovers: `OUR TEAM`, `OFFICES`, `(English)`, Hangul slugs in 005/008/009, English `subsistence` in 006.
- Advertising-risk sentences inherited from the Korean blog (003 typical sentences / “highest compensation”, 005 “at any time”, 008 high-severance anecdote).
- Register split: guidance + 001/002/004/007 are stiff vous-prose; 003 Q16–Q20, 006, 008, 009 keep first-person chat (`"Bonjour, je suis Wei Tseng"`, `"laissez-les dans les commentaires"`).

Column 007’s `droit de visite` in the guidance pack is clearer than the column’s `relations personnelles (會面交往)`. 001–002 and 004 are dense, usable legal French once the Korea-audience holes and `détention`/`PE` issues are closed. 006 is the most natural stretch of prose in this part, apart from the English word *subsistence*.
