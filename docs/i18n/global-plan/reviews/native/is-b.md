## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): n/a for guidance pack (part a: 3/5), 3/5 for columns
variety used: Standard Icelandic (íslenska), consistent; no Faroese. The Danish false friend `stemma við` continues from part a. Thousand-separator `.` and the decimal comma are already right (`1.579.589 TWD`, `0,5 metra`); running dates are already Icelandic (`24. janúar 2022`, `5. júní 2026`). Column 010 already glosses ROC year 109 as 2020.
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- Non-word `mishlöð` for “they vary”: "geta verið mishlöð eftir samningssambandi" (also 011, 012, 014).
- `mishár` / `mishátt` / `mishá` (“of uneven height”) used for “varies”: "hlutfall sameiginlegrar erfðar getur þó verið mishátt".
- Danish `stemma við` for “match”; Icelandic `stemma` means to stop: "stjórnin þarf ekki að stemma við innleggshlutfall".
- `einkamálalög` for the Civil Code (民法); an Icelandic lawyer reads that as civil-procedure law: "197. gr. einkamálalaga Taívan (民法)".
- Masculine `faglegan` on feminine `þjálfun`: "veitir faglegan tækniþjálfun (專業技術培訓)".
- Non-words and broken compounds in the sentence that carries the rule: "ógagnamátt", "staðvera", "séreinkaréttan", "hemlunarfrávik", "millihryggjaþófi".

012 is the cleanest file: the overtaking sequence is readable, the anti-guarantee close works, and `0,5` uses the Icelandic comma. 014 is the fullest labour article and gets replaceability (`leysa af`) and the signing bonus (`undirritunarbónus`) right, but agreement keeps slipping. 016 would be the strongest family-law text if 民法 were not called `einkamálalög`; `forsjá`, `skylduarfur` and `miskabætur` are the right Icelandic terms. 013 and 015 read as short Icelandic blog posts. 017’s definition sentence does not parse.

Gender: Wei Tseng is **ég** in 010 and 013 and **Lögmaður Wei Tseng (曾雋崴)** in 011, 014, 016 and 017. No **hann** for her. **lögmaður** stays the ordinary professional title, as in part a. **hann** in 012 is motorcyclist A; in 014 the employee; in 016 the heir or spouse; in 017 the foreign national who would work in Taiwan. 018 names the four consultation languages. 013 and 015 correctly drop the English “at any time” and the comment box, and they keep the no-immediate-reply line, but the invitation to a “samtal” still does not say that Icelandic is not a consultation language. No win-rate and no result guarantee. `hæfir best` is “best suited”, and `bestu hagsmunum barnsins` is the statutory welfare test.
