## L1 — physician diagnosis for pregnancy rest (all)

Changed. Cancer and 安胎 each require a physician diagnosis. KO: 의사가 요양을 필요하다고 진단한 기간만. JA: 医師が休養の必要を診断した期間に限り. EN: when a physician has diagnosed the need for that rest. ZH: 醫師診斷需要安胎的休養. The same paragraphs keep the leave-classification / Article 33 distinction. No new MOL URL. Article 4 already states 經醫師診斷 for both cancer outpatient care and pregnancy rest; the existing Article 4 link is the citation.

## L2 — ZH insurance exclusion

Changed. ZH no longer says every Article 4(2) day fails Labor Insurance Act Article 33. It says counting those days as hospitalized leave does not by itself constitute Article 33 hospital treatment, and days without actual hospitalization still fall short. The Article 33 link is in that sentence. The half-pay silence after 30 days is attached to 同條第3項, which is Article 4 (the Article 4 link is in the preceding sentence of the same paragraph). The invented five clinic days remain outside Article 33 because there was no hospital stay.

## L3 — 60-day clock (all)

Changed. The period runs from receipt of the complaint by the competent authority or inspection agency. That body investigates as necessary and, within 60 days of receipt, notifies the worker in writing of handling status (處理情形). The drafts do not say the dispute is resolved in 60 days, and they do not start the clock after the investigation. Complaint routes, the retaliation ban, invalidity, and confidentiality stay.

## L4 — EN Article 5 OR

Changed. Body and FAQ use personal leave or special leave, after the Article 4(1) ceiling, worker still unrecovered, then unpaid leave of absence of at most one year (may / 得予). No post-year termination rule was added. Article 13 still names only the Article 50 work-stop period and the Article 59 medical period.

## L5 — foreign law (all)
