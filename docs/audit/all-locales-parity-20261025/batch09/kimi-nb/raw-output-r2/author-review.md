## Meaning preservation and concrete wording decisions

### 915 — public-contract mediation and arbitration

- Original: a time-extension recommendation did not supply statutory consent for the later money claim. Draft: the chronology is presented as money → extension → rejected recommendation → money without a recommendation on that claim. The final annulment on 12 July 2017 and the NT$44,417,150 award remain intact.
- Original: current Article 85-1 is conditional. Draft: “byggearbeider eller tekniske tjenester,” “meklingsanbefaling eller et forslag,” and failure because the entity disagrees preserve the OR conditions and the agency-rejection requirement. A separate arbitration clause remains an independent basis.
- Original: document comparison is analytical, not an added eligibility test. Draft expressly calls it “en praktisk metode” and denies that every changed amount or refined legal argument creates a new dispute.
- Original: ITA opportunities are prospective. Draft uses “ventede” and denies any established open tender, qualification, or contract classification for late 2026/2027.

### 916 — parent-company pollution liability

- Original: ownership alone was insufficient. Draft retains the combined factual themes of control, knowledge, failure to act or disclose, capital reductions, offshore remittances, and evasion of liabilities.
- Original: the disposition was split. Draft uses “opphevet og tilbakeviste” for the portions concerning 246 claimants and “anker som gjaldt 262 klagere, ble avvist” for the other portion. It does not say that all claims ended or that all appeals were filed solely by those claimants.
- Original: Article 154 has cumulative requirements. Draft states all three in one sentence and then says “Samtlige vilkår må være oppfylt.” It also rejects treating the current provision as the sole historical basis for the 2018 decision.
- Original: USTR described conditional intentions. Draft limits the passage to possible financing in Taiwan with US private partners, subject to applicable law, and expressly denies an automatic funding right or an assumption that the entire agreement is in force.

### 918 — presale mortgage notice and handover

- Original: the appeal and additional claim were dismissed on the merits. Draft uses “som ubegrunnede,” not procedural inadmissibility, and retains the court, case identifier, 9 February 2022 date, delay interest, and no-further-appeal statement. The court name was corrected during self-review to `Taiwan High Court, Taichung-avdelingen` rather than a Norwegian phrase that could imply the Supreme Court.
- Original: the performance events were separate. Draft keeps 23 February, the absent utilities, July defects and incomplete elevator installation, August renovation/cleaning access, 5 November loan disbursement, and 28 November warranty handover date distinct. “Ikke fullført” does not assert that no elevator work existed.
- Original: four months was contractual; current point 15 uses six months. Draft ties six months to receipt of the occupancy permit and to notifying the buyer, not to a universal completed-handover deadline. Current point 14 duties remain operative.
- Original: bank processing cannot be excluded from the notification deadline, and the CBC version is not retroactive. Draft preserves the August 2025 audit, states the 18 September 2026 effective date, and expressly denies retroactive application to 2019.

### 919 — workplace bullying investigation and hearing

- Original: a civil servant received two heavy demerits on one occasion and was dismissed. Draft uses “To store anmerkninger i samme disiplinærsak” without inventing a points system. Annulment of the original dismissal and review decision is not described as a finding that no bullying occurred or as proven reinstatement; appealability remains stated.
- Original: the announced outcome, agency sequence, deficient notice and opportunity to speak, contradictory report, and uncured later response mattered. Each remains a separate part of the procedural analysis.
- Original: private employers fall under the new OSH regime. Draft retains the 1 July 2026 effective date and Article 22-2 requirements for objective, impartial and fair handling, adequate explanation and defence, and conflicts. “Mekling” was replaced with “koordinering” where the source concerns coordination: it may follow the complainant's wish, while unsuccessful coordination must be followed by continued investigation.
- Original: records advice is practical. Draft calls it “en praktisk dokumentasjonsanbefaling … ikke en ny lovfestet formregel” and does not promise that preventive measures guarantee no recurrence.

### 920 — apartment EV charger

- Original: the installation extended beyond the parking space. Draft preserves the common wall, ceiling, electrical box, breaker and connection to the resident's meter, while expressly stating that legal ownership of the meter was not adjudicated.
- Original: Article 11 and bylaw 15 supplied different requirements. Draft retains the owners' assembly route for the statutory work and both committee permission and power-company safety inspection under the bylaw. Article 6(1)(5), formal coordination, continuing noncompliance and Article 6(3) remain connected.
- Original: Article 6(1)(4) did not govern this case. Draft states the court's non-applicability conclusion neutrally and attributes no argument to an unidentified party. It does not refer to a third paragraph for this provision.
- Original: stop/use-lock/key assertions were considered even if true. Draft now says their legal effect was assessed on that assumption and that they should not be read as proven facts. The 22 June 2022 dismissal and removal/restoration outcome remain, with later finality expressly unknown.
- Original: the Kaohsiung grant did not confer installation permission. Draft retains the expired 30 September deadline and separates support eligibility from common-area consent.

### 921 — online-game coins

- Original: NT$243,548 was claimed, not awarded. Draft states this expressly. It also preserves Keelung District Court 109年度消簡上字第1號, 13 September 2021, dismissal of the player's appeal, and no further appeal.
- Original: remaining coins served as competitive chips while no unused paid points remained. Draft does not say that all value had been consumed, and it rejects any blanket exclusion of virtual assets from refunds.
- Original: current rules postdate the case. Draft retains the 1 January 2023 effective date and separates written rescission within seven days from starting play from termination at any time. Unused paid points or unused game fees, necessary-cost deductions, 30 days from termination, and mutually agreed handling all remain.
- Original: access to game history and independent evidence matter. Draft retains minimum 30-day retention, provision within seven days of a request, and the ban on making company data the sole criterion without declaring company data invalid.
- Original: the 2025 statistics concerned first-stage complaints. Draft uses “første trinns forbrukerklager,” second-largest category, and a year-on-year decrease. The title limits the denial to the Keelung case rather than predicting all future payment claims.
