# connectors — 001 en: 원문에 없는 문구·변경 (draft.md 기준, 전부 기록)

새 문장은 없다. 아래는 원문 문장에 가한 최소 변경과 새 링크 문구다.

C1 (§1, 004 링크, R2 수정) — 원문 문장 그대로, 기존 단어에 링크만:
`[When comparing organizational forms](/en/columns/taiwan-company-subsidiary-vs-branch), the business must therefore consider not only the scope of liability but also …`
(삽입 구 없음, "therefore" 유지)

C2 (§3 L89 s3 → L87 뒤) — 원문 "The required registrations and liabilities may also differ depending on …" → draft "… may differ depending on …" (삭제 "also")

C3 (§3, 015 링크, 기존 단어에 링크만) — `Taipei City operates an [advance inquiry for business premises](/en/columns/taiwan-company-setup-pitch-location) (營業場所預先查詢) for company and business registrations within its scope.` (원문 문장 그대로, 링크만 추가)

C4 (§4, 029 링크, 기존 단어에 링크만) — `As a general rule, an ordinary foreign national applying for [permanent residence](/en/columns/taiwan-permanent-residence-aprc) must have lawfully resided …` (원문 문장 그대로, 링크만 추가)

C5 (§4 자본 소절, 서식) — 원문 L109 s4·s5의 세미콜론 나열을 같은 단어로 글머리 목록화. 도입 문장 "For a company or branch established for less than one year, the employer generally must satisfy at least one of the following:" / "For a company or branch established for at least one year, the employer generally must satisfy at least one of the following, measured by the most recent year in Taiwan or the average of the preceding three years:" 는 원문 그대로. 항목은 첫 글자 대문자, 끝의 ";"·"or" 삭제:
- Paid-in capital or Taiwan working capital of at least NT$500,000
- Revenue of at least NT$3 million
- Import-export performance of at least US$500,000
- Agency commissions of at least US$200,000
(1년 이상 목록은 위 둘째~넷째 항목)

C5b (§4 자본 소절) — 원문 "A representative office established for at least one year must have a record of activities in Taiwan; this requirement is waived if it has been established for less than one year." → draft "… record of activities in Taiwan. This requirement is waived if it has been established for less than one year." (";"→".", "this"→"This")

C6 (§1→§5, PE 문단, R2 수정) — 원문 L45 s3은 R2에서 절 포함 원문 그대로 복원(변경 없음).
원문 L47 s1 "Under the agreement, a PE may include fixed facilities …" → draft "A PE may include fixed facilities …" (삭제 "Under the agreement,", "a"→"A"; 바로 앞 문장이 "under the agreement"로 끝나 중복).

C7 (§1→§5) — 원문 L45 라벨 "Country-specific note — Taiwan–Korea Income Tax Agreement:" 삭제(절 이동으로 라벨 불필요). 협정 문단 첫 문장은 L45 s1 "The Taiwan–Korea Income Tax Agreement entered into force on December 27, 2023, and applies from January 1, 2024." 그대로.

C8 (§5, "앞서 설명한 … 고정사업장 유형" 처리) — 원문 L125 s2 "When determining the right to tax business profits, all four PE categories discussed above must be considered." 와 s3 은 삭제(연결 구로 고치지 않음). 이유: 합친 문단 안에서 PE 4유형 바로 뒤에 L47 s2·s3 "These four categories have different conditions …"이 이어져 같은 요구를 하며, "discussed above"가 가리키던 §1 문단이 §5로 옮겨 왔다. 따라서 §1을 가리키는 문장이 draft에 남아 있지 않다.

C9 (프런트매터 FAQ3 a, 축약) — draft:
`Taiwan does not impose a generally applicable statutory minimum capital requirement for company formation.` (원문 L109 s1 그대로)
`Industry-specific laws may require separate capital or security deposits, and a bank may independently review the business plan and transaction risks.` (원문 L111 s2 그대로, 본문과 동일)
`For a foreign manager’s work permit, a company or branch established for less than one year generally must meet at least one of four employer thresholds (paid-in capital or Taiwan working capital of at least NT$500,000, or specified revenue, import-export, or agency-commission levels), unless the same employer hires more than one person through this route, in which case the general standards apply instead.` (원문 L109 s4의 축약 + F-010 반영 — 새 문구)

C10 (서식·순서, 문구 변경 없음) — 문단 합침/이동: L37+L39 한 문단; L79+L81 s1 한 문단; §4 L109 s1+L111 s2+L109 s3(+대표처 두 문장) 한 문단; L109 s8+L111 s1+s3 한 문단; L113·L115를 자본 소절에서 거류 소절로 이동; L103을 L105 뒤로. L101 s1을 H3 첫 문장으로 유지(문구 그대로).

C11 (출처 목록, fact fix F-009) — 링크 라벨 교체: `Taiwan Laws & Regulations Database — General Business Tax Rate` → `Standards of Withholding Rates for Various Incomes, Article 3 (dividends paid to non-residents)` (URL 동일). 출처 1개 추가: `Value-added and Non-value-added Business Tax Act, Article 10` → https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=G0340080&flno=10. 본문 문장은 변경 없음.

C12 (§4 자본 소절 마지막 문단 끝, fact fix F-010) — 리드가 지정한 새 문장(원문에 없음), 그대로 삽입: `If the same employer hires more than one person through this route, the foreign national’s and the employer’s qualifications are reviewed under the general standards in Chapter 2 instead (Qualifications and Criteria Standards for Foreigners Undertaking Jobs under Article 46, Paragraph 1, Subparagraphs 1 to 6 of the Employment Service Act, Article 38(2)).`

C11 갱신 (R2) — 라벨 문구: `Standards of Withholding Rates for Various Incomes, Article 3 (withholding on dividends)`, `Value-added and Non-value-added Business Tax Act, Article 10 (statutory range 5%-10%; the applied rate is set by the Executive Yuan)`. URL 불변.
