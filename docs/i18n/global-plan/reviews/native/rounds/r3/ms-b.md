systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):

- "objek" still stands in for the thing a rule covers or the subject of a review: "boleh menjadi objek denda pentadbiran"
- "justifikasi" still stands in for supporting documents or evidence: "kaitan sebab dengan kemalangan, justifikasi, nisbah tanggungjawab"

- Noun modifiers still follow the English stack, and a place is still inserted before the object: "Jenama asing kosmetik yang berhasrat mengedarkan"
