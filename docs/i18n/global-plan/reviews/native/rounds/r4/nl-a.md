## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): 4/5 for guidance pack, 4/5 for columns
variety used: Standaardnederlands (Nederland), u-aanspreking, consistent. Thousand separator is `.` (`500.000`, `3.000.000`); the decimal comma is in `0,5` and `1,57 miljoen TWD`. Running dates are Dutch (`13 september 2025`, `27 december 2023`, `1 januari 2024`, `17 november 2021`, `25 juli 2026`, `29 mei 2026`, `1 juli 2026`). A few Belgian items remain intelligible in the Netherlands: `naargelang`, `onderstellen`/`ondersteld`, `laattijdig`, `op dit ogenblik`, `artificiële intelligentie`. `klachtdelict` is the shared legal term.
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):

- A Chinese gloss still sits on the wrong Dutch words in 007, and 004 still numbers a source the URL does not open: "het mislukken van het huwelijk (子女最佳利益)"
