## Issue 1 — 戸籍法第48条: the start of the application period, and how the receipt date of a judgment / record relates to finality / establishment

### Verdict 1a — start of the 30-day period: ORIGINAL-RIGHT (ja, ko, en, zh-hant)

第48條第1項 keys the period to 「事件發生或確定後三十日內」. For a divorce judgment the registrable fact is fixed when the judgment becomes final (確定); for court mediation and settlement it arises when they are established (家事事件法第30條第1、2項, 第45條第1、2項). All four versions say thirty days counted from finality of the judgment or establishment of the mediation / settlement. That matches the article; the "generally / 一般" qualifier is consistent with the sixty-day proviso for birth registration. No change.

### Verdict 1b — receipt date versus finality / establishment: PARTLY (ja only); ko, en, zh-hant ORIGINAL-RIGHT

What is right: the legal conclusion in all four versions — the day a judgment or record is served or received is not the start of the period — follows from 第48條第1項, which names 事件發生或確定 and nothing else.

What is wrong, in ja only: the added condition 「受け取った日が、確定や成立より前だというだけなら」 ("if the receipt date merely precedes finality or establishment").
1. For a 調停調書 (調解筆錄) the order is the reverse. The record is served within ten days after the mediation is established (民事訴訟法第421條第3項, applied through 家事事件法第32條第3項); the same holds for a 和解筆錄 (民事訴訟法第379條第3項). Its receipt date cannot precede 成立.
2. For a judgment the order depends on the case: an appealable judgment becomes final when the appeal period, which runs from service, expires (第398條第1項, 第440條), so receipt comes first; a judgment that cannot be appealed is final on pronouncement or public notice (第398條第2項), so finality comes first.
3. The condition also narrows the rule. As written, it only warns against a receipt date that is earlier than finality / establishment, which leaves the reader free to count from a receipt date that is later — the mediation-record case, where counting from receipt would put the deadline later than 第48條第1項 does. The statute makes no such distinction.

Correct statement (as ko / en / zh-hant already have it): the date a judgment or record is served or received is not the common starting point; the period runs from finality or establishment regardless of when the document arrives. ko, en and zh-hant were used as the reference and are left unchanged.
