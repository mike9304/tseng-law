## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): n/a for guidance pack (part a: 3/5), 2/5 for columns
variety used: Bokmål intended; 010–013 and 015 are stiff comma-heavy Bokmål (German *Sie* → capital **De**). 014, 016 and 017 mix Danish into the Norwegian surface (`Efter`, `rimeligt`, `situationer`, `at` + infinitive, relative `der`, `siger`, `udlannet`, `stk.`, `Medmindre`). 017 also drops Swedish `påtänkte` and English `taking`. Not a Bokmål/Nynorsk mix.
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- Capital **De / Deres / Dem** throughout (German *Sie*); 2026 Norwegian sites use **du**. "Har De pådratt Dem en skade i et treningssenter"
- Danish leakage flooding 014/016/017: "Denne artikkelen skiller 3 situationer … at stifte … eller modtage overdragelsen"
- German thousands `.` (`TWD 25.000.000`, `1.579.589 TWD`) instead of space (`25 000 000 TWD`).
- FAQ answers pasted as body openings: sections start with "Nei." / "Det er ikke den samme prosedyren." with no question above them (011, 014, 016, 017).
- Digit-style legalese (`1. instans`, `tredjemann (3)`, `2 barn`, `rang 1`) instead of spelled forms.
- 013/015 still read as a Korean blog (😊, "legg igjen en kommentar", `Q.`), while 011/014/016/017 read as a German statute commentary.

Gender: Wei Tseng is correctly **jeg** (010, 013) / ungendered **advokat**. No masculine form, pronoun, or title for her. "han" in 012 is motorcyclist A; "tildeles ham" in 014 is a generic employee, not the attorney.

012 (overtaking) is genuinely the strongest file in this batch: clear structure, working anti-guarantee close, no Korea-reader leftover, no `/ko/` links. 014 and 017 are the weakest: 014 would mislead a Norwegian labour lawyer (*tegningspremie*, *erstatningsmulighet*); 017 is not readable Bokmål.
