## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): N/A for guidance pack (out of scope, part a), 4/5 for columns
variety used: standard Latvian (latviešu literārā valoda) as used in Latvia, consistent; no Latgalian forms, no Lithuanian or Russian interference. Quotation marks are Latvian „…“ throughout. The decimal comma is right (0,5 metru), the percent sign keeps its space (100 %), and in-body dates are correct Latvian (2022. gada 24. janvārī, 2026. gada 1. jūlijā, 2026. gada 5. jūnijā, 2026. gada 25. jūnijā, 2023. gada 1. janvāra). Two presentation defects survive from round 1: thousands are still split with a dot (TWD 1.579.589) and the byline still puts the day before the year (13. septembrī 2025. gadā) while the body of the same file uses the year-first form. 013 and 015 address the reader with the polite plural and keep it consistently.
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- A negated verb is coordinated with *un arī* where Latvian needs *ne … ne* / *nedz … nedz*: "nedrīkst dzēst, atdodot (繳銷), un arī nodot citam turētājam" (also 014, 017:115, 017:119)
- Debitive (*jā-*) clauses with no logical subject, or with the dative attached to the wrong noun so the obligation lands on the wrong person: "Kandidātam, kurš ilgstoši dzīvo ārvalstīs"
- Thousands are still split with a dot instead of a space: "TWD 1.579.589" (also 011, 017, 018)
- *gaita* stands in for every procedure, process and workstream, including safety procedures: "pamata drošības gaitas norādījums"
- Invisible U+200B paragraphs are still on disk in 013 and 015: "pietiek norādīt vietu (piemēram, Taipejas pilsēta)."
- Abstract loan-noun calques replace a plain Latvian verb: "Tiesiskā atbildības pieskaitīšana" (also "atbrīvošanas izpausme", "piemērošanas priekšmetā")

012 is native professional apart from the headlight verb and three short calques; 013 is clean apart from the invisible characters, the mistranslated *By contrast* and one duplicated closing sentence; 010 is the strongest file in this set — the round-4 fixes for the safety-level sentence, the photographed body part and the section-5 heading all landed, and only the number format and one preposition case remain. 011, 016, 017 and 018 are professional with local defects. 014 is readable and much improved (the round-4 A-findings are gone), but it still carries most of the grammar left in this batch: three subjectless or wrongly-cased debitive clauses, the *un arī* negation pattern, and a mistranslated "continuous service". 015 still stutters: the round-4 fix rewrote the first zoning sentence but left the two that repeat it, one of which has no English counterpart at all. 017's body was fixed for the freight-forwarding list but its frontmatter FAQ copy was not, so the FAQ and the article now disagree.

Product facts hold. Attorney Wei Tseng is feminine everywhere she is named or referred to (010 *procesuālā pārstāve*, *advokātes rakstveida pieprasījumu*; 011 *atbildīgo advokāti*, *Advokāte Wei Tseng*; 013 *man kā advokātei*; 014, 016, 017 *Advokāte*; 018 *atbildīgās advokātes*); no masculine form for her appears in this set. The consultation lock holds: 013, 015 and 018 name only English, Chinese (中文), Japanese and Korean, 013 says site comments are not handled, and nothing offers an interpreter, a free first meeting, a reply time or 24/7 contact. No win rate, guarantee or "best/only" claim appears: every *garantē* is negated or means a product warranty, *labāks* in 018 sits inside "nesola … ka kāds veids ir likumīgs vai labāks", and *vienīgais* is only about sole owners and sole shareholders.
