# A Taiwan custodian is slowing withdrawals. Is the new virtual-asset law already protecting the coins?

If a chat group says the new Virtual Asset Service Act (虛擬資產服務法) already protects coins you keep with a Taiwan exchange or custodian, that protection has not started. As of November 11, 2026, the [national law database](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawHistory.aspx?pcode=G0400163) lists the Act as not yet in force, with no effective date set. Promulgation was July 22, 2026, and [Article 56](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=G0400163&flno=56) leaves the commencement date to the Executive Yuan (行政院). [Article 18](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=G0400163&flno=18) would exclude custodied customer assets from the bankruptcy estate (破產財團). Customers cannot use that exclusion yet.

What already binds a custodian is the registration regulation for businesses and persons that provide virtual-asset services (提供虛擬資產服務之事業或人員洗錢防制登記辦法). The Financial Supervisory Commission (金融監督管理委員會) issued it on November 26, 2024. It has applied since November 30, 2024. [Article 23](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=G0400162&flno=23) requires a custodian (虛擬資產保管商) to keep assets held for customers separate from the firm's own property.

## Which registration duties already apply

Keeping or managing virtual assets, or the tools for doing so, is what [Article 2](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=G0400162&flno=2) calls a custodian, and swapping virtual assets for currency or for other virtual assets is what it calls an exchange (虛擬資產交換商). [Article 3](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=G0400162&flno=3) requires registration for each category the firm runs. Without that registration, the firm must not operate. [Articles 23](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=G0400162&flno=23) to [25](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=G0400162&flno=25) directly govern a custodian. An exchange that keeps customer fiat money under Article 11 must also follow Articles 23 and 25 to 27 by analogy, but not Article 24. The category on the approval matters.

An agreement that ownership of the customer's virtual assets transfers to the custodian is forbidden by [Article 24](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=G0400162&flno=24). Customer virtual assets must be kept apart from the custodian's own virtual assets, and an agreement to mix the two is forbidden. Except on the customer's instruction, to set off a fee the customer owes, or for another reason the Commission permits, [Article 25](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=G0400162&flno=25) says the custodian must not use the customer's assets. Those duties can support an argument that specific coins are not the firm's property. The regulation never says that about a given coin.

Cold-wallet (冷錢包) and hot-wallet (熱錢包) shares must be in the public custody policy [Article 26](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=G0400162&flno=26) requires, and that policy must sit in the internal controls. The Commission sets that share separately. This regulation states no percentage. [Article 27](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=G0400162&flno=27) requires a per-customer record of name, wallet address, amount, quantity, and transfers in and out, kept and given to the customer for five years after the custody relationship ends, or until a dispute ends. Reconciliation must be periodic and ongoing, and at least yearly the custodian must appoint an accountant to issue a report and must publish it.

With the customer's consent, [Article 11](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=G0400162&flno=11) lets the provider hold legal tender in a dedicated deposit account in the same currency that the provider opens at a financial institution and requires that balance to be placed in trust or covered by a bank's full performance guarantee, after which [Articles 23](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=G0400162&flno=23) and [25](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=G0400162&flno=25) through [27](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=G0400162&flno=27) apply by analogy while [Article 24](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=G0400162&flno=24) stays outside the cross-reference.

The anti-money-laundering registration approval number and date must be shown in a prominent place on the webpage or through another announcement method designated by the FSC, as [Article 14](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=G0400162&flno=14) requires. [Article 15](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=G0400162&flno=15) requires service records to be kept for five years after the relationship ends, or until a dispute ends if there is one.

## Contract label and a bankruptcy declaration

[Article 589](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=B0000001&flno=589) of the Civil Code (民法) calls it a deposit (寄託) when one person delivers a thing and the other agrees to keep it. [Article 590](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=B0000001&flno=590) requires a paid keeper to use a good administrator's care (善良管理人), and an unpaid keeper to use the care given to their own affairs.

Fungible things are a consumption deposit (消費寄託) under [Article 602](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=B0000001&flno=602) when the parties agree that ownership passes to the keeper and the keeper returns the same kind, quality, and quantity. Consumption-loan rules apply by analogy from receipt. If a return date is fixed, the depositor must not demand the thing early without an unavoidable reason, unless commercial custom provides otherwise.

An ownership-transfer clause for a custodian's customer virtual assets is what [Article 24](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=G0400162&flno=24) forbids. The regulation does not call that clause civilly void. It also does not place any coin outside the company's property. So is the customer the owner? The terms, read with these articles, decide.

Money (金錢) that is deposited is presumed a consumption deposit under [Article 603](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=B0000001&flno=603). A New Taiwan dollar balance fits that presumption. Digital New Taiwan dollars, foreign currency, currency issued in mainland China, Hong Kong, or Macau, securities, and other financial assets issued under law sit outside virtual assets in [Article 2](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=G0400162&flno=2).

At a bankruptcy declaration, [Article 82 of the Bankruptcy Act](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=B0010006&flno=82) (破產法) takes in all property then belonging to the bankrupt, future property claims, and property acquired before the proceeding ends. Strictly personal rights, and property that cannot be seized, stay out. [Article 110](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=B0010006&flno=110) lets the right-holder recover (取回) from the trustee (破產管理人), outside the bankruptcy procedure, property that does not belong to the bankrupt. Recovery is available if these coins are not the company's property. [Article 98](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=B0010006&flno=98) treats a claim against the bankrupt as a bankruptcy claim (破產債權) only when it arose before the declaration, except a claim with a right of exclusion (別除權). If the consumption-deposit claim arose against the debtor before the bankruptcy declaration, [Article 98](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=B0010006&flno=98) and [Article 99](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=B0010006&flno=99) treat it as a bankruptcy claim that may be exercised only through the bankruptcy procedure.

## Customer-asset rules still waiting on a date

No commencement date has been fixed. None of these Act rules operate yet.

Once the Act is in force, [Article 18](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=G0400163&flno=18) applies to a virtual-asset service provider (虛擬資產服務商) that keeps customer assets. Separation from the firm's own property must follow the competent authority's method. Creditors must not demand those assets or exercise other rights over them, and in bankruptcy they are not part of the estate. The provider must not use them, or instruct a financial institution to use them, except on the customer's instruction, to set off a fee debt as the law allows, or for another reason the authority permits. The registration regulation states no creditor bar and no estate exclusion.

[Article 26 of the Act](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=G0400163&flno=26) states that property rights in the customer virtual assets belong to the customer (財產權歸屬於客戶). The custodian must not agree to transfer those rights to itself and must not mix the customer's virtual assets with its own. The registration regulation forbids that agreement and that mixing, and it does not state that the property rights belong to the customer.

Withdrawals and transfers out must not be refused, [Article 20](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=G0400163&flno=20) says, except under another law or a special circumstance the authority prescribes. The provider may suspend transfers in, withdrawals, transfers out, or some or all trading when a transaction is suspected to be unlawful or is clearly abnormal. The tests are left to a later rule. [Article 19](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=G0400163&flno=19) would, with the customer's consent, allow legal tender to stay in a dedicated same-currency deposit account the provider opens at a financial institution and would require that balance to be placed in trust or covered by a bank's full performance guarantee.

Under [Article 55](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=G0400163&flno=55), a provider that finished anti-money-laundering registration under paragraph 1 of Article 6 of the Money Laundering Control Act (洗錢防制法) before commencement, or a financial institution already offering these services under the authority's rules, must apply within twelve months and must hold both the permission and the license within twenty-one months. A miss on the application, or on the permission and the license, means the firm must not continue. The license deadline may be extended once, by three months, when necessary. Both periods run from that unset date.

## What to keep while withdrawals slow

Keep the accepted terms and their date, any clause on ownership or on mixing customer coins with the firm's own, the wallet addresses, the transaction identifiers, and each withdrawal request with the reply. Save the approval number and date with them. If the firm is a custodian, ask for the per-customer record [Article 27](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=G0400162&flno=27) requires the firm to keep and provide.
