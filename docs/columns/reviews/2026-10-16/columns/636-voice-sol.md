# C8 cross-review and native-voice edit — 2026-10-16

Scope: `drafts/C8/ko.md`, `ja.md`, `en.md`, `zh-hant.md` and their shared `facts.md`. I checked the fetched Chinese text of Company Act Articles 15 and 8 against the current official single-article pages. The whole-law amendment date appears only in each sources list, not as an amendment date for either article. The four versions keep the same legal core: company funds lent to another Taiwan company; actual business dealings under Article 15(1)(1) or necessary short-term financing under Article 15(1)(2); only item 2 has the express 40% cap measured against the lender's net worth; a responsible person who violates paragraph 1 is jointly liable with the borrower for return, and owes damages if the company suffers damage.

Official text: [Company Act Article 15](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=J0080001&flno=15), [Article 8](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=J0080001&flno=8), and the [whole-law header](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawAll.aspx?pcode=J0080001).
