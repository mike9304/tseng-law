## First-two-paragraph deletion test

### 704

Opening type: concrete deadline and its trigger.

- Paragraph 1, sentence 1 identifies the incoming-resident group, ARC meaning and 30-day obligation. Removing it loses the article's practical deadline and addressee.
- Sentence 2 states that counting begins the day after entry. It cannot be merged away as mere rhythm; the starting day is legally material.
- Sentence 3 supplies Article 22 and the exemption for a valid document already incorporating ARC functionality, including the integrated four-permit card. Deleting it broadens the obligation.
- Paragraph 2, sentence 1 restricts in-country applications after visa-free or visitor entry to qualifying routes. Sentence 2 prevents mistaking the first-ARC period for permission to convert status. Sentence 3 names Article 23 or the professionals' Article 16 as distinct routes. Each sentence adds scope or legal basis.

### 705

Opening type: reader's actual eligibility question, “¿Puedo solicitar residencia si entro sin visado?”

- The question establishes the visa-free-entry condition that “permite hacerlo” in the next sentence answers; removing it loses that condition.
- Paragraph 1, sentence 2 identifies the conditional professional/family route under Article 16. Sentence 3 distinguishes ordinary study-route restrictions by entry status. Neither is a generic advance description of the article.
- Paragraph 2, sentence 1 assigns visa handling to BOCA/overseas offices and residence/ARC decisions to NIA. Sentence 2 identifies the resident-visa purpose and more-than-six-month horizon. Sentence 3 explains that qualifying direct NIA applications dispense with obtaining a new resident visa first. The three statements answer different procedural questions.

### 706

Opening type: concrete consequence — cancellation may precede the date printed on the ARC.

- Paragraph 1, sentence 1 identifies the loss of the residence ground as the trigger. Sentence 2 supplies Article 31, revocation/cancellation and the possibility of continuing under exceptions. Deleting either loses the consequence, source or qualification.
- Paragraph 2, sentence 1 distinguishes a move while the original residence ground continues. Sentence 2 gives the address/workplace registration obligation, NIA recipient and 30 days from the next day. Sentence 3 states that the later renewal does not defer that obligation. These were kept as practical distinctions, not opening decoration.
