## Issues found (Original → problem & reason → fix → facts preserved)

### ko

1. 「대만에서 신청을 받는 곳은 재정부(국제재정사)이고, 기한은 첫 과세통지부터 3년입니다.」 → The department (國際財政司) is a fact taken from the MOF release of 2020-08-14, but ko had no inline link for it and the release was missing from ko's sources list (brief rule 2: cite inline; sources complete). → Applied: inline link `[재정부 2020년 8월 14일 발표](…cntId=d0c33526270b4074a56300013f2b8973)` right after the claim, and the release added to 「참고한 공식 자료」. This is a citation addition, not a change of wording or meaning; flagged here so the coordinator can revert it if additions by the reviewer are out of scope. → Fact unchanged; source verified (release text 「向我國主管機關(該部國際財政司)提出MAP申請」).
2. 「4. 국세국이 수리하지 않거나 일부만 인정해 신청인이 이의를 제기하고 신청을 철회하지 않으면, …」 → 作業要點 第九點第三項 names three triggers: 「不受理、不同意全部或同意部分…經申請人異議者」; the draft omitted the middle case (case accepted but adjustment refused entirely). Minor completeness gap, same consequence. → Applied: 「수리하지 않거나, 조정을 거부하거나 일부만 인정해 …」. → 30-day MOF request, objection and non-withdrawal conditions unchanged.
3. 「3년은 한국의 과세통지를 처음 받은 날부터 셉니다(작업요점 제5점). 그 통지서에 찍힌 날짜가 대만 신청 기한도 정합니다.」 → Second sentence could be read as the date printed on the notice; 第五點 counts from the date of first receipt (「首次接獲…之日起」), which the first sentence already says. → Applied: 「그 통지서를 받은 날이 대만 신청 기한의 기산일입니다.」 → 3 years, 第五點 citation, receipt-date rule preserved.

### ja

4. 「国税局が受理しない、あるいは一部しか認めない場合はどうでしょうか。」 → Same omission as ko item 2. → Applied: 「国税局が受理しない、調整を認めない、あるいは一部しか認めない場合はどうでしょうか。」 → Deadlines and conditions unchanged.
5. 「金融口座の情報は、日台間で自動的に交換されています。」 → The present-tense assertion implies exchanges have already taken place; the brief lists the date of the first 2020 exchange as UNVERIFIED and the two cited MOF documents establish only the reportable-jurisdiction list (2021-04-16) and the planned September 2026 exchange. → Applied: 「日台間で自動交換の対象になっています。」 (Japan is a designated exchange partner), which the next two sentences then date. → 2021-04-16 notice, 2019年度以後, 145,530 accounts, September 2026, MOF's statement on use of CRS data all preserved.
6. 「日本で届いた通知の日付が、台湾での期限も決めます。」 → Same imprecision as ko item 3 (date of the notice vs date received). → Applied: 「日本の親会社がその通知を受け取った日が、台湾での期限の起算日です。」 → 第五點 rule preserved.

### en

7. 「A case may be presented "irrespective of the remedies provided by the domestic law" of the two sides.」 → Presented as a verbatim quotation covering all three agreements, but the Japan text reads "irrespective of the remedies provided by the law of those Territories" (no "domestic"); only Korea and Vietnam say "domestic law". → Applied: quotation marks removed, wording kept as a paraphrase. → Meaning unchanged.
8. 「For this kind of request, the three years run from the first tax notice issued by the partner (Art. 5).」 → Directions Art 5 (EN): "three years from the date of receiving the first taxation notification issued by the other Contracting Party"; "run from the first tax notice" leaves the start (issue vs receipt) open. → Applied: "run from receipt of the first tax notice issued by the partner (Art. 5)". → Period and citation unchanged.
9. 「a 1988 exchange of letters relieving income from the international operation of ships and aircraft」 → "relieving income" is not idiomatic; the letters "exempt from tax gross income derived from the international operation of ships or aircraft". → Applied: "exempting income from …". → Scope of the 1988 letters unchanged.

### zh-hant

10. 「如果國稅局不受理或只同意一部分，申請人有異議又不撤回的，財政部30日內向對方主管機關提出相互協議請求。」 → Same omission as ko item 2 (第九點第三項 「不受理、不同意全部或同意部分」). → Applied: 「如果國稅局不受理、不同意或只同意一部分，…」 → 30-day rule and conditions unchanged.
