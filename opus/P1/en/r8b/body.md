Nov 28, 2025, Taipei. A renter viewed a unit; rent, a 1-year minimum and rules came up.

At 2:51 p.m. they wired NT$21,800 and sent a LINE calling it 承租訂金, a "rental deposit."

That day they backed out, saying their stuff wouldn't fit and nothing was signed. The firm refused a refund.

In court, the renter filed that LINE.

The trial court found a deal before signing; the LINE supported it. It put backing out over space on the renter, with no company fault shown. The claim and interest failed.
