## Professional Italian and practical content

704 gives the applicable clock before the document checklist, separates NIA from BOCA, and follows filing through approval/payment/card collection. Terms such as `domanda`, `proroga`, `centro di servizio`, `certificato di iscrizione`, `ricongiungimento` and `permesso di reingresso` are usable professional Italian. It does not import an Italian Questura procedure. Its references to Italy concern certificate origin and the appropriate mission, not presumed citizenship. The standard fee, special-category limits and lack of universal work rights remain bounded.

705 starts from university admission and then distinguishes degree study, Mandarin, professional employment, specific migrant-worker procedures and family grounds. Article 16 is described as category-limited and conditional; the text never promises conversion for every visa-free visitor. Passport citizenship, actual entry title and intended activity remain separate. It states the Taiwan-national spouse’s residence-approved exemption without claiming every dependent ARC grants work permission. It does not attempt a full eligibility table for trial visa exemptions/TAC or every specialist-dependent work route, and gives no nationality-specific guarantee. The Gold Card and entrepreneur paragraphs are short, sourced branches. No Italian-law or firm service credentials are invented.

706 distinguishes continued-purpose renewal, address/workplace registration, purpose change, loss/replacement and departure. Ordinary three-month renewal is expressly non-universal, and the entrepreneur four-month business-based route is separated from the three-month no-business route. Address/workplace timing starts the next day; eligible purpose-change timing starts on the event day. The professional-online departure statement was checked against the actual Chinese notice §9; the separate student sentence is limited to visitor-entry applications. No universal six-month passport condition, automatic grace period, work authorization or guaranteed return is stated. IT706-R1-01 is the one source-sensitive procedural wording defect.

No fabricated personal/client experience, public human/AI byline, attorney/native-review claim, promotional consultation promise or unsupported promise of Italian consultation appears in the three drafts. No contact CTA is necessary for these practical guides.
