## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): N/A for guidance pack (out of scope; part a), 4/5 for columns
variety used: Italian of Italy, consistent. Lei when 013 and 015 address the reader; 010 and 013 keep first person. Dates are day–month–year (24 gennaio 2022, 4 febbraio 2026, 5 giugno 2026, 25 giugno 2026, 17 settembre 2026). The thousands separator is the Italian dot (1.579.589, 40.000, 25.000.000, 30.000.000, 100.000.000) and the decimal comma is right (0,5 metri); do not replace the dot with a space or a comma. The first of the month is still « 1 luglio » / « 1 gennaio » instead of « 1° ». 010–012 use the straight apostrophe; 013–018 use U+2019. Guillemets « » are used for cited labels.
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- An English “need not” or “seek” becomes the opposite instruction: "il controllo non deve coincidere con la proporzione dei conferimenti".
- “Whether” becomes a condition, so the questions no longer govern the verb: "Se i coniugi hanno convenuto un altro regime".
- The first of the month drops the ordinal: "A decorrere dal 1 luglio 2026".
- 013 and 015 still hide U+200B, including after "città di Taipei".
- A legal noun names the neighbouring thing: "entra nell'interdizione di sorpassare".
- An instruction loses its subject mid-sentence: "si usi il segnale di clacson o di fari prescritto, senza forzare il veicolo davanti a cedere, attenda un segnale".

No file is free of defects. 012 is native professional apart from the mood break and « interdizione ». 015 is the same register apart from the date, the blank characters, and the dangling « un altro ». 013 is professional apart from those characters and « locale commerciale di uffici ». 010, 011, 017 and 018 are professional with a short cluster each. 014 and 016 are usable, but each still has one sentence a client would act on wrongly. Attorney Wei Tseng is feminine wherever she is named (010 « Sono stata l'avvocata » ; 011 « Avvocata Wei Tseng » and « l'avvocata responsabile » ; 014 « avvocata a Taiwan » ; 016 and 017 « L'avvocata Wei Tseng »). 012 and 018 do not name her. 013 and 015 say « un'avvocata o un avvocato », not her. No masculine form for her remains. The consultation lock holds: no reply-time promise in 013 and 015 ; 018 asks for the preferred language and says the languages actually available must be confirmed at contact. No interpreter, no free meeting, no result guarantee, no win rate. « solo la società controllata » in 018 and « non garantisce di per sé » in 012 are not advertising claims. « interesse superiore del figlio (子女最佳利益) » is the statutory standard. « fattori che si considerano » in 011 is the correct impersonal, and « cessione del passo » in 012 is the right traffic term. The section jump from 5 to 7 in 018 matches the English source.
