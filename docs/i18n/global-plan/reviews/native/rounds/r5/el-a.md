## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): 4/5 for guidance pack, 4/5 for columns
variety used: Standard Modern Greek as written in Greece, monotonic, formal plural, consistent — no Cypriot forms. Thousands take a dot (500.000, 3.000.000, 2.500.000) and decimals a comma (1,57 εκατ., 0,5 μήνα); dates are day-first, in words (13 Σεπτεμβρίου 2025) or D/M/YYYY (27/12/2023, 1/1/2024, 29/5/2026, 25/7/2026); currency follows the amount (νέα δολάρια Ταϊβάν), except the inherited «USD 500.000». Guillemets «…» and the curly apostrophe ’ are used throughout, no space before punctuation, the Greek question mark (;) everywhere. The register is deliberately archaizing legal Greek (κατ’ αρχήν, ανωτέρω, εκ μόνου, διεξαγάγετε) — defensible for a law-firm page, but it is not applied evenly, and a handful of katharevousa aorists sit beside ordinary demotic in the same paragraph.
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- The masculine accusative article drops its final -ν before every consonant except κ/π/τ/ξ/ψ, so it is spelled like the neuter: "συνδέει το χαρακτήρα 昊" (≈40 places in part a, ≈19 more in 010–018); standard Modern Greek keeps τον always in the masculine, precisely to keep it apart from το.
- German percent typography survives into Greek: 37 instances of a space before the sign, e.g. "Ο γενικός συντελεστής του φόρου επιχειρηματικών συναλλαγών της Ταϊβάν (營業稅) είναι 5 %" (001, 003, 004); Greek sets 5%.
- 財產 / "property" is rendered «αγαθά» (goods) instead of «περιουσία / περιουσιακά στοιχεία» — ≈25 places, and in family law it produces a term that does not exist: "διακρίνει τα προγαμιαία αγαθά (婚前財產) και τα αγαθά μετά την τέλεση του γάμου (婚後財產)".
- Round 4's bare digit «1» glued to a unit the sentence already names is still on disk: "ανά 1 θύμα και ανά 1 ατύχημα" (003; also 005 «περισσότεροι από 1 αλλοδαποί», 008 «για κάθε 1 συμπληρωθέν έτος προϋπηρεσίας (年資)»).
