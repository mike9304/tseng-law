# S2 facts — "paper divorce" (假離婚): a registered 兩願離婚 attacked as fake

Statutes fetched 2026-10-20 from law.moj.gov.tw (cache research/cache/). Judgments fetched 2026-10-20 via judgment.judicial.gov.tw data.aspx (text research/judg/).

## Statutes

### 民法 (B0000001; law-level 修正日期 2026-08-17 — that amendment changed only Art. 1223, effective 6 months after promulgation; LawHistory checked)
- Art 1049 https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=B0000001&flno=1049 — 「夫妻兩願離婚者，得自行離婚。」 (EN official: "The husband and the wife may effect a divorce by themselves with their mutual consent.")
- Art 1050 https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=B0000001&flno=1050 — 「兩願離婚，應以書面為之，有二人以上證人之簽名並應向戶政機關為離婚之登記。」
- Art 73 https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=B0000001&flno=73 — 「法律行為，不依法定方式者，無效。但法律另有規定者，不在此限。」
- Art 87 https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=B0000001&flno=87 — 「表意人與相對人通謀而為虛偽意思表示者，其意思表示無效。但不得以其無效對抗善意第三人。…」 (EN official: "A fictitious expression of intent made by the expresser in collusion with other party is void…")
- Art 985 https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=B0000001&flno=985 — 「有配偶者，不得重婚。」
- Art 988 https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=B0000001&flno=988 — 「…三、違反第九百八十五條規定。但重婚之雙方當事人因善意且無過失信賴一方前婚姻消滅之兩願離婚登記或離婚確定判決而結婚者，不在此限。」
- Art 988-1 https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=B0000001&flno=988-1 — para 1 「前條第三款但書之情形，前婚姻自後婚姻成立之日起視為消滅。」; para 2 準用離婚之效力; para 3 「…剩餘財產差額之分配請求權，自請求權人知有剩餘財產之差額時起，二年間不行使而消滅。自撤銷兩願離婚登記或廢棄離婚判決確定時起，逾五年者，亦同。」; para 4 「…無過失之前婚配偶得向他方請求賠償。」; para 5 non-pecuniary damages.
- Art 1052 para 2 https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=B0000001&flno=1052 — 「有前項以外之重大事由，難以維持婚姻者，夫妻之一方得請求離婚。…」

## Judgments

### 臺灣新北地方法院 113年度婚字第455號 (2024-12-20) — divorce void
URL https://judgment.judicial.gov.tw/FJUD/data.aspx?ty=JD&id=PCDV%2c113%2c%e5%a9%9a%2c455%2c20241220%2c1
- 主文: 確認原告與被告間之婚姻關係存在。
- Facts: married 92-11-30; 「於99年12月8日簽立離婚協議書…並於同日至戶政事務所辦理離婚登記」 (2010-12-08). Plaintiff: 「因原告當時積欠外債甚多，欲聲請債務協商所為之不實離婚」.
- Witness 乙 testimony: 「當時原告是拿到公司給我簽名，那時候只有我跟原告在場…我簽名當下，沒有當面、也沒有用電話或其他方式確認過被告離婚意思」.
- Holding: 乙 not a qualified witness → no two qualified witnesses → 依民法第73條 無效; 「是否出於離婚之真意，或屬通謀虛偽意思表示而為無效，既不影響…爰不予贅述」.
- Standing recognized under 民訴247 (程序方面).
- "14 years": registration 2010-12 → judgment 2024-12.
- Genders not stated in drafts' ko/ja/en (neutral "plaintiff"); zh neutral.

### 臺灣臺東地方法院 113年度婚字第27號 (2025-01-09) — divorce void
URL https://judgment.judicial.gov.tw/FJUD/data.aspx?ty=JD&id=TTDV%2c113%2c%e5%a9%9a%2c27%2c20250109%2c2
- 主文: 確認原告與被告間之婚姻關係存在。
- Plaintiff (husband): 「被告向原告表示若係單親身分較容易取得補助，原告不疑有他才簽署」.
- Witnesses' testimony: asked through the car window whether they really wanted to divorce; both nodded / said 「對」.
- Holding: 「其二人於事前既不認識原告，於簽名之際復未核對確認與被告同往之人是否確為其配偶即原告，且未親見親聞原告離婚之真意…即非屬適格之證人」 → 無效 (民法73).
- Cites 最高法院68年台上字第3792號 for witness standard.

### 臺灣桃園地方法院 112年度婚字第449號 (2024-11-15) — claim dismissed, divorce valid
URL https://judgment.judicial.gov.tw/FJUD/data.aspx?ty=JD&id=TYDV%2c112%2c%e5%a9%9a%2c449%2c20241115%2c1
- 主文: 原告之訴駁回。
- Plaintiff: divorce registered 108-03-04; 「係因被告表示被告父母擔任他人保證人而負債，以免原告名下之財產遭被告父母債權人查封拍賣，兩造並無離婚真意」.
- Court: 民訴277 burden; 「原告並未提出任何證據以實其說」; LINE messages with 「愛你」 after registration not enough.
- Witnesses 乙、丙 are a married couple who know both; came to the residence; 「兩造均在場且說要離婚」; stamped seals personally → qualified.
- Witness standard stated: 「須對於離婚之協議在場聞見，或知悉當事人間有離婚之協議」.

### 臺灣高等法院 114年度家上字第28號 (2025-04-23) — divorce void, judicial divorce upheld
URL https://judgment.judicial.gov.tw/FJUD/data.aspx?ty=JD&id=TPHV%2c114%2c%e5%ae%b6%e4%b8%8a%2c28%2c20250423%2c1
- 主文: 兩造上訴均駁回。
- Facts: married 100-06-10 in mainland China (大陸地區), registered in Taiwan 100-10-17; agreement and registration 109-12-01.
- Witnesses = A02's younger brother and sister (「A02之弟、妹」); names/ID provided by A02, written by A01; witnesses testified they did not know of the divorce and did not authorize.
- 「兩造均因偽造甲○○、乙○○署押，以不實之系爭協議申辦離婚登記之行為，經法院以行使偽造私文書罪判刑確定」 (基隆地院113年度基簡字第1130號).
- A01's defense: 「伊於大陸地區成長，不知我國民法關於離婚要式之規定」 — not accepted; divorce void (民法73); 通謀虛偽 「毋庸再予審究」.
- Witness standard: 「須親見或親聞雙方當事人確有離婚真意之人，始得為證人（最高法院111年度台上字第1750號、109年度台上字第199號判決要旨參照）」.
- Divorce part: A01's claim under 民法1052 II granted at first instance; A02's appeal dismissed → 「A01依民法第1052條第2項規定，求為准兩造離婚，為有理由」.
- Privacy: drafts omit the parties' profession and business details; only the marriage place/time, witness relationship, conviction and outcomes are used.
- Second instance; further appeal possible — drafts say "High Court"/"二審" only, no finality claim.

Other judgments read, not used: TCDV 113婚268, CYDV 113婚127, HLDV 112婚58, KSHV 113家上44, TPHV 112家上99, SCDV 113婚120/144, TCDV 113婚395.

## Claims → sources (all locales)
- "Registered divorce is treated as a divorce; motive alone does not decide" → 民法1050 + TYDV 449 (burden, dismissed) + the three void cases decided on witnesses with 87 not reached.
- "Witnesses need not be present at signing but must personally see/hear both spouses' true intent" → TPHV 114家上28 (citing 最高法院111台上1750, 109台上199), TTDV 113婚27 (citing 68台上3792), PCDV 113婚455.
- "Foreign spouse too; a witness who only hears the Taiwanese spouse has confirmed only one intent" → application of the stated standard (both spouses' intent); PCDV 113婚455 is the example of a witness who heard only one spouse.
- "Courts recognized standing for a confirmation suit because the register still shows divorce" → all four judgments (程序方面/本院之判斷㈠).
- 988(3) proviso, 988-1 paras 1-5 → statute text.
- "Which country's law governs a remarriage abroad is a separate question" → caveat, no claim made.
