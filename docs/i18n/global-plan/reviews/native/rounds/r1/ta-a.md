## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): 4/5 for guidance pack, 3/5 for columns
variety used: Standard written Tamil, readable in Tamil Nadu and Sri Lanka; consistent Tamil script. The nav label is வினா; the FAQ title uses கேள்வி. Dates such as 13 செப்டம்பர் 2025 and 1 ஜனவரி 2024 follow the local day–month order. No Sinhala mix. 005 names the consultation language கொரியன்.
