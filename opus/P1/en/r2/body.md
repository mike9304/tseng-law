A renter in Taipei wired a deposit before signing a lease, backed out that day, and sued for it back, but lost.
The trial court said the lease already existed: rent and a one-year minimum were set at the viewing.

It was NT$21,800, a month's rent. Their stuff wouldn't fit, they said. The company refused a refund.

In the ruling, I noticed the renter filed that LINE chat as evidence. It called the money a "rental deposit" (承租訂金). Backing out over space was on them, with no sign of company fault.
