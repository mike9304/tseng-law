# Probation in Taiwan Is Not a Free-Exit Period: Grounds, Notice and Severance

Taiwan's labor rules have had no probation provision since June 12, 1997. That was the day an amendment deleted the probation clause from the Enforcement Rules of the Labor Standards Act. Probation still exists in contracts. What it does not do is suspend the rules on dismissal.

## What the Ministry of Labor says

The Ministry's note on [probation agreements](https://www.mol.gov.tw/1607/28162/28296/28340/28342/28356/47085/post) says employers and workers may agree on a reasonable probation period suited to the job, provided it is consistent with good faith. A worker on probation has the same rights as any other. To end the contract during probation or when it finishes, the employer still needs a ground under [Article 11](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=N0030001&flno=11) or [Article 12](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=N0030001&flno=12) of the Labor Standards Act, or the proviso to Article 13. A worker assessed as unsuitable is dismissed under Article 11, with notice and severance. The Ministry's [FAQ](https://www.mol.gov.tw/1607/28690/2282/2326/2288/7100/post) adds Articles 16 and 17 and Article 12 of the Labor Pension Act to that list.

Unjustified absence can also support dismissal. Three consecutive days, or six days in a month, without good reason allow dismissal without notice, and the employer must act within 30 days of learning of it ([Article 12(1)(vi) and (2)](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=N0030001&flno=12)). In that case the worker cannot claim notice pay or severance ([Article 18(i)](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=N0030001&flno=18)).

## How much room the courts give

Courts do give employers more latitude during probation. On January 31, 2024, the Supreme Court said that where a contract includes a reasonable probation clause, the employer may observe the new hire's ability, conduct, fit with the company and attitude, and may end the contract during or after probation if the person is unsuitable, so long as it does not abuse that right ([113 Tai-Shang No. 101](https://judgment.judicial.gov.tw/FJUD/printData.aspx?id=TPSV%2C113%2C%E5%8F%B0%E4%B8%8A%2C101%2C20240131%2C1)).

On August 18, 2026, the Taiwan High Court went further. During probation, it said, "broadly unable to do the job" is enough, and the principle that dismissal must be a last resort does not apply. It upheld the dismissal of a pharmacy sales assistant ([115 Lao-Shang No. 39](https://judgment.judicial.gov.tw/FJUD/printData.aspx?id=TPHV%2C115%2C%E5%8B%9E%E4%B8%8A%2C39%2C20260818%2C1)). The employer had three recorded interviews about lateness and similar problems, and an assessment score of 17.5 against a pass mark of 80.

That latitude has limits. A driver of an accessible transport bus in Pingtung started work on January 1, 2025, on a one-year contract with three months' probation. On February 7 another vehicle scraped the parked bus from behind. On February 19 the employer gave notice of dismissal, arguing that no statutory ground was needed during probation. The Kaohsiung Branch of the Taiwan High Court disagreed on October 7, 2026 ([115 Lao-Shang-Yi No. 11](https://judgment.judicial.gov.tw/FJUD/printData.aspx?id=KSHV%2C115%2C%E5%8B%9E%E4%B8%8A%E6%98%93%2C11%2C20261007%2C1)). A probation assessment, it held, must rest on objective facts reasonably connected with the worker's ability or attitude and is subject to good faith and the ban on abuse of rights. The employer also said the driver had failed to follow up on the next passenger pickup after the crash. The court found that the driver had reported to the dispatch center at once and could reasonably rely on dispatch to take over, so the handling of the aftermath showed no serious failure either. One accident that was not the driver's fault did not qualify. The court also confirmed that once probation ends, the original contract simply continues; no second contract is needed.

## Notice and severance

Notice runs at 10 days for at least three months but less than one year of continuous service, and 20 days for at least one year but less than three years ([Article 16](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=N0030001&flno=16)). The statute sets no notice period for service under three months. Severance is different. Under the Labor Pension Act system it is half a month's average wage per year of service, pro-rated for part of a year, capped at six months, and payable within 30 days ([Labor Pension Act Article 12](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=N0030020&flno=12)). Under the Labor Standards Act system it is one month's average wage per year, and part of a month counts as a full month ([Article 17](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=N0030001&flno=17)).

Which system covers a foreign employee changed in 2026. [Article 24 of the Act for the Recruitment and Employment of Foreign Professionals](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=A0030295&flno=24) now puts foreign professionals and foreign special professionals doing professional work under the Labor Pension Act system. Those already with the same employer before the revision took effect on January 1, 2026 had six months to elect in writing to stay under the Labor Standards Act's retirement rules. Anyone already under the new system, or who had already elected the old one, before that date keeps that arrangement (Article 24(5)); for those who switch, earlier service is preserved (Article 24(3), Labor Pension Act Article 11).

## Dismissal during a labor dispute

Once a labor dispute is in mediation, arbitration or adjudication, the employer may not terminate the contract because of that dispute ([Act for Settlement of Labor-Management Disputes, Article 8](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=N0020007&flno=8)). In the 2024 Supreme Court case, a dismissal notice sent during mediation was held unlawful; the later termination, after mediation failed, took effect. For what a dismissal means for a foreign worker's permit and ARC, see [our column on being told a Taiwan job is ending](/en/columns/foreign-professional-dismissed-taiwan).

The records that decided the two 2026 cases were different. In the pharmacy case, the probation assessment sheet and three interview records signed by both sides supported the dismissal. The bus driver's case turned on the LINE messages the driver exchanged with the dispatch center and the manager on the day of the accident, and the file held no evidence of earlier misconduct.
