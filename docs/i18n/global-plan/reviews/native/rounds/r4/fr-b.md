## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): N/A for guidance pack (out of scope; part a), 4/5 for columns
variety used: French of France, consistent. Vous when the reader is addressed; 010 and 013 keep first person. Dates are day–month–year (24 janvier 2022, 4 février 2026, 5 juin 2026, 25 juin 2026, 17 septembre 2026), but the first of the month is still « 1 juillet » / « 1 janvier » instead of « 1er ». The decimal comma is right (1,57 million ; 0,5 mètre). Colons before the continuation use a no-break space. Taïwan keeps the diaeresis; Taipei, Taichung, Kinmen and Matsu do not. Guillemets « » are used for cited labels.
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- Thousands still use a dot: "a ordonné au défendeur de payer [1.579.589 TWD]".
- The first of the month drops « er »: "À compter du 1 juillet 2026".
- 013 and 015 still hide U+200B, and 013 still continues a sentence in the next paragraph: "et sans carte de séjour".
- An English passive becomes a false pronominal: "des facteurs qui se considèrent dans cette appréciation".
- A legal noun is one step off the thing named: "un signe clair de cession".

No file is free of defects. 012 is native professional apart from the yielding noun and one agreement. 015 is the same register apart from the date and the blank characters. 018 is professional apart from the thousands separator and « proposition ». 010 and 011 are professional with local defects. 013 and 017 are usable, with a short cluster each. 014 and 016 still contain sentences a native would not sign. Attorney Wei Tseng is feminine wherever she is named (010 « J’étais avocate » ; 011 « Avocate Wei Tseng » and « l’avocate responsable » ; 014 « avocate à Taïwan » ; 016 and 017 « L’avocate Wei Tseng »). 012 and 018 do not name her. 013 and 015 say « une avocate ou un avocat », not her. No masculine form for her remains. The consultation lock holds: no reply-time promise in 013 and 015 ; 018 asks for the preferred language and says the languages actually available must be confirmed at contact. No interpreter, no free meeting, no result guarantee, no win rate. « seule la filiale » in 018 and « ne garantit à lui seul » in 012 are not advertising claims. « intérêt supérieur de l’enfant (子女最佳利益) » is the statutory standard. The section jump from 5 to 7 in 018 matches the English source.
