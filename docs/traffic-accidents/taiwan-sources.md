## Current content findings

1. **003 Q17, both languages: unsupported sentencing averages remain.** KO lines 238/250 and ZH lines 237/249 assert typical 3/4/6-month sentences. No empirical court dataset supports that claim. Replace with statutory framework and individualized sentencing; do not equate a statutory maximum with the likely outcome. The Sep10 revision correctly added TWD 1,000/2,000/3,000 conversion rates, but the example still needs eligibility/exception language or removal. Penal §41 does not make conversion automatic.
2. **003 Q3 KO line 42: limitation overstatement.** “2년/10년이 지나면 청구할 수 없습니다” implies suit becomes categorically impossible. Civil §197 states limitation periods; §144 permits the debtor to refuse performance after completion. Prefer “원칙적으로 … 소멸시효가 적용됩니다. 시효가 완성되면 상대방이 이를 근거로 지급을 거절할 수 있으므로 기산점과 중단 사유를 확인해야 합니다.”
3. **003 Q3 ZH line 44: incidental civil proceedings need prosecution first.** “刑事案件仍在審理或刑事程序繫屬中時” may include investigation. CPC §488 explicitly begins after criminal prosecution. Use “刑事訴訟起訴後 …” and preserve the existing bar between first-instance close of argument and filing an appeal.
4. **003 Q16/Q20 and closing: remove unverifiable generalizations.** Insurers’ supposed lack of financial incentive, how frequently victims seek attachment, and lawyer motives are not verified. ZH Q20 line 309 “確保能獲得最大賠償” promises a result. Replace with practical matters to discuss: evidence, deadlines, insurance, settlement scope, fees, communication, and foreseeable procedural choices.
5. **003 Q18/Q19: improve consistency with revised Q1/Q5.** Q18 must preserve the first-instance withdrawal deadline and inability to refile. A private settlement does not automatically end non-complaint-dependent prosecution. Q19 should say property-only accidents fall outside Penal §185-4's injury/death element while statutory handling duties and administrative consequences can still apply.
6. **012:** Sep10 already fixed the primary-source URL and KO wording about road signs. Core §101 rule matches the live statute. The “our firm handled” anonymous case at lines 34–50 in both versions is not publicly verifiable from legal sources. Retain it only with internal lawyer confirmation of facts, attribution and permission; otherwise rewrite as an explicitly hypothetical example without claims of an actual adjudicated outcome. Do not relabel an alleged real case as fictional while preserving it as a supposed factual account.

### English draft

**Taiwan accident police records: what to collect before leaving**

After a traffic accident in Taiwan, prioritize care and safety, then organize records for follow-up. Injury or death accidents require rescue measures, police notification and preservation of scene evidence. [Traffic Act, Article 62](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=K0040012&flno=62)

If safe, photograph vehicle positions, damage, lane markings and signals. Preserve original dashcam files. Record the handling officer's contact and case details; arrange medical records and receipts by date. These are practical preparation steps, not an exhaustive list of legal requirements.

**When can you request police records?** The participant registration sheet can be requested at the scene; scene diagrams and photographs after seven days; and the preliminary analysis sheet after thirty days. These are application times, not guaranteed delivery dates. [National Police Agency FAQ](https://wwwcdn.npa.gov.tw/ch/app/faq/view?id=2144&module=faq&serno=A1084129)

The NPA's national portal requires police attendance and injury or death. For property-only cases or authentication difficulties, contact the handling police unit. A non-party collecting records needs authorization. [Application portal](https://tm2.npa.gov.tw/NM105-505ClientRWD2/TM01A01Q_01.jsp)

**Before leaving Taiwan**, confirm how documents and notices will reach you. Authority to collect records differs from litigation authority. Certain civil acts, including settlement, require special authority; a criminal complainant with an agent may still be required to attend personally. [Civil guidance](https://www.judicial.gov.tw/tw/cp-1690-81113-995d9-1.html), [complainant representation](https://www.judicial.gov.tw/tw/cp-1654-2459-bd892-1.html)

For a consultation, bring your account, police records, original footage, medical evidence, insurer correspondence and any proposed settlement. Requirements and responsibility depend on the case.
