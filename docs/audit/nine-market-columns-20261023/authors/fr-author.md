### 674 — unpaid invoices, credit notes and returns

- [Choice-of-law Act, article 20](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=B0000007&flno=20): party choice, then closest connection. [Official full English text, article 36](https://law.moj.gov.tw/ENG/LawClass/LawAll.aspx?pcode=B0000007): prescription follows the law governing the legal relationship. The article does not infer Taiwanese prescription solely from the debtor's address or French prescription from the invoice's origin.
- [Civil Code 127](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=B0000001&flno=127), [128](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=B0000001&flno=128), [129](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=B0000001&flno=129), [130](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=B0000001&flno=130): merchant/manufacturer price claims, accrual, interruption and the six-month follow-up limit for a demand. A reminder is not described as preserving a claim indefinitely.
- [Civil Code 334](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=B0000001&flno=334) and [335](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=B0000001&flno=335): reciprocal same-kind due debts, exclusions and declaration for set-off. A unilateral debit note is not treated as automatically establishing a set-off.
- [Civil Procedure 508](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=B0010001&flno=508), [511](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=B0010001&flno=511), [516](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=B0010001&flno=516), [519](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=B0010001&flno=519), [521](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=B0010001&flno=521), [523](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=B0010001&flno=523), [526](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=B0010001&flno=526): payment order scope and supporting showing, 20 days after service for objection, transition after objection, execution title and provisional attachment grounds. No guaranteed collection or automatic freeze is promised.

### 675 — distributor registered the brand

- [Trademark Act 30](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=J0070001&flno=30), paragraph 1(12): prior use, mark/goods similarity, knowledge through the listed relationships, imitation intent and consent exception. The French registration alone is not said to create a Taiwanese right.
- [Articles 33](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=J0070001&flno=33) and [35](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=J0070001&flno=35): registration publication and designated goods/services. [Article 42](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=J0070001&flno=42): registration of assignment for third-party effect.
- [Articles 48](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=J0070001&flno=48), [57](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=J0070001&flno=57), [58](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=J0070001&flno=58): three-month opposition, interested-party invalidation, five-year period. The indefinite bad-faith exception is limited to grounds 9 and 11; it is not extended to ground 12. Invalidation is not described as transferring ownership.
- [Article 6](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=J0070001&flno=6): representation where there is no Taiwan domicile or business office. No uncommenced 2022 criminal-provision amendments were relied on.

### 676 — ending the distribution relationship

- [Civil Code 558](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=B0000001&flno=558) and [561](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=B0000001&flno=561): agency definition and the limited three-month rule for an agency contract without a fixed duration, including the exception. The rule is not universalised to independent resellers. [Choice-of-law article 20](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=B0000007&flno=20) prevents an unsupported Taiwan-law assumption.
- [Civil Code 148](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=B0000001&flno=148), [254](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=B0000001&flno=254), [259](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=B0000001&flno=259): good faith, cure before rescission in the stated setting, and restoration. No universal repurchase, minimum compensation or resale-price floor was introduced.
- [Trademark Act 36](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=J0070001&flno=36): exhaustion for goods put on the domestic or foreign market by the owner or with consent, and the listed legitimate exceptions. Existing stock is not automatically unlawful after termination.
- [Personal Data Protection Act 20](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=I0050021&flno=20): use within the collection purpose and statutory exceptions. An account handover is not equated with unrestricted transfer of customer data. The relevant current article is used; later amendments to other provisions are not assumed effective.

### 677 — heir signing from France

- [Choice-of-law 58](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=B0000007&flno=58): nationality of the deceased at death, including the Taiwan-national-heir proviso for property in Taiwan. [Article 2](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=B0000007&flno=2): multiple nationalities. [Article 38](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=B0000007&flno=38): situs for rights in property. Residence in France does not determine succession law. No shares or reserved-share conclusions are stated, and the not-yet-effective Civil Code 1223 amendment is not used.

- [France Diplomatie's country table, updated 15 September 2026, page 9](https://www.diplomatie.gouv.fr/files/files/leg-tableau-recap-du-droit-conventionnel-11-09-26.pdf): Taiwan entry is legalisation without diplomatic relations, not apostille. The PDF text was read; the screenshot tool did not return a usable rendered image, so no visual PDF-layout verification is claimed.
- [France Diplomatie's current competence guidance](https://www.diplomatie.gouv.fr/fr/services-aux-francaises-et-aux-francais/formalites-avant-le-depart/certificat-copie-legalisation-notariat/legaliser-ou-faire-apostiller-un-document-francais-a-destination-de-l-etranger): designated notarial structures since 1 September 2025 for metropolitan-France legalisation; overseas exceptions kept distinct. - [Taiwan MOFA law, article 15](https://law.mofa.gov.tw/EngLawContent.aspx?id=26&lan=E): authentication concerns signature/seal/form, not substantive truth. [Land Act 73](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=D0060001&flno=73): six months and an heir applying for all; no right to sell alone is inferred. [Estate and Gift Tax Act 23](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=G0340072&flno=23) and [26](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=G0340072&flno=26): declaration period and timely extension request with ordinary limit/exceptions.

### 678 — employer closure and unpaid wages

- [Labor Standards Act 14](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=N0030001&flno=14), paragraph 1(5): unpaid contractual remuneration and employee termination without notice. The 30-day rule belonging to other grounds is not misapplied. [Article 23](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=N0030001&flno=23): calculation detail and five-year payroll retention. [Article 28](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=N0030001&flno=28): covered claims, including the combined six-month-average-wage cap for pension/severance advance.
- [Current fund regulation, full text](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawAll.aspx?pcode=N0030005), articles 7–10 and 15: employer contributions including possible cure, recognised closure proof, proof of claim and filing with liquidator/administrator, collective application except special circumstances, designated foreign account. Individual article URLs were unavailable; the verified full text is cited.
- [BLI worker instructions](https://www.bli.gov.tw/0007239.html) and [current coverage FAQ](https://www.bli.gov.tw/0101924.html): eligible foreign employees follow the same conditions/procedures, unsuccessful demand, debt evidence, six-month wage period before the named events. [Government portal, updated 24 March 2026](https://www.gov.tw/News_Content_26_869668): temporary suspension with possible resumption is insufficient for the fund; local labour authority remains a route for the wage dispute.
