Document approval comes before the exit-permission application for an overseas draftee returning to Taiwan under the procedure introduced in September 2025. The [National Immigration Agency’s current instructions](https://www.immigration.gov.tw/5382/5385/7244/7250/7254/16054/16490/), updated July 6, 2026, set out both steps.

US–Taiwan dual nationals with existing Taiwan household registration must also qualify for the returning overseas-compatriot route. First registration and naturalization are outside this article’s scope, as are men who have completed service.

## Four categories of documents for the overseas-status review

Military-service age for this exit procedure runs from January 1 of the year after a man turns 18 through December 31 of the year he turns 36, for men who have not fulfilled the obligation. Lawful exemption or disqualification from service is addressed separately in [Articles 2 and 16 of the exit regulations](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawAll.aspx?PCode=D0040006).

Article 9 of the [returning overseas-compatriot regulations](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawAll.aspx?PCode=D0040005) identifies the OCAC-issued certificate for military-service purposes, 役政用華僑身分證明書, as the basis for recognition. US citizenship alone does not supply that certificate.

Article 5 requires the existing-household-registration applicant to submit these documents electronically to the review platform or in writing to the NIA before departure:

- His Taiwan passport.
- His foreign passport, or overseas permanent or long-term residence document.
- A valid military-service Overseas Compatriot Identity Certificate. A previously submitted certificate that remains valid need not be submitted again.
- Evidence of residence in his overseas home at a time within the four months before returning to Taiwan, such as relevant passport entry stamps, entry–exit records or travel tickets he actually used. Previously submitted evidence may be reused only while it still falls within the four months before the latest entry.

Foreign-language documents need Chinese translations prepared and submitted by the applicant under Article 5.

## Review approval and permission to leave are separate steps

After the documents pass review, the NIA directs the applicant to request exit permission through its online service or a service center. Its [service page links both application systems](https://www.immigration.gov.tw/5382/5385/7244/7250/7254/16054/16490/). Save the review result and the exit-permission result so their status can be checked before the planned departure.

What if the flight is close and the papers are still incomplete? The same NIA guidance permits an ordinary short-term departure application where no exit restriction exists. For this Article 5 route, the applicant must return within four months, counting from the day after departure under Article 6 of the exit regulations, unless he completes the specified document submission and obtains approval within that period.

An upload receipt is not the approval. Confirm the return deadline recorded for the departure and whether the subsequent review has actually passed before relying on the exception. This route also depends on eligibility for short-term departure; it does not override an existing restriction.

## Repeated visits can matter even without one long stay

For this overseas-status route, Articles 3 and 4 provide for conscription processing upon reaching 183 days, counted from the day after return. Either continuous residence or cumulative residence in a calendar year can reach the threshold. Entries using Taiwan and foreign passports are combined.

The calculation excludes periods of study in Taiwan that meet the conditions for deferment. It also excludes qualifying periods attributable to specified disasters or communicable diseases beyond the person’s control, where the local government reports the case and Ministry of the Interior approval is obtained. These are conditional exclusions; a school enrollment record or an extended stay during illness does not establish one by itself.

Because Article 3 expressly excludes those who have not complied with the specified Article 5 submission provisions from its stated 183-day treatment, ask the authorities which rule applies before planning a visit around that number.

Entering on a US passport does not remove these rules: Article 7 applies them to returning overseas compatriots who enter on a foreign passport. [Article 14 of the exit regulations](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawAll.aspx?PCode=D0040006) also requires dual-national draftees with existing Taiwan household registration to use a Taiwan passport for entry and exit, and addresses restrictions where conscription processing is required despite foreign-passport entry.

## Records to resolve before the return flight

Add all Taiwan entry–exit dates and military-service notices to the document-review file. Which office can resolve a discrepancy? Ask the NIA about the document and departure applications, and the household-registration-area military-service office about status, counted days and restrictions, including any examination notice or inclusion in a call-up group under Article 9 of the exit regulations.

The September 2025 amendment has a transition provision for men who had already returned before it took effect and have not departed since. Article 10 preserves specified earlier rules for that group. Someone continuously in Taiwan since before the change should have that branch checked instead of using the 2026-return procedure described here.
