## Article briefs: France

### FR674 — unpaid distributor invoices

- Reader/trigger: French brand finance/export manager; Taiwan importer leaves several invoices unpaid while requesting returns, credit notes or a stock allowance.
- Working title: `Factures impayées à Taïwan : quand le distributeur invoque les retours de marchandises`. Search phrase: `Taïwan facture impayée distributeur recouvrement`.
- Thesis: first reconcile each sale, due date, payment and claimed credit with the actual contractual debtor. If Taiwan law governs, certain merchant/manufacturer price claims have a two-year limitation; a demand alone is not indefinite protection. Payment order, contested merits and protective attachment are different routes (L01–L06).
- Outline: invoice ledger and parties → determine governing law and due date → real credit/return dispute → payment order suitability and 20-day objection → evidence of asset risk and proportionate next step.
- Conditions: no universal two-year limit for all debts; no automatic attachment merely because the creditor is French; no EU payment-order portability into Taiwan; no guaranteed recovery. A Taiwanese distributor's address does not itself select Taiwan substantive law.
- New angle/overlap: builds on core011's importer relationship but covers collection after goods have moved; distinct from FR653 purchase of an operating business. Adapt EN354 without its US assumptions.
- Useful next action/CTA fit: prepare a dated balance reconciliation with the debtor's registered name, disputed amounts and contract dispute clause; civil/contract inquiry through `/fr/contact` and the verified email. Do not send customer databases or bank credentials initially.
- Priority: P1 hypothesis; directly names an existing monetary dispute and a document set counsel can assess.

### FR675 — importer registers the brand

- Reader/trigger: French cosmetics, sport or other consumer brand; moving to a new importer reveals the Taiwan trademark is registered by the old partner.
- Working title: `Votre importateur a enregistré la marque à Taïwan : quels recours restent ouverts ?`. Search: `Taïwan marque déposée distributeur opposition`.
- Thesis: prior use, the partner's knowledge through the commercial relationship, intention to imitate and lack of consent matter under Trademark Act30(1)(12); registration dates choose opposition or invalidation routes (L07).
- Outline: search the Taiwan record and owner → separate Latin-name/logo/Chinese mark and designated goods → authorization and prior-use chronology → three-month opposition versus invalidation → stop promises of automatic transfer.
- Conditions: Article58 five-year rule includes ground12; the bad-faith exception is specifically grounds9/11, not every allegedly dishonest registration. EU/French registration alone does not establish a Taiwan remedy. Do not claim invalidation automatically transfers ownership or damages.
- New angle: core011 concerns cosmetics entry/regulation, not title/control after relationship breakdown; adapt KO091 with a French exporter/importer evidence trail, not an invented client story.
- Action/fit: public register number, announcement date, first-use dates and a brief description of permission given; IP inquiry. Actual distribution agreement/old packaging can be transmitted after secure intake.
- Priority P1 hypothesis: discrete loss of commercial control and potential procedural time limit.

### FR676 — distribution exit with inventory and accounts

- Reader/trigger: French brand changes its Taiwan reseller; existing stock, warranty obligations, e-commerce administrator access and use of the brand remain unresolved.
- Working title: `Changer de distributeur à Taïwan : le stock et les accès qui restent chez l’ancien partenaire`. Search: `Taïwan résiliation contrat distribution stock marque`.
- Thesis: identify whether the relationship is a buy-resell distribution or commercial agency before assuming notice rules; allocate remaining goods, obligations and credentials through the contract and negotiated handover (L08). Article561's three-month notice concerns indefinite commercial agency as defined in558, with an unavoidable-cause qualification; it is not a universal reseller rule.
- Outline: expiry/non-renewal/termination distinction → entity making sales in whose name → stock ownership and agreed return terms → separate product warranty and brand/account access → documented transition.
- Conditions: do not import French abrupt-termination compensation rules, EU commercial-agency law or a universal three-month notice into Taiwan. No general right to seize an account or customer database. Do not propose resale price floors or forced customer-data transfers; their legality is outside this brief.
- New angle: operational exit, not FR653 business acquisition or FR675 registration validity. EN353 is a question checklist only; narrow source-supported claims are required.
- Action/fit: renewal date, termination clause, stock title, who owns each account and outstanding orders; civil/IP scope confirmation.
- Priority P2 hypothesis; complements collection and trademark pages without becoming a second general market-entry guide.

### FR677 — heirs in France and Taiwan assets

- Reader/trigger: person residing in France inherits a deceased Taiwanese family member's Taiwan bank funds or property and is preparing documents with a French notaire.
- Working title: `Hériter depuis la France d’un compte ou d’un bien à Taïwan : quel dossier préparer ?`. Search: `succession Taïwan héritier France procuration`.
- Thesis: in Taiwan's conflict rules, the deceased's nationality at death, not the heir's French residence, generally identifies succession law; Taiwan property rights follow situs law (L09). Foreign heirship documents, a power of attorney, bank release and real-property registration answer different questions.
- Outline: establish deceased nationality/domicile and asset location → identify the whole heir group and existing proceedings → ask receiving bank/land office what foreign documents and authority it requires → define a limited power of attorney → obtain Taiwan-specific procedural advice.
- Conditions: Article58 has a Taiwan-national-heir proviso; renvoi/multiple nationality and non-Taiwanese deceased cases need separate review. Do not claim a French acte de notoriété or EU succession certificate automatically releases Taiwan money or changes title. Do not state fixed French authentication requirements without verified competent-office instructions. Exclude inheritance shares/reserved portions; 2026 amendment status noted below.
- New angle: remote evidence/authority handoff, not generic016's inheritance/custody overview; adapt EN092's bank questions but remove US notarization and apostille assumptions.
- Action/fit: non-sensitive family tree, death date/nationality, Taiwan assets and existing overseas documents; family/succession inquiry.
- Priority P2 hypothesis; concrete foreign-resident barrier, demand unmeasured.

### FR678 — unpaid wages after closure

- Reader/trigger: French employee on a Taiwan employment contract; company stops operating while wages, retirement pay or severance remain unpaid.
- Working title: `L’employeur ferme à Taïwan : salaires impayés et fonds d’avance`. Search: `Taïwan salaire impayé fermeture employeur salarié étranger`.
- Thesis: Labor Standards Act28 separates priority in the employer's estate from a conditional fund advance. Closure/liquidation/adjudicated bankruptcy matters; routine late payment alone is not the same event. L10 and BLI guidance confirm covered noncitizen employees may apply under the same conditions; contribution, covered employee status and evidence still matter.
- Outline: actual employer and covered employee status → event and wage dates → rank versus advance (different caps) → proof of demand/closure/debt → which records to retain while leaving Taiwan.
- Conditions: wages portion under six months; retirement/severance advance combined cap six months' average wage; rank is not a payment guarantee. Do not equate this with French AGS or France's severance rules. An overseas parent is not automatically liable. Secondment, mandate/director status and applicable coverage require separate assessment.
- Overlap: core008/009 explain severance grounds; FR654 covers working-holiday status. New article focuses unpaid claim recovery and fund conditions. Adapt EN606, not its entire average-wage arithmetic.
- Action/fit: employment entity, wage months, payslips, notice/closure document and whether demand was made; employment inquiry.
- Priority P2 hypothesis, useful to residents and complements business disputes.

## Article briefs: Germany

### DE679 — equipment acceptance and final payment

- Reader/trigger: German machinery supplier; Taiwan customer's FAT/SAT or commissioning acceptance remains unsigned and final instalment is withheld.
- Working title: `Abnahme in Taiwan offen: Wann wird die Schlussrate für eine Maschine fällig?`. Search: `Taiwan Maschine Abnahme Restzahlung Forderung`.
- Thesis: payment depends on agreed milestones, actual performance and contract classification; Civil Code229 default and264 reciprocal-performance defence cannot be replaced by an invoice alone (L01/L11). A custom-built installation may be a work or mixed contract, so do not assume sales rules govern.
- Outline: contractual SAT/FAT meaning → seller/buyer responsibilities (utilities, material, access, training) → unresolved test item versus whole-machine refusal → payment/date evidence → collection options and limitation assessment.
- Conditions: German-law Abnahme concepts do not automatically govern Taiwan; no automatic acceptance after a specified number of days; no universal right to withhold all money for a minor defect. Article264 exception for prior-performance obligation and good faith after partial performance stays.
- Overlap: core018 tells who supplies/supports equipment; new article starts when the contract is already being performed. EN354 provides procedure, EN506 covers a different buyer-side defect claim.
- Action/fit: milestone schedule, protocol revisions, commissioning records, unpaid balance and exact counterparty; civil/contract inquiry.
- Priority P1 hypothesis supported by machinery trade context, not evidence of common default.

### DE680 — defective Taiwan components

- Reader/trigger: German industrial buyer discovers failed parts after incoming inspection or during integration, while Taiwan supplier requests more root-cause tests.
- Working title: `Fehlerhafte Bauteile aus Taiwan: Mängelanzeige vor dem Abschlussbericht`. Search: `Taiwan Lieferant mangelhafte Ware Mängelanzeige`.
- Thesis: where Taiwan sales law applies, timely inspection/immediate defect notice and later exercise of a remedy are separate; engineers' internal quality hold is not communication to the seller (L12).
- Outline: delivery/discovery/notice dates → identify contractual seller (trader versus factory) → first factual notice while cause unknown → retain samples and revisions → choose reduction/rescission/replacement/damages within their conditions.
- Conditions: no generic seven-/fourteen-/thirty-day notice window. Six months after notice and five years from delivery are Article365 limits for defect-based rescission/reduction, not every damages claim. Intentional concealment affects Article356 and six-month rule differently from the five-year limit. Do not present HGB377 as Taiwan law.
- Overlap: core018 entry; adapt EN506 with German receiving inspection, component revision and downstream integration evidence; no invented German legal conclusions.
- Action/fit: lot IDs, specification revision, delivery/first-discovery/notice dates and desired remedy; retain samples, arrange secure technical-file intake.
- Priority P1 hypothesis; legally meaningful time sequence and actionable preservation.

### DE681 — physical tools and test fixtures

- Reader/trigger: German OEM transfers Taiwan production; supplier refuses to release customer-funded molds and test fixtures because of unpaid group-company invoices.
- Working title: `Werkzeuge beim Lieferanten in Taiwan: Eigentum, Zurückbehaltung und Herausgabe`. Search: `Taiwan Lieferant Werkzeug Form Herausgabe Zurückbehaltungsrecht`.
- Thesis: payment for a tool does not alone settle ownership or right to remove it; ownership, possession, contract return rights and statutory retention must be separately checked (L13).
- Outline: itemized serial-number inventory → ownership/delivery arrangements → holder versus contracting supplier → due debt and commercial connection → insolvency qualification/security → documented physical handover.
- Conditions: Article928 exclusions for unlawful possession/knowledge another owns item;929 commercial connection is relevant;930 agreement limits have931 insolvency qualification;937 adequate security may extinguish retention. No self-help entry or automatic police collection. Software/CAD permission is separate and should not turn this into IT686.
- Overlap: none in DE core; adapt EN505. New reader decision is relocating production with specific custody and group-company debt facts.
- Action/fit: identify owner, holder, claimed debtor and inventory; disclose imminent disposal/move with evidence; civil dispute inquiry.
- Priority P1 hypothesis; concrete asset/access problem and custody in Taiwan.

### DE682 — CAD and know-how after cooperation fails

- Reader/trigger: German engineering company shared nonpublic CAD/process settings with a Taiwan development or fabrication partner; access was retained or files are suspected to be used for another customer.
- Working title: `Konstruktionsdaten in Taiwan weiterverwendet: Was ein NDA noch nicht beweist`. Search: `Taiwan Geschäftsgeheimnis CAD NDA Lieferant`.
- Thesis: protected information must satisfy all three Trade Secrets Act2 conditions; an NDA is evidence of precautions, not proof of secrecy, economic value, ownership or misuse. Civil removal/prevention and damages have distinct conditions (L14).
- Outline: identify exact secret/version → establish restricted access/value → permitted purpose and recipients → preserve authorized logs without unauthorized access → remedy/deadline assessment.
- Conditions: avoid calling every engineering file a trade secret or alleging crime from a similar product. Damages12 requires intentional/negligent unlawful infringement and two years from knowledge of act/person or ten years from act. German/EU trade-secret law or export controls are outside this Taiwan remedy article.
- Overlap: core018 is entry; adapt EN170/KO116, narrow to civil evidence and not a general criminal technology-transfer survey.
- Action/fit: sanitized description of information, who had access and suspected use date; never send the secret corpus in the first email.
- Priority P1 hypothesis; supported by industrial-cooperation context, no claimed theft prevalence.

### DE683 — German judgment, Taiwan assets

- Reader/trigger: German company already holds a final German civil payment judgment but the debtor's reachable assets are in Taiwan.
- Working title: `Deutsches Urteil, Vermögen in Taiwan: der zusätzliche Weg zur Vollstreckung`. Search: `deutsches Urteil Taiwan anerkennen vollstrecken`.
- Thesis: Taiwan Code of Civil Procedure402 recognition grounds and Compulsory Enforcement Act4-1 enforcement permission are separate from the German title (L15).
- Outline: finality and service record → four refusal grounds → Taiwan permission judgment and venue → precise debtor/assets → provisional attachment only on supported grounds.
- Conditions: no automatic EU regime in Taiwan, no blanket claim German judgments meet reciprocity; no assurance a default judgment is always barred or always accepted. Arbitration awards are a different procedure. EU/German legal details need separate counsel.
- Overlap: none in DE core; adapt EN030 with German finality/service documents as facts to obtain, not invented mandatory forms.
- Action/fit: court/case, finality evidence, original service/appearance, partial payments and Taiwan assets; civil litigation scope review.
- Priority P1 hypothesis; late-stage dispute reader with identifiable Taiwan-side work.

- Working title: `Credito verso un cliente di Taiwan: chi deve pagare il saldo della fornitura?`. Search: `Taiwan recupero credito fattura macchinario saldo`.
- Thesis: identify seller, purchaser, installation contractor and due obligations before choosing collection; commercial price/work remuneration may raise short Taiwan limitation rules if Taiwan law applies (L01–L06/L11).

- Overlap: core017 logistics and018 local operations do not address collection; EN354 supplies Taiwan procedure. Different emphasis from DE679: debtor identity and split supply/service paperwork, not acceptance-test doctrine.
- Action/fit: contract debtor, invoice schedule, delivery/services, dispute clause and balance; contract recovery inquiry.

### IT686 — commissioned designs and OEM deliverables

- Reader/trigger: Italian design/equipment business paid a Taiwan OEM for prototypes, molds and drawings; switching production reveals that physical handover and editable-file/use rights were never clearly separated.
- Working title: `Stampi e disegni commissionati a Taiwan: che cosa può passare al nuovo produttore?`. Search: `Taiwan stampi disegni OEM proprietà diritti contratto`.
- Thesis: physical ownership/return under L13 is different from copyright ownership and licensed use of commissioned drawings/software (L16). Payment for design work does not universally assign copyright or authorize unrestricted use by a replacement factory. Copyright Act12 nevertheless preserves a commissioning party's statutory right to use when the commissioned creator holds economic rights: do not write that the customer has no rights.
- Outline: deliverables and file formats → who created/commissioned what → written ownership/licence scope → physical mold holder/return or retention → agreed transition record.
- Conditions: copyright subsistence, employee versus commissioned author and contractual assignment must be established; not all functional designs receive copyright. Taiwan conflict-law42 selects protection-place law for IP rights, with employment-contract law governing employee-created IP ownership. Do not say Italy/EU design registration automatically protects Taiwan. Do not promise unrestricted use of drawings or tooling for every product.
- Overlap: core011/018 entry only; adapts EN505 physical questions plus mature copyright article, but its centre is document/licence rights. DE681 remains physical retention and group-company debt.
- Action/fit: sanitized deliverables list, creator/employer relationship, ownership/licence clause and intended receiving producer; civil/IP inquiry.
- Priority P2 hypothesis backed by actual ICE design/textile promotion, not an assertion of sector litigation prevalence.

- Action/fit: dates, deceased nationality, relationship and known debt/asset categories; succession inquiry promptly, no full ID/bank details initially.

### IT688 — summons while visiting Taiwan

- Reader/trigger: Italian business visitor receives a police/prosecutor notice following a dispute, accident or account/payment investigation; unsure whether listed as witness or defendant and departure is approaching.
- Working title: `Una convocazione a Taiwan durante una trasferta: testimone, indagato e interprete`. Search: `Taiwan convocazione polizia italiano interprete avvocato`.
- Thesis: the actual status/document determines duties and rights. Defendant notice of rights, counsel and interpretation must not be conflated with witness rules (L18). A police witness notice under196-1 is also not interchangeable with a prosecutor/court witness summons under175/178.
- Outline: read authority/status/date → notice versus coercive warrant → defendant rights/counsel and interpretation → witness duty and refusal grounds separately → ask about date/communication/travel restrictions without assuming a flight cancels appearance.
- Conditions: no claim every witness may remain silent for any reason; no promise every foreigner receives appointed counsel; no automatic arrest or exit ban merely from receipt of a summons; consular office is not a substitute for Taiwan defence counsel. No nationality-specific allegations.
- Overlap: no IT criminal core article; EN521 provides status distinctions. FR655 plea bargaining is a later-stage different subject.
- Action/fit: authority, status, scheduled date and redacted document description; criminal inquiry, arrange secure notice delivery after intake. Do not promise Italian-speaking lawyer/interpreter services at the firm.
- Priority P2 hypothesis; concrete urgent-access question, demand unmeasured.
