# Coverage audit standard

This is an editorial audit of useful answers for the five requested audiences. It is not measured keyword demand, a claim about the proportion of all possible crimes, or a prediction of search rankings. Exact law, practical relevance and meaningful distinct answers determine whether a new article is needed. Existing adequate native articles may satisfy a row after source/content review and discoverability checks. A passing mention or generic related link is only partial coverage.

For every applicable row below, record locale, anticipated native questions, scenario, exact article/section answering them, existing/new/partial/missing status, official sources, and any still-unanswered material question. Add families if writer research or independent reviewers find an important omission; this list is a floor for the audit, not a cap on the user's scope. A country-specific row can be inapplicable only with an explicit reason. A general procedure answer may serve several offenses, but it cannot replace the substantive answer to each offense question.

## Procedure and participation

P01 — Reporting a crime and making a complaint: victim/complainant distinction, receiving agency, statement/evidence, receipt, unknown offender, reporting after return home.
P02 — Police/prosecutor summons: suspect vs witness, attendance, interpreter, statement correction, silence/counsel, self-incrimination.
P03 — Arrest and the first 24 hours: who can arrest, time calculation/transfer, notice to family, interpreter/counsel, court detention decision.
P04 — Detention, bail and restrictions: detention grounds/remedies, conditions/security/guarantor, release is not end of investigation, foreigner or no local guarantor issues where supported.
P05 — Exit restrictions and returning abroad: criminal vs administrative restrictions, applying/reviewing rather than simply buying a ticket, continuing service/contact.
P06 — Search, seizure and electronic evidence: warrant/consent/exceptions, recording what was seized, access/return channels, no evidence destruction instructions.
P07 — Family contact and counsel while detained: locating responsible institution, visitation/mail restrictions, remote options and documents, consular limits for relevant nationalities.
P08 — Complaint-based offenses and deadlines: eligible person, clock, exceptions, settlement vs withdrawal, withdrawal consequences, ordinary limitation periods distinguished.
P09 — Prosecution, non-prosecution and deferred prosecution: decision meanings, complainant remedies, requirements/obligations/revocation.
P10 — Court hearings and simplified/summary proceedings: roles, attendance, documents, legal aid/counsel, judgment route differences.
P11 — Criminal judgment, appeal and other remedies: service clock, filing court, reasons, first/second/third instance limits, judgment vs ruling remedies.

## Offenses and immediate responses

C01 — Fraud victims: investment/romance/online sale/business-email or rental fraud relevant to the audience, preserving transfer/platform evidence, reporting and money recovery limits.
C02 — Accounts, payment instruments, laundering and courier jobs: suspect exposure vs being deceived, account restrictions, evidence of recruitment/instructions, not treating every transfer as automatic guilt.
C03 — Drugs, cannabis/CBD and medicine/parcel import: Taiwan classification vs home-country legality, use/possession/transport/import distinctions, prescription/controlled-medicine boundary where relevant.
C04 — DUI, drug-impaired driving, traffic injury/death and hit-and-run: criminal/administrative/civil routes and material differences, evidence and immediate obligations.
C05 — Assault, injury, fights and self-defense: injury/evidence/complaint clock, response to attack vs retaliation, aggravated/serious outcomes.
C06 — Threats, coercion, extortion and confinement: messages or in-person demands, evidence/safety and reporting, distinctions from mere civil demands.
C07 — Sexual assault and sexual harassment: reporting/medical and evidence support, victim participation/privacy, material complaint exceptions and separate administrative channels.
C08 — Intimate images, hidden cameras, sextortion and digital sexual abuse: preserving evidence without redistributing, takedown/reporting/criminal routes, consent to recording vs disclosure.
C09 — Domestic violence and stalking: orders, emergency routes, independent criminal complaints and violations, spouse residence concerns where relevant.
C10 — Defamation, insult and online reviews/posts: statement/opinion/context, constitutional limits, complaint and proof, no universal 'criticism is a crime' shortcut.
C11 — Theft, shoplifting, lost property and entrusted property: taking/keeping/property-law distinctions, evidence and process, settlement limits.
C12 — Property damage and trespass: private-property/landlord or workplace disputes with genuine criminal issues, complaint requirements vs civil claim.
C13 — Work/business offenses: trade secrets, counterfeit/IP, breach of trust/embezzlement/forged invoices or documents where relevant; distinguish crime from contract nonperformance.
C14 — Exploitation, forced labor, passport withholding and immigration/work rules: criminal vs labor/immigration administration, victim status/support and status uncertainty without blanket deportation claims.
C15 — Serious violence, children/juveniles and vulnerable participants: procedural differences and immediate support where relevant, special rules not silently generalized from adult ordinary cases.

## Recovery and aftermath

R01 — Settlement and civil recovery: timing of accessory civil action, parties/damages/evidence, judgment vs collectible money, civil limitation clock kept distinct.
R02 — Freezing, confiscation and victim restitution: who applies, which property and stage, applicable deadline, priority/multiple-victim limits.
R03 — Sentencing and execution: fine conversion vs criminal fine vs administrative fine, community service, suspended sentence, duties/default/revocation.
R04 — Criminal records and employment/licensing: police certificate vs judicial record, omitted entries/correction, no false 'record erased' promises.
R05 — Residence, work permits and deportation after a criminal case: conviction/sentence/administrative discretion distinctions, nationality/residence category exceptions, departure and re-entry.
R06 — Legal aid, representation and costs: available eligibility/application channels, language/interpreter support, what family/consular officers can and cannot do; no invented prices or qualifications.
R07 — Victim participation, compensation/support and procedural safeguards: eligible case/person, application/support institution, criminal compensation vs damages, accessibility for foreign victims.

## Additional questions identified by independent review

The original 33 families remain stable IDs for existing inventories. The following are explicit subrequirements, not optional mentions or a new article quota. Each locale must map a substantive answer, justified inapplicability, or a still-open gap. A single article may answer several genuinely connected questions.

- P02a: Street stops and identity checks, distinct from criminal arrest; statutory grounds and the three-hour limit measured from the stop. False accusation, perjury and threatened witnesses require role, forum, oath/materiality and intentional-falsity distinctions. An unsuccessful complaint does not itself establish false accusation.
- P03a/P04a: Immediate judicial review through 提審 for qualifying custody by an authority other than a court; applicant (including another person), competent district court, oral application and priority of another immediate statutory review route. Distinguish court detention remedies and separate statutory 24-hour stages.
- P05a: Proceedings after return abroad: actual service, authorized recipient, attendance and possible wanted status according to participant role. Returning home does not terminate the case or automatically excuse a missed deadline.
- P06a: Formal preservation of endangered evidence, not only personal screenshots: CPC219-1/219-2/219-5 applicants, risk, application content, prosecutor's five-day procedure and court route after rejection/inaction. Trial file access under33 and detention access under33-1 have different scopes.
- P10a: Ordinary trial, simplified trial, summary judgment and negotiated judgment under455-2, with distinct eligibility, counsel, admission and remedy consequences. They are not synonyms.
- P11a: Restoration after a missed deadline without fault, CPC67–70; ten days after the cause ceases and simultaneous completion of the omitted act. Translation difficulty or overseas residence is not an automatic excuse. Retrial and extraordinary appeal are distinct from a fourth ordinary appeal.
- C07a/C15a: Current Criminal Code80(3) rule for enumerated sexual offenses against victims under20, separated from a complaint deadline. Juvenile suspects, child victims, disability, serious violence and citizen-judge scope effective2026-01-01 require separate analysis; no blanket adult-procedure answer.
- C13a: Gambling/online betting and operator/account roles; nonsexual privacy and computer/data offenses; false credentials, weapons/restricted luggage and cross-border jurisdiction where relevant. Criminal Code4 act-or-result jurisdiction and7 conditional nationality rules must not become an unlimited extraterritorial claim.
- R03a: Prison execution and parole eligibility/process where materially relevant; do not substitute fine conversion and suspension for every execution question.
- R07a: Criminal compensation for qualifying wrongful detention/disposition, distinct from crime-victim compensation and civil damages. Preserve exclusions, institution, proof and the applicable two-year/special later-knowledge/five-year rules; do not revive deleted foreign-national reciprocity restrictions.
- R07b: Threatened witness protection: statutory eligibility, prosecutor/court and immediate-police channels, procedural limits and what a protective measure actually covers. Self-incrimination privilege alone does not answer safety concerns.
- P08/P09/R02: Existing401–404 are narrow answers. Independent complaint holders and their clocks, limits after rejection of permission for private prosecution, reopening under260, return of stolen property and post-confiscation distribution remain separate coverage checks.

These subrequirements originate in independent planning audits. Each eventual legal proposition still needs its own current official-source verification; this internal checklist is not a legal source. No country-local demand or search-ranking percentage is inferred.
