## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): N/A for guidance pack (out of scope), 3/5 for columns
variety used: Standard Swedish (rikssvenska), consistent. Dates are day + lowercase month + year; thousands use a period (1.579.589, 25.000.000) and 012 uses a decimal comma (0,5 meter). No Nynorsk. *Taiwanesisk/taiwanesiskt* agrees. *Istadarätt*, *laglott* and *folkbokföring* (戶籍) are the right Swedish terms.
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- The regulated person is still called *subjekt*: "det centrala ansvarssubjektet i kosmetikaregleringen" (011; also 016 *rättighetens subjekt*, 017 *tillståndssubjekt*).
- Imperative lists slip into the present or the infinitive: "och ordnar tillsammans lån" (016), "samt följa godkännande" (017).
- *Dra slutsats* is missing its article: "hellre än att dra slutsats enbart för att avtalet är långvarigt" (014; also 016).
- 013 and 015 still break almost every clause into its own paragraph: "Utöver marknadsläget" / "är en annan viktig punkt".
- Attorney Wei Tseng is gender-neutral where she is named (*ombud*, *Advokat*). 018’s *den ansvarige* is masculine for the responsible attorney.
- 012 is native professional apart from one phrase. 014 and 018 are close. 010 and 011 still shift a few legal objects. 018 skips from §5 to §7, as the English does.
