## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): N/A for guidance pack (out of scope), 3/5 for columns
variety used: standard Slovak (spisovná slovenčina), consistent; no Czech. Dates are already Slovak (24. 1. 2022, 5. 6. 2026, 13. septembra 2025). 012’s “0,5 metra” correctly uses the decimal comma. The site’s own Slovak form “na Taiwane” is kept; it is not switched to “Tchaj-wan”.
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- “Sídlo” stands for the foreign head office or the parent: `"Zmizne zodpovednosť sídla úplne"` (018); also `"časť sídla (本公司)"` in 011.
- Thousands use a dot, which Slovak does not: `"1.579.589 TWD"` (010); the same pattern is in 011, 017 and 018.
- Stock calques of “see / operate / disputed points”: `"Spoločne sa vidí"` (014), `"treba prevádzkovať oddelene"` (011), `"za sporné body"` (010).
- 013 and 015 still put clauses on their own lines, including a sentence that ends on a comma: `"získalo pracovné vízum i preukaz na pobyt cudzinca (外僑居留證),"` (013).
- 014 still coins labour wording: `"prejav odchodu"`, `"okamih priradenia"` for vesting, `"predplatených plnení"`.
- The no-result sentence is a stock calque: `"nesľubuje určený výsledok"` (012); also 011 and 018. The negation itself must stay.

Wei Tseng is feminine wherever she is named (010 *zástupkyňa* / *Zastupovala som* / *advokátky*; 011 *zodpovednej advokátke* / *Advokátka*; 014, 016 and 017 *Advokátka*). 013 and 015 say *advokátkou alebo advokátom*. 012’s *A* is the motorcyclist. 018 has no gendered byline. No masculine form for her remains. 012 is native professional Slovak apart from the braking-time phrase and *blinker*. 017 is close apart from three terms. 013, 015 and 018 keep consultation in English, Chinese (中文), Japanese and Korean only, and do not promise an interpreter, a reply time, a free meeting or a result. No win-rate or “best firm” claim. “Najlepší záujem dieťaťa” in 016 is the best-interests test, not an advertising superlative.
