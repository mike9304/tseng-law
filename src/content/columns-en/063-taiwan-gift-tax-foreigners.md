# Gift tax when a foreigner gives Taiwan property

[Estate and Gift Tax Act Article 3](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=G0340072&flno=3) splits the tax base by residence and nationality. An ROC national who habitually resides in Taiwan is taxed on gifts of property in or outside Taiwan. An ROC national who habitually resides abroad, and a non-ROC national, are taxed only on gifts of property in Taiwan.

[Article 7](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=G0340072&flno=7) makes the donor the taxpayer in principle. The donee steps in in the situations the statute lists, such as when the donor cannot be found or fails to pay while holding no Taiwan property.

## Exemption and rates follow the year's notice

[Article 22](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=G0340072&flno=22) lets the taxpayer deduct an annual exemption from the gift total. [Article 12-1](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=G0340072&flno=12-1) indexes that figure to consumer prices. [Tax portal item 4501](https://www.etax.nat.gov.tw/etwmain/tax-info/understanding/tax-q-and-a/national/gift-tax/exemption-calculation/JENLVwO) explains that from 1 January 2022 the exemption is NT$2.44 million per donor per year. [Taipei National Tax Bureau examples](https://www.mof.gov.tw/singlehtml/384fb3077bb349ea973e7fc6f13b6974?cntId=91cb1acdc7ca48f2881347a231c057a6) stress that several donees in one year still share one exemption.

[Article 19](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=G0340072&flno=19) applies progressive rates of 10%, 15% and 20% to the taxable net gift. Bracket amounts are also indexed under Article 12-1. Use the Ministry notice for the year of the gift. Multiple gifts in one year are aggregated and prior tax paid is subtracted.

## Filing place and deadline

[Article 24](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=G0340072&flno=24) sends resident nationals to the office of their household registration, and non-resident nationals or non-ROC nationals who gift Taiwan property to the office at the seat of the central government. [Northern National Tax Bureau guidance (30 June 2026)](https://www.mof.gov.tw/singlehtml/384fb3077bb349ea973e7fc6f13b6974?cntId=2a66b8a5fcdc447e8a27ef57b61e6c90) describes a 30-day filing window after the gift that pushes the year's total over the exemption. Extensions need written application and justified grounds.

Transfers that need registration or name change often stall without tax clearance. Signing a private gift deed without tax steps can delay the transfer itself.
