## 1. Law and facts (all four languages)

Every statement below was checked in each language; the four versions agree with each other.

- §6 item 4 quotation: exact in all four. §2-1 taxpayer: correct.
- 施行細則 §4-1 three-part definition: correctly paraphrased. 規範 二(一)2 examples (線上遊戲、廣告、視訊瀏覽、音頻廣播、資訊內容): correctly listed; the statement that subscription software and online courses are not named is true of both texts.
- 境內自然人 criteria (規範 二(二)1): domicile/residence, device installed in Taiwan, country code 886, billing address / bank account / IP / SIM: correct.
- §28-1 I–III, threshold NT$600,000 from 2025-04-07, transitional rule for sellers already over NT$480,000, counting only sales to 境內自然人 (規範 三(一)), Q11 "previous or current year": all correct and dated.
- Registration route (eTax 專區, 規範 三(二)), 稅籍登記規則 §14 documents, authentication, English-or-Chinese translation for non-English documents: correct. Agent eligibility and approval by the agent's local tax office (§28-1 I, II): correct. Jurisdiction 臺北國稅局 (§12, A15): correct.
- §10 (5–10%, 徵收率 by 行政院) and 5% for e-services (MOF 2025-05-14; also A21): correct. §36 III + §35 I bimonthly filing within 15 days of the next period regardless of sales; 施行細則 §38-1 months: correct.
- §32 II (price includes tax), §16 I (sales amount excludes the tax): correct. Arithmetic: 1,260,000 ÷ 1.05 = 1,200,000; 1,200,000 × 5% = 60,000; 1,200,000 > 600,000. Correct in all four; matches 施行細則 §32-1 formula.
- 施行細則 §32-2 FX conversion (Bank of Taiwan closing spot buying rate on the last day of the period; cash buying rate where none), NT$ payment and remittance fees (§32-2 III, 規範 五(六)): correct. Input tax credit limited to purchases used exclusively for e-service sales to 境內自然人 (§38-3, 規範 五(三)): correct (the §19 non-deductible condition is omitted, which does not overstate the credit).
- Cloud invoice duty (統一發票使用辦法 §7-1 II) and the eTax start date 2020-01-01: correct. en only: §9 I(5) foreign-language invoice and foreign-currency amounts with currency noted: correct.
- B2B: §2 item 3, §36 I quotation and proviso: exact and correctly explained. "Not required" cloud invoice for 本國營業人或機關團體 (中區國稅局 2026-07-10): correct. zh-hant only: 第74欄 and 408 繳款書: correct.
- Platform cases 規範 四(二)(三) (and zh-hant 四(四)(五)): correctly stated, including that platform commissions and the seller's receipts from the platform are outside Taiwan business tax.
- Penalties §45, §51 I items 1–3, §49, §49-1: amounts, multiples and conditions correct; time-stamped "as of October 2026".
- Income tax directive name and 2023-10-13 date: correct. §28 ordinary registration and 稅籍登記規則 §17 item 3 cancellation within 15 days: correct.
- Foreign-law notes (ko Korean tax adviser; ja 税理士; en US adviser, state rules vary): general, no foreign law stated. Compliant.
