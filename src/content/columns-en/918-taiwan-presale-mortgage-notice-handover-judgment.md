The developer's February letter asked the buyer to arrange a mortgage. The home had no connected water or electricity. Months later, the buyer was allowed inside for interior work and cleaning, yet that access still did not establish delivery under the contract. The documents described different steps, as the [judgment collected by the Executive Yuan's Consumer Protection Department](https://cpc.ey.gov.tw/File/343C8EEBB5F122CF/6abbfdf1-cf31-4198-9eec-7421dab07bb8?A=C), printed pages 173–187, shows.

On February 9, 2022, the Taiwan High Court's Taichung Branch dismissed the developer's appeal and additional claim in case 109年度上易字第289號. The obligation to pay interest for delay remained. The judgment states that no further appeal was available.

## What the February letter actually requested

Dated February 23, 2019, the letter mainly asked the buyer to contact the lending bank and report progress. It did not say the property had reached a usable condition. The utilities came later. At the initial inspection in July, defects still needed repair and the interior elevator had not been completed.

In August, the buyer signed a consent allowing entry for interior work and cleaning. The court compared that consent with the repair situation and found that entry alone did not prove the agreed handover had occurred. The loan reached the developer's account on November 5. The parties' warranty document recorded November 28 as the handover date ([paragraph 4 of the judgment](https://cpc.ey.gov.tw/File/343C8EEBB5F122CF/6abbfdf1-cf31-4198-9eec-7421dab07bb8?A=C), printed pages 182–186).

Payment was examined separately. The developer argued that receiving the February letter required the buyer to pay the entire price, but the installment schedule showed payments made as agreed and full payment before handover. The court rejected the allegation of a payment breach. A letter about the bank could not perform every contractual function at once.

## Four months in this contract, six in the current terms

Four months was the notice period agreed in this contract. Under [point 15 of the current mandatory standard terms for presale home sales](https://www.ey.gov.tw/File/17FD66C7511F7121?A=C), the seller must notify the buyer of handover within six months after receiving the occupancy permit. That provision also addresses repairs, settlement of payments and delivery of documents at handover.

Receipt of the permit starts the clock. To assess a particular notice, the contract and the rules applicable when it was signed need to be read alongside the actual permit-receipt date, the contents of the notice and when it arrived. The six months govern notification; they should not be rewritten as a universal deadline for completing every handover step.

Point 14 also imposes obligations on the buyer concerning loan documents, related procedures and payments due before registration of the ownership transfer. A dispute over handover notice does not dispose of those obligations. Each demand needs its own answer.

An [official contract inspection published in August 2025](https://www.ey.gov.tw/Page/F7408A6FCA4B0A8A/40ffbcf6-e255-47b2-a208-767af361bd11) identified a further problem: terms excluding the time taken for bank verification and loan disbursement from the handover-notice period. The Consumer Protection Department listed that practice as a violation. Bank processing time therefore belongs in the contract review, not in an assumed pause to the notice period.

## The bank's timetable and the condition of the home

The [Central Bank's real-estate lending rules](https://www.cbc.gov.tw/tw/cp-4217-192880-d9bde-1.html) were amended with effect from September 18, 2026. Buyers checking present financing conditions can record the bank's expected disbursement date separately from the developer's inspection and handover appointments. Those current lending rules are not a retrospective explanation of the 2022 judgment.

Keep the envelope too. The complete letter and evidence of arrival, together with inspection sheets, repair photographs, the access consent and the warranty, can show what was possible when the notice arrived and what happened afterward. A subject line saying “handover” cannot establish the condition of the property.

If a developer demands a penalty or interest for delay, an [email to the firm](mailto:wei@hoveringlaw.com.tw) can identify the disputed clause, the notice date and the response deadline before documents are arranged for individual review. In the file itself, label the loan request, inspection notice and handover notice according to their contents. The disagreement can then be traced to a particular obligation and date.
