## Verdict

variety used: Standard Swedish (rikssvenska), consistent. Not mixed with Nynorsk. Dates are day–month–year (24 januari 2022, 5 juni 2026, 17 september 2026). The decimal comma is already right (0,5 meters, genitive). Thousands still use a period.
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- Kinmen and Lienchiang (Matsu) are joined with *och*/*eller*: "i Kinmen (金門) och Lienchiang eller Matsu (連江、馬祖)".
- False friends still name the wrong object: "Att ett visst formulär finns" (018, a paper blank), "slutsatsen i en post" (016, one issue), "inte tillräcklig bromsmarginal" (012, time to brake).
- 013 and 015 still publish one clause per paragraph, and several breaks are only a zero-width space: "Utöver marknadsläget" then "är en annan viktig punkt".
- A multiplier and a few statute sentences stay calqued: "skadebeloppet (1 gång)"; "inte en anordning som fysiskt eller rättsligt hindrar"; "mer exakt greppa den faktiska bördan".
- The currency code stands before the amount in 011, 017 and 018 ("TWD 40.000", "TWD 25.000.000"); 010 already puts the amount first, but still with periods.
- A voting rule and a market path are telegraphed: "har 1 delägare 1 röst"; "registrering på en tillväxtmarknad" without the emerging board (興櫃).
Attorney Wei Tseng is unnamed by gender wherever she appears (010 *Jag var ombud*; 011, 014, 016 and 017 *Advokat Wei Tseng*; 018 *den ansvariga advokaten*). No masculine form for her remains. The consultation lock holds: 013 and 018 name only English, Chinese (中文), Japanese and Korean. No Swedish consult, no interpreter, no reply-time promise, no free meeting, no result guarantee. 012 is close to native professional. 014 and 016 are close; they already use *istadarätt*, *laglott*, *bröstarvingar* and *särförmögenhet*. 013 and 015 still read as a chopped post.
