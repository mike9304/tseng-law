## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): N/A for guidance pack (out of scope; part a), 4/5 for columns
variety used: standard नेपाली in Devanagari, consistent. Dates are day–month–year with Nepali month names (13 सेप्टेम्बर 2025, 4 फेब्रुअरी 2026, 5 जुन 2026). Thousands keep the Western comma of the English figures (TWD 1,579,589); Nepal grouping would be 15,79,589, but the digits match the source, so this is not given a fix row. Read times were re-estimated (for example 010 is “7 मिनेटको पाठ” where the English says 10 min) and are not treated as wrong legal figures.
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- 主體 is rendered विषय, which a reader hears as “topic”: "अनुमतिको विषय लक्ष्य कम्पनी"
- 機關 is rendered अङ्ग, which a reader hears as a limb or a constitutional organ: "त्यसका सम्बद्ध अङ्गले"
- 013 and 015 are still one clause per paragraph, and several breaks are only U+200B: "भाडा सम्झौताअघि"
- 辦法 is named विधि, which reads as a method rather than a binding regulation: "उत्पादन सूचना फाइल व्यवस्थापन विधि"
- Every column’s reading time is still “N मिनेटको पाठ”: "7 मिनेटको पाठ"
- 過失 in the damages scale is गल्ती (a casual mistake) after the same article has already used लापरवाही: "गम्भीर गल्ती भए अधिकतम 3 गुणा"
