# A Delaware LLC Investing in Taiwan: Identity, Ownership and Signing Authority

A Delaware Certificate of Formation can be valid without listing every member of the LLC. [Section 18-201 of Delaware's Limited Liability Company Act](https://delcode.delaware.gov/title6/c018/sc02/index.html#18-201) requires the entity's name and its registered-office and registered-agent details, with other matters included at the members' discretion. Those mandatory particulars alone do not supply the complete, current ownership picture that Taiwan reviewers may request when examining who stands behind an applicant.

For a US business using a Delaware LLC to form a Taiwan subsidiary, the practical task is to connect three records: the entity's legal identity, its ownership chain, and the authority of the person signing. This discussion concerns an ordinary Delaware LLC. Series structures and other US entities fall outside its scope.

## The LLC as the legal investor

Delaware law makes the formed LLC a separate legal entity. Section 18-201(b) ties formation to filing, or a specified later effective time, subject to substantial compliance with the section. Check the filed certificate and subsequent changes before using the name in the Taiwan application. A proposed investor and its owner should not be substituted for each other merely because one controls the other.

Taiwan's Ministry of Economic Affairs lists current registration or legal-status evidence and a foreign-investor qualification declaration in [CED1 of its application guidance](https://www.moea.gov.tw/Mns/dir_e/investment/wHandDirApply_File.ashx?file_id=438). Verification is generally unnecessary for those materials but may be requested. Ownership information reaching the ultimate beneficiary, the holding structure and relevant agreements may also be required. Prepare for that inquiry without assuming every LLC must submit an identical packet.

## Management depends on the LLC agreement

Who can authorize the investment? [Section 18-402](https://delcode.delaware.gov/title6/c018/sc04/index.html#18-402) places management with members by default, while permitting an LLC agreement to allocate management to managers. It also gives members and managers default authority to bind the LLC unless the agreement provides otherwise. Start with the agreement.

A title may not settle it. Review the appointment alongside any agreement provisions that limit signing, reserve approval to members, or otherwise govern authorization of the particular Taiwan investment. Where several documents record those matters, keep their dates and versions together. This is a practical review method, not a claim that Taiwan invariably requires a document called a board resolution from every LLC.

For a foreign entity without a registered Taiwan branch, the ministry's CED5 requires a verified power of attorney. It calls for the entity's and agent's names, an authorized signature with the signer's name and title, and specific authorization. Chinese translations accompany foreign-language materials. Match the granted powers to the approved transaction before sending the papers for verification.

## Tax classification answers another question

Does a single-member LLC disappear from the application because it is a disregarded entity? The [IRS explanation of LLCs](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/limited-liability-company-llc) describes a federal tax classification that depends on membership and elections. A domestic single-member LLC generally receives disregarded treatment for income tax unless it elects corporate treatment; employment and certain excise taxes have separate treatment. That description does not erase the LLC's separate legal existence under Delaware law.

Identify the actual legal investor before asking which US tax returns follow. A tax election, registration record and Taiwan investment approval serve different functions. None should silently replace the others in the file.

Before submission, compare the LLC name on the formation evidence, the ownership chart, the internal authorization and the Taiwan power of attorney. An unexplained name change or an outdated manager appointment is a specific discrepancy to resolve. Keep both versions. Place the document establishing the change beside the one that still uses the old information, so the person reviewing the packet can follow what happened.
