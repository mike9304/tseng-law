## Content-fidelity contract

### Q1

- For injury or death accidents, state the duties to render immediate aid,
  notify police, and preserve the vehicle and scene evidence.
- Explain that informal consent or a recording does not replace required
  measures.
- Distinguish moving a vehicle, with both parties' agreement and after marking
  its position and traces, to avoid obstructing traffic from leaving the scene.
- For property-only damage where the vehicle can move, mark and record the
  scene and promptly move to safety; leaving without necessary measures can
  carry administrative consequences.
- Explain that Criminal Code Article 185-4 concerns a driver leaving after an
  accident causing injury or death and requires fact-specific analysis.

### Q2

- Put personal safety and warning measures first.
- Mention `119` for injury/rescue, `110` or `112` for crime or urgent police
  situations, and an appropriate traffic-accident police report.
- After safety is secured, cover wide and close photographs of vehicle
  positions, damage, road markings, signals, and weather.
- Cover witness contacts, requests to preserve CCTV and dashcam footage,
  party/vehicle/insurance information, and medical records.
- State that private photographs do not replace required police handling for
  injury or death.
- Accurately state the police-document timing:
  - party registration/contact form at the scene;
  - scene diagram and photographs after seven days from the accident;
  - preliminary analysis report after thirty days;
  - confirm current availability and application requirements with the
    competent police agency.

### Q3

- Criminal Code Article 284 negligent injury and serious injury are
  complaint-based under Article 287; the complaint period is generally six
  months from learning the offender's identity under Code of Criminal
  Procedure Article 237.
- Civil Code Article 197 uses both periods: two years from learning of the
  damage and liable person, and ten years from the tort.
- Explain the criminal-attached civil action under Articles 487 and 488 and
  its filing window through the end of second-instance oral argument while the
  criminal matter is pending.
- State that this route ordinarily avoids a separate advance court-fee
  payment, but not every outcome is cost-free. Mention the Articles 503 and 504
  transfer/cost caveats.
- End with the case-specific factors: interruption of limitation, defendants,
  evidence, insurance, and jurisdiction; no single route is always best.

### Q4

- Criminal responsibility requires each person's breach of a duty of care and
  causation of the other party's injury. Mutual fault alone does not
  automatically establish negligent injury.
- Under Civil Code Article 217, the court may reduce or exempt damages when the
  injured person's fault contributed to occurrence or expansion of damage.
- Preserve the Korean example exactly in substance: accepted damage of
  `新臺幣 1,000,000 元` and 50% claimant fault gives `新臺幣 500,000 元`
  before other adjustments.
- State that an appraisal or preliminary analysis can be important evidence
  but does not mechanically bind the court.

### Q5

- Cover accident time/place and parties, payment amount/timing, insurance
  handling, included and reserved claims, future treatment or later-discovered
  injury, document delivery, and the relationship between payment and
  complaint withdrawal.
- Explain Civil Code Articles 736 and 737: settlement is a mutual-concession
  contract, and extinguished rights depend on the scope relinquished in the
  agreement.
- Do not say every future claim automatically disappears.
- For complaint-based offenses, explain Article 238 withdrawal before the end
  of first-instance oral argument and the bar on filing again after
  withdrawal.
- State that private settlement does not automatically end prosecution for a
  non-complaint-based offense and does not compel complaint withdrawal in
  every case.
