## Article decisions and first-two-paragraph deletion tests

### 704

The title, practical filing sequence and French terminology are suitable. *Visa de résident*, *autorisation de résidence*, *ARC* and work permission remain separate. The first-application deadline, domestic visa-issuance deadline, qualifying direct applications, document authentication, route-specific portal and payment/collection stages are understandable without implying a universal conversion right.

At lines 24 and 26, sentence-by-sentence deletion would respectively remove the operative entry deadline; its limited population; the ARC/NIA definition; the distinction from the employer's work permit; or the separate question of authorization for the actual job. The work-permission sentences are related but answer opposite mistaken assumptions. They are not generic throat-clearing. Retain both opening paragraphs.

The three FAQs address a visa/ARC distinction, an outdated school instruction and a French-language marriage document. They add usable questions and agree with the body. The last substantive paragraph (line 72) ends with checking the received card and recording its expiry rather than a generic promotional conclusion.

### 705

The opening is the reader's actual visitor-to-study decision, followed by the qualifying professional route. This supports the French reader who has encountered PVT material without making PVT the organizing subject again. Language is explicitly separated from nationality at line 32; the PVT statement is confined to French nationals at line 68.

Deleting the question at line 24 would remove the immediate decision framing; the next sentence supplies the official distinction and the third explains why later admission does not cure an unsuitable route. The second opening paragraph at line 26 supplies the professional exception and its work-authorization condition. Removing either would blur why the answer differs by intended activity. No removable preview or generic introductory sentence was identified.

Degree study and Mandarin study are distinct. Article 16 is current; migrant-worker procedures are not routed into the professional portal. Family eligibility is delimited and a family ARC is not described as automatic work permission. The article does not purport to provide a complete guide to every professional spouse's work-permit route. This omission does not change the stated rule. Its FAQs and final paragraph direct the reader to identify evidence and the current authorization's end date.

### 706

The opening consequence of losing a residence basis differs from 704's deadline opening and 705's question opening. At line 24, the first sentence identifies the risk before printed card expiry, the second preserves conditional continued-residence possibilities, and the third connects that distinction to timing. At line 26, each sentence adds the address procedure, its independent next-day deadline, or its extension to the registered workplace. Deleting these paragraphs would remove the distinction on which the article rests.

The three-month ordinary window is separated from four-month Gold Card/business-based entrepreneur windows. The entrepreneur route after loss of the business retains its separate three-month window. Address/workplace changes and residence-purpose changes use different starting days. Passport updates do not acquire an invented universal deadline; loss and re-entry procedures do not promise admission. The FAQs remain bounded to the stated situations. The final substantive paragraph (line 79) ends with the reader's actual expiry/change/departure/return dates.
