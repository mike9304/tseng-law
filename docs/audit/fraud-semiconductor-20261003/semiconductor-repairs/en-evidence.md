## Primary-source support and independent checking boundaries

The repair worker independently reopened the following official material on October 3, 2026:

1. [MOJ Chinese SHTC regulations](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawAll.aspx?pcode=J0090013), Articles 15, 15-1, 16, 17, 18 and 21; and the [official English version](https://law.moj.gov.tw/ENG/LawClass/LawAll.aspx?pcode=J0090013). The database shows amendment October 31, 2023 and compilation cutoff September 24, 2026. The Chinese text governs the precise repair: Article 16(2) includes ITA consent, which the English rendering omits, and Article 15-1 says the ITA may apply the consequences. These conditions were preserved. Article 18 gives document relief, not a permit waiver. Its NT$150,000 example does not state FOB; no FOB condition was added. The U.S./Japan waiver under Article 15 retains its separate strictly-below-NT$300,000 FOB and screening conditions.
2. [ITA code 488](https://fbfh.trade.gov.tw/fh/ap/listIERegf.do?language=C&q_type=2), current Chinese and English table. Part I requires permit paperwork for twelve equipment categories and I(3) additionally invokes SHTC procedures for SHTC items. Part II also requires a permit and MOEA assessment for goods within the applicable classification rule but outside those twelve categories. The repair accordingly ties applicability to the tariff line and does not say only those twelve categories can need a general permit. Part III's domestic-area-transfer exemption was not expanded into an export exemption.
3. [January 16, 2024 Gazette, 經貿字第11350200060號](https://gazette.nat.gov.tw/EG_FileManager/eguploadpub/eg030011/ch04/type1/gov31/num11/Eg.htm), full official HTML. 4. [90 FR 47201](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2025-09-30/html/2025-19001.htm). Publication September 30, effective September 29, 2025; foreign entities, direct/indirect and individual/aggregate 50-percent ownership; ownership-based chains included. Inline and end sources now name the original instrument.
5. [89 FR 96830](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2024-12-05/html/2024-28267.htm). This Entity List instrument is now distinguished from 89 FR 96790, which created the referenced FDP provisions. A short clarification preserves the distinction between the narrow footnote-5 jurisdictional product test and license requirements for other items already subject to the EAR.
6. [Official 50 U.S.C. 4819](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2024-title50/html/USCODE-2024-title50-chap58-subchapI-sec4819.htm), subsection (b): criminal mens rea is now stated as willful violations; the US$1 million and individual 20-year maxima were not changed.
