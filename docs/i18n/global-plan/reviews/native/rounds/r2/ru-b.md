## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): N/A for guidance pack (out of scope; part a), 4/5 for columns
variety used: Standard Russian, consistent. Dates are day–month–year in the genitive (24 января 2022, 5 июня 2026, 17 сентября 2026). The decimal comma is already right in 012 («0,5 метра»). Thousand-grouping with a dot, and the currency code before the amount, is not. Guillemets «» are used, and ё is used («ещё»). No Ukrainian or Belarusian mix.
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- A single dot still groups thousands, so «TWD 40.000» can be read as forty: "TWD 40.000–200.000" (011); the same order and dot appear in 010, 017 and 018.
- «Совместно» is still a filler for English "together": "В экспертизе совместно учитывались" (012); also 010, 014, 016, 018.
- 013 and 015 still break one sentence across lines, and several of those breaks are only U+200B: "Помимо рыночной ситуации" then "ещё одним важным обстоятельством является".
- «Единообразно» stands in for "not automatically": "Нельзя единообразно решить лишь потому, что клиент находится на Тайване" (018); also "Единообразно судить нельзя" (017).
- Paragraph citations are sometimes reversed: "Статья 15-1 части 4" (014); the same file already has the right order in "Часть 1 статьи 15-1". Also "статьёй 7 частью 1 № 7" (011).
Attorney Wei Tseng is feminine where she speaks: 010 «я выступала», 013 «я согласна». The byline is «Адвокат Wei Tseng (曾雋崴)» in 011, 014, 016 and 017. «Представителем истца» in 010 is the procedural role; the verb is feminine, and there is no masculine pronoun or title for her. 012’s «он» is motorcyclist A. No masculine form for her remains. The consultation lock holds: 013, 015 and 018 name only English, Chinese (中文), Japanese and Korean, and they refuse a reply-time promise. No Russian consultation, no interpreter, no free first meeting, no result guarantee, no win rate, no "best/only" claim for the firm. 015’s «бесплатно» is the city inquiry system, not the firm. 014 and 017 are close. 011 and 016 are strong legal Russian apart from the rows below. 013 and 015 still read as a chopped post.
