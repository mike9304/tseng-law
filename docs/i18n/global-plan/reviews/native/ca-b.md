## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): n/a for guidance pack (part a), 3/5 for columns
variety used: Central Catalan for Catalonia and Andorra, consistent: vostè, « », gemination, thousand dot (1.579.589) and decimal comma (0,5 metres). Dates follow Catalan order (13 de setembre de 2025, 24 de gener de 2022).
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- «Estatut» names a Taiwanese 條例; in Catalonia that word is the Statute of Autonomy: "Estatut d’inversió d’estrangers (外國人投資條例)" (also 017). Company "estatuts" in 018 is the right word and must stay.
- Spanish leftovers in otherwise Catalan sentences: "coherederos", "Salvo disposició", "1 destell", "s’ha d’annexar", "l’entrega del lloc".
- The apostrophe before a vowel drops, and a singular verb is paired with "junts": "després de ultrapassar-los"; "Es veu junts si el treballador"; "amb quin ordre s’hereta junts".
- Latin and English scaffolding left in the sentence: "el dies a quo", "de forma primerenca", "s’han d’operar per separat", "recollir orientació individual".
- Columns 013 and 015 are still a blog cut mid-sentence, one line at a time: "Recentment moltes persones han constituït una societat a Taiwan i han obtingut el visat de treball i el certificat de residència,".
- Amounts keep the English currency code in front ("TWD 25.000.000", "de TWD 40.000 a TWD 200.000"). The thousand dot itself is already the Catalan separator.
Gender of attorney Wei Tseng: feminine where the text marks it ("Advocada Wei Tseng", "l’advocada responsable", "una advocada o un advocat"). First person in 010 and 013 ("vaig actuar", "hi estic d’acord") does not take a masculine title. No masculine form, pronoun or title for her. The consultation refusals in 013 and 015 stay negative and read naturally ("no es promet cap termini de resposta"). 010, 011, 014 and 016 are usable legal Catalan once the terms below are fixed. 012 is short and mostly clean. 017 and 018 are the same register, with three sentences a client would misread (finestreta, cau, coherederos).
