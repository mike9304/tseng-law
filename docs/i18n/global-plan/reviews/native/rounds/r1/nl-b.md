variety used: Standaardnederlands with a consistent Flemish legal tint (gelijkaardig, anciënniteit, de kost, vervolledigen, bijkomend). systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- Chinese glosses are pasted onto the wrong Dutch word: `"geen rechtsadvies (損害賠償)"` (010); also `"feiten (民法)"`, `"bewijzen (消費者保護法)"`, `"dienstverlener (化粧品)"`.
- 主體 is still calqued as *subject*: `"het subject worden vastgelegd"` (011); also `"een subject nodig"` in 018.
- False friends name the wrong legal object: `"inlichtingendienst"` (015, an intelligence service), `"ongeschiktheid"` (010), `"formulier"` (018), `"aanmaning met inhoudsbewijs"` (010), `"arbeidsbemiddeling"` (017).
- 013 and 015 still break almost every clause into its own paragraph: `"Naast de marktsituatie"` then `"is een ander belangrijk punt,"`.
- Two closings drop the verb ending: `"bevestig men de meest recente officiële bronnen"` (016; also 017).
- Attorney Wei Tseng is *Advocaat* (the bar title for women and men) in 011, 014, 016 and 017, and first person in 010 and 013. No masculine pronoun or title for her. 012’s *hij* is motorcyclist A. 018 has no gendered byline.
