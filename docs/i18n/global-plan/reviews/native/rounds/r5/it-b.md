## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): N/A for guidance pack (out of scope; part a), 4/5 for columns
variety used: Italian of Italy, consistent. 013, 015 and the close of 018 address the reader with *Lei*; the other columns use the impersonal. Dates are day–month–year with the month in lowercase (*13 settembre 2025*, *1 luglio 2026*, *1 gennaio 2023*). The thousands separator is the point and the decimal comma is right (*1.579.589*, *0,5 metri*, *25.000.000 TWD*).
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- A few English collocations are still translated word for word: "informazione di una transazione in appello".
- Small counts in running prose are still digits: "fare 1 lampeggio dei fari".
- U+200B is still in 013 and 015, including lines whose only character is that space: "l’indicazione della regione basta".
- *Estensione* still stands in for scope throughout 014: "non deve superare l’estensione ragionevole".
- *Aggiustare* still stands in for a legal adjustment: "il tribunale può aggiustare l’importo della ripartizione".
- *Cauzione* still stands in for a guarantee or a security interest: "la responsabilità di cauzione".

012 is close to native professional aside from the sentences in the table; the Article 101 sequence and the closing disclaimer read as a native would write them. 014 is professional labor-law Italian aside from the calques below; *preavviso* and *clausola penale* are the right words, and *estinguere il contratto* is ordinary Italian. 016 is professional civil-law Italian aside from the terms in the table: *indegnità*, *successione per rappresentazione*, *riserva ereditaria*, *rappresentante speciale* and *potestà genitoriale* for 親權 are the right words, and *interesse superiore del figlio* is not an advertising superlative. 013 and 015 read as a native *Lei* FAQ once the zero-width spaces are removed and the repeated sentence in 015 is cut. 017 and 018 are professional regulatory Italian with the local defects below. Prior repairs hold: punitive damages distinguish dolo, colpa grave and colpa; the settlement is not treated as the confirmed appeal outcome; compliance with Article 101 does not itself guarantee a result; the licence in 017 does not pass automatically to the buyer; 興櫃 is *mercato emergente*; each shareholder *ha 1 voto*; a non-public company may have no *consiglio di amministrazione*. Attorney Wei Tseng is feminine wherever she is named (010 *Sono stata l’avvocata*; 011 *Avvocata* and *avvocata responsabile*; 014 *avvocata*; 016 and 017 *L’avvocata*). 013 and 015 say *avvocata o avvocato*. 015’s *un avvocato* is a generic helper at the land office. 012’s *A* is the motorcyclist. 016’s *lui* agrees with *genitore*. 018 has no gendered byline. 010, 011, 012, 014, 016 and 017 stay negative. 013, 015 and 018 name the four consultation languages; 013 and 015 say no reply time is promised. No win rate, no "best/only", no free meeting, no interpreter, no consultation in Italian.
