## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): N/A for guidance pack (out of scope; part a), 4/5 for columns
variety used: Bokmål, consistent. Not mixed with Nynorsk. Danish forms still sit in 014, 016, 017 and 018 (comma before restrictive *som*, *brud*, *genstand*, *ministeriet*, *styrelsen*, *ideell skade*, *fastholdelsesbonus*). Dates are day. month year (24. januar 2022, 5. juni 2026, 1. juli 2026, 25. juni 2026, 17. september 2026). The decimal comma is already right (0,5 meter, 1,57 millioner). Thousands still use a period.
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- One Danish word changes the meaning, and several others are only Danish: "arbeidstakerens brud"; also "tilbakebetalingsgenstand", "ministeriet for arbeid", "immigrasjonsstyrelsen".
- Kinmen and Lienchiang (Matsu) are joined with *og*/*eller*: "i Kinmen (金門) og Lienchiang eller Matsu (連江、馬祖)".
- A stock calque of "the mere fact", Danish commas before restrictive *som*, and *Ut over* for *utover*: "Den rene kjensgjerning at det finnes en underskrevet tekst"; "det beløp, som skal tilbakebetales"; "Ut over kvalitet".
- 013 and 015 still publish one clause per paragraph, and several breaks are only a zero-width space: "Utover markedssituasjonen" then "er et annet viktig punkt".
- The currency code stands before the amount in 011, 017 and 018 ("TWD 40.000", "TWD 25.000.000"); 010 already puts the amount first, but still with periods ("1.579.589 TWD").
- A digit stands where Bokmål wants a word: "1 stemme per 1 deltaker"; also "for 1 ulykke", "hos 1 part", "en 3. har båret", "blinke med frontlyktene 1 gang".
Attorney Wei Tseng is unnamed by gender wherever she appears (010 *Jeg var* prosessfullmektig; 011, 014, 016 and 017 *Advokat Wei Tseng*; 018 does not pronoun her). No masculine form for her remains. "han" in 012 is motorcyclist A. "når den tildeles ham" in 014 is the worker. The consultation lock holds: 013 says advice is not given in Norwegian; 015 and 018 name only English, Chinese, Japanese or Korean. No Norwegian Bokmål consult, no interpreter, no reply-time promise, no free meeting, no result guarantee. 012 is native professional. 010 and 011 are close. 014 and 016 already use *bindingstid*, *kontraktsbot*, *pliktarv*, *særformue*, *foreldreansvar* and *livsarvinger*, and still read as Danish-influenced Bokmål. 013 and 015 are still a chopped post.
