## Issues and minor fixes

All locations below refer to `drafts/C8/zh-hant.md`. No major issue was repaired or concealed as a wording edit.

1. Line 28 — editorial instruction in reader-facing prose.

   원문/Original: 「這裡沒有雙方當場對話的完整紀錄，不能替任何一方補上更激烈的話。」 → Problem & reason: The second clause tells a writer not to invent dialogue; it distracts from the relevant limit of the source. → Fix (applied): 「判決沒有記載雙方當場對話的完整內容。」 → Facts/conditions preserved: The judgment supplies no complete on-scene conversation. Neither a new quotation nor an inference that no words were spoken was added. Attribution and the adjacent citation remain unchanged.

2. Line 54 — unclear referent in the comparison.

   원문/Original: 「檢察官對這兩項並未採相同結論：」 → Problem & reason: “Not the same conclusion” can sound as if the prosecutor reached opposite outcomes on the two allegations. The paragraph's intended comparison is between the prosecutor and the complainant. The detailed account following the colon was already correct. → Fix (applied): 「檢察官對這兩項罪嫌的判斷，與告訴人不同：」 → Facts/conditions preserved: Both allegations, the prosecutor's distinct reasons, the limited evidentiary findings, and their status as prosecutorial analysis remain unchanged. No acquittal or separate non-prosecution disposition is asserted.

3. Line 56 — editing instruction instead of a direct procedural distinction.

   원문/Original: 「這是附件中檢察官對另外兩項罪嫌的分析，不能改寫成法院另判兩罪無罪。」 → Problem & reason: “不能改寫成” addresses the drafting process. Readers need the distinction itself. → Fix (applied): 「這是附件中檢察官對另外兩項罪嫌的分析，並非法院另就這兩罪作出的無罪宣告。」 → Facts/conditions preserved: The speaker is still the prosecutor in the attachment. The court's operative conviction is unchanged. The following conditional explanation of 想像競合, the scope of prosecution, and 不另作不起訴處分 is untouched.

4. Line 64 — editing instruction in the payment-status caveat.

   원문/Original: 「本案沒有記載實際繳款結果，不能寫成駕駛已付錢結案。」 → Problem & reason: “不能寫成” reads like an internal review note. The evidentiary limit can be stated directly. → Fix (applied): 「判決沒有記載實際繳款結果，無法據此確認駕駛是否已繳款。」 → Facts/conditions preserved: Actual payment remains unknown. There is no assertion of payment, nonpayment, execution, or finality. The conditional 易科罰金 wording, NT$1,000 daily conversion, Article 41 eligibility and exceptions, and the separate appeal/finality paragraph remain unchanged.

5. Line 70 — generic practical-tip introduction resembling adjacent drafts.

   원문/Original: 「對遇到類似糾紛的駕駛而言，從本案證據能得到一個實際做法：保存行車紀錄器時，」 → Problem & reason: The prefatory promise of “one practical approach” repeats an ending pattern found in the adjacent drafts without adding information. → Fix (applied): 「保存行車紀錄器影像時，」 → Facts/conditions preserved: The existing recommendation to retain footage of passing traffic after the stop is unchanged.
