## Audience and distinct question

Taiwan consumers whose gym has stopped providing service but whose monthly bill continues. The decision is which contract and payee support the next charge: recurring merchant charge, a prior card purchase repaid in installments, a separate designated-purpose consumer loan, or an assigned gym receivable. Membership and personal-training contracts require separate treatment. This is a civil/consumer article, not a claim that closure itself proves fraud. No real gym incident or consultation history is invented.
