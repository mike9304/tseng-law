## Accuracy

Both versions carry the same legal content, and I found no misstatement in either.

- Art. 349: 20 days from service, and an appeal filed after pronouncement but before service is effective.
- Art. 350: the notice is filed with the original court, with copies for each opposing party.
- Art. 351: a detained defendant who gives the notice to the head of the facility within the period is treated as having appealed within it.
- Art. 358: waiver and withdrawal are in writing, or orally at the trial date.
- Art. 361: the High Court hears the appeal, and the notice must give specific reasons. Missing reasons can be supplied within 20 days after the appeal period ends ("송달부터 세면 최대 40일 안팎"). If they are not supplied, the original court first orders correction.
- Art. 367: the High Court dismisses by judgment.
- Art. 3: the parties are the prosecutor, the private prosecutor ("자소인") and the defendant.
- Art. 344: a complainant or victim can ask the prosecutor to appeal, with reasons, and the prosecutor may appeal for the defendant's benefit.
- Art. 377: a third-instance appeal needs a violation of law as its ground.
- Art. 376: the full list is present and matches the official text. It covers offences with a maximum of 3 years or less, detention or fine only, CC 277(1) injury, theft, embezzlement, basic fraud, breach of trust, extortion, receiving stolen goods, and some drug offences. The proviso (acquittal, dismissal, non-acceptance or jurisdiction error reversed and convicted) and the remand rule are both present.
- Art. 382: 20 days after filing.
- Travel period: Art. 66 and Standards Art. 3, Asia 37 days. Both versions keep the caveat "판례로 확인하지 못했습니다" and the advice to work from 20 days.
- All three MUST VERIFY items are stated correctly in both versions. They are Art. 376 items and proviso, Art. 361 versus Art. 382, and Art. 344 para. 3 with the Art. 3 parties.

Omissions shared by both versions, so they do not separate them:
- Art. 376's proviso also lets "피고인을 위해 상소할 수 있는 사람" appeal, and neither version says so.
- Art. 367's carve-out (the presiding judge orders correction first when the original court has not yet done so) is missing.
- Art. 382's "no order to supplement needed" (the contrast with Art. 361) is missing.
- The remand rule is written as a general sentence in both, although Art. 376 para. 2 applies it only to appeals brought under the proviso.

These are narrowing gaps, not misstatements. I did not zero either version for them. A reviewer who wants the MUST VERIFY contrast written out should fix both.

Where the two versions differ on accuracy:
- X opens with "상해, 단순 사기, 절도, 공갈, 배임. 이런 죄는 대만에서 2심 판결로 끝납니다. 3심에 상고할 수 없습니다." This is an unconditional claim with no citation and no proviso. The proviso appears three paragraphs later, and the title and summary (identical in both versions) make the same broad claim. Y never states the bar without its source in the body.
- In X, the sentence "일부 죄는 2심 판결로 사건이 닫힙니다" and the offence list carry no 제376조 link. The link comes only after the exception and remand sentences. Y states "그 죄의 목록은 [형사소송법 제376조]에 있습니다" and links 제66조 inline. X links 제66조 only in the reference list.
- Y closes with "기간은 송달받은 날부터입니다." This repeats the starting point of the period and is accurate. It also clarifies Y's opening phrase "송달받은 뒤".

## Voice

Both versions use the 합니다체. Neither has bold text, an invented first person, a checklist heading or a generic opener.

- X: "기한부터 봅니다." is a signposting sentence, a small breach of "예고하는 문장은 생략한다". Otherwise the prose is compact and natural, for example "고소인이나 피해자는 당사자가 아니어서, … 요청하는 길이 열려 있습니다."
- Y: the Art. 376 paragraph is padded and stiff. "일부 죄는 2심 판결로 끝납니다. 그 죄의 목록은 형사소송법 제376조에 있습니다. 목록의 죄는 … 입니다. 그 죄는 3심에 상고할 수 없습니다." repeats "그 죄" and "목록" three times and says "끝납니다" and "상고할 수 없습니다" twice. The comma in "항소도, 형사소송법 제349조에 따르면 효력이 있습니다" is awkward, and "~에 따라/따르면" appears six times.

## Variety

Checker: both OK (X cv 0.499, Y cv 0.484). My own read of rule 2-2 differs from the checker's pass.

- X: the second paragraph has four consecutive sentences that end in a parenthetical citation: "…효력이 있습니다([제349조])." "…붙입니다([제350조])." "…칩니다([제351조])." "…있습니다([제358조])." Rule 2-2 allows two in a row per paragraph, and section 1 of the rule names this exact four-in-a-row pattern as the most mechanical. X also has one question and the longer sentences.
- Y: citation positions are mixed. In the Art. 351 sentence the article is a prefix ("제351조에 따라"), and "형사소송법 제3조의 당사자는" and "같은 조에 따르면" put it in the subject or the start of the sentence. The cite_end figure is 0.176, against 0.345 for X. Y also has two questions ("항소는 며칠까지일까요?", "고소인도 항소할 수 있을까요?") and short sentences at 20.6%, against 17.2% for X. Its weakness is the repeated "~에 따라" and the three-time "그 죄".

## Hook

- X: "상해, 단순 사기, 절도, 공갈, 배임. 이런 죄는 대만에서 2심 판결로 끝납니다." This is result-first and adds specifics the title lacks. A reader with an injury, fraud or theft case recognises their own offence. The third sentence repeats the second.
- Y: "형사 판결문을 송달받은 뒤, 항소는 며칠까지일까요? 20일입니다." The deadline question matches the reader who is holding a judgment, and the answer comes straight away. It restates the first half of the title, so it adds less new information than X's opening.

I rate the hooks as roughly even, with X slightly ahead.

## Net

Y is cleaner on citation rhythm and source attachment, and X is more natural prose with a stronger opening. The gap is 1 point, which the rules count as a TIE. If the editor must pick one, Y needs only wording cleanup in the Art. 376 paragraph. X needs its second paragraph re-rhythmed and the Art. 376 citation attached to the bar.

WINNER: TIE
