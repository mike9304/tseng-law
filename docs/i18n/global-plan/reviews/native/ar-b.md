## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): n/a for guidance pack (part a), 3/5 for columns
variety used: Modern Standard Arabic aimed at Gulf/Levant readers, consistent in 010–012 and 014/016–018 (guillemets «» in 015/017, comma ،, question mark ؟). 018 addresses the reader as أنتم (انظروا، رتّبوا) and is the most native of this set. 013 and 015 drop into a Korean-blog register (emoji, **نصيحة**：, مثال), chopped one-line paragraphs). Guidance-pack الإنجليزية vs 016 الإنكليزية (Levant) — same mix as part a, not Egyptian dialect. Traditional Chinese glosses (臺中, 工作許可, 外僑居留證, 未成年監護) stay Traditional and should stay. No Hangul in the body; see-also footers already point at `/ar/…`.
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- Stock calques from part a still run: "على نحو ملموس", "القضية المفردة", "وليس المعنى وجوب" (native: تفصيلًا / القضية المعيَّنة / ولا يعني ذلك أن).
- Korean-blog leftovers in 013 and 015 only: "😊", "نصيحة：", "مثال)", "س.", "هذه النقطة!", comment CTA, "في أي وقت".
- 信託 (trust) is rendered as الوصاية, the same word already used for 未成年監護 (guardianship); surviving parent becomes "الأب الباقي" (father).
- Gender for Wei Tseng is correct in 011/014/016/017 bylines ("المحامية") and 010 "شاركت"; 010 still uses masculine "الوكيل القضائي", and 015’s firm CTA uses "محامٍ".
- Korea is no longer the default reader except 011 "العلامات الكورية" and 016 "الشرح بالكورية" (English already generalized both).
- Unit-as-event calque of Korean/Chinese "1": "شاحنة صغيرة مرة واحدة", "أقل من 1 ثانية", "مثل مرة واحدة من مبلغ الضرر".

010 reports a public judgment, hedges the appeal/settlement, and uses الدرجة الأولى in words (not "الدرجة 1"). 011, 014, 016 and 017 are dense and usable once the term-errors below are fixed; 017 correctly says الشاحن for 貨主 (unlike some sister locales). 012 is short and readable until the heading turns an anonymized case into an unidentified one. 018 is the strongest of this set (tables, consultation-language hedge, تدقيق for the annual audit) except "مراقب" without 監察人. Do not treat 013’s success opening or 013/015 "في أي وقت" as polish — they are advertising-rule problems. The Korean-student facts in 010 are the actual plaintiff, not a default-reader frame; leave them.
