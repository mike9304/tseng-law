# Enforcing Taiwan child support abroad, and foreign support judgments in Taiwan

A Taiwan judgment or settlement that fixes child maintenance is a monetary claim for compulsory enforcement. [Family Act Article 189](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=B0010048&flno=189) temporarily waives enforcement fees for maintenance claims, with fees recovered from enforcement proceeds. Other Family Act enforcement features should be checked on the statute as filed.

## Bringing a foreign judgment into Taiwan

[Compulsory Enforcement Act Article 4-1](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=B0010004&flno=4-1) allows enforcement of a foreign final judgment only if none of the Civil Procedure Act Article 402 grounds apply and a Taiwan court has declared enforcement permitted. [Article 402](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=B0010001&flno=402) screens lack of jurisdiction, defective service on a defaulting defendant, conflict with public policy, and lack of reciprocity. Overview: [Enforcing a foreign judgment in Taiwan](/en/columns/enforce-foreign-judgment-in-taiwan).
