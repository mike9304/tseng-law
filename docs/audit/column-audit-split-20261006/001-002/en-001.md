## MISSING-COND

### E1 · L47·L125 (초안 L122) · 협정 §5(3)(b) 용역 PE 요건 누락 (ko-001 C8과 같은 건, 초안에 그대로 남아 있음)

- 칼럼 문장(L47, 초안 L122): "…services performed for more than 183 aggregate days in any 12-month period…"
- 원문(협정 통합본 PDF, Article 5, paragraph 3(b)): "the furnishing of services, including consultancy services, by an enterprise through employees or other personnel or persons engaged by the enterprise for such purpose, but only if activities of that nature continue (for the same or a connected project) within a territory for a period or periods aggregating more than 183 days in any twelve-month period."
  paragraph 4: "the period during which activities are carried on in a territory by an enterprise associated with another enterprise shall be aggregated with the period during which activities are carried on by the enterprise with which it is associated if the first-mentioned activities are connected with the activities carried on in that territory by the last-mentioned enterprise, provided that any period during which two or more associated enterprises are carrying on concurrent activities is counted only once."
  URL: https://www.mof.gov.tw/download/9679b16a87cd418ca479db4f947a588e (MOF 게시 통합본)
- 문제: "같은 또는 관련 프로젝트"(same or a connected project)와 특수관계 기업의 관련 활동 합산이 빠져 183일을 프로젝트와 무관하게 센다고 읽힌다. 법문은 「but only if」로 제한한다.
- 최소 수정(초안 L122의 해당 항목): "…the furnishing of services, including consultancy services, through employees or other personnel, but only if activities of that nature continue (for the same or a connected project) within a territory for periods aggregating more than 183 days in any 12-month period, with connected activities of associated enterprises aggregated and concurrent periods counted once (Article 5, paragraphs 3(b) and 4)…"
  (주어는 협정의 "the term permanent establishment also encompasses"이므로 문장 앞부분 "A PE may include"는 그대로 둬도 된다.)

### E3 · L18·L109 (초안 L95) · §38①1 "승인된 투자" 조건과 대표사무소 허가 조건 누락

- 칼럼 문장: "…covers, among others, the manager (經理人) of a company in which overseas Chinese or foreign investors collectively hold more than one-third of the issued shares or total capital, the manager of a Taiwan branch of a foreign company, and the representative of a representative office."
- 원문(審查標準 第38條①): 「外國人受聘僱從事本法第四十六條第一項第二款規定，於華僑或外國人經政府核准投資或設立事業擔任主管，應具備下列資格之一：一、依華僑回國投資條例或外國人投資條例核准投資之公司，其華僑或外國人持有所投資事業之股份或出資額，合計超過該事業之股份總數或資本總額三分之一之公司經理人。二、外國分公司經理人。三、經中央目的事業主管機關許可設立代表人辦事處之代表人。四、符合第六條第二項專案同意之具創新能力之新創事業，其部門副主管以上或相當等級之人員。」
  URL: https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=N0090031&flno=38
- 문제: 1호는 「依…投資條例核准投資之公司」, 즉 투자 승인을 받은 회사의 경영책임자다. 3호는 「經中央目的事業主管機關許可設立」 한 대표사무소의 대표자다. en은 두 조건을 쓰지 않아, 승인받지 않은 지분 구조나 허가받지 않은 사무소도 이 범주에 들어가는 것처럼 읽힌다. ko L107도 같은 문구다. "among others"는 4호(스타트업)를 포괄해 맞다.
- 최소 수정: "…the manager of a company approved for investment under the Act for Investment by Foreign Nationals (外國人投資條例) or the overseas-Chinese investment statute (華僑回國投資條例), in which overseas Chinese or foreign investors collectively hold more than one-third of the issued shares or total capital, the manager of a Taiwan branch of a foreign company, and the representative of a representative office established with the approval of the competent authority."
  (법무부 영문 제목은 "Act For Investment by Foreign Nationals" — J0040002. 華僑 조례의 영문 공식명은 이 환경에서 확인 못 해 중문명을 병기했다.)

### E4 · 초안 L110, 초안 FAQ3(L18) · F-010 문장의 강도·범위 (초안에 반영된 문장 점검)

- 초안 문장(L110): "If the same employer hires more than one person through this route, the foreign national’s and the employer’s qualifications are reviewed under the general standards in Chapter 2 instead (…Article 38(2))." / FAQ3: "…unless the same employer hires more than one person through this route, in which case the general standards apply instead."
- 원문(審查標準 第38條②): 「雇主依前項第一款至第三款規定所聘僱之人數超過一人者，其外國人、雇主資格或其他資格，應符合第二章規定。」
  URL: https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=N0090031&flno=38
- 문제: 강도가 다르다. 원문은 「應符合」(must satisfy)인데 초안은 "are reviewed under"/"apply instead"로 풀었고, 대상 자격이 「外國人、雇主資格或其他資格」 셋인데 초안은 둘만 쓴다. 범위도 「前項第一款至第三款」(1~3호)로 한정된다. 4호(스타트업)는 별도로 ③ 「雇主依第一項第四款規定所聘僱之人數超過一人者，其外國人薪資或所得報酬不得低於中央主管機關依第八條公告之數額」를 따른다. 초안의 "through this route"는 1~3호로 읽히므로 맞지만 "must"를 살려야 한다.
- 최소 수정(L110): "If the same employer hires more than one person under items 1 to 3 of this category, the foreign national’s qualifications, the employer’s qualifications, and any other qualifications must satisfy the requirements of Chapter 2 of the Standards instead (Article 38, paragraph 2)." FAQ3: "…unless the same employer hires more than one person under this category, in which case the Chapter 2 general standards must be satisfied instead."
- 공식 영문 제목은 "Qualifications and Criteria Standards for Foreigners Undertaking the Jobs Specified Under Subparagraph 1 to 6 of Paragraph 1 of Article 46 of the Employment Service Act"(법무부 영문 사이트 제목). 초안 괄호 속 제목은 줄여 쓴 번역이므로 공식 제목으로 바꾸는 편이 안전하다.

### E5 · L93 (초안 L77) · 타이베이 영업장소 사전조회는 사실상 등기 첨부서류 (ko-001 C12와 같은 건, 초안에 그대로 남아 있음)

- 칼럼 문장: "Taipei City operates an advance inquiry for business premises (營業場所預先查詢) for company and business registrations within its scope."
- 원문(타이베이시 영업장소 사전조회 시스템 안내, https://www.businesslocationinfo.gov.taipei/BLBQS/Home/Notice, 인증서 오류로 `curl -k`): 「自112年1月1日起，公司商業登記營業場所全面預審。」「公司及商業(含分公司及分支機構)辦理設立、遷址及新增營業項目登記，請申請人先以「營業場所預先查詢系統」申請查詢營業場所及營業項目是否符合土地使用分區管制與建築管理規定，並隨公司商業登記案件檢附營業場所預審符合規定之查詢結果。」「如未檢附，將以宣導函請申請人檢附預審結果，始續行辦理登記事宜。」「查詢結果效期為審核完成日起算6個月內。」
- 문제: "operates an advance inquiry"만 쓰면 선택 제도로 읽힌다. 원문은 2023-01-01부터 설립·이전·영업항목 추가 등기에서 결과 첨부를 요청하고(「請…檢附」), 첨부하지 않으면 첨부 뒤에야 등기를 이어 간다.
- 최소 수정: "Since January 1, 2023, an applicant for company or business registration in Taipei City (including branches and other branch offices) for establishment, relocation, or the addition of business items is asked to run the advance inquiry for business premises (營業場所預先查詢) first and attach the result to the registration case; if it is not attached, the office asks the applicant to submit it and continues processing the registration only after that. The result is valid for six months from the date the review is completed."
  (원문 동사는 「請」(요청)이고 「須」가 아니므로 "must" 대신 "is asked to"를 썼다.)

### E6 · 초안 L132 · 출처 라벨 "Article 3 (withholding on dividends)"는 개인 수취인만 포괄

- 초안 라벨: "[Standards of Withholding Rates for Various Incomes, Article 3 (withholding on dividends)](…G0340028&flno=3)". 법무부 영문 제목 "Standards of Withholding Rates for Various Incomes"와는 일치한다(F-009 수정은 올바른 방향).
- 원문: 扣繳率標準 第3條① 「非中華民國境內居住之個人，如有公司分配之股利…按給付額、應分配額或所得數扣取百分之二十一」, 第4條 「總機構在中華民國境外之營利事業，因投資於國內其他營利事業，所獲配或應獲配之股利或盈餘，由扣繳義務人於給付時，按給付額或應分配額扣取百分之二十一」(ko-001이 인용한 원문).
- 문제: 본문은 "dividends paid to a nonresident"(개인·법인)이고 칼럼이 다루는 사례는 외국 모회사(법인)다. §3은 개인만, 법인은 §4다. 라벨이 "withholding on dividends"로만 되어 있어 §3 하나로 법인까지 근거가 된다고 읽힌다. 숫자 21%는 둘 다 같아 본문 오류는 아니다.
- 최소 수정: 라벨을 "…Article 3 (dividends paid to nonresident individuals)"로 바꾸고 `https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=G0340028&flno=4` "…Article 4 (dividends paid to enterprises whose head office is outside Taiwan)"를 추가한다.

## OVERBROAD

### E2 · L47·L125 (초안 L122) · 대리인 PE는 "독립대리인 제외·계약체결 권한은 기업 명의" 조건 누락 (ko 공통, 초안에 그대로)

- 칼럼 문장: "…or an agent who repeatedly exercises authority to conclude contracts."
- 원문(협정 Article 5, paragraph 6·7·5): "where a person - other than an agent of an independent status to whom paragraph 7 applies - is acting on behalf of an enterprise and has, and habitually exercises, in a territory an authority to conclude contracts in the name of the enterprise, that enterprise shall be deemed to have a permanent establishment in that territory in respect of any activities which that person undertakes for the enterprise, unless the activities of such person are limited to those mentioned in paragraph 5 which, if exercised through a fixed place of business, would not make this fixed place of business a permanent establishment under the provisions of that paragraph." paragraph 7: "An enterprise shall not be deemed to have a permanent establishment … merely because it carries on business … through a broker, general commission agent or any other agent of an independent status, provided that such persons are acting in the ordinary course of their business."
- 문제: "an agent"가 독립대리인과 구별되지 않고 "기업의 명의로"(in the name of the enterprise) 조건도 없다. 중개인·일반 수수료 대리인이 계약을 맺는다는 사실만으로 PE가 생긴다고 읽힐 수 있다. 같은 문단의 "These four categories have different conditions"가 한정하지만 해당 항목 자체는 넓다. 영향은 중간 이하다.
- 최소 수정: "…or a person other than an independent agent who acts on behalf of the enterprise and habitually exercises authority to conclude contracts in the enterprise’s name (unless that person’s activities are limited to the preparatory or auxiliary activities listed in Article 5, paragraph 5; Article 5, paragraphs 6 and 7)." ("habitually"는 협정 문언 그대로이며 칼럼의 "repeatedly"보다 정확하다.)
