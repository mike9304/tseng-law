## Issues from r1 — resolved, independently verified

### F1 — Earlier crowding allegation

원문/Original → “The court never decided who crowded whom.”

Problem & reason → The r1 wording obscured the affirmative finding that the defendant twice blocked the complainant by crowding the vehicle. The undecided allegation concerned the complainant's earlier conduct, not both drivers' conduct generally. 犯罪事實與理由二㈢⒉ expressly finds “被告2度以逼車方式阻擋告訴人車輛行向” and calls the allegation about prior crowding by the complainant unrelated to that finding.

Fix (already applied by the writer; verified, no r2 edit) → At `drafts/C7/en.md:50`: “The court did not decide whether the complainant had crowded the defendant's car first.” The paragraph retains the explanation of irrelevance and an immediate judgment citation.

Facts/conditions preserved → The defendant's blocking remains a finding; the complainant's alleged earlier crowding remains unresolved. The title and later discussion concern that earlier-conduct allegation and do not erase the finding against the defendant.

### F2 — Scope of the coercion finding

원문/Original → “The judgment does not say that stopping in front of another car is coercion.”

Problem & reason → The r1 sentence could exclude blocking from coercion, although the court relied on blocking as part of the conduct it assessed. 二㈢⒉ and二㈣⒈ support the combined assessment; Article 304(1) does not make a bat or a curse a necessary element.

Fix (already applied by the writer; verified, no r2 edit) → At `drafts/C7/en.md:80`: “The court assessed the two blocking incidents, the wrong-way overtake, the bat and the curse together. Its decision does not establish an automatic rule for every stop in front of another car”, followed immediately by the judgment citation.

Facts/conditions preserved → The coercion finding and all conduct relied on remain intact. The qualification limits what can be inferred from one judgment, without creating a blanket exemption for blocking or a blanket rule that every stop is coercion.

### F3 — Failed mediation and civil damages

원문/Original → “On civil compensation it records only that mediation failed”.

Problem & reason → The judgment discusses unsuccessful mediation as a sentencing circumstance in 二㈣⒉. It does not specify a civil claim, the proposed terms, a compensation sum or a civil damages award.

Fix (already applied by the writer; verified, no r2 edit) → At `drafts/C7/en.md:82`: “The judgment records failed mediation as a sentencing factor. It does not state any civil damages award”, followed immediately by the judgment citation.

Facts/conditions preserved → The willingness to mediate, failure to agree on terms, and sentencing relevance are preserved. No civil outcome, payment history or mediation terms are inferred. Administrative penalties remain separately labelled as background.
