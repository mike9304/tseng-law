## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): N/A for guidance pack (out of scope; part a), 4/5 for columns
variety used: Bokmål, consistent. Not mixed with Nynorsk. Dates are day–month–year with a lowercase month (24. januar 2022, 13. september 2025, 4. februar 2026, 1. juli 2026, 5. juni 2026, 25. juni 2026, 1. januar 2023, 17. september 2026). The decimal comma is right (0,5 meter, 1,57 millioner, 1/3, 2/3). Thousands still use a period. 014 and 016 still use Danish single definiteness and a comma before a necessary subordinate clause.
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- Thousands still use a period: "1.579.589 TWD".
- English *may* (permission) is rendered as *må* (obligation): "automatisk må arbeide i Taiwan".
- Danish single definiteness, without the Bokmål ending: "den gjenlevende ektefelle mottar ikke nødvendigvis halvparten".
- A comma stands before a necessary *om*-clause: "Man etterprøver også, om betalingsdatoen".
- 013 and 015 still hide U+200B, and several lines are only that character: "angivelse av området er tilstrekkelig (eksempel: byen Taipei)."
- A third party is written as a numeral: "en 3. har båret den".

012 is native professional apart from one genitive. 015 is native apart from the invisible characters, *gjeldende adresse* and *10 grener*. 018 is close apart from *må* for permission. 010, 011, 013 and 017 are professional with local defects. 014 and 016 are legally careful but still read as Danish-influenced formal translation. Attorney Wei Tseng is feminine or unnamed wherever she appears (010 *Jeg var … prosessfullmektig*; 011, 014, 016 and 017 *Advokat Wei Tseng*; 018 *den ansvarlige*). No masculine form for her remains. The consultation lock holds: 013 says advice is not given in Norwegian; 015 and 018 name English, Chinese, Japanese and Korean and omit the 中文 gloss. No Norwegian consult, no interpreter, no reply-time promise, no free meeting, no result guarantee, no win rate, no "best/only". *Barnets beste* is the best-interests test. *Uten gebyr* in 015 is the Taipei location inquiry. The section jump from 5 to 7 in 018 matches the English source. Kinmen is *eller* Lienchiang (Matsu).
