## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): 4/5 for guidance pack, 4/5 for columns
variety used: ühiskeel, teie-vorm, consistent on the guidance pages and in columns 001, 002, 004 and 007. FAQ questions are the visitor’s first person („Kas ma saan“), which is normal. „Kuva kõik“ is sina-imperative. 006 is colloquial but native. 003 Q16–Q20, 005, 008 and 009 are still a personal blog. No masculine form for Wei Tseng: „ta“, „juhtiv advokaat“, „Olen Wei Tseng, advokaat Taiwanis“ are gender-neutral. „härra Lin“ (006) and „härra B“ (008) are male third parties.
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- „lubama“ is used where the sentence must deny a promise, and the first reading is a ban: "Me ei luba tõlki, vastamise tähtaega ega kohtumist selle lehe kaudu."
- Residence (居留) is called the Estonian right of residence: "ei anna tööluba (工作許可) ega elamisõigust."
- A statute (條例) is called a määrus, while the Company Act beside it is a seadus: "Tööstusinnovatsiooni määruse (產業創新條例)".
- Prose dates leave the month in the nominative: "jõustus kuupäeval 27. detsember 2023".
- Thousands use a period: "TWD 500.000 (新臺幣)" (also 002, 003, 004, 006, 007). The decimal comma in „0,5“ is right.
- 003 Q16–Q20, 005, 006, 008 and 009 still pad with U+200B blank lines: "Q16. Kas pärast õnnetust võib kõik jätta kindlustusandja hooleks?"

Consultation lock stays negative (only four languages; no interpreter; no reply-time; no free first meeting; no result promise). Household registration is „leibkonnaregister (戶籍)“. 資遣費 is „seadusjärgne lahkumishüvitis“ and the guidance pack says it is not another country’s severance or unemployment benefit. Third-party insurance is „vastutuskindlustus kolmanda isiku suhtes“, and the six-month criminal period is a kaebus. 001, 002, 004 and 007 are native professional legal Estonian aside from the rows below. 006 is colloquial but native. 005 is short and usable except the foreign-employee count.
