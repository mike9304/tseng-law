## Verdict
naturalness (1 = machine, 5 = native professional): n/a for guidance pack (part a: 2/5), 2/5 for columns
variety used: standard Finnish (yleiskieli) for readers in Finland, consistent; no Finland-Swedish mix. Formal **te**-address is used throughout (valid register), but 012/013/015 capitalise **Te** like German *Sie*, which Finnish never does. Number punctuation follows German (`TWD 25.000.000`), not Finnish (`25 000 000 TWD`). Dates drop the partitive (`24. tammikuu 2022` instead of `24. tammikuuta 2022`).
systemic patterns (max 6, each one line, with 1 example quote):
- Digit-memo style from Korean/German: "1. oikeusasteen asia", "kolmas (3)", "sija 1", "2 yhtiömiestä".
- German pivot, not Finnish legal style: "Ulkomaisten sijoitusten asetuksen" (*Verordnung* for 條例), "astuu globaalisti" (*global*), "Suoritukset, joiden edunsaaja" (*Leistungen*).
- FAQ answers pasted as body openings: sections start with "Ei." / "Se ei ole sama menettely." / "Näin ei aina ole." with no question above them (011, 014, 016, 017).
- Register split: 013/015 are leftover Korean blog (😊, `Q&A`, "jättäkää kommentti", `Esim.)`); 011/014/016/017 are stiff Kanzlei-Finnish; 012 is the one file a native could almost publish.
- Company-law vocabulary collapse: 股東 of a 有限公司/股份有限公司 rendered as "yhtiömiehet" (partnership partners); 監察人 and 會計師 both as "tilintarkastaja".
- Stock glue in every paragraph ("tutkittava", "vahvistettava", "tosiasiallinen", "lähtökohtaisesti"): readable, never how a Finnish firm writes.

Gender: no masculine form, pronoun or title for Wei Tseng. Finnish *hän* / *asianajaja* are epicene; 010 first person (*Olin tässä asiassa … oikeudenkäyntiasiamies*) and 013 (*olen tästä samaa mieltä*, *suosittelen*) do not mark sex. The male Korean student in 010 and the deceased passenger B in 012 are not the attorney. Consultation-language lock is not restated in these nine files; 012 keeps the no-result negative intact. 014 correctly splits *korvaus* (合理補償) from *korvaamismahdollisuus* (人力替補); 016 *sijaantulo* (代位繼承) is the right Finnish institute. 012 is the strongest file; 013 is the weakest; 018 is the most useful until *yhtiömies* and *tilintarkastaja*.
