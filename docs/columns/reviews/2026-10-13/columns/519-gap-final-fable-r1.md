## 1. Law and facts — claim-by-claim result (all four languages)

Each claim below was read in all four versions and compared with the live article text. Result: no unsupported, overstated, outdated or mixed-up statement found; the four versions agree on every point of law.

- 10-day period, start day excluded, rest-day rule: 第65條 → 民法 第120條 第2項 (始日不算入), 第122條 (星期日、紀念日或其他休息日 → 次日). All four say the receipt day is not counted and name Sunday, memorial day and other rest days. Titles, summaries, FAQs and both ten-day sentences are consistent with the next-day count (Grok item 5 fixed).
- Reconsideration: written reasons, through the original prosecutor, to the chief prosecutor of the directly superior office or the Prosecutor General; no lawyer requirement in 第256條 (reading of the text, correctly stated as "not required", not as a quoted rule); 第2項 應記載 period and addressee on the served copy — all four, with 應 strength ("정합니다", 「書かれていなければなりません」, "requires", 「應記載」).
- Who cannot apply: informant served under 第255條 第2項 but not an applicant under 第256條 第1項; complainant who consented to a 第253條 or 第253-1條 disposition (但書). All four. 第256條 第3項 ex officio path limited to (a) 嫌疑不足 non-prosecution in death / life / minimum three-year cases and (b) a 第253-1條 deferred prosecution — all four state exactly these two and no more.
- 第257條: 應撤銷 and continue or indict if well founded; 應即 send file and exhibits up if groundless; late request 應駁回. All four with "must" strength; the passive for the dismissal matches 第3項, which names no actor. 第4項 is not stated (recorded in facts.md; not a defect).
- 第258條: dismissal if groundless; incomplete investigation → personally / another prosecutor / original office continues; complete → order original office to indict. All four keep the split and the three actors. "Order to indict is not a conviction" is a correct inference, not presented as a rule.
- 第258-1條 第1項: 10 days from receipt of the dismissal, retained lawyer, statement of reasons, competent first-instance court; 第2項 bar for those already barred from private prosecution, with the 第321條 前段 and 第323條 第1項 前段 exceptions; 第3項 檢閱、抄錄、重製、攝影 with 限制或禁止 for another confidential investigation or other legally secret matters. All four. The exceptions are stated in the right direction (those two bars do not block the motion).
- 第258-3條: panel (合議); dismiss if 不合法 or 無理由; grant with a set period; defendant may 抗告 a grant; dismissal 不得抗告. All four, each link on its own claim.
- 第258-4條 第1項 (未於該期間內提起自訴者，不得再行自訴) and 第319條 第2項 (應委任律師). All four.
- 第66條 and the two standards: correctly described (deduction of travel time for a person whose 住、居所或事務所 is outside the court's locality). "10일에 그만큼이 더해집니다" / 「十日再加37日」 restate the deduction as arithmetic; the result is the same number of days and the statute is linked. Acceptable.
- History: 第258-1~258-4條 amended by the order promulgated 2023-06-21 (entry 50, no separate effective-date clause — the column gives the promulgation date only, correctly); 第257, 258條 2023-12-27 (entry 52); 第256條 2020-01-15 (entry 45; 256 is not in that entry's six-month-delay list); latest order 2026-05-13, 第101-1條 only (entry 55). All four source lists match the live 沿革.
