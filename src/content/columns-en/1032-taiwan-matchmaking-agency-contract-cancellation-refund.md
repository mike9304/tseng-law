# Quitting a Taiwan Matchmaking Agency: Refunds and Doubtful Members

Ten percent. That is the most a Taiwan matchmaking agency may keep as a handling fee when a client ends the contract early.

It comes from the Mandatory and Prohibited Terms for Standard-Form Dating and Matchmaking Service Contracts (交友媒合服務定型化契約應記載及不得記載事項). According to the [Executive Yuan's Department of Consumer Protection](https://www.ey.gov.tw/Page/DFB720D019CCCB0A/21b2859d-f4cb-4981-bfd9-438364060433), the Ministry of the Interior announced them on May 8, 2026 (Tai-Nei-Hu No. 1150114590) and they took effect on September 1, 2026.

An American engineer in Taipei signs up for twelve one-on-one introductions and pays NT$120,000 up front (an invented example). After three dates, two of the people he met seem to have a different job and age from their profiles. How much comes back?

## Which agencies are covered

Covered is paid, in-person matchmaking aimed mainly at starting a relationship. The explanatory notes include supporting digital tools but leave profile-browsing apps for separate rules.

Mandatory terms bind even if omitted, and prohibited terms are void ([Consumer Protection Act art. 17](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=J0170001&flno=17)(4) and (5)). Clients get at least three days to review the contract and member rules (point 1). A clause not offered for review is not part of the contract, though the consumer may still choose to rely on it ([art. 11-1](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=J0170001&flno=11-1)(3)).

What about contracts signed before September 1, 2026? The announcement is silent, so [art. 12](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=J0170001&flno=12), which voids standard terms clearly unfair to consumers, and the Civil Code apply. The notes treat the service as brokerage, like job placement. [Civil Code art. 573](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=B0000001&flno=573) denies any claim to a fee for brokering a marriage, but the text does not say whether dating introductions count or whether fees already paid come back. If the contract is a mandate, either side may end it at any time, owing damages for ending it at a time unfavorable to the other unless forced to by causes beyond its fault ([art. 549](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=B0000001&flno=549)).

## Quitting and the refund formula

Clients may end the contract at any time before it expires, and the agency may not refuse (point 16(1)). The refund is due within the days set in the contract, at most 30 from termination.

Seven days is the dividing line. Quit within seven days of the contract taking effect, before using any service, and the full contract price comes back. After those seven days, or earlier if a service has been used, the agency deducts the services already used and any video courses or books you signed off as used or opened. Used services are valued at the contract's per-session or per-period rate, or pro rata by time. The joining fee may be left out of the refund, and the handling fee may not exceed 10% of what remains (point 16(2)).

Say the engineer's joining fee was NT$6,000 and each introduction NT$9,500. Three dates cost NT$28,500. The fee cap is 10% of the remaining NT$91,500, or NT$9,150. Even after keeping the joining fee and the full fee, the agency owes NT$76,350.

Joining fees are capped at 5% of the total price (point 7). Unlisted items cannot be charged, discounted packages must state unit prices for settlement, and gifts cannot be clawed back or deducted (point 8).

Refusals count too: turning down or ignoring a set number (at least three) of suitable matches counts as one meeting (point 5). So records matter. The agency must log services, have the client confirm them, hand over the original and keep a copy for two years after the contract ends (point 12).

Void outright: forfeiting payments on breach, no refunds after first use, losing unused services after a deadline, automatic renewal, promissory notes as security (prohibited terms 2, 3, 9, 10 and 14), and any extra refund formula or penalty (prohibited term 12).

The agency may not download, log in or unseal course materials for the client; online access left unused and deactivated, or physical items returned unopened, get a full refund within an agreed period of at least seven days, and later a refund in proportion to the remaining contract days if notice is given while the contract runs (point 6).

## When the agency is at fault

Point 13 lets a client quit if the agency arranges no meeting within one month of the contract taking effect, changes terms or prices without consent, cannot continue because of serious trouble such as insolvency, or otherwise breaches the contract. The agency then refunds under point 16(2), charges no fee of any kind, and adds a penalty at the contract rate, capped at 10% of the remaining amount.

An agency, in turn, may end the contract if a client deliberately gives false personal details that seriously affect other members' service, or seriously breaches the member rules (point 14). The notes cite false ages, identity papers or forged degrees. The agency still refunds under point 16(2), and its penalty is capped at the same 10%. When neither side is at fault, as with illness or death, no penalty or other charge is allowed (point 15).

## Challenging doubtful members

Before signing, the agency must explain its services, quantities and prices orally and in writing, and that writing forms part of the contract (point 4). The contract states whether matches are members and how many members the agency currently has (point 5). Ads bind too: they must be truthful, and the agency's obligations cannot fall below them ([Consumer Protection Act art. 22](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=J0170001&flno=22)). Clauses calling ads non-binding, and words like "lifetime" or "permanent," are banned (prohibited terms 7 and 8).

Breach or fraud? That depends on the facts. A breach supports termination under point 13(1)(iv). If the agency's deception induced the contract, [Civil Code art. 92](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=B0000001&flno=92) allows rescission, within one year of discovering the fraud ([art. 93](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=B0000001&flno=93)); the money paid must then be returned as unjust enrichment ([art. 179](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=B0000001&flno=179)). Either way, save profiles, messages, the point 12 records and ad screenshots, with dates.

## Card instalments and loans

Stopping instalments does not end the contract by itself. Within 10 days of a missed due date, the agency must give 20 days to pay; if nothing comes, the contract ends when that period expires and is settled under point 16. Charges run up because the agency sent no notice cannot be collected (point 17).

Card instalment authorizations must say the agency will tell the issuer to stop charges within 24 hours of a written termination (point 9). A loan from a lender the agency introduced can be cancelled in writing within seven days of approval and ends with the matchmaking contract, though if the agency proves the ending was the client's fault, the lender may still collect instalments for services or goods already provided; undisclosed, these terms let the client treat the loan as ineffective (point 10). Prepayments above NT$50,000 must be secured for the excess by a bank guarantee, trust, escrow or similar arrangement (point 11).

## Your profile data

Client data must be handled under the Personal Data Protection Act during and after the contract (point 18). You may ask to see, copy, correct or delete it or stop its processing, rights that cannot be waived in advance ([PDPA art. 3](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=I0050021&flno=3)). Once the purpose ends, the data must be deleted or its use stopped, unless still needed for the business or kept with written consent ([art. 11](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=I0050021&flno=11)(3)). Put a deletion request in the termination notice; see [Asking a Taiwan Business for a Copy of Your Personal Data](/en/columns/taiwan-personal-data-access-copy-request). An agency that unlawfully handles data is liable for the harm unless it proves no intent or negligence ([art. 29](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=I0050021&flno=29)).

## Cross-border marriage brokering

Cross-border marriage brokering may not be run as a business, may not involve fees, and may not be advertised by anyone ([Immigration Act art. 58](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=D0080132&flno=58)); only foundations and non-profit associations licensed by the National Immigration Agency may do it ([art. 59](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=D0080132&flno=59)).

## If the agency will not cooperate

Complain to the agency, a consumer group or a consumer service center; the agency must respond properly within 15 days. Next comes the local consumer ombudsman ([Consumer Protection Act art. 43](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=J0170001&flno=43)), then the consumer dispute mediation committee ([art. 44](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=J0170001&flno=44)). [This guide](/en/columns/taiwan-online-consumer-dispute-foreigners) covers those channels.

Contracts made by phone or online without seeing the service, or after an uninvited street approach, may be distance or door-to-door sales, which the client may cancel in writing within seven days of receiving the service, without reasons ([art. 19](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=J0170001&flno=19)(1); point 16(3)), unless a distance sale falls within an Executive Yuan exception.
